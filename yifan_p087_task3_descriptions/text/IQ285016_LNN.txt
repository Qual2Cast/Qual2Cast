Lindsay Corporation supplies irrigation equipment, agricultural technology, and infrastructure products. Its irrigation portfolio includes Zimmatic center pivot and lateral systems, control panels, tracking solutions, precision irrigation technology, and replacement parts. These products help growers deliver water across fields and manage irrigation equipment. The company's FieldNET platform provides tools for remotely monitoring and controlling irrigation operations and using information to guide water application. Its agricultural offering combines irrigation machinery with digital management tools and dealer support. Customers use these products to manage field operations, water use, and irrigation system performance. Lindsay's infrastructure business supplies road safety and traffic management products, including crash cushions, end treatments, temporary barriers, gate systems, and truck-mounted attenuators. Its Road Zipper system supports the management of traffic lanes using movable barriers. Additional offerings include remote monitoring and control technologies, railroad products, and electronic manufacturing services. Lindsay also provides contract manufacturing capabilities such as steel tubing, galvanizing, metal fabrication, assembly, and kitting. The business therefore serves agricultural customers as well as organizations operating, building, or maintaining transportation and other infrastructure.
