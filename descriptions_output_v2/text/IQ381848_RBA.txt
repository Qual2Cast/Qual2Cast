Providing strategic oversight, governance, and long-term vision to ensure sustainable growth and corporate accountability across all global operations. brothers Dave, Ken, and John Ritchie held an auction to clear surplus furniture from their store in Kelowna, British Columbia, and pay off a bank note. It worked so well that the brothers founded Ritchie Bros. Auction Galleries Ltd., the start of what would grow into the world's largest marketplace for heavy equipment. Dave ran the company's first equipment auction in Radium Hot Springs. When rain threatened to shut it down, he and his brothers moved the sale under a shop's eaves and drove each piece of equipment past bidders one at a time, an improvised fix that became the "ramp-and-stage" method used at auctions for decades to come. His insistence on unreserved auctions, where the highest bid always wins, and his belief in treating customers like friends shaped the culture that carried Ritchie Bros. from a single yard in Kelowna to a global platform. That same platform now connects buyers and sellers across the world, offering more ways to sell than Dave could have imagined from that first Kelowna storefront, but still running on the same idea: let the market set the price, every time.
