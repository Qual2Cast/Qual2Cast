SAPS™ cleaning technology: for flat and patterned wafers If you’re producing both flat and patterned wafers, [COMPANY] proprietary Space Alternated Phase Shift (SAPS™) technology is your go-to. SAPS uses alternating phases of megasonic waves to deliver highly uniform megasonic energy to flat and patterned water surfaces on a microscopic level.
