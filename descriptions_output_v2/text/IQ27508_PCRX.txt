The impact of pain on America’s workforce Standing Strong is a national initiative designed to spotlight the everyday impact of pain on America’s workforce, particularly those working on their feet. The Standing Strong: The Impact of Pain on America’s Workforce survey was commissioned by [COMPANY] and conducted by Wakefield Research among 1,000 U.S. adults. Insights from family caregivers on surgery and recovery Ready, Set, Recover is a collaborative initiative from [COMPANY] and the Caregiver Action Network (CAN) designed to elevate caregiver voices and better understand the support family caregivers need when helping loved ones prepare for surgery and navigate recovery. The initiative includes findings from a survey of more than 200 family caregivers, along with a roundtable discussion and one-on-one interviews exploring their experiences. Expanding access to non-opioid options We believe everyone deserves access to safe pain management options. That’s why we proudly provided the seed funding to help establish Voices for Non-Opioid Choices, a nonpartisan coalition of more than 130 organizations dedicated to preventing opioid addiction before it starts. Voices is now the leading advocacy group on Capitol Hill for reimbursement policy reform, driving legislative changes to expand patient access to non-opioid options, including the recently passed NOPAIN Act.
