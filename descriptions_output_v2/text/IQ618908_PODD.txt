Over years of technological advances, we developed Omnipod ®, a line of tubeless, wearable, Pod-based insulin management systems. U.S. requested and prescribed AID system 2025* *Omnipod 5 product specific; Sources: dQ&A HCP AID Algorithm Study –. p.40 n=414. Omnipod provides continuous insulin delivery through a wearable, tubeless, waterproof* insulin pump called a Pod, with no multiple daily injections. These simple, connected devices offer unprecedented freedom to people living with diabetes around the world. *The Pod has an IP28 rating for up to 25 feet for 60 minutes.
