High Desert Medical College, Central Coast College and Contra Costa Medical Career College are accredited by the Accrediting Council for Continuing Education & Training (ACCET) – A Partnership for Quality® ACCET is listed by the U.S. Department of Education as a nationally recognized accrediting agency. All [COMPANY] colleges are licensed by the Bureau for Private Postsecondary Education (BPPE). Licensed to operate means the institution is in compliance with the state standards as set forth in the California Private Postsecondary Education Act of 2009, Ed. Code §94897. The Licensed Vocational Nursing programs at all [COMPANY] colleges are approved by the California Board of Vocational Nursing and Psychiatric Technicians. The Veterinary Technology program at Central Coast College has been awarded full Accreditation by the Council on Veterinary Technical Education and Activities of the American Veterinary Medical Association – AVMA. The MRI & Ultrasound Tech programs at High Desert Medical College are approved by the American Registry of Radiologic Technologists (ARRT). Pharmacy Tech programs at Central Coast College and Contra Costa Medical Career College are recognized as Pharmacy Technician Certification Board programs. The Surgical Technology program at Contra Costa Medical Career College is programmatically accredited by the Accrediting Bureau of Health Education Schools (ABHES). Integrity College of Health maintains Institutional Accreditation with the Accrediting Bureau of Health Education Schools (ABHES). The RN-BSN program at ICH is programmatically accredited by NLN Commission for Nursing Education Accreditation (NLN CNEA).
