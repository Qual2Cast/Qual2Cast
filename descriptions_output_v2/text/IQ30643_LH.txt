Real-world data (RWD) refers to health-related information routinely collected during the delivery of healthcare. Common sources include electronic health records (EHRs), insurance claims, laboratory results, registries, and digital health technologies. RWD reflects how patients are diagnosed, treated, and managed in real-world clinical practice. Real-world evidence (RWE) is the clinical insight generated through the systematic analysis of RWD. While RWD represents the raw healthcare data, RWE represents the conclusions and findings derived from rigorous analytical methods applied to that data. These insights can inform regulatory decisions, clinical guidelines, reimbursement strategies, and development planning. Traditional clinical trial data is generated in controlled research environments with defined protocols and selected patient populations. RWD reflects routine care across diverse patient populations RWE provides insights derived from analyzing that data Clinical trials measure safety and efficacy under controlled conditions RWE helps assess effectiveness and utilization in real-world settings Both approaches are complementary within evidence
