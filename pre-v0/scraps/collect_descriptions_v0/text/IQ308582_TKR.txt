Timken designs and manufactures engineered bearings, mechanical components, and motion systems for industrial equipment and machinery. Its portfolio includes engineered bearings, industrial and precision gearing, linear motion systems, chains, augers, couplings, brakes, clutches, lubrication systems, and seals. These products support the transfer and control of motion and the reliable operation of equipment. The company serves markets including aerospace and defense, power and electrification, automation, industrial equipment, infrastructure, transportation, and mobility. Its products are used in applications that require controlled movement, dependable mechanical performance, and efficient operation. The portfolio covers individual components as well as application-specific systems that combine several motion technologies. Timken also provides services and repair capabilities that support equipment throughout its operating life. Its engineering approach focuses on matching components and systems to the requirements of particular applications. Bearings, lubrication, sealing, gearing, and related products can work together to support equipment reliability and performance. The business therefore extends beyond bearings to a broader range of motion and power transmission technologies used by customers building, operating, and maintaining machinery.
