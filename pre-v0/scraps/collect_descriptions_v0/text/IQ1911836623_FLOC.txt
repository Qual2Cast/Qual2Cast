Flowco Holdings supplies equipment, technology, and field services for optimizing oil and natural gas production throughout the life of a well. Its production solutions include high-pressure gas lift, conventional gas lift, plunger lift, electric submersible pump systems, and horizontal pumping systems. These technologies address different production conditions and help operators lift fluids, sustain well output, and manage surface fluid movement. The company designs, installs, and maintains lift systems tailored to operating requirements. High-pressure gas lift supports early production, while conventional gas lift and plunger lift address changing conditions as wells mature. Electric submersible pumps provide downhole artificial lift, and horizontal pumping systems support applications such as injection, disposal, and boosting. Flowco also supplies natural gas compressors, vapor recovery units, and methane abatement technologies. These products help producers capture gas and reduce fugitive emissions. Its digital offerings include downhole gauges, remote monitoring, automation, and controls that connect field equipment with operational information. By combining equipment with maintenance, repair, monitoring, and production expertise, Flowco serves oil and gas operators seeking to manage production performance and adapt systems as well conditions change.
