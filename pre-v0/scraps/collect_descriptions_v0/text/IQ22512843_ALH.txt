Alliance Laundry Holdings operates through Alliance Laundry Systems, a manufacturer of commercial and residential laundry equipment. Its brands include Speed Queen, UniMac, Huebsch, Primus, and IPSO. The product portfolio includes front-load and top-load washers, washer-extractors, tumble dryers, stacked washer-dryer equipment, barrier washer-extractors, and ironers. Products are designed for applications ranging from household laundry to heavy-duty commercial and institutional use. The company serves self-service laundromats, shared laundry facilities in apartment buildings, hotels, healthcare and long-term care facilities, athletic facilities, restaurants, fire departments, and other organizations that process laundry on site. Its residential offering supplies washers and dryers for home use through the Speed Queen brand. Alliance combines laundry machinery with electronic controls and operating technology. Depending on the brand and product, these tools provide equipment information, remote management, and controls intended to improve laundry operations and manage water, energy, and drying performance. The business also provides customer support and service resources through its partner network. Its offering centers on supplying laundry equipment and supporting its use across commercial, institutional, and residential settings.
