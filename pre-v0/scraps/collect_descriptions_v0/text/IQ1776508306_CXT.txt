Crane NXT provides industrial technology used in payments, authentication, security, inspection, and product traceability. Its offerings combine specialized hardware, materials, software, and services for businesses, governments, and central banks. Crane Payment Innovations supplies coin and banknote validation devices, cashless payment technology, cash processing equipment, and self-service systems. Applications include retail checkout, vending, kiosks, gaming, financial institutions, transportation, and parking. Supporting software provides machine monitoring, diagnostics, transaction information, and operating analytics. Related services include maintenance and repair. Crane Currency supplies banknotes and security features, including micro-optic technology designed to make authentication easier and counterfeiting more difficult. Crane Authentication provides technologies and services for product protection, identity security, and compliance. The broader capabilities portfolio also includes inspection and track-and-trace software for applications in life sciences, food and beverage, and government. Across these activities, Crane NXT serves customers that need to accept payments, verify authenticity, protect products or identities, and monitor transactions or supply chains. Its business combines equipment and security features with software connectivity, integration expertise, and ongoing service support.
