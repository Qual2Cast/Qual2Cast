[COMPANY] offers laboratory developed tests (LDTs) performed in our CLIA-certified, high-complexity labs for four cancer types: thyroid, prostate, bladder, and breast. Our tests provide clinicians with insights based on each patient’s unique tumor biology to help guide personalized treatment decisions. To learn more, visit Technology and Pipeline. In the United States, healthcare providers order [COMPANY] tests. Patients cannot order tests directly. A healthcare provider must order them as part of clinical cancer care. For our in vitro diagnostic (IVD) tests, global healthcare providers or local hospital labs order these tests. Patients cannot order IVDs directly.
