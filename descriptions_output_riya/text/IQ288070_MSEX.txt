Steps to check your service line: Find the water meter in your basement or outside your home and look at the service line that enters the water meter. Use the house key or coin to carefully scratch the pipe surface. If it looks shiny and silver, the pipe is made of lead. A magnet will not stick to a lead pipe. If it is a dull gray color with no noticeable scratch on the surface, the pipe is galvanized steel. A magnet will stick to a galvanized steel pipe. If it is the same color as a penny, the pipe is copper. A magnet will not stick to a copper pipe. If it is smooth and red, blue, white, or black, the pipe is plastic. A magnet will not stick to a plastic pipe.
