Ethylene is a key building block used to produce a number of key derivatives, including polyethylene (“PE”) and polyvinyl chloride (“PVC”). These derivatives are used in a wide variety of end markets including packaging, construction and transportation, which in turn make life better for people every day. Like our parent company, Westlake, we are a quality manufacturer and dependable global supplier of ethylene.
