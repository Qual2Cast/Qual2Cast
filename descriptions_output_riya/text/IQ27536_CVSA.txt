[COMPANY] is America's largest healthcare educator, serving 100,000 students through five accredited institutions: American University of the Caribbean School of Medicine, Chamberlain University, Ross University School of Medicine, Ross University School of Veterinary Medicine and Walden University. Our purpose is to open doors and unlock potential—developing skilled, compassionate professionals who strengthen healthcare and improve patient outcomes in their communities. Our outcomes demonstrate this commitment: a 97% first-time residency attainment rate* at our medical schools, #1 and #3 rankings for our nursing schools (Chamberlain and Walden) and the #1 provider of DVM graduates (Ross Veterinary). Through partnerships with hospitals and healthcare providers, we’re connecting education directly to employment pathways and building sustainable workforce pipelines that support America’s healthcare needs. We reach students that traditional programs miss—working adults, career changers, and non-traditional students who thought healthcare careers were closed to them. We design education around students' lives—offering flexible pathways that make healthcare careers accessible to people who thought that door was closed. Our graduates are practice ready from day one. Through partnering with leaders such as Google Cloud and, Hippocratic AI, we work to integrate AI and technology directly into clinical education. Students develop skills in our simulation labs and complete clinical rotations at hundreds of partner hospitals and healthcare systems—learning the workflows, technology and protocols they'll use in practice.
