Products and Customers

At LSB, we leverage multiple competitive advantages to provide our customers with the products they need, while ensuring they receive the best value for their investment. Our manufacturing facilities are strategically located in close proximity to key user end markets with multiple transportation options to ensure timely delivery to our customers. Our energetic Commercial and Sales teams are dedicated to meeting our customers’ needs with quality products, security of supply and superior customer service.

Industrial

YOU MAY NOT SEE THEM, BUT YOU USE OUR PRODUCTS EVERY DAY

LSB supplies industrial and high purity metallurgical grade ammonia for many specialty applications, including water treatment, refrigeration, and power plants emission abatement. Nitric acid is also an important product in our industrial portfolio that is used to manufacture semi-conductors, nylon, and polyurethane intermediates. Our knowledge, expertise, and supply position in the nitrogen products we market, when combined with our logistical advantages, provide us with the capability to offer significant value to our customers.

MINING OPERATORS TRUST OUR PRODUCTS

Industrial grade, prilled low-density AN and AN solutions are needed by mining customers around the world as they extract coal, copper, gold, and other vital minerals. Transported in a non-explosive state to their destination mining, quarry, or construction site, our products are then altered to suit the specific use for which they are intended.

Agriculture

HELPING FARMERS TO GROW BETTER CROPS MORE EFFICIENTLY

Our agricultural products are used for growing a variety of crops and grains including corn, cotton, wheat, alfalfa, vegetables, and citrus – all across North America. Our fertilizer products – ammonia and derivative products – are used as nitrogen fertilizers that enable farmers to grow with efficacy, effectiveness, and efficiency.
