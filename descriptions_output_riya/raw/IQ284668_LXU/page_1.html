Who We Are

Formed in 1968 and headquartered in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, LSB manufactures ammonia and ammonia-related products at our facilities in El Dorado, Arkansas, Cherokee, Alabama, and Pryor, Oklahoma. We also operate a facility for Covestro LLC in Baytown, Texas. We are a vital supplier to the world’s leading chemical and industrial companies and to the U.S. agricultural market. The chemicals we manufacture are included in hundreds of products used by thousands of people every day.

LSB is committed to playing a leadership role in the production of low and no carbon products that build, feed and power the world. Our team is dedicated to building a culture of excellence in customer experiences as we deliver essential products across the industrial and agricultural end markets and, coming soon, the energy markets.

LSB is listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker “LXU.”
