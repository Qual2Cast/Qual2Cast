Solutions that Build, Feed, and Power the World

Our innovative solutions drive critical industries forward, ensuring sustainable growth and development globally.

LSB INDUSTRIES

Improving the quality of life for current and future generations by creating and delivering a variety of essential products across the industrial and agricultural end markets is nothing new for the people of LSB Industries, Inc. (LSB). But it doesn’t end there. We are committed to playing a leadership role in the production of low and no carbon products that not only build and feed the world, but power it, as well.

Perpetually guided by our Core Values – Protect What Matters, Make it Better, Practice Integrity and Be One Team – LSB is building a culture of excellence through a focus on continuous improvement, sustainable and repeatable processes and clearly defined metrics defining our success.

Whether you’re a customer, a job seeker, an investor or are simply interested in our efforts to help decarbonize our world, we’re confident you will find what you are seeking at LSB.
