Meet Nuvera

More than 120 years of commitment to a future of growth and innovation.

Nuvera is a regional broadband provider delivering premium fiber internet, voice, and television services to residential and business customers. Built on a robust fiber-to-the-premise network, Nuvera connects communities with fast, reliable technology while providing advanced business solutions that help organizations scale securely and efficiently.

Nuvera at a Glance

As a Fiber Minnesota partner, Nuvera also provides carrier-grade fiber connectivity across Minnesota, supporting wholesale and enterprise customers with 24/7 local technician support.

2026 Key Highlights
  • 50,000+ fiber passings
  • Route fiber miles: 4,075
  • 100G Core Network 
  • 5 and 8 Gig symmetrical residential speeds
Nuvera Gig Cities

Nuvera’s $200+ million Gig Cities Project is the largest fiber investment in the company’s history. Now in its fifth year, the initiative has reached 50,000 fiber passings, connecting more than 65% of Nuvera’s residential and business customers. With over 75% of construction complete, Nuvera is on pace to achieve 80% fiber coverage by 2027.

Minnesota Border-to-Border Broadband Development Grant Program

Nuvera partners with the State of Minnesota through the Minnesota Border-to-Border Broadband Development Grant Program to expand fiber-to-the-premises (FTTP) infrastructure in rural and underserved areas. These projects are delivering symmetrical 1 Gbps service to homes, farms, and businesses, far exceeding the State’s 2026 broadband speed goal.
