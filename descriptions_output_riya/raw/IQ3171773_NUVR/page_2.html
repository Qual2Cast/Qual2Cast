Network Services for Business

Designed, Monitored, and Supported Locally

Get Started

Your network isn’t a “set it and forget it” system, and neither is our support. Nuvera provides business network services that are designed for your environment, continuously monitored, and supported by local technicians who know your network.

Managed Wi-Fi 

Fast, Secure Connectivity, For Employees and Customers 

Reliable Wi-Fi is essential. Nuvera designs, installs, and supports fast, secure networks tailored to your space, without adding work for your team. 

What You Can Expect 

  • Coverage designed for your space and usage. 
  • Ongoing monitoring and performance optimization. 
  • Security controls for safe connections. 


Business-Ready by Design 

  • High-performance connectivity for critical workloads. 
  • Optional segmented employee and guest networks. 

As your business changes, Nuvera ensures your Wi-Fi evolves with your business needs.

Managed Firewall & Security

Cyber threats change constantly. Your network security should too. 

Nuvera helps protect your network with firewall solutions configured for your business and maintained by our IT team. We monitor activity, apply updates, and help block threats before they cause disruption. 

Security That Works in the Real World 

  • Customized firewall configurations. 
  • Continuous monitoring and updates. 
  • Content filtering and compliance support. 


Local Support, Built In 

Whether your firewall is hosted in Nuvera’s data center or installed at your location, our technicians can access and support it quickly, without waiting on third parties. 

Contact an Expert

Managed Switching

Smarter Traffic Control Across Your Network 

Your Local Area Network (LAN) determines how quickly and securely information moves between devices. 

Nuvera configures and supports your switching infrastructure to: 

  • Keep public and private data separated. 
  • Prioritize critical applications and systems. 
  • Monitor performance and detect issues early. 


Our team manages advanced features like IP routing and Quality of Service, so your network runs efficiently without managing all the complexity.  

Learn More

Workstation & Server Health Reporting 

Slow or unstable systems quietly chip away at productivity. 

Nuvera’s Workstation and Server Health Reporting gives you clear visibility into the health and performance of your workstations and servers. Systems are remotely monitored, and Nuvera’s team reviews the reports to help identify issues early and support smoother day-to-day operations. 

Why it matters: 

  • Fewer disruptions for employees. 
  • Faster, more informed troubleshooting. 
  • Better insight to keep systems updated and secure.
