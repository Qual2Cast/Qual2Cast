Our values

As a fast-growing public company with a startup mindset, our values guide us and reflect how we operate as individuals, team members and as a company.

Hustle

Find a Way. Creative solutions are part of our DNA, it’s a solution seeking mindset that gets the job done.

Global

Open to New Ways. We embrace our global teams and diverse perspectives. Having a global mindset is core to our mission and success.

Authenticity

Be Your Way. We say what we mean and mean what we say by bringing our true self to work. We admit and learn from our mistakes.

Accountable

Own Your Way. Take ownership of responsibilities. You’re in the driver’s seat: see it, own it, solve it, do it.

Freedom

Work Your Way. We get it, a flexible environment makes balancing life and work easier. Choose the way you work best.

Laugh

Enjoy Your Way. We spend valuable time at work that's why we make sure to have fun by celebrating big and small wins.
