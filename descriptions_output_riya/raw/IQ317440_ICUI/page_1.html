We believe the heart of healthcare is human connection.

Human connection is what turns procedures into care, clinicians into healers, and treatments into trust.

But in a world of growing complexity, it’s easy for that connection to get lost.

That’s why we design technology to support human moments, not compete with them. Tools that stay quietly in the background so clinicians can step forward. So instead of troubleshooting or juggling workarounds, they can focus fully on the person in front of them.

Because when tools safely do their job—with precision, clarity, and trust—people are free to do theirs: protect patients, solve problems, and lead with care.

We see what care demands because we listen closely to those who deliver it.

Every solution we build is shaped by deep collaboration with clinicians, pharmacists, and administrators—working together to solve real-world challenges, help eliminate preventable harm, and simplify complexity at every level.

That collaboration fuels a system of tools that are thoughtfully connected—integrating data, workflows, and insight—to help teams move faster, stay aligned, and deliver better care.

And because healthcare doesn’t wait, we don’t either. We act with urgency and flexibility, supporting clinicians with reliable and responsive partnership where and when it matters most.

Because when clinicians are supported—when tools are seamless, safe, and built around their realities—human connection thrives.

That’s how preventable harm is reduced.
That’s how trust is built.
That’s how care becomes more human.
Our Purpose

Support a healthcare experience free from preventable harm.

Because behind every safety breakthrough is a human life made safer—and a connection made stronger.

 

Our Mission 

Empower clinicians to drive measurable improvements in every patient’s care.

When clinicians are supported with clarity, precision, and trust, human connection thrives—and so does care.

 

Our Vision

Move with urgency to collaboratively develop and reliably deliver meaningful innovation that simplifies complexity, enhances precision, and helps improve safety.

We listen closely, act quickly, and never lose sight of the people at the center of every decision, every design, and every delivery.

This is just the beginning.

As healthcare evolves, we’re evolving with it—working shoulder to shoulder with clinicians helping to build a healthcare experience where fewer things go wrong, more things go right, and human connection thrives.
