DELIVERING INTEGRITY THROUGH INNOVATION

At PERMA-PIPE International Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: PPIH), integrity is at the core of everything we do. We deliver durable, high-performance pipe systems that meet the toughest standards while continuously innovating to enhance quality, efficiency, and sustainability. Guided by our core values, we remain committed to customer satisfaction, product excellence, and environmental responsibility—ensuring solutions you can trust.

With expertise in anti-corrosion coatings, insulation solutions, containment systems, custom fabrication, leak detection systems, and comprehensive technical support, field service, and logistics, we’re ready to be your trusted partner in piping system solutions.

Let’s build the future of pipe systems—together.

Supporting the Industries That Keep the World Moving

  • District Energy

    Efficient Energy Distribution
    District energy systems use pre-insulated piping to transport steam, hot water, and chilled water, providing reliable heating and cooling for commercial and industrial sites. They optimize performance, reduce costs, and enhance energy efficiency and sustainability at scale.

    District Energy Solutions

  • Energy & Industrial

    Reliable Piping Solutions
    From energy production to essential manufacturing, the energy and industrial sector drives modern economies and demands safe, reliable transport solutions. PERMA-PIPE’s pre-insulated piping and containment systems provide robust, end-to-end sealing to prevent corrosion and ensure long-lasting durability.

    Energy and Industrial Solutions

  • Mining

    Essential Mining Solutions
    Reliable piping is essential for transporting water, slurry, chemicals, and gases in demanding mining conditions. Pre-insulated pipes offer a durable solution, protecting against damage, optimizing energy efficiency, and enhancing safety. Ideal for harsh climates and remote locations, they ensure long-term performance in extreme environments.

    Mining Solutions
