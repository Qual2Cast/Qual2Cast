Why Federated Hermes?

Federated Hermes has thrived as a leading global, active investment manager. Our goal is to deliver competitive performance consistent with our clients’ needs and expectations by setting a high standard for active investment management. Our rigorous approach to vetting investment opportunities and careful attention to prudently allocating risk have been key aspects of our approach for decades.
