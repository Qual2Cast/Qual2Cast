Applications

Craft a cabin that feels like home

From immersive audio to intuitive controls, shape a cabin that sounds, feels, and responds as one.

  • Personalized Audio Playback

    Coordinate playback across seating zones and personal audio areas with precise timing and low-latency networking across ECUs.

  • Clear Sound & Voice

    Support in-car communication, telematics, and voice paths with audio converters, processing, diagnostics, and protection designed around the vehicle architecture.

  • Noise Management & Vehicle Alerts

    Synchronize road-noise cancellation, AVAS, alerts, and chimes across the vehicle while managing timing, power, and integration requirements.

  • Bespoke Digital Controls

    Use configurable haptics with reduced cognitive load to deliver clear, responsive, personalized feedback across displays and smart surfaces.

Benefits

One system. Exceptional sound and touch.

Backed by over 40 years of expertise and innovation, we give OEMs the confidence to make their vision a reality.

Keep every zone in sync

Coordinate immersive audio, voice, and tactile response with low-latency networking and precision timing across centralized, distributed, and zonal systems.

Put every watt to work

Use 48V and zonal architectures to reduce wiring weight, ease thermal load, and improve efficiency without compromising acoustic performance, control responsiveness, or cabin functionality.

Built for system-wide designs

Evaluate audio, haptics, connectivity, and timing together so performance, integration, cost, and lifecycle decisions stay aligned from the start

More function, less space

Integrated automotive devices combine power, control, diagnostics, and protection, reducing component count and board demands while enabling more capability.

Quality & support

From first evaluation to long-term production

Cirrus Logic works alongside OEM teams and ecosystem partners with the automotive experience, engineering depth, and quality discipline needed to solve complex challenges for modern in-cabin needs.

  • Quality built for automotive programs

    Build confidence for long-running vehicle programs with disciplined design reviews, qualification, process control, and lifecycle support.

  • Confidence beyond launch

    Support vehicle programs with proactive supply management and product lifecycles that typically span 10-plus years.

  • Quality you can plan around

    Rely on documented reliability testing, ISO 9001-certified quality management, and ongoing product monitoring to support consistent performance throughout the product lifecycle.
As screens replace physical controls in vehicles, the industry has recognized the need for sophisticated tactile feedback to bridge the gap. We are moving past basic vibration to advanced, high-definition haptics that simulate physical textures and clicks. This shift is essential for reducing distraction and is the primary force driving the automotive haptics market today.
Peter Cooney
Chief Technology Analyst, DATAportl
