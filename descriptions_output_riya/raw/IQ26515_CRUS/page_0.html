Who we are

Driving innovation in low-power, mixed-signal technology

Cirrus Logic is the trusted expert solving the most demanding mixed-signal challenges. Cirrus Logic develops high-performance, low-power audio and mixed-signal processing solutions for a broad range of consumer, automotive, and industrial applications. With global headquarters in the US, we invest in talent, research and development, and innovation with locations throughout the US, Europe, and Asia.
