Engineering What's Next

We create high-performance audio and mixed-signal solutions that help turn real-world signals into smarter, more seamless experiences. From sound and images to touch and data, our technology brings power efficiency and reliable performance to create differentiated user experiences across consumer electronics, including smartphones, tablets, and laptops, in addition to a variety of applications in automotive, industrial, and prosumer audio.
