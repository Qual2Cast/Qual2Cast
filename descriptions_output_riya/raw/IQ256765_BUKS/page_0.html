Corporate
Overview

Butler National Corporation is an aerospace-focused holding company that delivers engineering, aircraft modifications, electrical systems integration, commercial and defense electronics, and professional management services. Our businesses support commercial operators, defense partners, and original equipment manufacturers.

Our primary operations include Avcon Industries, BNC Tempe, and the professional services segment anchored by the Boot Hill Casino. Together, these businesses combine advanced engineering with reliable service delivery to support long-term growth.

Our Business Segments & Subsidiaries

Butler National Corporation is comprised of 5 key subsidiaries operating across industries such as aerospace, avionics, defense, and professional services.

Avcon Industries

Avcon provides FAA-approved aircraft modifications, engineering, avionics and electrical systems integration, and special mission solutions.

BNC Tempe

BNC Tempe supplies commercial and defense electronics, wiring harnesses, and system controls to OEMs and government agencies.

Boot Hill Casino

Our professional services segment provides management and operational expertise in gaming and hospitality.
