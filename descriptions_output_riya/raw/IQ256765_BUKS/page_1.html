About Butler National Corporation

Aboutbnc_it2026-08-25T09:09:03-05:00

Butler National Corporation is a public holding company with more than six decades of experience in aerospace engineering, aircraft modifications, avionics integration, special purpose electronics design and manufacturing, and professional gaming management services. Established in 1960 through the merger of an aviation research firm and the National Connector Corporation, Butler National has evolved into an aerospace-led organization serving commercial operators, defense customers, OEM’s, and agencies across the globe.

We emphasize engineering excellence, operational reliability, and long-term value creation for shareholders. Butler National is OTCQX listed under the ticker BUKS.

What
We Do

Butler National oversees subsidiaries that provide:

  • Aircraft modifications, avionics and electrical systems integration, and special mission solutions
  • Commercial and defense electronics, wire/cable harness assemblies, and system components
  • Professional management services for gaming operations

This combination of engineering capability and disciplined operational management supports sustainable growth across our business segments.

Leadership and Governance

Butler National is guided by an experienced leadership team and a Board of Directors committed to strong governance, compliance, and transparency. Our governance framework includes established committees, corporate policies, charters, and a code of ethics that guide responsible conduct across all subsidiaries.

Our Subsidiaries

Our operating subsidiaries focus on aerospace engineering and modifications, commercial electronics, and professional management services.

Avcon Industries

Avcon provides FAA approved aircraft modifications, engineering, avionics and electrical systems integration, and special mission solutions.

BNC Tempe

BNC Tempe supplies commercial and defense electronics, wiring harnesses, and system controls to OEMs and government agencies.

Boot Hill Casino

Our professional services segment provides management and operational expertise in gaming and hospitality.
