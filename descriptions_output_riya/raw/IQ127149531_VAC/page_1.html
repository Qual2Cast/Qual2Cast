Our History

Innovation. Integrity. Excellence. This is the story of Marriott Vacations Worldwide. And while the company spans brands and businesses, decades and continents, our shared inspiration continues to drive us forward: delivering unforgettable experiences that make vacation dreams come true.

Our Culture

Grounded in our culture of caring and collaboration, we empower people to live their most fulfilling lives. We believe that how we do business is as important as the business we do. We put our customers at the center of every decision we make. And we hold ourselves to the highest standards of integrity and excellence, all while having fun and winning together as one team.

Fun is at the heart of our business. Each associate helps create memorable vacation moments for our customers.

We embody a culture of caring by supporting the communities in which we live and work. And while giving back to others is at the heart of our Caring Culture, we also share a commitment to the beautiful world we all share. Our planet sustains us all, so we feel it’s our responsibility to protect it. This focus is championed by our more than 22,000 passionate associates who help lead the way through a variety of sustainable efforts at our resorts, offices, and local communities.

Matt Avril

Chief Executive Officer

Matthew (Matt) E. Avril is the CEO of Marriott Vacations Worldwide (NYSE: VAC), having previously held the position of Interim President and CEO since November 2025. He joined the Board of Directors in March 2025.
 
Mr. Avril has over 30 years of executive leadership experience in the hospitality and vacation ownership industry. He has been a self-employed consultant since March 2017 and previously served as Chief Executive Officer of Diamond Resorts International, Inc., a hospitality and vacation ownership company, from November 2016 to March 2017. Prior to that, he was Chief Executive Officer-elect for Vistana Signature Experiences, Inc. (“Vistana”), a vacation ownership business, from February 2015 to May 2016, after his retirement as President, Hotel Group, for Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide, Inc., a publicly traded hotel and leisure company (“Starwood”) – a position he held from September 2008 to December 2012. In this role, he was responsible for hotel operations worldwide for Starwood’s nine hotel brands, consisting of 960 properties in more than approximately 97 countries. He has also held senior leadership roles with Vistana and Starwood Vacation Ownership.

In addition to his operational leadership, Mr. Avril previously served as a director and Chairman of the Board of Directors of Franchise Group, Inc. from 2019 to 2023 and Babcock & Wilcox Enterprises, Inc. from 2018 to 2022. He is a certified public accountant (inactive) and has contributed his financial and governance expertise as a chair and member of various public company boards and committees. Mr. Avril’s extensive industry experience and board service provide valuable insight and guidance to the Company and its Board.

Mike Flaskey

President and Chief Operating Officer

Michael (Mike) A. Flaskey is president and chief operating officer of Marriott Vacations Worldwide (NYSE: VAC), a role he assumed in February 2026.

Mr. Flaskey is a seasoned vacation and hospitality executive with more than 25 years of leadership experience. Most recently, he served as chief executive officer and a member of the Board of Directors of Hornblower Group, a leading maritime hospitality company, from August 2024 to December 2025. Prior to that, he spent more than a decade leading Diamond Resorts International, including serving as chief executive officer from 2017 to 2021, where he led a transformation that ultimately culminated in the sale of the company to Hilton Grand Vacations. Under Mr. Flaskey’s leadership of Diamond Resorts, the company experienced a period of unprecedented growth and executed several strategic acquisitions to expand the company’s footprint. Earlier in his career, he held senior executive leadership roles with Starwood Vacation Ownership and Fairfield Resorts (now Travel + Leisure), where he developed deep expertise across brand, sales, marketing, operations and resort management. 

In addition to his operating experience, Mr. Flaskey currently serves on the board of Invited Clubs. He also previously served on the board of Holiday Inn Club Vacations

Jason Marino

Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer

Jason Marino is executive vice president and chief financial officer of Marriott Vacations Worldwide Corporation (NYSE: VAC).

Mr. Marino leads the finance, accounting and development organizations while partnering with MVW’s business executives to provide financial support and a strategic perspective to drive the overall financial results and growth of the Company.

Mr. Marino joined the Company as Vice President, Corporate Finance in 2014, where he helped drive the creation and execution of the Company’s capital efficient inventory strategy, as well as the Company’s $4 billion acquisition of Interval Leisure Group in 2018, including subsequent integration and transformation programs. He also led the Company’s acquisition of Welk Resorts in 2021 and provided critical financial leadership in the Company’s capital raising activities. Mr. Marino went on to serve as Senior Vice President, Strategy, Financial Planning & Analysis and Operational Finance – Vacation Ownership, leading a team responsible for enterprise-wide finance and business functions, including strategic planning, M&A, budgeting and forecasting, capital structure allocation and financing activities. He also led the teams providing operational financial support for the vacation ownership segment, including sales and marketing, development and inventory and revenue management.

Prior to joining the Company, Mr. Marino served as Managing Director and head of business development and corporate finance at Cantor Commercial Real Estate, as well as Director of Real Estate Investment Banking at Credit Suisse Securities. While at Credit Suisse, he worked with public and private companies in the real estate, lodging and timeshare industries helping clients raise capital and drive strategic growth.

Mr. Marino received a Masters of Business Administration from Cornell University, Johnson Graduate School of Management.

Raman Bukkapatnam

Executive Vice President and Chief Information Officer

Raman Bukkapatnam serves as chief information officer of Marriott Vacations Worldwide Corporation (NYSE: VAC).

A member of the company’s executive leadership team since 2023, Mr. Bukkapatnam leads the company’s efforts to modernize the technology footprint across global sites and resorts, improve processes to maximize productivity for associates, drive adoption of automation, and deliver new digital tools to improve the vacation experience for Owners, members, and guests.

Mr. Bukkapatnam is a strategic leader with over 25 years of experience in technology consulting, retail, consumer products, manufacturing, media, and supply chain. Prior to joining Marriott Vacations Worldwide, he served as Vice President, Global Technology at Nike, Inc., where he played a critical role in reshaping digital retail, improving enterprise resource planning and supply chain platforms, and making significant contributions to productivity, growth, and margin improvements.

Prior to Nike, Mr. Bukkapatnam had a more than 20 year-career at Starbucks serving in multiple leadership roles. As a Global Vice President at Starbucks, he was responsible for overseeing and leading critical functions like global supply chain and finance technologies, data engineering and analytics, and store development. He led a number of digital transformation initiatives impacting the company’s technological landscape, including Starbucks’ introduction of customer mobile applications.

Mr. Bukkapatnam holds degrees in both Civil Engineering and Computer Sciences.

Tony Walker

Executive Vice President and Chief Sales and Marketing Officer​​

Tony Walker is executive vice president and chief sales and marketing officer for Marriott Vacations Worldwide Corporation (NYSE: VAC). 

A seasoned sales and marketing executive with more than two decades of leadership experience in the vacation ownership and hospitality industry, Mr. Walker oversees the company’s global sales and marketing strategy, with a focus on driving growth, strengthening execution, and building high performing, culture driven teams. 

Mr. Walker brings deep expertise across sales, marketing, and operations, having held senior leadership roles at several leading hospitality and vacation ownership organizations. Earlier in his career at Travel + Leisure, he served as Vice President of Sales and Marketing, where he opened and scaled multiple sales operations, including flagship locations in Orlando, St. Louis, and Las Vegas. In these roles, he led teams that consistently delivered strong performance and profitability. 

He later joined Starwood Vacation Ownership as Vice President of Sales and Marketing, where he led the company’s two Orlando flagship resorts—Sheraton Vistana Resort and Sheraton Vistana Villages—through record turnarounds and sustained growth. Mr. Walker went on to join Diamond Resorts, initially serving as Senior Vice President of the West Business Unit with full P&L responsibility for the company’s largest and most profitable division. He was subsequently promoted to Chief Sales and Marketing Officer; a role he held until Diamond Resorts was acquired by Hilton Grand Vacations in 2021. 

During his tenure at Diamond Resorts, the company consistently achieved industry leading VPG and tour growth, supported by a strong culture first approach and the development of innovative sales and marketing capabilities that enabled sustained revenue and adjusted EBITDA growth. 

Most recently, Mr. Walker served as Chief Sales Officer of Hornblower Corporation, where he led the company’s overall sales strategy and transformed its enterprise sales organization. 

Scott Weisz

Executive Vice President and Chief Hospitality Officer

As Executive Vice President and Chief Hospitality Officer, Scott Weisz leads Marriott Vacations Worldwide’s owner services, resort operations, asset management, and architecture and construction functions.

Mr. Weisz previously led the company’s modernization efforts as Executive Vice President, Strategic Business Operations. During his more than 20 years with the company, Mr. Weisz has held a variety of leadership roles, including Senior Vice President, Global Inventory & Revenue Management, where he led inventory and revenue management strategies for MVW’s vacation ownership portfolio. Prior to that role, he oversaw a portfolio of 18 resorts and eight sales galleries from north Florida to Boston as Senior Vice President of Vacation Ownership for the Americas East region.

Mr. Weisz joined Marriott Vacation Club International in 2002 as Director of Feasibility and Business Planning after completing the management development program with Marriott International at the JW Marriott in Washington, D.C. Early in his career with MVW he held leadership roles in feasibility and business planning and asset management, where he was involved in new project development, delivery and optimization. Mr. Weisz has also served on several resort condominium association boards of directors, including as president for two associations.

Mr. Weisz holds a Bachelor of Science degree in hotel administration from Cornell University. Outside of his role at MVW, he is currently a member of the Orlando Health Foundation Philanthropy Council and the Orlando Health Children’s Foundation Philanthropy Council, having previously served as Chair of both. He also chairs the American Resort Development Association Resort Owners’ Coalition Board of Directors (ARDA-ROC) and is an Advisory Board Member for the Greater Orlando Sports Commission (GO Sports).

Marcos Agostini

Executive Vice President and Managing Director, Interval International

Marcos Agostini is executive vice president and managing director for Interval International, an operating business of Marriott Vacations Worldwide Corporation (NYSE: VAC).

Also a member of the company’s executive leadership team, Mr. Agostini joined Interval International in 1993 in the company’s Latin America operations area and has held increasing roles of responsibility in both operations and sales.

Prior to his current role, Mr. Agostini led global sales and business development for the Interval International exchange program, as well as other membership programs offered by Interval. He was responsible for oversight of all business development teams and initiatives, client services and program operations.

He is an active participant in ARDA, and a member of the ARDA International Foundation board of trustees.

Mr. Agostini studied international business at the University of Westminster in London.

Vladimir Anokhin

Chief Strategy and Transformation Officer

As Chief Strategy & Transformation Officer, Vladimir (Vlad) Anokhin leads transformation initiatives, data analytics, enhanced adoption of artificial intelligence capabilities, and product development for Marriott Vacations Worldwide. In this role, he is focused on helping strengthen alignment across the business, advancing key enterprise priorities, and positioning the Company for long-term success. He joined the Executive Leadership Team in August 2026.

Mr. Anokhin brings extensive leadership experience across hospitality, vacation ownership and financial services. Most recently, he served as Chief Strategy Officer at Invited Clubs, the largest owner and operator of private golf and country clubs, where he helped advance the company’s analytics, technology and business performance capabilities.

Prior to Invited Clubs, Mr. Anokhin served as Chief of Staff and Global Head of Product Development at Diamond Resorts, where he helped drive companywide strategic initiatives and led the development of new products and experiences for owners. Earlier in his career, he held investment banking roles focused on real estate, lodging and leisure, including advising on the sale of Vistana Signature Experiences to Interval Leisure Group in 2015.

Mr. Anokhin holds an MBA from Columbia Business School and has earned CPA and CFA qualifications.

Denise Haeggberg

Chief Human Resources Officer​

Denise Haeggberg is chief human resources officer of Marriott Vacations Worldwide Corporation (NYSE: VAC), where she leads the company’s global people and talent strategy.  

With more than 20 years of experience in the vacation ownership industry, Ms. Haeggberg brings deep institutional knowledge to the company’s executive leadership team and plays a key role in strengthening organizational effectiveness and performance. Prior to assuming her current role in February 2026, she served as senior vice president of global talent management, where she helped foster the collaborative culture for which Marriott Vacations Worldwide and its brands are known. With prior senior leadership roles at Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide Inc., Starwood Vacation Ownership, Vistana Signature Experiences, Inc., and Interval Leisure Group, Ms. Haeggberg has spent the majority of her career in the travel and vacation ownership industries. Earlier in her career, she held leadership and consulting roles supporting organizations through talent and organizational initiatives.

Ms. Haeggberg holds a Ph.D. in Industrial/Organizational Psychology and a Master of Science in Industrial/Organizational Psychology from Ohio University. She earned a Bachelor of Science in Psychology from Xavier University, graduating summa cum laude.

Cameron Klaus

Chief Brand and Communications Officer​

As Chief Brand and Communications Officer, Cameron Klaus leads Marriott Vacations Worldwide’s global brand strategy, enterprise communications, community impact, creative services and digital customer experience functions, helping advance the company’s reputation, strengthen stakeholder engagement and drive long-term brand value. 

Prior to assuming her current role in August 2026, Ms. Klaus led the company’s communications function for nearly four years. During her tenure, she built the communications function into an integrated enterprise capability, strengthening executive communications, associate engagement, media relations, crisis preparedness and stakeholder trust while supporting periods of significant business growth and transformation.

Before joining Marriott Vacations Worldwide, Ms. Klaus led global communications and public relations for Tupperware Brands during a transformative period in the company’s history. She advised senior leaders and partnered across marketing, operations, supply chain, investor relations and corporate social responsibility to advance strategic priorities and strengthen corporate reputation. Earlier in her career, she spent nearly a decade at Weber Shandwick, where she counseled some of the world’s most recognized brands on reputation management, employee engagement, crisis communications and strategic communications planning.

Ms. Klaus earned a Bachelor of Arts in Public Relations from the University of Georgia.

Andrew Marcus

General Counsel

As general counsel and a member of the company’s executive leadership team, Andrew Marcus oversees an international legal team supporting all of the company’s brands and global operations. In previous leadership roles with the company, Mr. Marcus has provided legal oversight for global sales and marketing, new product innovation and structuring, government affairs and advocacy, information and technology, privacy, intellectual property, labor and employment, non‑U.S. operations, and the Interval International exchange and travel benefits business.

Prior to joining Marriott Vacations Worldwide, Mr. Marcus served as chief legal officer and chief operating officer of an Orlando‑based timeshare resort and golf course developer. Earlier in his career, he was a partner in the Orlando office of BakerHostetler, where he focused on real estate and related business law with an emphasis on the hospitality industry.

A member of the Florida Bar, Mr. Marcus holds a bachelor’s degree from the State University of New York at Binghamton and a Juris Doctor, with honors, from the University of Florida, where he served as an editor of the Florida Law Review. He is an active participant in the American Resort Development Association (ARDA), having served on its Legislative Policy Council and as chair of its Federal Issues Committee, State Legislative Committee, and Resales Forum. He also serves on the industry advisory board for the University of Florida’s Department of Tourism, Hospitality & Event Management and the Eric Friedheim Tourism Institute.
