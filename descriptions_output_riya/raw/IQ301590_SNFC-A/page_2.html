Our Companies

We are built upon our family of companies that keep us strong. Each company is unique, but they all carry the same positive impact on our community.

Security National Life Insurance Company

Security National Life Insurance Company is a market leader in “end of life” insurance products and services. Based on conservative and sound financial principles, you can be assured that we are only interested in long-term relationships and business strategies.

C&J Financial

Did you know that C&J financial is the Leader in Insurance Assignment Funding? Their services relieve the funeral industry of the hassle, headache and cash flow delay in processing insurance death claims by providing the fastest funding possible while offering the friendliest, most knowledgeable and courteous customer service available.

Security National Commercial Capital

Did you know that Security National Commercial Capital enhances the mortgage banker’s traditional long-term lender relationships? With a faster closing, flexible, interim loan product, Security National Commercial Capital provides a bridge until a property stabilizes and conventional long-term financing can be obtained.

Memorial Mortuaries and Cemeteries

Memorial Mortuaries & Cemeteries is Utah’s pre-eminent funeral and cemetery service provider. Memorial operates three different brands of funeral homes, totaling 10 different locations; Memorial Mortuaries & Cemeteries, Probst Family Funerals & Cremations, and Affordable Funerals & Cremations.

• Dedicated to providing peace of mind
• Committed to treating families as their own
• 60+ years of industry experience

Proud Best of State Winner

Memorial Mortuaries and Cemeteries have been the proud winner of Best of state for the past eight years, highlighting our top-quality funeral services and expertise to Utah residents and their families.
