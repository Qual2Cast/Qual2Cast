ABOUT US

About Old Republic

Old Republic is a leading provider of specialty insurance, built on over a century of disciplined underwriting, financial strength, and consistent performance.

Built On Discipline. Trusted For The Long Term.

Since 1923, we have operated with a clear and consistent approach. We take a measured view of risk, make decisions with accountability, and stand behind the commitments we make. Our focus is on protecting the businesses we serve and supporting them through changing conditions.

Backed by strong financial ratings, we provide the stability and reliability upon which our clients depend on.
