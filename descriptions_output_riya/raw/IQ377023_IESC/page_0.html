Who We Are

IES designs and installs integrated electrical and technology systems
and provides infrastructure products and services to a variety of end markets,
including data centers, residential housing, and commercial and industrial facilities.
Our 16,000+ employees serve clients in the United States.

We’re committed to the growth of our customers, employees, investors, and communities.






Strengthening Our Structure



IES is proud to introduce our newest line of business: IES Structural.

Through our acquisition of DBM Global,
one of the largest structural steel fabrication and erection platforms in the US,
IES Structural greatly expands our capabilities and capacity
in engineering, fabrication, erection, and industrial services.

By adding more steel to our structure, the future has never looked stronger!

“We’re excited to welcome DBM Global’s talented team to the IES family.
DBM Global brings scaled engineering, fabrication and erection capabilities
and a long track record of successfully delivering complex structural projects,”
says Matt Simmes, President and CEO of IES.

“DBM Global is a premier business with an outstanding team and
substantial long-term growth potential, and is an excellent addition to IES,”
adds Jeff Gendell, Executive Chairman of IES.

Rustin Roach, President and CEO of DBM Global, says,
“Joining IES marks an exciting next chapter for our businesses and our people.
Together, we can accelerate investment in our operations and
bring additional capabilities and capacity to our customers’ projects.”
