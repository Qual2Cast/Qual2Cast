About Us

Throughout our history we’ve focused on providing safe, reliable and responsible energy service. It’s been a core motivation since 1889. Today, we are on the cusp of a very big shift. There’s a new generation of technology that has the potential to provide our customers and communities with more efficient ways to use and manage energy. In a world characterized by an expanding array of options competing for attention, our focus is to offer solutions that will benefit our environment, provide value to our customers and contribute to a healthy and vibrant community in the areas we serve.

Together, we are Avista — past, present, and future — driving innovation, fostering community, and enabling progress."

- Heather Rosentrater, President and Chief Executive Officer

Our Mission

We enable vibrant communities through energy. Safely. Responsibly. Affordably. We put those we serve at the center of everything we do.

Join our team and move energy forward.

Our employees are motivated by a deep commitment to customer-centered service and innovation. We are always looking for passionate, hard-working individuals to join our team. Learn more about our current opportunities.
