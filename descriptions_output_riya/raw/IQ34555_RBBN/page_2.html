Solutions

IP Routing, Optical Networking & Cloud Communications Solutions for Service Providers & Enterprises

Contact a Network Solutions Expert
Lottie file

Ribbon is a global leader in secure cloud communications software solutions for service providers, enterprises, independent software vendors, systems integrators and developers. Ribbon's communications solutions are widely deployed in over 1000 customers globally, provide high scale, reliability and performance, and are deployable from the cloud (public, private, hybrid), in-network or on the enterprise premise.

Ribbon's IP Routing and Optical Networking solutions delivers integrated IP routing, packet transport, optical networking and automation solutions that enable organisations to design, build and operate secure, resilient and scalable communications networks,with the agility to evolve as business needs evolve. The portfolio combines service-aware IP routing, deterministic packet transport, coherent optical networking and AI-driven automation to optimise network performance, simplify operations and accelerate service delivery, with these solutions Service Providers increase network agility, improve operational efficiency, reduce total cost of ownership and create a foundation for future growth

Ribbon's service provider solutions range from fixed network transformation, mobile network evolution, secure network interconnects, network functions virtualization, cloud communications and security solutions enabling secure and innovative business and consumer services offers.

Ribbon's enterprise solutions range from legacy Nortel PBX evolution, secure unified communications, collaboration, contact center, Microsoft S4B/Teams, session management and innovative cloud communications solutions to enable highly productive communications experiences for employees and customers using the web, mobile and fixed endpoints and clients.

Ribbon's mission critical solutions are designed for utilities, rail and transportation operators, defence organisations, and government agencies that depend on highly reliable, secure and scalable communications networks. As a recognised leader in network modernization, Ribbon's solutions are particularly well suited to organisations undertaking network transformation initiatives, whether retiring legacy SDH/SONET infrastructures, converging IT and OT services, breaking down traditional IP and optical network silos through integrated IPoDWDM, IPoOLS and IPoOT , or evolving from proprietary vendor-specific environments to open, disaggregated and highly automated networks.
