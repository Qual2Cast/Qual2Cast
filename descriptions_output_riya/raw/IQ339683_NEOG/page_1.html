Neogen® solutions can be found at every step of the food chain

Our comprehensive range of data-driven solutions and services for the food safety and animal health industries help to protect the world’s food supply. We’re also proud to serve the companion and performance animal industries, as well as the fields of life sciences and toxicology.

With this ever-advancing portfolio, we’re made to do more, empowering customers around the world to produce and operate more proactively, efficiently, and confidently than ever before. Together, we can help safeguard your business, your customers, and the world that you nourish.

We make the job easier.

Our data-driven solutions are delivering reliable results quicker, setting new standards for both accuracy and efficiency in the food safety world.

It started in 1982

Scientific innovations blossoming out of Michigan State University needed an avenue to reach the people who could use them to better the world — solutions searching for problems. The university reached out to the men who would become Neogen’s founders, Ted Doan and James Herbert, and soon after, the company was born.

What started as a small team that held organization-wide meetings in a single hallway in Lansing, Michigan expanded into new industries and new states, soon reaching across one ocean, then another. Along the way, our goal solidified: to partner with our customers, farm to fork, to elevate the world’s level of food and animal safety.

Today, we’re achieving that by providing solutions for a vast array of needs all along the food supply chain. It doesn’t matter the business segment, field, or industry — we offer tools our customers use to make their products stronger and safer, while growing their businesses and protecting their brands. We do so across the globe, with a commercial presence in nearly 40 countries.
