The Lindblad Family of Brands

Travel That Transforms

Travel that inspires curiosity and fosters connection has the power to transform—inviting us to engage more deeply with the places we visit, the people we meet and the natural world we share. Rooted in a long heritage of meaningful exploration, Lindblad Expeditions has brought together a collection of travel companies united by a commitment to crafting journeys that immerse guests in remarkable places while upholding the highest standards of responsible travel.

Together, these brands open pathways to exploration, from walking tours to wildlife safaris, offering travelers diverse and deeply meaningful ways to discover the world.

How You See it Matters

Scroll to Learn More

With the Lindblad family of brands, journeys are designed with intention—prioritizing meaningful access, close connection and a deep respect for the landscapes and communities that make each destination unique. While each brand brings its own expertise and point of view, all are guided by the same belief: that small, thoughtfully crafted experiences—led by knowledgeable guides and rooted in authenticity—have the power to transform the way you see the world.

Photograph by: Sean Neilson

How You See it Matters

Immersive Exploration

These companies open doors to places few others can access, offering rare, once-in-a-lifetime moments. Itineraries are crafted from decades of local insight to reveal your destination’s wild beauty and rich human spirit, whether you’re cycling through the idyllic fishing villages of Puglia or rafting the Snake River into the heart of the Grand Tetons.

Photograph by: Branka Dajak

How You See it Matters

Small Groups

Small groups unlock deeper access and more meaningful experiences. With fewer travelers, you’ll spend more time immersed in your surroundings, enjoying the flexibility to linger during a bear sighting in Alaska or share stories with a local winemaker on the Amalfi Coast. It’s an opportunity to connect more deeply to the landscapes and communities you explore—and to your fellow travelers.

Photograph by: patitucciphoto

How You See it Matters

Expert Guides

Whether you’re traveling with someone who calls your destination home, or a seasoned expert who has spent a lifetime studying it, you’ll be guided by those who know it best. Capture exceptional photos of Galápagos wildlife with a top photographer, wander Roman ruins alongside an archaeologist or explore the Serengeti ecosystem on foot with a Maasai guide. Special guests include local luminaries and distinguished voices, such as scientists, authors and master chefs.

Photograph by: Andrew Peacock

How You See it Matters

Premier Accommodations

Accommodations are thoughtfully selected for comfort and character. They may include boutique hotels, tented safari camps or historic castles. No matter where you travel, you’ll stay in places that celebrate your destination’s natural and cultural treasures.

Photograph by: Court Whelan

How You See it Matters

Local Cuisine

With these companies, you’ll find a focus on authentic flavors that reflect the land and its people—because the table is where culture comes to life. Whether it’s a Michelin-starred meal, farm-fresh produce from a family-run estate or a guided wine tasting with a sommelier, every bite and sip has a story to tell.

Photograph by: Andrew Peacock

How You See it Matters

Responsible Travel

Each of our partners is dedicated to minimizing environmental impact, giving back meaningfully to communities and conservation efforts, and inspiring guests to deepen their connection to the planet—and their commitment to protecting it.

Photograph by: Carlos Aguilera

White Desert

For more than 20 years, White Desert has pioneered luxury expeditions into Antarctica’s remote interior—a realm reached by only 1 percent of the roughly 100,000 people who visit the continent each year. Guests land on an airstrip of blue glacial ice and stay at intimate camps set amid Antarctica’s vast expanse. Expert-led journeys reveal everything from blue rivers and sculpted ice tunnels to an emperor penguin colony and the geographic South Pole. Every journey reflects White Desert’s commitment to responsible exploration, and the same aviation expertise that carries guests into the interior helps scientists reach research stations across the continent.

Learn More

Echo Charlie Modern Odysseys

Echo Charlie Modern Odysseys reimagines the golden age of exploration through aerial journeys into some of the world’s most remote and culturally rich places. A meticulously restored 1940s DC-3 carries just 12 guests on expeditions connecting destinations from Peru and Patagonia to Colombia and Guatemala. Every flight offers a front-row view as glaciers, jungles, deserts and coastlines unfold below, while immersive experiences on the ground reveal hidden places and ancient cultures. Throughout, the romance of exploration is complemented by refined comfort and rare access, creating journeys that are both intrepid and effortless.

Learn More

Natural Habitat Adventures

For more than four decades, Natural Habitat has been connecting travelers to the closest wildlife encounters that nature has to offer. Driven by a mission of conservation through exploration—and strengthened by a longstanding partnership with the World Wildlife Fund—every naturalist-led journey is meticulously crafted to foster a deeper understanding of our planet and inspire a lasting commitment to its future.

Learn More

DuVine Cycling + Adventure Co.

DuVine's luxury cycling tours are an opportunity to refresh your perception of the world. Informed by 30 years of expertise, itineraries cater to riders of all abilities with a unique focus on cuisine and wine—because every destination is revealed through its flavors. Local guides facilitate connections across cultures—like a candlelit dinner at the historic home of a winemaker in Alentejo, Portugal—followed by restful recovery at exceptional hotels, such as La Residencia in Mallorca, Spain.

Learn More

Classic Journeys

Set out on a walking adventure that fuels your soul, one step at a time. With well-connected local guides as your cultural hosts, you’ll explore at eye level, meeting artisans, chefs, vintners and other remarkable residents along the way. Each handcrafted journey invites you to slow down, savor meaningful moments and return home transformed by the experience.

Learn More

Off the Beaten Path

Since 1986, Off the Beaten Path has been planning distinctive journeys that are deeply connected, localized and true to place in every way. In each of their destinations, spanning five continents, OBP forges real friendships with local people who share the position that immersive, authentically rich encounters deliver travel of a higher order. Not a vacation, but a journey; not a tour, but an experience; not a moment, but a memory for a lifetime.

Learn More

Thomson Safaris

With more than 40 years devoted to revealing Tanzania’s wild beauty, Thomson thoughtfully crafts safaris that immerse you in extraordinary wildernesses from the the Serengeti to the Ngorongoro Crater. Guided by Tanzanian naturalists and featuring elegant, sustainable accommodations, each safari champions conservation and fosters connection with local culture and wildlife.

Learn More
