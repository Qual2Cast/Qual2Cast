Expedition Stories

Lindblad Expeditions: A Pioneering Legacy

“We have always had a very distinct north star,” says Lindblad Expeditions Founder Sven Lindblad. “If we can provide people with extraordinary experiences in the world’s most charismatic places … they form a connection with the natural world that is truly profound.”

That guiding principle can be traced back to 1966, when Sven’s father, Lars-Eric Lindblad, led the first non-scientific voyage to Antarctica, pioneering expedition travel and charting a new course for modern exploration. Sixty years later, that same spirit endures as a global legacy of discovery—driven by curiosity, guided by purpose and rooted in a deep commitment to the planet.
