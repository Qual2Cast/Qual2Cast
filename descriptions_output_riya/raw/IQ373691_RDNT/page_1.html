ABOUT RADNET

Leading Radiology Forward

RadNet is the national leader in outpatient imaging. Backed by 45+ years of experience, we deliver high-quality, cost-effective solutions for all 442 ctr (as of 6/30/26) in our network—and for our strategic partners.

Our Mission

RadNet’s mission — comprehensive radiology solutions — aligns with the future of healthcare.

We are the only radiology provider with the resources to create greater opportunities for our strategic partners. We leverage our national profile and a broad portfolio of clinical and technical services to support best outcomes.

We make it easy for everyone involved in healthcare decisions — administrators, physicians, IT professionals, technologists — to work together. Above all, that includes patients. Recent findings show that patients who are actively involved in their healthcare experience better outcomes and incur lower costs.

Our Values

Our focus is simple. We deliver high quality, conveniently accessible care in the most cost effective manner possible. Quality, access, and value are at the core of what we do — in every new imaging center we establish, every health system that we partner with, every patient we encounter. That is the RadNet difference.

Get to Know RadNet

  • Founded in 1981 – over 4 decades of experience.
  • Company has quadrupled in size since 2006.
  • Publically Traded (Nasdaq: RDNT); RadNet’s management owns over 25% of common stock.
  • 442 locations (largest in the United States), about 1,000 contracted radiologists and more than 11,000 employees worldwide.
  • Largest provider of radiology clinical AI solutions worldwide.
  • Regional networks in Arizona, California, Delaware, Idaho, Indiana, Florida, Maryland, New Jersey, New York, Texas and Virginia.
  • Emphasis placed on scale and multi-modality strategy – one stop shopping for referral sources and health plans.
  • Facilities offer state-of-the-art equipment in a modern and patient friendly setting.
