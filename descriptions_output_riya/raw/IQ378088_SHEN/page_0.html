WHY SHENTEL
TV, Phone, & Internet Services You Can Trust

Shentel has been here for you for more than 100 years. We’re committed to providing the phone, TV, and internet services you need to stay connected throughout Virginia, West Virginia, as well as in locations in Kentucky, and Maryland.


Questions? Ask Away.

High-Speed Internet refers to a reliable broadband connection that delivers fast download and upload speeds. Unlike older technologies like DSL, it allows for seamless streaming, gaming, and multi-device connectivity without lag or buffering.
Shentel customers praise the service for its reliability, speed, and excellent customer support. Many highlight the consistent performance for streaming, gaming, and remote work, as well as the transparent pricing with no hidden fees or contracts.
Shentel’s High-Speed Internet is designed for reliability, offering strong connectivity with minimal disruptions.

Choosing the right Shentel High-Speed Internet for your home depends on how many devices you have, the number of family members in your home, your household's online activities, and the speed you need to enjoy seamless connectivity. Here’s a quick guide to help:

400 Mbps: Ideal for small households with moderate internet use.

600 Mbps: Great for larger families, remote work, and 4K streaming.

1 Gbps: Best for power users, gaming, and smart homes with multiple connected devices.

Shentel’s reliable network delivers consistent speeds with low latency, so whichever plan fits your needs, you’ll enjoy a stable connection. We can help you choose based on your needs.

Shentel offers a variety of TV solutions to fit your entertainment needs, with a great selection of live channels, local programming, popular networks, HD programming, and premium options like sports and movie packages, all with an easy-to-use interface. Customers can also enjoy DVR capabilities, On Demand content, and access to streaming apps. With flexible plans, no contracts, and convenient viewing across multiple devices, Shentel TV gives you a seamless entertainment experience tailored to your needs.
Switching to Shentel High-Speed Internet means faster speeds, more reliable service, and strong whole-home Wi Fi coverage. Installation is easy, and our local customer support is available to assist at every step.
Customers receive a monthly bill with clear breakdowns of service charges. Payment options include online, phone, automatic draft, or by mail. Auto Pay is available to avoid late fees. By enrolling in Auto Pay and Paperless Billing you can get a monthly discount on your bill.
Shentel’s customer support team is made up of real people from your local area who are ready to assist you when you need help. When you call, you’ll be connected to a knowledgeable representative, who understands our services and can provide quick, reliable solutions. Whether you need technical support, have billing questions, or just need guidance on your service, our team is available to help. You can reach our customer support by phone at 1-800-SHENTEL (1-800-743-6835), online chat, or by visiting one of our local offices.
