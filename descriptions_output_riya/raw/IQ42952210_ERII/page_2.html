Desalination

As the global need for freshwater increases, desalination—the process of turning saline water into freshwater—is becoming more important. Energy Recovery’s solutions lower the energy consumption of seawater reverse osmosis (SWRO), making it more efficient and cost-effective.

Find Your Product View Testimonials

How it works

The challenge

Droughts, extreme weather, and growing populations are driving many water-stressed regions to look for diversified and sustainable water production strategies to build a secure water future. SWRO desalination can reliably produce clean water by physically filtering it through a membrane. Although this process can be energy intensive, energy recovery devices significantly lower the energy consumption of water production, making it cost-effective while reducing emissions.

Without an Energy Recovery Device (ERD), a high-pressure pump pushes water into a reverse osmosis system where there is rejected seawater that ends up being wasted energy. Equipped with a PX® Pressure Exchanger®, the rejected water is fed back into an ERD that recovers energy and then powers a circulation pump that reduces energy demand on the high-pressure pump—creating a desalination process that’s both optimized and maximizes savings, making desalination affordable®.

Industry Applications

Brackish Water RO Systems

Brackish water reverse osmosis (BWRO) systems are increasingly critical for treating challenging water sources in industrial and municipal operations. These systems demand high energy input, driving up operational costs—especially at scale. Energy Recovery’s PX® Pressure Exchanger® technology delivers a high-efficiency solution, significantly reducing energy consumption and improving cost-effectiveness in brackish water desalination. Whether treating produced water, surface water, inland brackish groundwater, or industrial wastewater streams, PX technology enables efficient, sustainable operations—even under fluctuating feedwater conditions.

Learn More

Large-Scale RO Systems

Water scarcity is a major challenge for many regions around the globe. Energy-intensive, large-scale desalination systems offer the solution, and PX technologies enable unparalleled energy recovery when producing significant amounts of clean water from seawater or brackish water.

Learn More

Modular RO Systems

While a small-scale desalination solution still needs to meet high energy requirements, it’s more viable than ever before. PX® Pressure Exchangers® and turbochargers enable unmatched savings and reliability for small-scale, compact, and modular seawater reverse osmosis (SWRO) desalination systems.

Learn More

Retrofits

Whether you operate a reverse osmosis system with no energy recovery device (ERD), an aging ERD that has been in service for 15 to 25 years, or a plant ready to expand, our PX® Pressure Exchanger®, turbochargers, and pumps are built to integrate into existing infrastructure and deliver measurable results.
