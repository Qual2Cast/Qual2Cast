Government Solutions

AAR provides parts, comprehensive maintenance and supply chain programs, and shelter and sustainment systems to governments across the globe.

AAR's customized government solutions enhance readiness, wherever the mission takes you.

Parts

As your trusted aircraft parts supplier and distributor, AAR delivers a broad range of products across all major commercial and government platforms globally.

Government Programs

AAR's comprehensive Government Programs increase readiness and decrease costs by applying commercial best practices to support the warfighter across the globe.
