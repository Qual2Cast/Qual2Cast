Differentiated aviation aftermarket solutions

AAR delivers award-winning aerospace and defense services to airlines, OEMs, and governments across the globe.

The AAR advantage

Based in the Chicago area since 1955, AAR is the number one aviation maintenance, repair, and overhaul provider in North America. With a unique combination of industry-leading parts, repair, government solutions, and software, AAR is a trusted partner to commercial and government customers worldwide.

AAR CORP.

AAR is a global aerospace and defense aftermarket solutions company with operations in over 20 countries. Headquartered in the Chicago area, AAR supports commercial and government customers through four operating segments: Parts Supply; Repair, Engineering, and Software; Government Solutions; and Legacy Commercial Programs. Additional information can be found at aarcorp.com.
