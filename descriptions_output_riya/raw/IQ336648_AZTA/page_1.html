Enabling Breakthroughs Faster

Azenta life sciences is dedicated to enabling life sciences organizations around the world to bring impactful breakthroughs and therapies to market – faster.

Azenta encapsulates our commitment to helping customers reach new heights in their pursuit of scientific progress. By integrating our industry-leading capabilities, Azenta Life Sciences enterprise-wide sample exploration and management solutions will accelerate discovery, development and delivery, with greater speed and precision. At Azenta, we strive to keep elevating each other, our customers’ work and our industry – building a healthier world for people everywhere.

Customer Impact

AskBio

AskBio is a leader in the gene therapy space, focusing on the development of new gene therapy technologies to address debilitating genetic diseases. The technologies and treatments make significant impact to patient lives, and the Azenta genomics solutions for AAV enable them to overcome their challenges around AAV analytical approaches.

Cornell Veterinary Biobank

The Cornell Veterinary Biobank, the first ISO 20387 accredited biobank, faced challenges in finding the right tools and technology to enhance its processes and comply with ISO standards. They worked with Azenta on supplies and equipment to improve their processes and ensure quality assurance, while also exploring innovative technologies for collection, cryopreservation, transportation, and storage.
