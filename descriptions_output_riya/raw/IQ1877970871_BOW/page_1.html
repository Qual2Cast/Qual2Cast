FAQs

Bowhead Specialty provides tailored primary and excess insurance coverage solutions across three core divisions: Professional Liability, Healthcare Liability, and Casualty. Visit our appetite page for details of coverages offered.

We serve a wide range of clients, from well-established public companies to growing private businesses and nonprofits. Our underwriters work to provide custom coverage for complex risks across multiple industries.

We don’t believe in one-size-fits-all policies. Our underwriting-first model is backed by deep sector expertise, advanced analytics, and technology. Our core craft solutions are custom-built to meet the specific needs, risk profiles, and goals of our clients.

Bowhead Specialty has expertise across a wide array of industries, including but not limited to construction, manufacturing, financial services, healthcare, hospitality, distribution, and technology.

Yes. We offer both primary and excess coverage across multiple lines, including General Liability, Professional Liability, and Healthcare Liability, giving clients the flexibility to build layered programs that align with their risk management strategies.

We offer both admitted and non-admitted coverage options, depending on the line of business and jurisdiction. This allows us to provide flexible solutions tailored to regulatory and client-specific needs.

Bowhead Specialty offers solutions in all 50 US states. Our business is primarily written on an E&S (Excess and Surplus) basis. We have offices in New York City, and Chicago, and teams located across over 30 states.
