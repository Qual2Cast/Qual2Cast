    Frequently asked questions

    How can I upgrade from GPON to XGS-PON with readiness for future technologies?

    The versatile cOS platform and family of network edge and middle mile devices ease deployments and upgrades. Learn more in the solution brief.
    Contact us to learn more about broadband services

    How can I maximize speeds per subscriber?

    The Beacon Intelligent Speed Maximizer runs on cOS platform to leverage edge compute data for greater scalability, security, network reliability, and responsiveness.
    Explore cOS Central

    Do I have to deploy fiber to achieve multigigabit speeds?

    No, you don't. You can boost DOCSIS speeds with the cOS platform's vCMTS to achieve fiber-like speeds with your existing network. Check out the BoostD 3.1 capability to learn more about how to boost broadband speeds.

    Can I deploy PON fiber technologies with my DOCSIS network?

    Watch this  video how to deploy XGS-PON leveraging your existing DOCSIS network. 

    How can I save power and space to reduce costs and meet sustainability requirements?

    Save money on space and power costs with a virtualized CMTS. Learn more about how in this article.

    How can I streamline my operations and reduce truck rolls?
