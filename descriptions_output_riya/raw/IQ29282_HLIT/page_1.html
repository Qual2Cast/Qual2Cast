    Powering the Future of Broadband

    Harmonic, the global leader in virtualized broadband, is transforming how operators deliver multi-gigabit connectivity. Our industry-leading cloud-native cOS™ platform featuring AI-powered network intelligence and end-to-end managed services empower broadband service providers to simplify their networks. We enable operators worldwide to streamline operations, boost revenues and ensure that subscribers enjoy a superior, reliable internet experience. Backed by a massive global deployment footprint and a legacy of innovation, Harmonic is the trusted partner for the next generation of broadband.

    Revolutionizing broadband networks with market‑leading technology

    Harmonic is driving the market to multi-gigabit broadband services and greener operations. We develop cloud-native, virtualized broadband solutions to make intelligent connectivity possible and provide a pathway to a 10G future.
