Every day, healthcare professionals see patients needing mental health treatment. As a trusted member of their care team, they look to you to provide guidance on their path to well-being. LifeStance Health’s clinicians treat a broad range of conditions and are in-network with more than 240 insurance plans. In most states, patients can be scheduled for therapy and/or psychiatry services in less than one week.

    Why Refer Your Patient to LifeStance Health

    As a healthcare provider, you want nothing but the best for your patients. When your patient receives care at LifeStance, you can be sure they are receiving the compassionate, specialized care they deserve. LifeStance Health employs psychiatrists, psychologists, psychiatric nurse practitioners, and licensed therapists who practice as a unified care team to create personalized treatment plans and ensure continuity of care.

    LifeStance Health offers both in-person and online therapy and psychiatry appointments. Our hybrid model allows patients to overcome traditional barriers to receiving care. We meet your patients where it’s most convenient for them.

    Expert Personalized Care

    Behavioral health integration supports whole person care, and LifeStance makes that integration efficient and accessible. With LifeStance, providers can partner with a structured behavioral health system that reduces fragmentation and ensures consistent, high-quality care for patients of all ages and stages. Together, we can improve patient outcomes faster, reduce overall health care costs and streamline operations, allowing you to focus on what you do best—providing exceptional care.

    Dr. Ujjwal Ramtekkar Chief Medical Officer at LifeStance Health

    Stay Involved in the Care Your Patient Receives

    When you refer someone to LifeStance Health, you gain a collaborative team. We are your partners in caring for your patients. We keep you informed of your patient’s treatment plan so you can take their mental health needs into account as part of your overall treatment plan.

    Care Access Pathway

    LifeStance’s Care Access Pathway is a dedicated referral solution developed in partnership with health systems, provider groups, and enterprise partners to streamline access to high-quality outpatient behavioral health care.

    Designed in collaboration with our trusted CAP partners, this streamlined process guides individuals through every step—from initial outreach to continuing care—using best-in-class referral pathways and personalized support. CAP is built to reduce administrative burden, close the care loop, and strengthen continuity of care — while empowering partners with meaningful data to support population health goals. Most importantly, CAP ensures that patients are quickly and compassionately connected to the care they need, when they need it most.
