CHICAGO GAMING

Video Gaming Terminals are coming to Chicago.

Chicago is on the verge of a major policy shift that could open new opportunities for local businesses. As part of the city’s recently passed budget, video gaming terminals (VGTs) are set to become legal within city limits—ending a long-standing municipal ban and aligning Chicago with the rest of Illinois, where regulated video gaming has been in place for more than a decade. Learn how this change can affect your local Chicago business - and how you can partner with Accel Entertainment today.

RESPONSIBLE GAMING

At Accel Entertainment, we believe that gaming should always be safe, enjoyable, and responsibly managed. We are committed to promoting responsible gaming practices across all of our partner establishments and ensuring compliance with all state regulations. Through employee training, on-site signage, and access to support resources, we help players make informed decisions and seek help when needed. We’re proud to work alongside our partners to maintain a safe and responsible gaming environment in every community we serve.
