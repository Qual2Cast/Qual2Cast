  • Education for Real Life

    Learning is a lifelong thing. From preschoolers learning colors and shapes to adults pursuing new career paths, Stride has something for every step of the learning journey.

What we offer students of all ages and stages:

K–12 Education

More than 3 million students have chosen K12 to help them learn in an environment that adapts to their learning styles, goals, and needs.1

  • K12-Powered Online Public and Private Schools: Learning isn’t one size fits all. With full- and part-time school programs personalized to each child’s needs, there’s something for every student. Many schools also offer career and college prep to help students get ahead in popular industries—like healthcare, business, and information technology—with specialized classes, prep for certification exams, and skill-building for the world beyond high school.
  • K12 Tutoring: Whether their goal is to catch up or get ahead, K12 Tutoring helps students in grades K–12 level up their learning. Our experienced tutors are all state-certified teachers dedicated to providing personalized tutoring that fits their learning style and schedule.
  • K12 Learning Hub: With a no-frills interface and outstanding capabilities, Learning Hub delivers personalized academic support in an easy-to-follow, fully customizable design. Engaging lessons help learners develop and hone their skills across a wide array of subject areas, and you can select specific lessons, build your own playlist, or work on pre-built collections.

Career & Workforce Development

Whether we’re ushering in the next wave of talent or helping established professionals develop new skills, Stride’s career-powering programs help all learners make their next big move.

  • Tallo: A talent platform that provides growth opportunities for emerging professionals—from scholarships and college admissions to internships, apprenticeships, jobs, and more.
  • Tech Elevator: A coding bootcamp and career prep program offering hiring options, employee reskilling, and aptitude testing—all to help their graduates become software developers with fulfilling careers in tech.
  • MedCerts: A leader in online education that helps medical and IT professionals upgrade their careers with nationally recognized certifications.

Join our team and make a big difference.

Across the country and in all kinds of roles, the Stride team shares a common goal: to help learners get the education they deserve. Explore the wide range of roles available and learn how you can change the future of education with us.
