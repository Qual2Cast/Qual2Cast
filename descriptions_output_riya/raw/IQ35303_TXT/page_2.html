BUSINESSES

Bell

Bell is a leading supplier of military and commercial helicopters, tiltrotor aircraft and related spare parts and services.

Above and Beyond Flight

For more than 90 years, Bell has reimagined the experience of flight and expanded the possibilities of where it can go. The company pioneered aviation milestones, becoming the first to break the sound barrier and certify a commercial helicopter. Bell contributed to NASA’s first lunar mission and introduced advanced tiltrotor systems to the market. Today, Bell is shaping the future of advanced air mobility.

Headquartered in Fort Worth, Texas, as a wholly owned subsidiary of Textron Inc., Bell maintains strategic locations worldwide. Nearly one quarter of its workforce has served in the military, reflecting a deep commitment to supporting mission success. Above all, Bell’s breakthrough innovations deliver exceptional customer experiences—efficiently, reliably, and with safety at the forefront.

Military

Bell is harnessing our world-renowned military technology to equip modern warfighters with the aircraft they need to dominate the battlefield. Our combat-proven, dynamic platforms are first to the fight and ready to carry troops through demanding environments.
