A Long History of Stragegic Growth

Founded in 1951, Hallador has a long history of strategic growth through a pragmatic, shareholder-driven approach that inspires trust, builds lasting relationships, generates value, and meets the energy demands of a changing world.

Its most recent transformation begins in 2022 with the acquisition of the Merom Power Plant, a 1-gigawatt electric generation facility in Indiana. This bold move shifts the company’s focus from coal production to electric generation, positioning it as a vertically integrated power provider at a critical moment, as the country faces a growing imbalance between supply and demand in its power grid. Today, more than half of the company’s revenue comes from electricity sales.

With a strong history in the energy sector, a growing contracted revenue base, and an expanding power portfolio, Hallador Energy is a trusted partner in dispatchable energy, providing reliable power through a vertically integrated platform that meets the energy demands of a changing world.
