Meet the Brands Setting the Industry Standard in Clean Laundry.

As the world leader in commercial laundry, Alliance Laundry Systems is represented by five premier brands that cater to the diverse needs of the laundry care industry.

Known for exceptional quality and best-in-class technology, our washers and dryers set the standard in commercial and residential laundry care.

Discover how our collection of trusted brands consistently delivers award-winning results and successfully meets the needs of our customers globally.

Speed Queen commercial machines have set the standard in laundromats and on-premises laundry across the world for over a century. Speed Queen leads the charge in providing efficient, eco-friendly and reliable washers and dryers worldwide.

With advanced technology like Quantum™ Controls and ProCapture™, Speed Queen supplies real-time data, efficient operations and the lowest total cost of ownership to laundry care businesses worldwide.

Award-Winning Laundry Solutions

With 82 million families today sharing their homes with a pet, cleaning up pet hair can be a major challenge.1 That’s why Speed Queen developed the award-winning DR7 dryer. The first of its kind to feature innovative Pet Plus™ cycles, the DR7 dryer was recently recognized for its game-changing reliability and awarded Good Housekeeping’s Best Clothes Dryer for Pet Owners in 2024. This model was also a winner in the 2021 Good Housekeeping Cleaning Awards for the perfect dryer for pet owners.

Validated through testing at dog rescues, service animal organizations and veterinary clinics, the DR7 earned high marks for its sanitizing steam capabilities.
