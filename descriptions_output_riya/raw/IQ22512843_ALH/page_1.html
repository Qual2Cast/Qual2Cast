Our Commitment to a Clean, Sustainable Future

Sustainability shapes every decision we make. It’s the driving force behind our commitment to reducing environmental impact, strengthening communities and delivering laundry solutions that work today and for generations to come. At Alliance Laundry Systems, we don’t just build laundry equipment. We build a future where progress and responsibility go hand in hand.

As the global leader in laundry solutions, we set the bar for more than just clean laundry. We are pushing the boundaries of what’s possible—designing innovative solutions that minimize energy use, water consumption and waste. We are focused on reducing our environmental footprint through continuous improvements in our products, processes and operations.

But our focus extends beyond products. We invest in communities, helping to create a world where businesses can grow responsibly, and people can thrive. We uphold the highest standards of corporate governance, ensuring that every action we take reflects our dedication to the environment and the people we serve.

Through these efforts, we’re not just meeting today’s needs—we’re building a sustainable, more responsible future, one laundry solution at a time.

Driven by People,
Guided by Purpose

Born in a Midwest basement laundry room, Alliance Laundry Systems has grown into a global powerhouse, driven by the same spirit of discovery that sparked its beginnings.

At the heart of our legacy is a deep dedication to the people who move our company forward: the engineers innovating solutions for a cleaner future, the customers who trust those solutions and the global communities where our brands make a difference. Every Alliance machine and partnership is part of something bigger—a shared vision of a better tomorrow for all.

Making Laundry Easier Since 1908

Founded in Ripon, Wisconsin, U.S., Alliance Laundry Systems has spent over a century emphasizing the impact of innovation and the power of progress.

From our first days as Barlow & Seelig Manufacturing, Co., to our current position as the global laundry leader, our mission remains the same: To make the world cleaner and more sustainable as the premier provider of laundry solutions.

Today, with a clear vision for the future, and more than $100 million invested over the past five years, we’re exploring new research, development and manufacturing capabilities. We look forward to growing and evolving for years to come.

About Innovation
1908

Barlow & Seelig Manufacturing, Co. is established in Ripon, WI.

1928

Speed Queen® becomes the brand name for Barlow & Seelig products.

1953

First automatic washer is manufactured.

1978

Company acquires Huebsch® to expand mid-market offering in North America.

1994

Company acquires UniMac®, the global brand leader in on-premises laundry.

1998

Company is renamed Alliance Laundry Systems.

2006

Company acquires IPSO.

2013

Alliance opens offices in Brazil, China and Spain.

2014

Alliance acquires Primus® to expand its international footprint.

2016

Speed Queen-branded laundromats launch around the world.

2019

Alliance Laundry Systems opens new production sites in Manitowoc, WI, and Chon Buri, Thailand.

Shaping Your Success, Together

Alliance Laundry Systems is the world leader in laundry solutions for a reason—we do one thing, and we do it better than anyone else on the planet. While global competitors divide their focus across multiple appliances, we’re all in on laundry. That means we know it better, build it better and support our customers better.

See our laundry solutions in action and discover how our advanced products, innovative technologies and exceptional customer care can help you streamline operations and drive success.
