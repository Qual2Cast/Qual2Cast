Advanced Energy

Amentum provides innovative energy solutions to drive the energy transition. Explore how our advanced capabilities promote sustainability and resilience.

Amentum is a leading provider of innovative energy solutions, committed to advancing the transition to a sustainable energy future. With a focus on advanced technologies and integrated systems, we deliver high-performance energy solutions that meet the evolving needs of our clients across various sectors.

Contact Us

Nuclear offers reliable energy to accelerate the shift away from fossil fuels and power a range of other uses including space exploration, desalination plants and data centers. We deliver safe, innovative solutions for high-risk, technically complex nuclear projects worldwide.

Amentum is at the forefront of conceptual design and optimization of new nuclear technologies like Generation IV, small modular and advanced reactors and fusion power.

We support clients with strategic advice, program and project management, siting and environmental assessment, regulatory compliance, full lifecycle design and engineering. We also help with remote handling and robotics, testing and rigs, construction and commissioning, field services, maintenance and visual engineering. By combining engineering and technical excellence with innovation, research and development, we’re helping clients solve the world’s most complex nuclear challenges safely and effectively.

Amentum is sharing its multi-technology expertise, deep nuclear knowledge, skills in advanced engineering and licensing, and special technical capabilities with small modular and advanced reactor developers from the USA, UK and continental Europe.

We have a decades-long involvement in ITER, the world’s largest nuclear fusion project, where our work ranges from designing prototypes for key components to complex remote handling systems essential for reactor operations. For the UK Atomic Energy Authority, we supported the Joint European Torus throughout its operational life and continue to work on other key fusion research programmes.
