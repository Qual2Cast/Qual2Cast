About Us

The UFP Story

Founded in 1963 and headquartered in Newburyport, Massachusetts, UFP Technologies is a contract development and manufacturing organization that specializes in single-use and single-patient medical devices. UFP is an important link in the medical device supply chain and a valued outsource partner to many of the top medical device manufacturers in the world. Our single-use and single-patient devices and components are used in a wide range of medical devices and packaging for minimally invasive surgery, infection prevention, wound care, wearables, orthopedic soft goods, and orthopedic implants.

Our History

Forging A Path Forward

Much like our focus on shaping design and engineering innovation, UFP was forged through decades of continuous advancement. Discover the milestones that made us who we are today.

1963

United Packaging Corporation was founded. The company supplied protective packaging for computers and electronics.

1964

Expanded operations and relocated headquarters to Georgetown, MA.

1976

Started precision molding cross-linked polyethylene foam.

1988

Introduced compression molded medical protective packaging using specialized foams.

1993

Acquired Moulded Fibre Technology, a pioneer in custom molded fiber packaging.

1993

Completed IPO and changed name to UFP Technologies, Inc.

1994

Invested in cleanroom production for our growing medical customer base.

1997

Acquired FCE Industries to expand foam and plastic market reach to the Midwest.

1998

Acquired Pacific Foam Technologies to enhance West Coast foam plastic capabilities.

2001

Introduced TPU-based pouches and bags as an alternative to foam packaging for the orthopedic implant market.

2008

Acquired Stephenson & Lawyer to expand polyurethane foam fabrication.

 

Sales exceed $100 million.

2009

Acquired Advanced Materials Group, E.N. Murray, and Foamade Industries to expand our foam fabrication capabilities.

2013

Acquired Packaging Alternatives Corporation to expand polyurethane foam capabilities for the medical market.

2013

Named to The Boston Globe’s “The Globe 100” for the 8th consecutive year.

2016

Expanded operations and relocated headquarters to Newburyport, MA.

2018

Acquired Dielectrics, founded in 1955 and located in Chicopee MA, a leader in medical device manufacturing using thermoplastic materials as well as the innovator of the Reebok® Pump introduced in 1989.

2021

Acquired Contech Medical, founded in 1987 and located in Providence, RI, with partner manufacturing in Costa Rica, a global leader in the development and manufacture of class III medical device packaging primarily for catheters and guidewires.

2021

Sales exceed $200 million.

2022

Acquired DAS Medical, located in Atlanta, GA, with manufacturing in the Dom. Rep., a medical device contract manufacturer specializing in single-use surgical equipment covers, robotic draping systems and fluid control pouches.

2022

Acquired Advant Medical, located in Galway, Ireland, with operations in Costa Rica and partner manufacturing in Mexico, a manufacturer of Class I, II, and III medical devices and packaging.

Divested Molded Fiber business.

2022

Sales exceed $300 million.

2022

Opened new medical manufacturing operation in Tijuana, Mexico.

2022

Received the Kestra Medical Technologies “Execution Award” for our contributions on their ASSURE® Wearable Cardioverter Defibrillator (WCD) system.

2024

Acquired AJR Enterprises of St. Charles, IL, with additional manufacturing in the Dom. Rep., to expand our cut and sew capabilities for patient surfaces and transfer devices.

2024

Acquired Welch Fluorocarbon of Dover, NH, a manufacturer of thermoformed, and heat sealed implantable medical device components utilizing thin, high-performance films.

2024

Received the Freudenberg Home and Cleaning Solutions (FHCS) “Global Quality System Triangle Award.”

2024

Acquired Marble Medical of Tallahassee, FL, a manufacturer of adhesive based medical components and single-use devices.

2024

Acquired AQF Medical of Navan, Ireland, with joint venture operations in Singapore, a manufacturer of custom-engineered foam and thermoplastic components used in a wide range of medical devices and packaging.

2024

Sales exceed $500 million.

2025

Acquired sister companies of AJR Enterprise: AJR Specialty Products and AJR Custom Foam Products of St. Charles, IL, to expand our cut and sew capabilities for patient surfaces and transfer devices.

2025

Acquired Universal Plastics & Engineering Company (UNIPEC) of Rockville, MD, a manufacturer of precision thermoformed and heat-sealed polymer components used primarily for shielding batteries in Class III implantable medical devices.

2025

Acquired Techno Plastics Industries (TPI) of Anasco, Puerto Rico, a manufacturer of precision thermoplastic injection-molded components.

2025

Named one of the National Association for Business Resources’ “Best and Brightest Companies to Work for in the Nation” for the 7th time.
