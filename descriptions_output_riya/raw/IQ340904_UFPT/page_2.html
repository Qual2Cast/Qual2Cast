Advanced Components: Capabilities

Manufacturing Processes

UFP’s manufacturing resources combined with our access to an array of materials gives us the ability to create custom solutions that best fit your needs. Our manufacturing capabilities include the following:

Scroll to first section
  • Compression Molding
  • Thermoplastic Welding & Heat Sealing
  • Vacuum Forming
  • Thin Film Thermoforming (offered through Welch Fluorocarbon, a UFP Technologies company)
  • Injection Molding (offered through Techno Plastics Industries, a UFP Technologies company)
  • High-Speed Die Cutting
  • CNC Routing & Water Jet Cutting
  • Foam Cutting & Convoluting
  • Technical Fabric Joining
  • Cut and Sew (offered through AJR Specialty Products, a UFP Technologies company)
  • Lamination
  • Felting
  • Printing & Decoration (Laser Etching, Pad Printing)

Services

Exceptional Manufacturing Services

Rapid Prototyping

Rapid Prototyping

We understand the importance of getting to market quickly and efficiently. Our nimble team of engineers, technicians and machinists will work with you to create prototypes that are designed for manufacturability. Our multiple lab locations and in-house machining allow us to produce your prototype in 24-to-72 hours.

Tooling

Tooling

UFP Technologies has multiple in-house tooling departments. The ability to manufacture the tooling ourselves provides us and our customers with the control we need to satisfy the quality work they expect. Most importantly, it allows us to provide a fast turnaround to keep your project on time and on budget.

Kitting

Kitting

UFP can assemble multiple products into a single package, or kit, which we then deliver to you. We’ll select and package all the necessary components for the manufacture of your product. Kitting simplifies your fulfillment process and makes it significantly more efficient.

Custom Equipment

Custom Equipment

What if your new product or innovation is so revolutionary that the tools needed to produce it don’t even exist? With our independent mindset and creativity, UFP can develop the custom equipment necessary to manufacture your product. Now nothing can stop you.

Contract Manufacturing

Contract Manufacturing

UFP acts as an extension of your R&D and manufacturing teams. We will work with you to design and develop your product and/or packaging, select the proper materials, manufacture, package and ship your goods with your labels. It’s a seamless experience through the lifecycle of your product.

Turnkey Solutions

Turnkey Solutions

UFP Technologies offers complete turnkey solutions, from packaging design and development to drop-shipping, we will work with you from start to finish to make your product launch successful.

Quality and Certifications

UFP Technologies continually invests in our quality management systems and operations to provide the most seamless experience for our clients. UFP Technologies currently holds quality certifications in key markets across the country.
