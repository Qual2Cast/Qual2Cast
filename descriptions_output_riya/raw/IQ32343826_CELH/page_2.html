BRANDS

CELSIUS®

With just the right blend of refreshing energy, CELSIUS features fruit-forward flavor profiles that satisfy and energize. CELSIUS is balanced, energy to support your fitness journey and promote everyday wellness. With a wide variety of unique and familiar fruit flavors, CELSIUS was created to motivate and inspire you to LIVE FIT™.

CELSIUS® VIBE

The CELSIUS Vibe Line features refreshing flavor combinations that transport you. Highlighting vibrant and nostalgic flavor profiles, the CELSIUS Vibe Line brings the ultimate experience to life with each refreshing sip, as there is no limit to where the vibe can take you. The CELSIUS Vibe Line is multidimensional by nature, with a seamless fusion of exotic flavors. Created with the same proven ingredients as CELSIUS, these tantalizing flavors deliver energy and an immersive experience all in one sip. What’s Your Vibe?™

CELSIUS® FIZZ-FREE

The CELSIUS Fizz-Free Line features a subset of Fizz-Free flavors for those who want energy without the carbonation. Featuring the same METAPLUS® formula and vitamins, the Fizz-Free line flavors are smooth, fruit-forward and energizing without the bubbles.

CELSIUS® ESSENTIALS

Created for the individuals that want to elevate their performance to a new level, the CELSIUS ESSENTIALS Line features a 16oz blend of our METAPLUS formula with three aminos added to help boost endurance and improve focus, supporting the enhancement of physical ability overall. With a specially formulated lineup of bold flavor combinations, CELSIUS ESSENTIALS is a game changer for those who want a little bit more out of their energy.

CELSIUS® ON THE GO

Created for those who want to LIVE FIT™ on the go, the CELSIUS On The Go powders contain the same premium ingredients as CELSIUS but are compact and easy to take with you wherever you go. The On The Go powders are perfect for traveling and provide you with the same refreshing energy boost in a variety of delicious fruit flavors. Just add water and go!
