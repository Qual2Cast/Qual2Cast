EXPLORE RV TYPES

We’ll help you figure out what kind of RV is right for you.

View All

Travel Trailer

Travel trailers are the most popular type of non-motorized RV. They can be towed by a variety of vehicles and are made for a variety of uses, from hauling to vacationing to full-time RVing.

Learn More

Toy Hauler

Many people think of toy haulers as the wild-child of the RV world, but these RVs have multi-purpose utlitity for every adventurer. The open "garage" can serve as storage for toys, an extra bedroom or even an on-the-go office.

Learn More

Fifth Wheel

Easy to hitch, easy to tow, and stable, fifth wheels offer plenty of comfort for the whole family. Versatility and space are the reasons why RVers choose fifth wheels for their trips — or for their full-time homes.

Learn More

Class A Motorhome

Built with the same framing and construction as commercial trucks and buses, Class A motorhomes are some of the largest vehicles on the road. AKA lots of room to stretch out and get comfortable.

Learn More

Class B Camper Van

Small, streamlined and ready to roll. Class B motorhomes (also known as camper vans) offer luxurious living space best suited for small groups.

Learn More

Class C Motorhome

Recognizable by their raised sleeping or storage areas which extend over the cab of the RV, Class C Motorhomes offer more living space than Class B Motorhomes but are smaller in size and can offer better gas mileage than Class A Motorcoaches.

Learn More
