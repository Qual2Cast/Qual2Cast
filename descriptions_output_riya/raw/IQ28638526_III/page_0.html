    ENTERPRISE AI ADVISORY

    AI strategy, in motion

    Every AI decision now affects performance, competitiveness and the future of the enterprise. By the time you’ve finalised your AI strategy, the market may already have moved on.

    ISG brings independent market intelligence, enterprise benchmarks and practical advisory experience to help you decide what matters, where to act and how fast to move. We connect AI market shifts to your strategy, operating model, workforce and economics so you can move earlier, avoid stranded investment and keep your strategy relevant.

    Learn More
    AI STRATEGY & AI AT SCALE

    Choose where AI matters and make it scale

    AI strategy is not a catalogue of use cases, it's the choice of where the enterprise will place its bets, how work will change and what deserves to scale. We help you connect AI ambition to business priorities, select the use cases worth scaling and define the operating model, roadmap and partner strategy to move forward.

    This gives you a clear portfolio of priorities, a funded path to execution and a practical definition of what success looks like.

    Learn More
    AI GOVERNANCE

    Put accountability behind every AI decision

    Without clear decision rights and controls, pilots stall, autonomous systems create unmanaged exposure and accountability breaks down. 

    Across use cases, models, agents and workflows, we establish clear ownership, classify risk, design lifecycle controls, monitor performance and create reporting that gives you a usable view of exposure and trust.

    Learn More
    AI ECONOMICS

    Know what AI adoption is doing to your P&L

    Without a clear model for spend, savings and performance, you can increase AI investment without knowing whether it is improving the P&L.

    ISG helps you understand consumption and token economics, set funding priorities, redesign pricing and commercial models and connect increasing autonomy to savings, ROI and business performance so you can fund the right use cases, negotiate from evidence and hold enterprise performance to account.

    AI Maturity Benchmarking

    Redesign work around human and AI strengths

    If work is not redesigned, adoption fragments, shadow AI spreads and the benefits of deployment stay trapped in isolated teams.

    The ISG AI Maturity Index gives you a data-driven baseline across teams and roles. Combined with our workforce and change expertise, it helps you assess how AI will change work and define the skills, role changes and adoption actions needed to move forward. With this foundation in place, adoption is stronger, roles are clearer and your workforce is equipped to work effectively with AI.
