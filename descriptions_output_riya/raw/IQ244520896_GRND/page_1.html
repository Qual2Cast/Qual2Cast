About

THE GLOBAL GAYBORHOOD IN YOUR POCKET.


Since 2009, Grindr has grown into the world’s largest network for gay, bi, trans, and queer adults. Each day, millions of people in nearly every country open the app to connect, chat, and build community.

Today that foundational utility is evolving into the Global Gayborhood—an AI-powered platform where proximity unlocks instant access not only to one another but also to curated products, services, and trustworthy information, all in one place. By stitching these essentials into a single, fluid experience, Grindr now serves as the growth engine of gay life worldwide.

Our mission is clear: become the Global Gayborhood in Your Pocket, so wherever life takes you, the community, support, and opportunities that move us and our business forward are never more than a tap away.
