Bringing our platform story to life

The NCR Voyix Customer Experience Center (CEC) is where our technology moves from slideware to real-world execution. Located at our Global Headquarters in Atlanta, the CEC is designed to help customers and prospects experience our solutions the same way they operate their business every day. On the floor, behind the counter, and across real workflows.

Instead of static demos, the CEC features immersive environments that mirror modern retail and restaurant operations. This allows teams to quickly see how our platform, applications, and services work together in practice. Not in theory.

Our Values

At NCR Voyix, our values reflect how we show up for our customers, our partners and each other. They guide how we innovate, how we make decisions and how we deliver results.

We bring our values to life through I.D.E.A.: four action-oriented principles that link our innovation legacy with the future we're shaping.

Innovate

Build what's next. Innovation is at the heart of NCR Voyix. We challenge the status quo, turn ideas into action and create solutions that make a meaningful impact.

Deliver

Put customers first. We take ownership and follow through. From big bets to daily execution, we focus on outcomes that matter, moving with urgency, accountability and a bias toward action.

Engage

Win together. We believe the best results come from collaboration. We value diverse perspectives, share ideas openly and work as one team.

Adapt

Make it happen. We stay flexible, anticipate needs and respond quickly to our customers, embracing new opportunities to drive growth and results.
