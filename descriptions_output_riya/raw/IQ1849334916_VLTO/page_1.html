Our story

Meet Veralto

Veralto launched in October 2023 as a $5 billion global leader in essential technologies with a bold vision for creating enduring positive impact for the world.

Our operating companies are building on a long-standing legacy of success, innovation, and deep customer trust as they work to create a safer, cleaner, more vibrant future.

A higher truth

Veralto is inspired by the Latin root words veri for truth and alto for height, reflecting our commitment to the higher truth that drives us to create a better future for all. Our name inspires us to stay true to our highest ideals and to continually raise the bar as we pursue our powerful purpose: Safeguarding the World’s Most Vital Resources™.
