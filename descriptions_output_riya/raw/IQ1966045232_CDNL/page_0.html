Civil Infrastructure Services, Delivering Integrity from the Ground Up

Across the Southeast, the neighborhoods, commercial developments, roads and other infrastructure taking shape all start in the same place: the ground. Cardinal Infrastructure Group (NASDAQ: CDNL) is a vertically integrated civil infrastructure and site development platform serving residential, commercial, industrial and municipal projects.

Our operating companies self-perform site work from preconstruction to paving, including grading, site clearing, erosion control, underground utilities, drilling and blasting, and asphalt paving. Bringing multiple phases of the work under one accountable team gives our clients a single point of responsibility across the site.

At Cardinal, relationships come first and that shows in how we treat our clients, team members, partners and communities. Every decision is guided by doing what's right and what creates long-term value for everyone.
