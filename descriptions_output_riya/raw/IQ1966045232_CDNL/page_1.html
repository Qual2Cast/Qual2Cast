Relationships Come First

Founded in 2013 and led by executives with more than 30 years in the field, we know the value of relationships. We've built our reputation on delivering for everyone who works with us. At Cardinal, we plan carefully, communicate early, and work to the agreed on schedule and specifications. Partners run their businesses to take advantage of the opportunities in front of them. Employees know they can make the right call so that work moves forward safely. We show up, communicate clearly and deliver on what we promise.

And we grow with the same intent, by doing work right the first time and acquiring companies whose people and standards match our own. That foundation guides every decision we make. It's how we deliver Integrity from the Ground Up.

Why Vertical Integration Matters

Accountability

Bringing multiple phases under one team means one point of accountability for the site, and fewer gaps between phases. Our team is responsible for the scope, and you get work done to our standards and results we answer for directly.

Schedule Certainty

With work sequenced in-house, we anticipate what's next and keep work moving. Having access to the crews, equipment and resources to keep your project moving helps us deliver on tight schedules.

Partnership

Being a partner starts before the work does. We plan scope, schedules and budgets with you before crews mobilize. From preconstruction through closeout we stay close, communicate early and honestly, and bring solutions.

Safety

We put our people first. Safety is a commitment we make to each other and to the communities where we build, backed by ongoing training and a dedicated safety team. It's a standard we hold every day, on every site.
