About Us

Who We Are

InfuSystem provides services to hospitals, oncology practices, surgery centers and other alternate-site health care providers to positively impact the continuity of care from the clinic to a patient’s home. With over three decades of experience, InfuSystem is your trusted partner in care.

Our guiding principle is patients first. We are dedicated to doing the right thing for the patients whose lives we touch, for our customers and for our employees. More than 500 dedicated employees – including registered nurses (RNs), customer care coordinators, insurance specialists and expert biomedical technicians – demonstrate compassion and innovation in their daily work.

What We Do

Patient Services

We provide last-mile solution for clinic-to-home health care where the patient’s continuing treatment involves complex durable medical equipment and services. InfuSystem has over three decades of delivering trusted and innovative therapy services to hospitals, oncology practices, surgery centers and other alternate site health care providers. InfuSystem delivers therapy services to support Oncology, Pain Management and Wound.

InfuSystem’s dedicated team of registered nurses provides continuous patient support to minimize risk and improve outcomes. Our workflow solutions for clinicians encourage best practice standards. From patient resources to education, we’re here 24/7 delivering compassionate and caring support for patients and clinicians in all therapy areas.

Device Solutions

Our device solutions medical equipment services provide equipment and consumable sales to hospitals, clinics and home infusion providers, as well as complete biomedical services. InfuSystem is a leading U.S. and Canadian provider of medical equipment and services with a 100K+ device fleet. From new or pre-owned devices, we provide sales, rental and leasing with flexible financing options, helping you to conserve capital while keeping your device fleet running efficiently.

Patient care requires diligent attention, and your medical equipment is a vital part of your health care system. InfuSystem delivers cost-efficient depot and on-site biomedical repair and maintenance services from preventative maintenance to extensive repairs. Our team of biomedical technicians share their combined expertise across the U.S. and Canada.
