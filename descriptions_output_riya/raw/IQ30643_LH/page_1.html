About Labcorp

Every day, we help people around the world live healthier lives

Improving health and improving lives

The right insights make health decisions clearer. By pairing deep scientific expertise with advanced technology, we help our partners across healthcare take action with greater confidence. We work with health systems, providers, payers, and biopharma to expand access to testing, support affordability, and speed breakthroughs.

We deliver the science and insights that make a difference

We are a company of scientists, technologists, and innovators working tirelessly to advance health and improve lives for people at every stage of their health journey. As a global laboratory leader, we partner to bring technical and high-quality healthcare to the people and communities we serve.

Our Leadership

Your partner in advancing health

Leading with science and technology

Our deep scientific expertise is at the heart of everything we do. And technology is the spark that fuels our scale, speed, efficiency, and progress.

Providing data & insights that matter

Powered by the largest clinical global data set, we deliver critical insights that inform decisions, empower action, and create healthier communities.

Delivering trusted experiences

Everything we do starts with the people we serve. We design experiences that simplify complexity and support the people who count on us for answers.

Scaling quality and innovation

Fueled by technology, we bring scientific expertise and high-quality care to people when and where they need it, without compromises.

Our core values guide how we work, collaborate, and innovate every day.

We have more than 71,000 employees across the globe supporting our mission.

As pioneers and partners helping to discover and define the next iteration of healthcare, our more than 71,000 employees across the globe support and provide services to anyone in any community affected by healthcare—from individuals to organizations.

Bring your talents to Labcorp

Advancing health on a global scale takes a wide range of perspectives and skills. Every day at Labcorp brings new opportunities to improve health and improve lives for people all over the world. If you're looking to play a part in advancing the future of healthcare, join us.
