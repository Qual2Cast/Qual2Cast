United Parks & Resorts is deeply commitment to creating experiences that matter for guests and inspiring them to help protect animals and the wild wonders of the world. The company is best known for its world-class portfolio of seven brands across 13 parks in the United States and Abu Dhabi, including SeaWorld®, Busch Gardens®, and Sesame Place®. The parks offer guests a variety of inspiring up-close experiences, from animal encounters that invite exploration and appreciation of the natural world, to thrilling rides and spectacular shows.
