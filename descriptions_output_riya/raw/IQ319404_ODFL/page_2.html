OD Services

Old Dominion Freight Line is a National LTL Carrier offering complete coverage of the U.S. as well as door-to-door service throughout North America.

  1. OD Services

Premium Service from a Team you can Trust

For more than 90 years, OD has been helping the world keep promises by providing premium transportation services to get your shipments delivered on-time and damage-free. From less than truckload shipping services, expedited, full truckload and everything in between, we offer a wide variety of freight delivery services to meet all your shipping needs. With an industry-leading on-time record and low claims rate, OD leads the way in providing a premium experience for our customers.

New to Old Dominion?

By setting up an account, you will have access to every OD shipping service as well as a local Solutions Specialist for account management, credit terms and competitive pricing tailored to your specific business needs. 

Get Started Today

Standard LTL

OD's network of more than 260 Service Centers helps you manage your domestic LTL freight needs with confidence from coast to coast. Our nationwide coverage provides direct transit from door-to-door with reduced freight handling and some of the fastest transit times in the industry. 

Learn More

OD Expedited

Ensure fast, reliable, and on-time deliveries for the most time-sensitive shipments with OD Expedited LTL shipping. Whether you need a guaranteed delivery, faster than standard transit, expedited air, or MABD retail solutions, we've got you covered. 

Learn More

OD Critical

Built for your most time-sensitive and specialized freight needs, OD Critical delivers when your shipments demand more. This service offers same-day delivery, complex job site handling, and unmatched flexibility. 

Learn More

Global North America

In addition to our nationwide network of OD Service Centers, we are proud to offer global secure shipping throughout North America. We offer seamless service to and from Canada, Mexico, Hawaii, Alaska, and Puerto Rico.

Learn More

Container Drayage

OD offers container drayage services for your LCL and FCL shipments. With OD company drivers and chassis as well as hazmat certifications, we handle container shipments with the same premium level of service you experience with OD LTL.

Learn More

International Freight

Whether it’s by ocean or air, streamline your import and export shipments with OD. Contact us today for secure, reliable, and efficient international freight services you can trust.

Learn More

Truckload

Get reliable and efficient truckload services across North America. OD Truckload Brokerage offers dry van, flatbed, refrigerated equipment as well as solutions for special projects.

Learn More

Special Services

Trade Show, Grocery and Food Service, and Security Divider are several of the specialized shipping services OD offers to meet the demands of any shipping need.
