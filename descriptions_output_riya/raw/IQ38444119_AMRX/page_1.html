We’re reimagining
what’s possible to make medicine accessible for all

Amneal is a global biopharmaceutical company. For over two decades, we’ve helped people gain access to healthier lives by developing and delivering a diverse portfolio of generic and specialty pharmaceuticals.

Founded in

2002

Headquartered in Bridgewater, NJ

“We witnessed firsthand the difficulty many patients face in affording their medicines. Believing there had to be a better way, we formed Amneal in 2002 to help ensure every person gets the medicine they need.”

Chirag & Chintu Patel
Co-CEOs and Co-Founders of Amneal

The Patel family’s 20+ year commitment guiding Amneal’s contributions to patients and healthcare

Chirag and Chintu’s vision for founding Amneal was inspired by their father, Kanu Patel, an industrial pharmacist who worked as a pharmaceutical regulatory inspector in India. Raised on the Gandhian philosophy of truth and service, Kanu taught his sons to achieve prosperity through hard work and by conducting business with honesty and integrity.

The family immigrated to the United States in 1987, providing Chirag and Chintu with access to higher education and a business environment that fosters and rewards entrepreneurial spirit. Chirag began his business pursuits with several information technology start-ups while Chintu followed in his father’s footsteps as a pharmacist. During his time as a pharmacist, Chintu witnessed firsthand the struggle many patients had in choosing between buying food or their medication. The desire to provide affordable medicine to patients ignited a passion within Chintu, and with his father’s guidance and Chirag’s business savvy, Amneal was born.

Chirag and Chintu have grown the Amneal family, its portfolio of innovative and affordable medicines, and the company’s impact on the world. Family is not a buzz word here, but rather a through-line of our culture that unites, inspires, gives back and succeeds. Today, we’re pursuing a future for Amneal that is focused on what matters most: responsibly helping patients live better lives through high-quality, accessible medications.

We're a global biopharmaceutical company advancing medicine for all

After getting our start in generic medicine, we’ve since expanded to offer world-class scientific rigor, production capabilities, and commercial infrastructure.

Our products

Complex generic medicines

We have developed our generic portfolio for 20+ years across a range of increasingly complex product formats.

Bioscience and biologic medicines

We are building a leading U.S. position in biosimilars across oncology, movement disorders and beyond.

Injectable medicines for institutions

We’ve brought 40+ commercial injectables to U.S. hospitals and health systems, many of which are helping to address persistent drug shortages.

Specialty branded medicines

We have a growing portfolio of branded pharmaceuticals focused primarily on central nervous system and endocrine disorders.

Partnerships

When we can’t meet the need ourselves, we make sure to partner with other industry leaders to bring people the medicines they need when they need it.

Distribution

Through our AvKARE segment, we distribute pharmaceuticals and other products for the U.S. federal government, retail, and institutional markets.
