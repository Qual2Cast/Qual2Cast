    Frequently Asked Questions

    Learn more about our surplus asset solutions.

    What is surplus asset management?
    Identifying, valuing, and selling surplus assets

    Surplus asset management is the process of identifying, valuing, and responsibly selling or redeploying assets that are no longer needed—such as equipment, vehicles, inventory, or materials. A structured surplus program helps organizations recover value, reduce risk, improve sustainability outcomes, and gain visibility across the full asset lifecycle.

    Learn more about the Solutions Liquidity Services offers →
    How does Liquidity Services help organizations sell surplus assets?
    Connecting surplus assets to the right buyers

    Liquidity Services provides end‑to‑end surplus asset solutions, including valuation, compliance, marketing, buyer access, and reporting. We match assets to the most relevant buyers across specialized marketplaces, manage the selling process, and deliver transparent results—helping organizations maximize recovery while simplifying operations.

    How is Liquidity Services different from a traditional auction company?
    Multiple marketplaces beyond traditional auction services

    Unlike single‑channel auction companies, Liquidity Services operates a multi‑marketplace platform combined with consultative services, technology, and compliance expertise. This allows us to route assets to the right buyers, support complex programs, and deliver better outcomes for enterprise, government, and retail sellers.

    What types of surplus assets can Liquidity Services manage and sell?
    Wide range of surplus assets across industries

    Liquidity Services manages and sells surplus assets across 900+ categories, spanning every major industrial sector. Whether you're clearing a production line, closing a facility, or disposing of idle equipment, we handle assets including industrial machinery and equipment, energy and biopharmaceutical equipment, laboratory and medical equipment, heavy equipment and construction assets, vehicles and transportation assets, IT equipment, and retail and consumer goods inventory.

    If your organization generates surplus, we can sell it.

    Do I need to transport my equipment to sell it?
    Sell in place without moving your equipment

    No. Many assets can be sold directly from their current location. Liquidity Services supports sell‑in‑place and on‑site sales models that reduce transportation costs, speed up recovery, and minimize disruption—while still exposing assets to qualified buyers globally.
