BioMarin is a leading, global rare disease biotechnology company focused on delivering medicines for people living with genetically defined conditions.

Founded in 1997, we have a proven track record of innovation, with nine commercial therapies and a strong clinical and preclinical pipeline. Using a distinctive approach to drug discovery and development, BioMarin seeks to unleash the full potential of genetic science by pursuing category-defining medicines that have a profound impact on patients.
