Corporate Responsibility Overview

AMG believes that sound corporate citizenship and adherence to strong governance principles are elements of our success and that of our Affiliates.

We are committed to:
  • Developing our employees
  • Operating with integrity
  • Contributing to the local communities surrounding our global offices
  • Enhancing our organization’s diversity of perspective
  • Being thoughtful stewards of natural resources

Philanthropy

Through employee engagement in our corporate philanthropic initiatives across our global offices, we are committed to giving back to the communities in which we operate. The AMG Charitable Foundation makes meaningful donations to a variety of non-profit organizations and community programs globally in communities where AMG team members live and work.

AMG Sustainability Committee

AMG’s Sustainability Committee oversees policies and operational controls of environmental, health and safety, and social risks, and reports to the Board of Directors at least annually.

The cross-functional Committee includes multiple members of the Company’s executive management team.

AMG fosters an open environment for discussing environmental and broader sustainability issues, and we encourage our employees to approach our Sustainability Committee with questions and suggestions regarding our practices and policies. We are committed to supporting dialogue on how AMG can enhance its position on sustainability issues consistent with the goals of our various stakeholders – including AMG’s stockholders, our employees, our Affiliates, and the investors in our Affiliates’ products.

Sustainable Investing

While our Affiliates retain operational and investment autonomy, and develop and maintain their own separate policies and procedures relating to environmental and sustainability matters, we collaborate with a number of Affiliates to support their efforts in product development, including certain Affiliates developing products that incorporate responsible investment principles, and in enhancing their sustainability reporting. We incorporate climate-related risks and opportunities in our assessment process for evaluating prospective Affiliate partners.

Today, a majority of our Affiliates are signatories to the UNPRI, and approximately 10% are signatories to the UK Stewardship Code. As of December 31, 2025, more than 10% of AMG Affiliates’ aggregate assets under management were dedicated to sustainable investment strategies.

Tax Disclosure

Taxes paid in the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Canada, and Hong Kong account for more than 80% of the Current Controlling Interest Foreign Tax Provision as disclosed in our 10-K.
