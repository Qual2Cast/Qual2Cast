Consistency with Power Capacity Solutions

Bring stability to your business with steady lane and freight options through Power Capacity Solutions (PCS), a J.B. Hunt power-only offering.

Email Our Team

What Is Power Capacity Solutions?


PCS is designed for power-only carriers who want more efficiency and consistency in how they run and grow their business. Rather than booking one-off loads, PCS gives you a more predictable operating model by pairing recurring opportunities with structured planning. As a carrier with J.B. Hunt PCS, you get access to:

  • A fixed rate structure
  • The ability to stack loads
  • A private power-only load board with repeat lanes and freight
  • A direct point of contact through a J.B. Hunt capacity coordinator
 

“If you're looking to build your business and for it to be a success, you want J.B. Hunt.”

John

J.B. Hunt PCS Benefits

 

Save money, keep schedules booked and run more efficiently with PCS.

 

Cost Savings

Save on equipment and maintenance costs with our J.B. Hunt 360box® drop-and-hook solution – we've invested in more than 12,000 trailers so you don't have to.

Consistent Freight

Run steady lanes with consistent, repeat power-only loads available only on our private J.B. Hunt load board.

Greater Efficiency

Stack up to four loads at a time to deliver more freight and drive fewer empty miles.

 
 

Streamline Your Day

Our free and easy-to-use Carrier 360® technology makes efficiency simple for our PCS carriers. Access your load details and trip information, manage check calls and see status updates for your power-only loads – all in one place. Available on both desktop and mobile, Carrier 360 can help you spend less time coordinating and more time focused on the road ahead.

Help When You Need It

PCS carriers have a dedicated J.B. Hunt capacity coordinator who provides a hands-on onboarding experience, gets to know your business and serves as a consistent point of contact when you need assistance. Reach out to them for help with things like:

  • Managing load schedule details in your Carrier 360 account, including appointments and timing updates
  • Booking loads and moving through each step in Carrier 360
  • Receiving load status updates and communication support as needed during execution of each load
  • Resolving questions related to load details, Carrier 360 technology or other J.B. Hunt systems and processes
  • Viewing and updating route-related information in Carrier 360 – while maintaining full control over your planning decisions
