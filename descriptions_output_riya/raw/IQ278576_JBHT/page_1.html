Our Company

When customers see the iconic J.B. Hunt scroll, they know what to expect. But you don’t become an industry-leading logistics company overnight. We’ve invested in our people, technology and capacity so that we can deliver excellence.

    A Legacy of Impact

    We got our start with five trucks, seven trailers and Mr. Hunt’s vision to create something big. We’ve grown a lot since 1961, but our legacy of innovation and impact lives on. Together, we’re working toward our vision to create the most efficient transportation network in North America.

     

    "To get where we’re gonna go, it takes all of us. I can’t do it, you can’t do it, but we can do that."

    Mr. Hunt
    Founder
     

    Our Vision

    Our vision is to create the most efficient transportation network in North America. This statement keeps us focused on the future and what we’re aspiring for as a company. It encourages us to not only innovate solutions that make our company better, but to find ways to improve the greater supply chain. 

    Our Vision
     

    Our Core Foundations

    After more than 60 years in transportation, we’ve found everything we do comes back to these core foundations: 

    People you trust. 
    Technology that empowers. 
    Capacity to deliver. 

    Investing in these areas fuels our growth as a company so we can continue to innovate and drive transportation forward.

    Our Foundations
     

    Our Mission

     

    Driving long-term value for our people, customers and shareholders.

     

    Our People

    Our people-first commitment was originally instilled into our company culture by our founders, Johnnie Bryan and Johnelle Hunt. From the beginning, they knew that investing in our people was vital to our company’s success.

    People You Trust

    Our Customers

    Whether you’re managing a small business or a global supply chain, we have the infrastructure and capacity to meet your business where it is today and work with you to build a supply chain that helps your business grow in the future.

    Our Shareholders

    The ongoing investment of our shareholders allows us to build J.B. Hunt and serve our customers for the long term. We strive to be good stewards of their investments and deliver compounding returns for them.

    Our Values

    Our company values – integrity, respect, innovation, safety and excellence – are rooted in what Mr. and Mrs. Hunt stood for and how they treated people. These values represent the behaviors that define who we are and guide how we work.

     

    Driven for You™

    Every day, our goal is to deliver excellence to the people we work with and serve. We have a passionate pursuit of excellence because we believe you deserve the very best. That’s why we are driven for you.

    Driven for You
     

    The J.B. Hunt Difference

    We’ve worked hard to distinguish our company as an industry leader. Powered by our foundations, we create innovative supply chain solutions and follow through on our commitments to our customers, finding new ways to drive value, boost efficiency and optimize their operations. It’s why our customers trust us as their transportation provider.

    Environmental, Social and Governance

    We’re committed to doing business in a way that promotes the well-being of our employees, our investors and our planet.

    • We’re pioneering new standards for sustainable shipping by exploring alternative energy, creating efficiencies, driving out waste and reducing carbon emissions. 
    • We invest in our people by creating a work environment that fosters growth and collaboration.
    • We believe in sound and transparent corporate governance that positions us for long-term growth and establishes trust with our stakeholders.
