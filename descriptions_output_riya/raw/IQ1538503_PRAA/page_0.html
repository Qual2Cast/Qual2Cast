Who We Are

Global Reach. Local Touch.

With its global headquarters located in Norfolk, Virginia, PRA Group, Inc. is a publicly traded company that has grown to become one of the largest acquirers of nonperforming loans in the world. Its portfolio operations span 18 countries, 12 languages and 12 currencies. Through its subsidiaries, PRA Group returns essential capital to banks and other lenders, helping expand financial services available to consumers and businesses. For 30 years, PRA Group companies have focused on improving their customers’ financial futures, creating fulfilling careers for thousands of employees worldwide and giving back to local communities.
