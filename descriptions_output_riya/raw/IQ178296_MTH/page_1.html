At close of escrow for the sale of the home, Meritage Homes will pay a real estate commission solely in accordance with, and subject to, the terms and conditions of a fully executed Broker Registration and Commission Addendum that accompanies the applicable Purchase Agreement. As a condition to the payment of any commission, the real estate agent must provide a completed and fully executed Broker Registration and Commission Addendum (in the form provided with the Purchase Agreement) within 24 hours after the buyer executes the applicable contract for the home.

 

Any real estate agent representing the buyer in a transaction for the purchase of a Meritage home represents only the prospective buyer as a “Buyer Broker.” The agent does not represent, act on behalf of, or owe fiduciary duties to Meritage Homes Corporation or any of its affiliated entities.

 

Real estate agent must hold an active real estate license in the state where the home is located.

 

If Addendum is executed in connection with a contract for the sale of a home, the real estate agent shall be solely responsible for obtaining a signed release from the broker previously identified on the Broker Registration and Commission form as a condition precedent to changing the broker of record. Absent such release, Meritage Homes will not recognize any party other than the broker named in such form as sole to the original broker identified in section 5.

Meritage Homes further reserves the right, in its sole discretion to revoke a participant's membership or status in Agents Rock Rewards Program at any time without prior notice or obligation. Eligibility to hold open houses and/or to represent Meritage Homes listings is subject to the sole and absolute discretion of Meritage Homes.

 

All agents are eligible to participate in Meritage’s Agents Rock Rewards Program in any state in which an agent is licensed or otherwise authorized to serve as a residential home broker. Closings required for promotion to Platinum and Diamond Tiers include closings for any homes.
