Who we are

Dexcom empowers people to take control of health through innovative glucose biosensing technology. For more than 25 years, our technology has transformed how people manage diabetes and track their glucose, helping them feel more in control and live more confidently.

Bridging the gap in type 2 diabetes management with CGM

In April 2026, Dexcom surveyed more than 3,000 people with type 2 diabetes and healthcare providers across eight countries to explore how attitudes and experiences related to type 2 diabetes and technology have evolved, revealing an urgent opportunity to improve awareness and access to treatment tools. The survey findings are highlighted in Dexcom's new State of Type 2 Report: Global Access and Attitudes to Diabetes Technology.
