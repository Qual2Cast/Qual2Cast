A BEAUTIFUL HISTORY

Over a century ago, Coty began a journey that helped transform beauty into the global industry it is today.

A legacy of innovation and excellence has guided the business and driven our mission to enable and empower all visions of beauty, from our very first fragrance to the newest portfolio launch. It is a history we are proud to own and build upon…

Our Vision

Our Vision

We believe science and passion are the driving force behind transformation. Together with our people, our partners, and our customers we will make over the world of beauty.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to create forward-thinking beauty through products that provide new, innovative, and simply better science-based solutions.

At Coty, we believe that no one can control or dictate what is or is not beautiful.

Beauty is formless,
like water, a constantly evolving and adapting concept.
Beautiful is whatever makes us feel good in our own skin, and that can change day by day.
We are here to unleash every vision of beauty that exists today and that will exist tomorrow.
It’s whyfearlesskindness isat the heartof everythingwe do.

Fearless Kindness

to ourselves

We will be fearlessly kind to ourselves. We know that beauty starts from the inside. We will bring the best of who we are, including our quirks and our singularities, to the table.

BEAUTY OF OUR PEOPLE

to others

We will be fearlessly kind to others. We embrace and include – we don’t judge and we don’t divide. We are simply one.

BEAUTY OF OUR PRODUCT

to the planet

We will be fearlessly kind to the planet. We prioritize sustainability. We do better with less. We favor quality over quantity.
