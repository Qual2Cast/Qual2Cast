Passion meets expertise.

We are Meridian Holdings.

Founded in 2001 and listed on NASDAQ (MRDN) in 2024, Meridian Holdings is a globally recognized technology and iGaming solutions provider. Built on a foundation of proprietary technology and strategic acquisitions, the company operates the full stack of the gaming business, spanning sportsbook, online casino, raffles and competitions across more than 25 international markets.

With an international team of over 1,400 professionals, Meridian Holdings combines deep operational expertise with in-house technology, allowing the group to control its platforms, optimize margins and continuously develop new products for both B2C and B2B partners.

Today, we are present on all continents, and our portfolio includes a number of global brands: Meridianbet, Expanse Studios, R Kings Competitions, Classics For A Cause, GM-AG and MexPlay—all powered by a scalable proprietary platform designed to adapt to the regulatory, operational and consumer requirements of diverse markets worldwide.

From street-level retail betting shops to premium online gaming experiences, Meridian Holdings brings every element of the gaming ecosystem under one roof, positioning the company as a vertically integrated iGaming powerhouse delivering technology, content and operations at global scale.
