OUR ANSWERS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Bridger Aerospace provides disaster response through aerial firefighting, special-mission aviation and wildland firefighting technology, supported by in-house engineering and MRO (Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul) services. Its operations include initial attack fire suppression, multi-mission mapping and intelligence, air attack coordination, and real-time data delivery through the IGNIS platform to support wildfire and natural disaster response.

Aerial firefighting is the use of specialized aircraft to support wildfire management, containment, and suppression by delivering water or retardant, providing real-time intelligence and mapping, coordinating airspace through Air Attack, and supporting ground firefighters during active incidents. 

Bridger Aerospace ensures safety and compliance through FAA-certified operations, adherence to USFS and DOI standards, rigorous pilot training, standardized operating procedures, and in-house maintenance and engineering programs designed to support high-risk aviation missions.

The Bridger Aerospace fleet includes CL-415EAF and CL-215T Super Scooper amphibious firefighting aircraft, along with Pilatus PC-12 and Daher Kodiak aircraft configured for air attack (aerial supervision) and Multi-Mission intelligence, mapping, and detection operations

IGNIS is Bridger Aerospace’s wildfire technology platform that provides a common operating picture for wildland firefighters. It enables teams to share intelligence, stay up to date on evolving incidents, and access aircraft livestreams and the latest aviation map products. IGNIS supports offline data sharing, air-to-ground integration, wildfire-specific map layers, and fire weather updates, helping firefighters, incident management teams, and agencies coordinate more effectively during active wildfire operations.

Air Attack is the airborne command function over a wildfire, where an Air Tactical Group Supervisor (ATGS) coordinates firefighting aircraft, manages airspace, and aligns aerial tactics with ground operations to improve firefighter safety and overall wildfire suppression effectiveness. 

Bridger Aerospace conducts aerial firefighting operations across the United States and Canada, supporting federal, state, provincial, and local agencies during wildfire operations.

Aerial firefighting is a critical component of wildfire management, supporting ground-based suppression and firefighter safety. It is incredibly effective when used early and in close coordination with ground crews, where rapid, high-volume suppression can slow fire spread, reduce fire intensity, and increase the likelihood of containment before fires become large, complex incidents. Even during extended incidents, aerial firefighting remains essential for protecting firefighters, supporting suppression efforts, and enabling effective incident management.
