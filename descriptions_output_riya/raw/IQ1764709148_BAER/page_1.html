The Company

WHO WE ARE

Bridger Aerospace is a mission-driven organization fueled by the purpose to modernize and strengthen how wildfires are fought. Through the curation of aviation, technology, and engineering, Bridger is a leader in fighting wildfires – from detection to suppression.

Bridger Aerospace deploys the most advanced technologies in aviation to protect lives, property, critical infrastructure, and the environment, delivering these capabilities where they matter most, from wildfire response to defense and beyond.

Bridger Aerospace is a publicly traded aerial firefighting and aerospace services company headquartered in Belgrade, Montana, at Bozeman Yellowstone International Airport (KBZN), with a secondary headquarters in Huntsville, Alabama. Founded in 2014, the company has grown from a single aircraft operation into a leading provider of aerial firefighting, operational intelligence, and specialized aviation services supporting government agencies and emergency response partners across the world.

COMPANY TIMELINE

  • 2014

    Bridger Aerospace Founded

    Founded by combat veterans to bring the principles of close air support to wildland firefighting. Bridger began with a single aircraft in Montana with a mission to improve aerial support for firefighters protecting communities.

  • 2015

    First Fire Season

    Bridger’s first year supporting wildfire response, flying three Twin Commander aircraft in the Air Attack role and establishing the operational foundation for future aerial firefighting operations.

  • 2020

    First Super Scoopers

    Bridger acquired its first two CL-415EAF Super Scoopers, expanding into aerial fire suppression and rapid initial attack.

  • 2021

    Fleet Expansion

    The fleet grew to four Super Scoopers and 20 total aircraft, dropping more than 5 million gallons of water during the fire season.

  • 2022

    Air Attack Modernization

    Bridger added its fifth Super Scooper and introduced the Pilatus PC-12 to modernize Air Attack and aerial intelligence operations.

  • 2023

    Public Company Growth

    Bridger became publicly traded on NASDAQ (BAER), added its sixth Scooper, completed a third hangar, acquired IGNIS, and began building PC-12 Multi-Mission Aircraft.

  • 2024

    Aerospace Engineering Expansion

    Bridger acquired FMS Aerospace, adding aircraft engineering, modification, flight testing, and certification capabilities while supporting early-season wildfire deployments in Texas and Oklahoma.

  • 2025

    Expanded Fire Operations

    Bridger executed the earliest deployment in company history to California and secured a State of Alaska firefighting contract, expanding operational coverage across North America.
