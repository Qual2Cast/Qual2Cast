Fraud doesn’t happen at one moment. 
It happens across the entire customer journey.

Mitek continuously evaluates identity — from onboarding to login to transaction — using biometrics, device intelligence, and AI.

New account origination

Verify real identities in seconds, not days - before fraudsters ever get through the door.

  • Detect synthetic identities and deepfakes at the point of signup
  • Validate documents and biometrics with real-time liveness checks
  • Reduce friction so genuine customers glide through onboarding

Account management

Continuous, passive authentication keeps watch over every login, so risk gets caught, not just checked once.

  • Monitor behavior and device signals across every session
  • Flag anomalies in real time without interrupting trusted users
  • Stop account takeover before compromised credentials turn into losses

Transacting & payments

Not every threat shows up at login. Mitek evaluates risk as money moves.

  • Screen transactions for high-risk patterns and check fraud
  • Catch injection attacks and manipulated data in real time
  • Approve legitimate payments fast while freezing suspicious ones
