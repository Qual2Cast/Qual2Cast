Cornerstone Brands is a family of aspirational lifestyle brands that deliver inspiration and delight customers at every touchpoint. Comprised of four home and apparel brands: Ballard Designs, Frontgate, Grandin Road, and Garnet Hill, each operates a separate ecommerce site, and collectively the brands mail over 90 million catalogs annually and manage 33 retail and outlet stores. Cornerstone Brands Shared Services supports this family of brands with Technology, Distribution Center & Retail Operations, Transportation & Logistics, and Project Management & Transformation.

Ballard Designs is an omnichannel retailer that offers exclusive home furnishings and décor that inspire welcoming spaces that are both timeless and fresh. Through its online store, iconic catalog, 25 retail locations across America, and popular podcast, “How to Decorate” (approaching its 500th episode, with 30,000 unique monthly listeners), the brand brings tradition to modern life.

Shop ballarddesigns.com.

Join the Ballard Designs team.

Frontgate is a retailer devoted to elevating every moment at home with livable luxury: furniture, décor, and accessories that are thoughtfully crafted to enhance the everyday. Especially well known for its high-quality outdoor furniture, customers can experience the Frontgate brand across multiple channels, including catalog, online, and in-person at its beautiful retail stores and popular outlets throughout the South and Midwest.

Shop frontgate.com.

Join the Frontgate team.

For over 50 years, Garnet Hill has been dedicated to creating well crafted, enduring products from renewable natural fibers. The Garnet Hill brand offers women’s, men’s and kids’ apparel as well as premium home textiles in organic cotton, linen, and cashmere – staying true to its core of beautiful, high-quality designs made from nature’s finest fibers.

Shop garnethill.com.

Join the Garnet Hill team.

Grandin Road focuses on bringing joyful touches to any home with unique furnishings, accents, and the very best seasonal décor, from everyday to Christmas. The brand’s haunting Halloween Haven collection has become a go-to source for lovers of fun, spookily themed indoor and outdoor décor.

Visit grandinroad.com.

Join the Grandin Road team.

Cornerstone Brands Shared Services is the organization that supports our four brands with Call Center Services, Distribution Center & Retail Operations, Finance, Marketing & Ecommerce, People & Organization, Project Management & Transformation, Technology, and Transportation & Logistics. The shared services group operates four fulfillment centers located in West Chester, Ohio; Monroe, Ohio; and, Goodyear, Arizona. Every day over 1,600 dedicated and high-performing team members focus on delivering exceptional customer experiences.

Join the Cornerstone Brands Shared Services team.
