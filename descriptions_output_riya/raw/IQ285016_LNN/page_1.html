Progress made possible.

Lindsay prides itself on innovating state-of-the-art technology, manufacturing dependable products, and cultivating genuine partnerships with our customers and each other. Our purpose is simple—to conserve natural resources, expand our world’s potential, and enhance the quality of life for people. Since 1955, we’ve led in the development of innovative products that save time and deliver peace of mind—because real progress isn’t just about technology; it’s about making a tangible difference in people’s lives.

Boldly powering change. Bravely creating opportunity.

Our vision is to be the innovation and market leader in our core segments, including irrigation and infrastructure equipment and technology. We are at the forefront of research and development of sustainable solutions to meet the food, fuel, fiber, and transportation needs of the world’s rapidly growing population.

A global leader with small town values.

At Lindsay, we are committed to building relationships with customers and each other. Headquartered in the Midwest, our values guide us as we innovate customer-first solutions that address the complex challenges of agriculture, infrastructure, and technology. Our global footprint is driven by our commitment to fostering personal connections and maintaining reliability and trustworthiness.

One Lindsay values.

We’re driven by our core values of safety, quality, integrity, collaboration, and creativity. Through trusted long-lasting relationships, innovative, dependable products, and truly caring support, we help make our customers’ work and lives easier, safer, and more fulfilling so the world can keep progressing. Together.

The people who make progress possible.

Lindsay is driven by a team of more than 1,300 hardworking individuals across the globe who are united by pride and passion for our work. We’re proud to foster an inclusive workplace where individual talents are respected and career development is encouraged, no matter where you start. And our team members’ safety is our top priority. We want to ensure every person returns home safely every night.

Are you ready to help us preserve natural resources, feed the global population, and improve road safety? See our careers.
