About Goosehead

Leading the way for our clients and our industry.

In 2003, real estate investor Robyn Jones and her husband Mark Jones, a senior partner at Bain & Co., founded Goosehead Insurance and began transforming the insurance industry by providing clients unprecedented information and product choice. Goosehead has since grown into a national leader by following one simple principle: putting the client at the center of our universe and building the business around them. We provide a wide array of options by shopping the market on our clients’ behalf, educating them, and helping them select the right protection with the right insurance carrier at the best value.
