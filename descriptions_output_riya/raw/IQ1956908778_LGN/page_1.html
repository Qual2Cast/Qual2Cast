About Us

We Are Legence

Legence makes buildings better by providing essential services that improve energy efficiency, enhance reliability, and create healthier indoor environments.

We offer a range of solutions inclusive of engineering, consulting, installation, and maintenance services. We specialize in designing, fabricating and installing HVAC, process piping and other mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) systems for new facilities and upgrading HVAC, lighting, and building controls in existing facilities to make them more energy efficient and sustainable.
Our Values

VISION

Create a future in which energy-efficient, healthy, and resilient buildings are the norm, not the exception.

MISSION

Legence delivers high-performance buildings by leveraging our teams’ integrated approach, technical expertise, and passion for our customers, communities, and colleagues.

PURPOSE

Improve the well-being of people and the planet by unlocking the energy efficiency potential of buildings.
