Viasat is a leading global technology company with a DNA built on pioneering innovation and a belief that everyone and everything in the world can be connected.
We don’t just provide just satellite communications; we solve complex connectivity challenges, combining advanced technologies, digital platforms and deep expertise to power smarter, safer, more efficient operations, and a more sustainable future in space. 
With 90 offices worldwide and a global satellite constellation, we bring high-quality, resilient, and secure connectivity to the places and missions that need it most.

A network built for what’s next

Across land, sea, air, and space, Viasat helps governments, businesses, and communities reach above and go beyond to achieve greater security, operational excellence, and outcomes that matter.

By turning connectivity into a catalyst for real-world impact, we transform operations, fuel innovation, and unlock entirely new experiences. It all starts with a network built around you. 
Trusted

Decades of mission-critical experience as a leading satellite connectivity operator, with the track record to prove it.

Open

Scalable, open, multi-orbit ecosystem integrated with the best partner technologies, giving you control, not dependency.

Resilient

Always on connectivity engineered for congested, contested, and remote environments - where failure isn't an option.

Future-proof technology
Viasat designs, builds, and operates one of the world’s most advanced multi-orbit, multi-band satellite networks, integrating our space and ground infrastructure with that of world-class third-party partners, removing the limitations and risk of a single-network approach. 
It’s a seamless, flexible, scalable, resilient and future-proofed network – delivering the right connection, anywhere, under any conditions.
