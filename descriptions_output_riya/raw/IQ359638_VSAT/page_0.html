After your twelfth month, regular rates apply. To receive the discount, you must remain an active subscriber on an eligible plan and maintain your account in good standing. Offer is not available with any other promotions.

 

The Viasat Essentials plan comes with 150 GB of High-Speed Data and Unlimited Standard Data. After you use 150 GB of High-Speed Data, you will continue to receive unlimited data (referred to as "Standard Data"); however, Viasat may prioritize Standard Data behind other customers during network congestion, which will result in slower speeds.
