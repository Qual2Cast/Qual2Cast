    Business Units

    Equifax serves consumers and business customers through three business units. Each business unit focuses on the needs of unique market segments, putting customers first in everything they do. They are each aligned to our strategic priorities — accelerating innovation and driving new products and services built on the Equifax Cloud™.
    U.S. Information Solutions

    Providing U.S. consumers and businesses with a wide range of solutions  — from consumer credit monitoring and identity theft prevention to business risk information, commercial identity & fraud solutions, financial marketing and analytical services.

    Workforce Solutions
    Partnering with thousands of businesses across the U.S. and government entities to provide human resource, employment, tax, management, and payroll-related services.
    International
    Leveraging unique data sources and analytics to provide consumers, business, government entities around the world with more access to credit and financial opportunity.

    The Equifax Cloud

    The Equifax Cloud™ is the foundation of our business. It is the result of our investment to create a world class enterprise-wide infrastructure tailored to highly regulated data workloads.

    Global Capabilities

    Only Equifax has a powerful combination of differentiated data, advanced analytics, a cloud-native data fabric and scalable global platforms. Equifax brings unrivaled decision intelligence to a wide spectrum of global customers in industries ranging from lending and fraud detection and prevention to HR management, marketing and more.

    Credit Risk

    Gain more predictive power. Make smarter, faster decisions with direct access to superior data and analytics to understand business and consumer behavior for better lending outcomes.

    Verifications

    Confidently verify employment, income, and education information for credit products, mortgages, and government programs with more comprehensive insights delivered seamlessly and quickly.

    Data-driven Marketing

    Turn marketing precision into customer value with data-driven solutions and a unique understanding of household economics. From prospect identification and qualification through origination, and activation — we can help navigate each phase of the acquisition path.

    Workforce Management

    Deliver a great employee experience and meet compliance needs with award-winning HR technology that supports every stage of the employment life cycle; from talent acquisition and onboarding through active service and post-employment.

    Identity & Fraud

    Better insights mean better business. Gain visibility into customers and prospects at the point of interaction — verify and authenticate identity, assess fraud risk, understand device risk, and help ensure regulatory compliance. We offer powerful solutions to mitigate fraud risk.

    Leader in Security

    We’ve completely rebuilt our security and technology systems and are continuously investing in the very best processes, technology and protocols to secure your information.

    We conduct rigorous, frequent and thorough technology and regulatory auditing which gives us full visibility into the data that is coming in and out of our environment.

    A cloud-native technology stack provides the opportunity to exceed an on-premise security posture when combined with the implementation of our rigorous security controls.

    We can quickly and more accurately devalue data to the point that it is useless to attackers.

    Our state-of-the-art cyberfusion center and cybersecurity talent successfully defend against millions of threats every day.

    Community, Foundation and Philanthropy

    Through the Equifax Foundation, we help people live their financial best by supporting the financial wellbeing of the communities in which we live and work. We do this by leveraging our world class data and analytics solutions together with foundation resources in pursuit of building economically resilient communities.
    Our Values in Action
    In 2020, the Equifax Foundation gave nearly $1.7 million in direct grants and $840,000 in matching gifts to organizations of our employees’ choosing.

    We believe that credit building can be a tool for tackling income inequality and decreasing the racial wealth gap. We are proud to have helped thousands of our neighbors through our multi-year partnerships with Greater Atlanta Builds Credit and St. Louis Builds Credit.

    In addition to our significant relationships with Invest America, MoCaFi, Prosperity Connection, Quest Communities, the Tower Grove Neighborhood CDC and the Westside Future Fund, Equifax has heavily invested in the On the Rise Financial Center. Through On the Rise, we are helping people in the Atlanta area earn economic independence through free financial coaching, homebuyer education and money management tools.

    Environment

    At Equifax, we are reducing our global environmental impact by increasing the efficiency of our workplaces, enhancing our approach to utilizing our office space, managing our waste, optimizing our data storage strategy and managing our carbon footprint.
