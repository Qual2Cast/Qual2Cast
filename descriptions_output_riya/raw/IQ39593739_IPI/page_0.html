Essential Minerals for Certified Organic Farms

Intrepid is committed to providing essential minerals for organic crops with OMRI Listed Muriate of Potash (0-0-60) and OMRI Listed Trio (Sulfate of Potash Magnesia).

Intrepid OMRI Listed Muriate of Potash (MOP) is derived from our solar evaporation mines in Utah and provides an immediately available (60% K2O) potassium source. Potassium often referred to as the regulator nutrient aids in photosynthesis, improves water efficiency of the plant and improves overall plant health helping resist pests and disease.

Intrepid OMRI Listed Trio provides a long-lasting and readily available source of low-chloride potassium, magnesium and immediately available sulfur in the sulfate form. The magnesium in Trio is water soluble aiding the plant in photosynthesis and supporting overall plant growth in magnesium-deficient soils.The sulfur in Trio is immediately available helping to support organic compounds and boost nitrogen efficiency uptake in the plant.
