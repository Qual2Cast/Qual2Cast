Our Savers® cycle.

Here’s how it works.

We accept your donations on behalf of a local nonprofit.

We pay our nonprofit partners for your stuff, helping them fund programs in your community.

Your one-of-a-kind finds are given a second chance to be loved again by thrifters like you.

Together, we champion reuse and keep millions of reusable items out of landfills every year.

Pretty cool, right? We can’t do it without you.
