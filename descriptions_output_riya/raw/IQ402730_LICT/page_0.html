Company Overview

LICT Corporation (OTC: LICT) is a publicly-traded holding company with subsidiaries providing integrated broadband, voice and video services to business and residential customers in rural markets across the United States. The company, with operations in Utah, New Mexico, Kansas, Wisconsin, Iowa, California and Oregon, provides services through its existing and expanding network infrastructure, including thousands of miles of fiber, copper, coaxial cable, wireless towers and owned wireless spectrum. The company also provides fixed wireless services through its wholly-owned subsidiary, Sound Broadband. LICT is expanding its market coverage areas through an aggressive network construction program, and is continually evaluating non-organic growth opportunities through M&A.
  • Broadband through advanced fiber networks, Digital Subscriber Line (DSL) and cable modem services
  • Fixed wireless broadband, including 5G
  • Voice, local and long-distance. Video, including cable television services (CATV) and Internet Protocol Television Services (ITV)
  • Wholesale network access for other carriers
  • Enterprise solutions, including hosted/managed services, private lines and virtual switchboard
  • Public access services, including 911 emergency service
