Our Products

Largest Refrigerant Selection in the Industry

Choose the refrigerants you need, select the size and we'll quote for delivery to anywhere in the country. Through our nationwide network, Hudson maintains and distributes the largest selection of refrigerants in the country. All are tested to meet or exceed AHRI-700 purity standards.

In addition to our Hudson brand AmeriPure, we also distribute certified reclaimed EMERALD Refrigerants®, and are a proud licensed provider of reclaimed FREON™ Refrigerants (R-11, R-12, R-123, R-22).

Hudson offers a full catalog of next-generation, lower GWP alternatives. As the HVACR industry in the United States moves towards more environmentally healthy options, A2Ls have emerged as a replacement for higher GWP refrigerants such as HFCs and HCFCs. If you’re interested in supporting the transition and making a change, view AHRI’s A2L Refrigerant Building Code Map, for all the latest local and state building codes, standards, and legislation.

Brands we also distribute: Arkema Forane®, Chemours™, DuPont™ ISCEON®, Honeywell Genetron®, Refron®, Mexichem Fluor®, and more!
