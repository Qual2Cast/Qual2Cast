Corporate Overview

Trinity Industries, Inc. owns market-leading businesses that provide railcar products and services in North America under the trade name TrinityRail®. TrinityRail’s unique platform provides a single source for comprehensive rail transportation solutions that optimizes our customers’ ownership and usage of railcar equipment and enhances the value-proposition of the rail modal supply chain.

Our purpose is to Deliver Goods for the Good of All. We remain committed to rail solutions that deliver goods—safely, efficiently, and sustainably - for the good of our customers, shareholders, and employees in all the communities we serve.

Trinity reports its financial results in two principal segments: the Railcar Leasing and Management Services Group and the Rail Products Group. 
Our common stock trades on the New York Stock Exchange and NYSE Texas under the symbol TRN.

More About Us


Delivering Goods for the Good of All™

It’s more than a motto — it’s our purpose. Rail transports the goods that keep our lives, our communities, and our economy thriving, and TrinityRail is proud to keep North America’s rail shippers moving forward.

TrinityRail is an industry-leading railcar leasing company, enhanced by our manufacturing and service capabilities. We are a leader in providing leasing and logistics services, and act as an integrated and strategic partner to stakeholders across the industry. We are on a mission to increase rail’s modal share and convenience by delivering more sustainable, end-to-end solutions that bridge the first and last miles for our customers.
