About Us

  • About Us
  • Trinity Platform
Trinity began providing industrial products and services to customers more than 85 years ago. We started as a small butane-tank manufacturer and evolved over time into a premier provider of railcar products and services in North America with a leading railcar lease fleet and manufacturing, maintenance and repair operations.   We have a long history of creating value for our shareholders by providing quality products and services to our customers.

Our rail-related products and services are marketed under the trade name TrinityRail. Our purpose is to Deliver Goods for the Good of All. We remain committed to rail solutions that deliver goods—safely, efficiently, and sustainably - for the good of our customers, shareholders, and employees in all the communities we serve.

The TrinityRail platform plays an important role in the North American supply chain by facilitating the transportation of bulk commodities and goods by rail. Railcar transportation is sustainable and is the most economical land-based mode of transportation. At Trinity, we strive to employ company resources in ways that make positive contributions to our stakeholders for the benefit of current and future generations.
 
TrinityRail’s platform aims to deliver innovative solutions that optimize the life-cycle ownership and usage of railcar equipment and enhance the value-proposition of the rail modal supply chain. We differentiate our value proposition to all stakeholders by:
  • leveraging our rail expertise across a broad market viewpoint,
  • working collaboratively to provide an unparalleled customer experience, and
  • delivering significant financial and commercial synergies that create a strategic advantage.

 
 
 
 
 
 
Our market leadership, performance and growth through the years are attributable to:
  • the skills, talents, and integrity of our people,
  • the synergies within our platform,
  • the quality of our rail products and services,
  • the depth of our operational capabilities, market insight and expertise, and
  • our cultural commitment to excellence and continuous improvement.
