About

The Gabelli Research Process

Gabelli is a global investment firm. Its mission is to deliver superior results and an exceptional client experience. 

We offer an array of investment choices across funds (Open and Closed-End) and separate accounts. Today, we combine research with our accumulated, compounded knowledge to craft our value, growth, fixed-income, and convertibles strategies. 

The Gabelli Research Process rests solidly on three pillars: 

People

The Gabelli Equity Research Team includes over 30 sector-focused analysts who support equity portfolio management and provide sector insights to all investment disciplines.

Process

Our trademarked Private Market Value (PMV) With a Catalyst™ approach utilizes fundamental, bottom-up research to identify companies selling at less than their intrinsic value. Our ability to identify mispriced companies with strong business fundamentals and the presence of a catalyst that will surface value underscores our ability to deliver superior absolute returns. 

Philosophy

Our proprietary research process begins with an examination of a company’s intrinsic value. From our universe of over 2000 companies, we invest in companies as owners of those businesses.

who we do it for

Gabelli Service

Institutional and individual clients have entrusted Gabelli with their assets for nearly five decades.

We join our investors’ unique needs with Gabelli’s trademarked investment approach into bespoke strategies. We aim to earn a superior absolute return for our clients.

Institutional Investors

We provide institutional clients with tailored strategies to generate long-term growth. Our research-driven approach ensures we can navigate complex market environments, helping institutions confidently achieve investment objectives.

Private Wealth Clients

For high-net-worth individuals and families, we offer personalized wealth management services. We aim to preserve and grow wealth, support their financial goals, and create a lasting legacy for future generations.

Investment Professionals

We partner with investment professionals to deliver innovative strategies aligned with their client’s needs. By leveraging our proprietary research and investment insights, we help advisors enhance their offerings and achieve superior outcomes for their clients.

our inspiration

Gabelli Legacy

For nearly 50 years, Mario Gabelli has driven our commitment to helping clients achieve their financial aspirations. 

As a pioneer of value investing, Mario Gabelli founded Gabelli with a clear purpose: to leverage rigorous research to generate superior returns. His innovative strategies and commitment to excellence have defined Gabelli as an investment leader known for integrity, performance, and the ability to navigate the complexities of the financial markets.

Under Mario’s leadership, Gabelli continues to refine its proprietary research methodologies to consistently deliver strong, risk-adjusted returns and build an unparalleled reputation with clients and investors.
