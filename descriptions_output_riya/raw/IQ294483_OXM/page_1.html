An 80-year legacy of reinvention.

Growing from a WWII-era manufacturer into a leading brand steward, we have consistently evolved alongside the world, investing through headwinds, anticipating consumer shifts, and embracing change as a growth strategy.

  • Foundation

1942

Founded as a manufacturer of private label apparel

  • Going Public

1960

Becomes a public company and has paid a dividend every quarter since

  • ACHIEVEMENT

1983

Achieves Fortune 500 status

  • TRANSFORMATION

2003

Acquires Tommy Bahama, launching transformation away from manufacturing into managing lifestyle brands

  • Expansion

2010-21

Expands further into womenswear, menswear, and childrenswear with Lilly Pulitzer, Southern Tide, Duck Head, and The Beaufort Bonnet Company

  • DIVERSIFICATION

2022-23

Diversifies across geographies, categories, seasons and price points with the acquisitions of Johnny Was and Jack Rogers

  • INVESTMENT

2024

Invests in retail expansion and scalable infrastructure with state-of-the-art distribution center
