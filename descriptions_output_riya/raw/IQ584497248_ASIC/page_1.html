About Ategrity

At Ategrity, we believe that insurance should be fast, intelligent, and effortless for agents and brokers. By leveraging advanced risk analysis and cutting-edge technology, we are making the underwriting process for small to medium-sized businesses smarter and more efficient. This enables us to offer consistent, accurate solutions with minimal hassle.

Our commitment to data-driven risk-taking and ease of use is matched with financial strength, a cornerstone of our partners’ confidence in Ategrity. We have purposefully maintained a conservatively capitalized balance sheet with no financial leverage, which supports our “A-” (Excellent) financial strength rating from A.M. Best. This strong foundation ensures we can continue to provide innovative solutions and exceptional service to our partners while adapting to the evolving needs of the market.
