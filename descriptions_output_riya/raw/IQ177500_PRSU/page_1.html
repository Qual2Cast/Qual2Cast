About Us

Places you dream of. Experiences you’ll never forget.

Connect to Iconic

Pursuit began as a spark—an idea that the world’s most incredible destinations deserve experiences just as special as they are. What started with a handful of storied properties has grown into a collection of world-class attractions and hospitality experiences. Since 2016, we’ve been driven by a clear mission: to connect you to iconic places through unforgettable, inspiring experiences.

Adventure Awaits

At Pursuit, we invite you to explore places that will stir your spirit—from the vast wilderness of Denali and the otherworldly landscapes of Iceland to the rugged peaks of Banff, the lush rainforests of Costa Rica and the coastal charm of Victoria, British Columbia. Whether you're embarking on a river trip in Montana, watching whales breach off Vancouver Island, or unwinding by a serene lakeside lodge in Jasper, every destination offers a range of one-of-a-kind adventures.

A World of Experiences

Pursuit’s experiences include 18 world-class point-of-interest attractions and 29 distinctive lodges, along with thoughtful restaurants, retail, and transportation that enable our guests to discover and connect with what makes each location unique. In every experience, you will find friendly team members, thoughtful amenities, and delightful details along the way. So join us—adventure awaits, and a world of experiences is ready to welcome you.
