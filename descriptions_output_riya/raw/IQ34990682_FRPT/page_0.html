Freshpet earns the Clean Label Project Purity Award

Freshpet has received Clean Label Project’s Purity Award across our portfolio, which means our recipes meet their highest purity standard based on independent testing for contaminants. Clean Label Project is a non-profit that tests products beyond traditional food safety standards looking for “hidden” contaminants such as heavy metals (e.g., lead, mercury, arsenic, cadmium) as well as pesticides and plasticizers.
