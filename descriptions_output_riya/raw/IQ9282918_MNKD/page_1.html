How MannKind is changing lives for the better.

Drawing on the legacy of innovator and humanitarian Alfred E. Mann, we aim to develop patient-centric treatments that can help people manage serious medical conditions so that they can experience the very best that life has to offer.

With our ultra rapid-acting inhaled insulin, Afrezza® — featuring our innovative Technosphere® technology — we are changing the way diabetes is treated. Similarly, Furoscix ReadyFlow™ enables episodes of fluid buildup to be managed at home rather than in a hospital setting. We are developing new ways for people with cardiometabolic and orphan lung diseases to truly thrive.

We are driven to find new ways to change lives for the better. To keep working toward a world without limit for all.

To help more people live life more humann.®

our humann values

Our values embody the spirit and drive of our founder Alfred Mann and reflect how we aspire to drive our business forward. They define who we are, how we act, and guide our interactions every day—both with each other and the people we serve.

  • DO RIGHT

    Treat everyone with respect and do what’s right–period.

    DO RIGHT

    We keep it real with each other and those we serve, treating others how we’d like to be treated—with integrity and humanity—even when no one is looking. And we always do the right thing. In our eyes, there’s no alternative.

  • WIN TOGETHER

    Build strong relationships and help each other succeed.

    WIN TOGETHER

    We’re strongest as a team—united, we can do extraordinary things. We proactively break down barriers and build strong relationships across functions and geographies—rolling up our sleeves and sharing ideas, perspectives, and feedback to help each other succeed.

  • OWN IT

    Commit to continuous improvements & lasting impacts.

    OWN IT

    We see every challenge as an opportunity, and approach each one prepared, engaged, and accountable. Our commitment to continuous improvement drives our performance and delivers a lasting impact on every life we touch.

  • BE TENACIOUS

    Set aggressive goals– then exceed them.

    BE TENACIOUS

    Fueled by passion and a high sense of urgency, we don’t wait for tomorrow if we can do it today. We set aggressive goals and strive to surpass them—overcoming obstacles and delivering insistently on our priorities every day.

  • PUSH BOUNDARIES

    Challenge conventional wisdom and think beyond the possible.

    PUSH BOUNDARIES

    We look for creative solutions to the most pressing problems. We think beyond what is possible—seeking open feedback and new ideas, encouraging expansive thinking, and challenging conventional wisdom at every turn.

A team of dedicated humanns.

Executive Leadership Team

Michael Castagna, PharmD

Chief Executive Officer

Michael Castagna, PharmD

Chief Executive Officer

Michael Castagna became CEO of MannKind Corporation and joined the company’s board of directors in 2017, bringing more than 25 years’ experience in healthcare, pharmaceuticals and biotechnology to the company's product and medical device development strategy. Under his guidance, MannKind is successfully establishing solid partnerships and growing its pipeline, capitalizing on Michael's extensive knowledge of the commercial pharmaceutical industry. Michael’s deep understanding of the biopharmaceutical industry and dedication to helping people live their lives without limits is grounded by his extensive pharmacy background.
Previously, Michael served as a Vice President at Amgen, guiding both the Lifecycle Management and Global Commercial Biosimilar business areas of the company. Before Amgen, he spent a combined 10 years at Bristol-Myers Squibb, focusing on medical, marketing and sales from 1997 to 2005, returning to the company in 2010 to direct the Immunoscience division from 2010 to 2012. Other industry experience includes serving as a Vice President at Sandoz, as well as a Director at both Merck, Serono and Pharmasset.
When he's not focusing on company strategy and operations or how to make innovative new treatments available to people living with diabetes and orphan lung diseases, Michael spends time traveling with his family, scuba diving, and visiting car events.

Chief Financial Officer

Ajay Ahuja, MD, MBA

Chief Medical Officer

Ajay Ahuja, MD, MBA

Chief Medical Officer

Dr. Ajay Ahuja is a pediatrician and cardiologist who joined MannKind Corporation in 2025 as Chief Medical Officer. A seasoned biopharmaceutical leader, he has over two decades of industry experience, spanning medical devices, drug delivery platforms, and biopharmaceuticals – with expertise in therapeutic areas including diabetes, cardiovascular disease, respiratory medicine, and infectious diseases.
Prior to MannKind, Dr. Ahuja held leadership roles across clinical development, medical affairs, and commercial strategy at companies such as Kardigan Bio, Takeda, Pfizer, GSK, and Novartis. He trained in pediatric cardiology at Harvard Medical School and Boston Children’s Hospital, where he served on staff for more than a decade. Dr. Ahuja earned his Doctor of Medicine from Washington University School of Medicine and completed his residency and fellowship training at Northwestern University and Harvard Medical School, respectively. He also earned an MBA from Harvard Business School before embarking on his industry career.
Outside of work, Ajay enjoys spending time with his family, sports, cooking, and international travel. He holds dual U.S.–U.K. citizenships and speaks four languages.

Dominic Marasco, RPh

President, Endocrine Business Unit

Dominic Marasco is President of MannKind’s Endocrine Business Unit, leading the team’s commercial operations and development. With a more than 25-year distinguished career with a proven track record in driving strategic growth and operational excellences, he guides the future of MannKind’s endocrine-related business unit and the company’s groundbreaking and innovative treatment options for people living with diabetes.

Prior to that he served as Chief Commercial Officer at BioAgilytix Labs, Inc. Dominic served as EVP, Global Business Development, Commercial Group at Syneos Health after serving in progressive leadership roles at Amgen, Inc. including Head of U.S. Sales for the Neuroscience Business Unit and prior to that Global Commercial Head, Amgen Biosimilars. His career began as a pharmacist, having earned his RPh as a graduate of the Philadelphia College of Pharmacy, and is an alumnus of the Harvard Business School’s Advanced Management Program.
Outside of work, Dominic enjoys spending his time with family, traveling, skiing, and mentoring entrepreneurs in life sciences.

Chairman Emeritus

Kent Kresa

Kent Kresa is Chairman Emeritus of MannKind Corporation. Mr. Kresa joined MannKind’s Board of Directions in June 2004 and spent more than two decades as part of the company’s governing body. In November 2011, he became our lead independent director and from February 2017 until December 2020, he was our Chairman.

Mr. Kresa was Northrop Grumman’s president from 1987 to 2001, CEO from 1990 to 2003, and Board Chairman from 1990 to 2003. After retiring from Northrop Grumman, in 2009 he was chosen by the Obama administration to become chairman of the board of General Motors and lead that company through bankruptcy.

A member of the National Academy of Engineering and the American Academy of Arts and Sciences, the past chairman of the board of governors of the Aerospace Industries Association, and chairman of the Defense Policy Advisory Committee on Trade, Kresa is also an honorary fellow of the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics and was its president for a one-year term in 2002. Kresa holds three degrees from MIT: a BS, an MS, and an EAA, all in aeronautics and astronautics.

Alfred E. Mann

Alfred E. Mann (1925-2016) was a visionary in aerospace, medical technology, and biopharmaceuticals. But his true passion was helping people. During his career, he founded 17 companies and developed breakthrough medical devices, including cardiac pacemakers, insulin pumps, cochlear implants, retinal prostheses, and other technologies that have enabled people to live fuller, healthier lives.

At MannKind we continue to approach innovation by drawing on the spirit of Al’s mission – that holds true today and into the future.

Altruism was a cornerstone value for Mr. Mann, and he was a member of The Giving Pledge, a group founded by Warren Buffet, and Bill and Melinda Gates, in which billionaires commit to giving away at least 50% of their net worth.

Mr. Mann served as a Trustee for the University of Southern California, a member of the Board of Overseers of the Keck USC School of Medicine, and established the Alfred E. Mann Institute for Biomedical Engineering at USC. Al Mann’s vision was to provide the necessary funding to accelerate the commercialization of bioengineering and medical inventions. In 2023, as part of the legacy gift that was made by Mr. Mann during his lifetime, the Pharmacy school was named in his honor as the USC Alfred E. Mann School of Pharmacy and Pharmaceutical Sciences.
