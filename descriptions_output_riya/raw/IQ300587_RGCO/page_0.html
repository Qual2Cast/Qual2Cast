RGC Resources

Nasdaq: RGCO

Investing in energy solutions since 1883.

RGC Resources is an energy services company engaged in the sale and distribution of natural gas to approximately 64,500 residential, commercial and industrial customers in Roanoke, Virginia, and the surrounding localities, through its Roanoke Gas subsidiary. In addition to Roanoke Gas, RGC Midstream is a subsidiary that invests in natural gas transportation infrastructure including the Mountain Valley Pipeline (MVP), MVP Southgate and MVP Boost.
