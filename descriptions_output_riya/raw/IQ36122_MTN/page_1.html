Learn More About How Our Guest Experience Comes To Life

Vail Resorts Retail is the premier outdoor specialty retail company in the industry and the largest snow sports retailer with over 250 owned and operated locations throughout the U.S. and Canada, including branded stores such as Burton, Columbia and Patagonia, specialty rental shops, luxury retail stores as well as online ski rentals and direct-to-lodging delivery services at all Vail Resorts properties. Coming soon: My Epic Gear, a membership program that reimagines gear ownership, with more choice and lower cost. 

Learn more

Our mountain resorts are home to the world’s finest ski and snowboard schools, led by thousands of the industry’s top professional instructors from around the globe. We are proud to cultivate positive learning experiences and a genuine passion for snowsports. In recent years, lesson offerings have expanded to include adaptive programs, advance terrain park education, and highly certified experts to guide even the most advanced skiers and riders.

Vail Resorts Hospitality owns and operates luxury hotels under the RockResorts brand, a wholly owned subsidiary of Vail Resorts that sets the standard for belonging in some of the world’s most admired destinations. The company also manages hotels and condominiums in mountain resort communities, several award-winning golf courses and three destination resorts in the Grand Teton National Park.

learn more

With over 300 food and beverage outlets across our family of mountain resorts, guests can find a broad range of dining options including coffee shops, grab-and-go and quick service outlets, as well as full service and fine dining restaurants. Our food and beverage offerings are located in resort base areas, mid-mountain, and high-alpine lodges as well as within our hospitality footprint. Our team also offers incredible dining experiences, such as sleigh rides and snow cats that carry guests to award-winning restaurants in historic cabins and homesteads. 

Epic Mountain Express provides the easiest way from Denver International Airport to popular Colorado mountain resort areas, including Vail, Breckenridge, Keystone, Frisco, Beaver Creek, Bachelor Gulch, and surrounding ski resort areas. Part of our Lodging division, Epic Mountain Express offers airport transfers for all budgets and all travelers.

Summer Operations

World-class mountain landscapes and trails, including those found at the legendary Whistler Blackcomb Bike Park, allow us to deliver an Experience of a Lifetime year-round. Our list of summer activities and experiences at our mountain resorts spans lift-served mountain biking and hiking, mountain coasters, zip lines, kids’ activities and Epic Discovery zones, plus much more. Vail Resorts also operates a number of high-altitude golf courses with exceptional views, including Red Sky Golf Club, Beaver Creek Golf Club, and Canyons Golf , as well as fly fishing operations, including Taylor Creek Fly Shops and Gore Creek Fly Fisherman. ​

Grand Teton Lodge Company​

Vail Resorts owns the Grand Teton Lodge Company, which is based in the Jackson Hole area in Wyoming and operates within Grand Teton National Park under a concession agreement with the National Park Service. Grand Teton Lodge Company operates hotels, dining, campgrounds and activities, with three lodging options: Jackson Lake Lodge, a full service, 385-room resort; Jenny Lake Lodge, a small, rustically elegant retreat with 37 cabins; and Colter Bay Village, a facility with 166 log cabins, 66 tent cabins, 337 campsites and a 112-space recreational vehicle park.

We also own the Jackson Hole Golf & Tennis Club and Flagg Ranch, centrally located between Yellowstone National Park and Grand Teton National Park. Grand Teton Lodge Company’s operating season generally runs from mid-May through the end of September.
