About Joe

About JOE

CONNECT. PEOPLE. PLACE.

The St. Joe Company is a diversified real estate development company headquartered in Panama City Beach, FL. With landholdings primarily concentrated in five Northwest Florida counties, St. Joe develops its land for residential, commercial and hospitality assets. Its history and its employees firmly rooted in Northwest Florida, St. Joe has adopted a long-term vision for the region, thinking not only of short-term gains but lasting benefits.

For St. Joe, development is about more than building on land—it’s about creating meaningful connections between people and place. Every project is designed to foster opportunities for community, whether through walkable town centers, welcoming accommodations, or thoughtfully planned neighborhoods. By blending natural beauty with purposeful design, we aim to create environments where people can truly belong, engage, connect and thrive.

“The history of the company is deeply intertwined with the history of this region and its people. What makes that relationship especially unique is the many different ways we are connected to the community. In many places, a company’s impact may center around a single facility or industry, but here, our involvement extends across many aspects of community life and economic growth.”

- Jorge Gonzalez, President, CEO and Chairman of the Board, The St. Joe Company

Company History

1923

Vacation Turns into Investment

In the 1920s, Alfred I. duPont visited Florida seeking a vacation home. His interest soon turned to large-scale timberland acquisitions across Northwest Florida. DuPont's holdings expanded to nearly 100,000 acres of newly cleared forestlands and in the early 1930s, DuPont acquired an additional 240,000 acres in Northwest Florida, including Port St. Joe.

1936

St. Joe is Founded - Timberland and Paper

In 1935, Alfred I. duPont passed away, leaving his estate and holdings to his brother-in-law and business partner, Edward Ball. In 1936, Ball incorporated the St. Joe Paper Company, continuing duPont's vision. Operations began at the paper mill in Port St. Joe with a focus on timberland and paper production. Over the next 20 years, the company acquired vast tracts of timerland, reaching more than 1 million acres by 1960.

1961

Railroad & Sugarcane Operation Acquired

In 1961, the St. Joe Paper Company acquired Florida East Coast Railway Company and nearly a decade later, Florida Talisman Sugarcane Operation, a 50,000-acre farm and sugar mill.

1982

Real Estate Division Established

Jacob Belin, Chair of the St. Joe Paper Company, was elected as Chair of the Board of the duPont Trust after Ball’s death in 1981. Belin began selling off the company's industrial operations and shifted the focus to land development, positioning the company for the real estate boom of decades to come. The St. Joe Real Estate division was formed, seeking "the highest and best use" of St. Joe lands.

1990

Listing Earned on NYSE

In 1986, the St. Joe Paper Company sold 14% of its common stock to 602 shareholders and in 1990 the duPont Trust sold an additional 17% of the St. Joe Paper Company's shares, raising approximately $230 million and earning a listing on the New York Stock Exchange.

1996

Focus Shifts from Timber to Development

In 1996, the Port St. Joe paper mill was sold, shifting focus from timber to land development to maximize the company's landholdings. The following  year, former Disney executive Peter Rummell became CEO and in 1998 renamed the company, The St. Joe Company. The iconic bluebird logo was introduced and the new ticker symbol, JOE.

1998

Assets Sold to Focus on Development

As the company continued to hone its focus on real estate development, in 1998, it sold Talisman Sugar followed by more than 500,000 acres of land for conservation to The Nature Conservancy and the State of Florida from the late 1990s to the early 2000s.

1999

Development Begins on WaterColor® Community

In 1999, construction began on the WaterColor 499-acre master-planned community in South Walton County, including WaterColor Inn & Resort. This was the company's first hotel project and featured 67 guestrooms and suites just steps from a private beach. The hotel is still operational today and a favorite of 30A visitors.

1999

Foundation Established to Support Local Communities

The St. Joe Community Foundation was established in 1999 as a way for St. Joe communities to support the efforts of nonprofits related to education, health, the environment and the arts. The Foundation is funded exclusively by a transfer fee of one half of 1 percent on the sale price of real estate in certain residential communities The St. Joe Company develops.

2010

Headquarters Moves to Northwest Florida

In 2010, the St. Joe headquarters moved from Jacksonville, Florida to Northwest Florida where a majority of land holdings and development was occurring. The corporate office was first located in Inlet Beach near Scenic Highway 30A in Walton County but eventually moved to Panama City Beach in Bay County (current corporate office pictured).

2010

Airport Opens on Land Donated by JOE

Recognizing the economic vitality and opportunity in the region, The St. Joe Company donated 4,000 acres for construction of Northwest Florida Beaches International Airport (ECP), which opened May 2010. ECP was the first international airport built in the United States post-September 11 and continues to see record growth in number of passengers each year.

2013

Land Sold as Northwest Florida Takes Priority

In 2013, The St. Joe Company sold nearly 400,000 acres of non-strategic land to AgReserves, Inc. With more and more development happening in Northwest Florida, the sale allowed St. Joe to focus on its core real estate development business in the region and served as another example of the company's gradual move away from timber farming and paper production over the past two decades.

2015

50-Year Bay-Walton Sector Plan Approved

In 2015, The St. Joe Company's Bay-Walton Sector Plan, a long-term framework for future development encompassing 110,500 acres, gained approval from county and state governments. In addition to proposed development, the plan includes conserving more than 53,000 acres of regionally significant natural resources or nearly 50 percent of the project area. (Map for illustrative purposes only.)

2016

New Company Strategy Emerges

The St. Joe Company formulated and started to execute a new company strategy with a focus on growing recurring revenue and diversifying the residential, hospitality and commercial segments to include a broad range of price points, product types and demographics.

2018

Construction Begins on First Apartment Community

In June of 2018, St. Joe and HomeCorp began the construction of the first phase of apartment homes at Pier Park Crossings located on North Pier Park Drive adjacent to Frank Brown Park, the first of several more multi-family communities to come.

2018

Sports Complex and K-8 Public School Initiated

The St. Joe Company, TDC, Bay County, Bay County School District executed a Public-Private Partnership for the construction of a new state of the art amateur sports complex and a new K-8 public school in east Panama City Beach. The sports complex was completed in 2019, and the school was completed in 2021.

2019

Magnet Innovation Center Established

St. Joe partnered with Walton County Schools to transform its former corporate headquarters into the Magnet Innovation Center, a STEAM-focused public school. Through hands-on, project-based learning, the MIC equips students with critical thinking, collaboration, and problem-solving skills designed to prepare them for future careers and emerging technologies. The partnership was awarded the Commissioner's Business Recognition Award by the Florida Department of Education.

2019

St. Joe Invests in Hurricane Michael Recovery

After the devasting impact of Hurricane Michael in October of 2018, The St. Joe Company committed to making significant investments in Panama City to help the area recover with residential communities of College Station, Park Place, Titus Park, North Bay Landing and Mexico Beach Crossings, all located in eastern Bay Co, the part of the county that was the most impacted by the path of Hurricane Michael.

2019

Development Begins on Latitude Margaritaville Watersound

Master developer Minto Communities USA, global lifestyle brand Margaritaville Holdings and The St. Joe Company announced commencement of land development activities for Latitude Margaritaville Watersound located in Panama City Beach, FL. Latitude was the first active adult (55+) community in the St. Joe residential portfolio.

2020

New Downtown Panama City Hotel and Restaurant Approved

The Panama City Commission approved a ground lease with St. Joe to help with the re-development of downtown Panama City with a new waterfront hotel and restaurant, a process that started in 2018 when St. Joe sent a letter to the city offering to make an investment in downtown. Construction of the hotel and restaurant was completed in 2022 and 2023 exclusively with a capital investment made by St. Joe and with no public funds.

2023

FSU Health Medical Campus Breaks Ground

The St. Joe Company, Florida State University and Tallahassee Memorial HealthCare celebrated the construction kick-off in January 2023 of a healthcare campus in Panama City Beach with the goal of bringing together clinicians, researchers and students to better serve the medical needs of Northwest Florida.

2023

Five Hotels Opened in First Half of Year

In 2023, St. Joe opened five new hotels. These hotels included The Lodge 30A, Embassy Suites by Hilton Panama City Beach Resort, Hotel Indigo Panama City Marina, Home2 Suites by Hilton Santa Rosa Beach and Camp Creek® Inn.

2024

First Building on Medical Campus Opens

The St. Joe Company and Tallahassee Memorial Healthcare announced the opening of the first building at the medical medical campus in Panama City Beach, which currently houses primary and urgent care, cardiology and pulmonary services, obstetrics and gynecology, dermatology and an outpatient surgery center.

Connections: A JOE Video Series

Connections is a storytelling video series that explores the many ways St. Joe brings people, places, and experiences together across its communities. Each video, hosted by St. Joe Senior Vice President and Chief Operating Officer Rhea Goff, offers an inside look at individual properties or curated groups of assets through engaging visuals and insightful interviews. “Connecting people to place” serves as the unifying theme, illustrating how our assets collectively contribute to a thoughtfully planned ecosystem for residents and visitors alike.
