THE ST. JOE COMPANY

Connect. People. Place.

On the surface, The St. Joe Company is a Northwest Florida based real estate developer and asset manager—focused on thoughtfully planned destinations to live, work, play, shop, and vacation near the region's beautiful white sand beaches. Dig a little deeper and you'll discover that the essence of what we do is connect people to place. We seek to create enduring communities that embody a distinct lifestyle and to cultivate experiences inspired by the natural beauty and character of the Emerald Coast.

What to know more? Read our brochure online or have one mailed to you.

Long-Term Vision

The Bay-Walton Sector Plan is a Long-Term Master Plan on 110,500 acres of the company’s land holdings located in Bay County and Walton County.  The sector plan was not required by law, but the company volunteered to undergo the process because it saw a unique opportunity to create a long-range vision in collaboration with the community that was an innovative, comprehensive, and proactive approach to land use planning as opposed to the more traditional short term, fragmented, and reactive approach.  The framework was finalized after extensive public participation at workshops, open houses, and public hearings from 2013-2015.  The plan was approved by Bay County, Walton County, and the State of Florida in 2015 pursuant to Section 163.3245, Florida Statues.

An integral component of the Bay-Walton Sector Plan is the creation of a regionally significant water-shed level environmental framework that contains 53,229 acres or 48 percent of the master plan in a series of well-connected conservation and preservation areas, especially for the protection of West Bay.
