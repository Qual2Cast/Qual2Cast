Who We Are:
About Balchem.

For over five decades, Balchem has created innovative health and nutritional solutions for people and animals worldwide. Our diverse portfolio impacts millions, offering quality and technology that help our customers succeed. Balchem operates in three segments: Human Nutrition & Health, Animal Nutrition & Health, and Specialty Products.

Learn More

Our Businesses.

Human Nutrition & Health

Our Human Nutrition & Health business leverages decades of expertise in market insights, proven science, and product development to create winning solutions in health, wellbeing, and nutrition.

Animal Nutrition & Health

As a global leader of choline production and encapsulation technology, our Animal Nutrition & Health business provides a portfolio of nutrition products for ruminants, monogastrics, and companion animals.

Specialty Products

Our Performance Gases specializes in the packaging and global distribution of select gases.  Our Plant Nutrition business  provides Metalosate® chelated minerals to the agricultural market worldwide.

Innovative Health Solutions.

At Balchem, innovation drives us to make the world a healthier place. Our R&D expertise fuels business growth and solves tough challenges. With eight Innovation Centers in the US and Europe, we create hundreds of new products annually. Discover our core technologies.
