Specialty Products.

Our Specialty Products business segment complements our vision of making the world a healthier place. Plant Nutrition provides Metalosate® chelated minerals to the agricultural market worldwide. Performance Gases specializes in the packaging and global distribution of select gases.

Plant Nutrition

Our Plant Nutrition business provides Metalosate® brand chelated minerals to the agricultural market worldwide, enabling growers to improve the yield and quality of their crops.  The amino acid chelate technology in our Metalosate products enables targeted delivery of micronutrient minerals to the crop using highly efficient foliar application spray methods.

Learn More

Performance Gases

Our Performance Gases business specializes in the packaging and global distribution of select gases, especially Ethylene Oxide for sterilization of medical devices and Propylene Oxide for spice and nutmeat fumigation and pasteurization and other applications. We have the packaging and distribution know-how to ensure the safe delivery of these and many other products in durable, reusable containers.
