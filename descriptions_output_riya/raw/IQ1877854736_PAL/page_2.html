Transport Services

Comprehensive Auto Transport Solutions

PAL specializes in new vehicle delivery to dealerships, but we’re built to handle much more. We serve OEMs, EV makers, dealers, auctions, rental fleets, and leasing providers with full-spectrum transport services. And with dedicated repair facilities operated by Proficient Repair Services, we keep our fleet running reliably—and support external fleets—to meet transport needs of any size or complexity.

VIN Search

new vehicle transport to dealerships

We specialize in secure, timely delivery of brand-new vehicles directly from manufacturers, rail terminals, or ports to dealerships across both the United States and Canada. Our team understands the high standards required for new inventory, striving to deliver every vehicle in pristine condition and on schedule.

Open Carrier Shipping for All Vehicle Types

The open carrier service is the most popular and cost-effective method for transporting standard vehicles across both short and long distances. With a fleet of modern, well-maintained trucks and highly trained drivers, Proficient Auto Logistics ensures that vehicles are delivered safely and on schedule.

Enclosed Transport for Luxury, Classic & Exotic Cars​

For vehicles requiring premium protection, the enclosed car shipping service offers the highest level of care. Ideal for luxury, vintage, and high-performance automobiles, enclosed trailers shield vehicles from weather conditions, road debris, and public exposure throughout the journey.

North American Coverage for Vehicle Transport

Proficient Auto Logistics provides efficient vehicle transport services for individuals, businesses, and fleet operators throughout the United States and Canada. With integrated route planning, real-time tracking, and dispatch, every delivery is executed with security, precision, and timeliness.

Fast, Professional Dealer Relocation & Lot Transport​

The dealer relocation service supports the seamless transfer of vehicles between showroom locations, satellite branches, auctions, and partner sites. Designed to minimize downtime and maintain availability, this service meets the precision and speed demanded by auto dealers.

Custom Solutions for Every Transport Need

Whether managing large-scale dealer relocations or specialized one-off shipments, Proficient Auto Logistics offers tailored transport solutions that align with clients’ exact requirements. Each move is handled with industry expertise and an unwavering focus on reliability and care.

Andrew Sutter

Vice President and General Counsel

Andrew Sutter is an experienced legal executive in the transportation and logistics industry. As Vice President and General Counsel, he oversees legal strategy and provides counsel across commercial, regulatory, employment, litigation, and risk matters in support of PAL’s operations and strategic objectives. Prior to joining PAL, he spent nearly 15 years at CSX Transportation, most recently serving as Assistant General Counsel, where he advised leaders across the company’s intermodal, automotive, and trucking operations on commercial agreements, regulatory compliance, strategic initiatives, and corporate matters. Earlier in his career, he practiced law at Holland & Knight LLP.
