Frequently Asked Questions

What are IoT managed services?

IoT managed services are outsourced services that help businesses design, deploy, monitor, and maintain their IoT solutions. They provide ongoing support, optimization, and management across the entire IoT lifecycle. 

Why do businesses need IoT managed services?

Managing IoT deployments at scale can be complex and resource-intensive. Managed services help reduce operational burden, improve performance, ensure compliance, and allow teams to focus on core business priorities. 

What does KORE’s IoT managed services offering include?

KORE’s managed services include: 

  • Consulting and solution design 

  • Device lifecycle management 

  • Connectivity management 

  • Engineering support 

  • Ongoing monitoring and optimization

What is IoT device lifecycle management?

Device lifecycle management involves managing devices from deployment through operation, updates, maintenance, and eventual replacement, ensuring long-term performance and reliability. 

How do managed services improve IoT performance?

Managed services proactively monitor systems, identify issues early, optimize connectivity, and ensure devices are properly maintained, resulting in better uptime and efficiency. 

How does KORE support global IoT deployments?

KORE provides expertise in global compliance, connectivity, and operations, helping businesses deploy and manage IoT solutions across multiple regions while meeting regulatory requirements. 

What is proactive IoT support?

Proactive support involves identifying and resolving potential issues before they impact operations, using monitoring, analytics, and predictive insights to prevent downtime. 

What kind of engineering support is available?

KORE offers 24/7 access to experienced network engineers who can assist with troubleshooting, performance optimization, and strategic planning for IoT deployments. 

How do managed services help reduce IoT costs?

Managed services reduce costs by improving efficiency, minimizing downtime, avoiding costly errors, and optimizing resource usage across devices and connectivity. 

Can managed services scale with my IoT deployment?

Yes, managed services are designed to scale alongside your deployment, supporting everything from initial pilots to large, global implementations. 

How does KORE help future-proof IoT solutions?

KORE supports technology evolution by helping businesses adopt new connectivity standards, migrate hardware, and test new solutions to stay ahead of industry changes. 

What is advanced connectivity management?

Advanced connectivity management includes managing carrier relationships, optimizing network performance, integrating eSIM solutions, and controlling device connectivity at scale. 

How does KORE ensure compliance in IoT deployments?

KORE helps businesses navigate global regulatory requirements, ensuring devices and connectivity solutions meet industry and regional standards. 

What industries benefit from IoT managed services?

Industries include: 

  • Healthcare 

  • Transportation and logistics 

  • Energy and utilities 

  • Retail 

  • Industrial IoT

What are the benefits of end-to-end IoT managed services?

End-to-end services provide: 

  • Simplified operations 

  • Improved performance and uptime

  • Faster deployment and scaling 

  • Reduced risk and complexity 

  • Better return on investment
