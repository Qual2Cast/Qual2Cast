What We Do

We build and service the infrastructure that enables our economy to run, our people to move and our country to grow.

As our country’s infrastructure needs are changing, so are we. Sterling is poised to meet the future head on with the innovative solutions, the new technology, and the people to make it work. Our expansion beyond heavy civil projects has broadened our focus and resulted in unprecedented growth and strong returns for investors.

Sterling operates through a variety of subsidiaries specializing in different segments, including E-infrastructure Solutions, Transportation Solutions, and Building Solutions. Our business segments are leaders in their fields, geographically positioned in the right markets executing and delivering solid returns through a customer-centric culture.

E-infrastructure Solutions

A leading provider of advanced, large-scale specialty site infrastructure improvement contracting services, including site selection and preparation.

Markets: Next-generation manufacturing, data centers, e-commerce distribution centers, commercial, warehousing, energy and more

As our fastest-growing and most profitable segment, our E-infrastructure Solutions plays a critical role in enabling Sterling to consistently surpass our strategic growth goals. We are one of the largest specialty site development companies serving large, blue-chip customers in the e-commerce, data center, distribution center, warehousing, and energy sectors. Whether it’s selecting a location to delivering a turnkey site ready for buildout, we have the solutions for the most demanding needs.

At Sterling, we:

  • Partner with clients from site selection through turnkey construction
  • Excel at the largest, most complex site development projects but are nimble enough to handle smaller ones
  • Have more horsepower and resources to move more dirt and rock
  • Offer client relationship managers that end-customers consistently request by name

Have an especially challenging site development project? Bring it on!

Transportation Solutions

Infrastructure and rehabilitation projects for highways, roads, bridges, airports, ports, light rail, water, wastewater and storm drainage systems

Sterling has long been known as a leader in heavy civil construction projects. Our stronger focus on alternative delivery and aviation has resulted in higher margins and better returns.

At Sterling, we:

  • Reduce risk by establishing the right contracts from the start
  • Understand complex scheduling, planning, and project phasing to avoid impacting critical operations
  • Build for durability, resiliency, and the best value to the customer.

Looking for innovative, on-time construction solutions? Look to Sterling!

Building Solutions

Residential and commercial concrete foundations for single-family and multi-family homes, parking  structures, elevated slabs, and other concrete work

Building Solutions is another one of our fast-growing and highly profitable sectors enabling our strategic growth. The nation’s top home builders’ confidence in Sterling has fueled our expansion into the top markets across the U.S.

At Sterling, we:

  • Work with the nation’s top builders in the most desirable markets
  • Alleviate the builder’s need to manage multiple subcontractors
  • Pour quality slabs faster than any other foundation company
  • Consistently deliver on-time, in-budget, high-quality results

Are you a homebuilder looking for the right partner? Partner with us!
