    About Us

    TXNM Energy, Inc.

    TXNM Energy is an energy holding company based in Albuquerque, N.M., with 2023 consolidated operating revenues of $1.9 billion. Through its regulated utilities, PNM and TNMP, TXNM Energy provides electricity to more than 800,000 homes and businesses in New Mexico and Texas. PNM serves its customers with a diverse mix of generation and purchased power resources totaling 3.1 gigawatts of capacity, with a goal to achieve 100% carbon-free energy by 2040. TXNM Energy is publicly traded on the New York Stock Exchange as TXNM (previously PNM).

    TXNM Energy strives to create enduring value for customers, communities and shareholders. At the core of our business, we are focused on our Vision, our Purpose and our Values.

    Our Vision

    Create a clean and bright energy future

    Our Purpose

    Work together with our customers and community to serve their energy needs

    Our Values

    • Safety for ourselves, our co-workers, our customers and our communities depends on every employee, every day
    • Caring about the welfare of others is a company tradition that fosters a positive workplace, a focus on customers and dedicated customer service
    • Integrity and honest communications guide our dealings and keep us accountable to our stakeholders and each other
