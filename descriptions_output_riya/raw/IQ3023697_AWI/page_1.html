Let the Buyer Have Faith

Our values first emerged in the last 19th century, when founder Thomas Armstrong adopted the motto: Let the buyer have faith." While our operating principles have remained steadfast, we have continued to innovate and evolve our business to meet the needs of the people we are here to serve - our customers, our employees, our communities and our shareholders.

Explore Our Values

A Culture of Leadership

Armstrong's founder, Thomas Armstrong, set the company on a customer-centric course with a personal guarantee of quality inscribed with his name in each bag of corks he sold.

Today, the leadership team at Armstrong continues to cultivate a culture of excellence and integrity while delivering results for our customers, employees, shareholders and communities.

Meet Our Leadership

165+ Years of Innovation

Armstrong World Industries started as the Armstrong Cork Company in 1860. The company has flourished since then, thriving in times of prosperity and enduring times of uncertainty. Today, we continue our founder's legacy of trust and quality, delivering on our promises to the highest degree of professionalism and service.
