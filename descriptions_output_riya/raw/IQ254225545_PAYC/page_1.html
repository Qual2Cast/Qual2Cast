What is Paycom?

Request Meeting

Paycom is a cloud-based human capital management (HCM) software provider that helps organizations manage payroll and HR in one place. Built on a single database, Paycom automates the employee life cycle — from hiring to payroll to time and labor — while reducing the manual work created by disconnected tools.

What does Paycom do?

Paycom provides an HCM software that connects payroll and HR processes through a single database. This design supports automation across core areas like talent acquisition, payroll, talent management, time and labor management and HR management.

Explore the platform

What that looks like in practice

Hiring

Candidate and new-hire information populates systemwide to reduce reentry.

Payroll

Payroll self-starts using live employee data, identifies errors and guides employees to fix them before submission.

Time and labor

Approved time flows into payroll automatically to reduce manual entry.

And many more

Who is Paycom for?

Paycom supports organizations across company sizes, industries and roles — including HR, finance, IT decision-makers, executives and operational leaders.

Paycom is fit for organizations that need:

  • payroll and HR data in one software to reduce manual work and improve accuracy
  • support for complexity such as multistate operations, growth and increasing compliance needs
See fit by size, role and industry

What makes Paycom different?

What makes Paycom different?

A single database built to support automation

Paycom’s single database is designed to reduce the work created by separate HR and payroll systems.

Why that matters:

  • Multiple systems require duplicate data entry, increasing the chance of errors.
  • Disconnected workflows can lead to rework after payroll runs when issues surface late.
  • More systems can increase training and support overhead through added handoffs and logins.

Built and improved as one platform

Paycom’s growth is built on a single database and ongoing enhancements rather than separate, stitched-together systems.
