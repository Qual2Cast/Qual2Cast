Our Mission

Thousands of Providers are Growing their Practices
with CareCloud

In today’s challenging healthcare landscape, healthcare organizations need an innovative partner who can help enhance clinical workflows, increase revenue, modernize the patient experience, and reduce operational expenses.  CareCloud (Nasdaq: CCLD, CCLDP, CCLDO), formerly known as MTBC, delivers the most comprehensive technology-enabled solutions to healthcare organizations of all sizes, spanning a wide range of specialties, all across the country.

Empowering the Future of Healthcare

Healthcare is evolving, and so are the demands on providers. CareCloud’s comprehensive cloud platform brings together Revenue Cycle Management, Practice Management, Electronic Health Records, Business Intelligence, Telehealth, and Patient Experience Management to help organizations simplify complexity, unlock growth, improve financial outcomes, and deliver outstanding patient care with confidence. 

Optimize Care Delivery

Optimize practice performance – financial, clinical, and patient – through integrated cloud-based solutions that delight patients and make care delivery more efficient.

Reduce Operational Expenses

Our flexible, consultative approach, combined with our end-to-end services and specialized back-office team, meet your needs head-on. Let us help your medical group improve profitability and eliminate administrative burdens.

Increase Collections

Increase collections by 7% or more and reduce staffing costs with our specialized back-office team handling critical parts of your revenue cycle – from claims submission to A/R followups.

Improve the Patient Experience

Patient registration and intake solutions seamlessly integrate with your Practice Management and Electronic Health Records, making every interaction between you and your patients easier than ever.

Grow Their Practice

We help practices run smoother and drive better financial results. The flexibility and power of CareCloud’s suite of technology-enabled solutions is designed to help you take your practice further.

Expand Patient Access to Care

CareCloud Live allows you to easily access patients across iOS, Android, or desktop. Telehealth also empowers patients who may face external hurdles to access healthcare, ultimately minimizing daily no-shows and last-minute cancellations.

We are truly energized by our ability to enable physicians to focus on care delivery during this pressing time and into the future, all while trusting us to support the day-to-day needs of their operations.

Hadi Chaudhry - CSO
