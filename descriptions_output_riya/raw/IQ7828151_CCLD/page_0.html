Clinical Decision Support

Let cirrusAI Guide lighten your load with automated data input and real-time, evidence-based recommendations via Vertex AI’s generative AI tools for providers to consider. It’s your go-to for better diagnoses and treatment plans.

AI-Powered Clinical Notes

Using Generative AI technology, cirrusAI Notes can capture natural patient-provider conversations, automatically generate clinical documentation, and save them directly into EHR. cirrusAI Notes saves 20% of your time per day, allowing you to refocus on what you do best, caring for your patients.

Virtual Support Assistant

Need help navigating your EHR? cirrusAI Chat facilitates natural language conversations with practice staff, offering valuable assistance. This tool streamlines post-training and onboarding, reducing response times and providing real-time assistance, ultimately saving time.

AI-Driven Appeals

Boost your reimbursement rates with cirrusAI Appeals. cirrusAI Appeals generates customized appeal letters by analyzing patient claim details, the appeal’s reason, and the specific payer involved for healthcare workers to review, edit, and send. We make sure you never leave money on the table.
