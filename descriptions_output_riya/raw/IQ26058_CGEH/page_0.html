Powering What Matters Most

We deliver low-emission, oil-free microturbine systems that run cleaner, last longer, and perform where others can’t. With unmatched reliability, fuel flexibility, and ultra-low maintenance, we power what matters most—anywhere in the world.

Flexible Solutions for Every Industry

From 65 kW to multi-MW systems, our scalable, ultra-reliable on-site, on-demand solutions power data centers, microgrids, ports, EV charging, oil & gas operations, and even festivals and events.

Our systems cut costs and emissions by capturing waste heat, run on fuels from natural gas to biogas, and provide ultra-reliable power anywhere, on-grid or off. Modular deployments can expand as your needs grow without increasing site footprint.

Our Edge: The Strength of Capstone Technology

Our cooling, heating and energy solutions deliver reliable, high-quality power when and where the grid can’t. All while reducing costs, lowering emissions, and operating on a wide range of fuels, from natural gas to renewable biogas and hydrogen blends.

Unlike reciprocating engines, our oil-free systems run quieter, cleaner, and with far less maintenance. And unlike solar, wind, or other weather-dependent solutions, our power is always on—on-grid, off-grid, or in hybrid microgrid applications.
