About

Deliver a Better World.

Meet the first global producer of 100% sustainable, paper-based packaging.

We create innovative solutions for protecting products in ways that benefit businesses, customers and the environment.

Making history since 1972

Our company was built on a revolutionary business model rooted in sustainability, innovation and service. We have been driven forward by a leadership team of packaging veterans and strong distributor relationships. You can discover the major milestones of our history when you read our 50th Anniversary eBook, available below:

Today, we are an internationally renowned company, with more than 800 employees around the globe. Our operations now span across North America, South America, Europe and Asia Pacific.
