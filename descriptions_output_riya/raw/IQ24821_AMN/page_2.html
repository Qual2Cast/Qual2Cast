Frequently Asked Questions

What types of healthcare professionals can AMN Healthcare staff?

AMN Healthcare provides the full spectrum of clinical and nonclinical professionals, including nursing, allied health, international, leadership, locums, and revenue cycle specialists, available for travel, temp-to-hire, per diem, and permanent placements.

What workforce solutions does AMN Healthcare offer healthcare organizations?

AMN Healthcare offers end-to-end staffing solutions, integrated workforce technology, and strategic consulting services, with options ranging from self-service to fully managed programs tailored for cost-effective, high-quality workforce outcomes.

How does AMN Healthcare's technology help reduce staffing costs?

AMN Healthcare's technology helps health systems lower staffing costs through smarter workforce planning, faster hiring, and better staffing utilization. By improving visibility and automating key processes, organizations can reduce labor expenses, fill roles more efficiently, and deliver more consistent patient care.

How does AMN Healthcare approach data security?

AMN Healthcare's technology is SOC 2 certified, undergoes Rapid7 Penetration Testing, and uses a Managed Detection and Response (MDR) platform, meeting the highest security and testing standards in the healthcare industry.

Does AMN Healthcare offer strategic consulting in addition to staffing?

Yes, AMN Healthcare's advisory services include labor model redesign, retention and engagement optimization, and predictive workforce planning, with experts working directly alongside organizational leadership to drive long-term success.

Which healthcare leaders does AMN Healthcare design its solutions for?

AMN Healthcare offers tailored workforce solutions for Chief Nursing Officers, Chief Financial Officers, and Chief Human Resources Officers, addressing challenges such as labor costs, workforce sustainability, and operational efficiency.
