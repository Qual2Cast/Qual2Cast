Berkley Industrial Comp

During hazardous winter conditions, a utility worker climbed an ice-covered pole to restore service. While repositioning, he removed his primary fall restraint, resulting in a 15-foot fall and severe spinal fractures that required complex surgery. Situations like this demand more than transactional claims handling. Berkley Industrial Comp applies a holistic, high-touch approach, combining medical strategy, behavioral health support, and coordinated claims leadership, to guide injured workers toward safe, sustainable recovery across high hazard industries.

Berkley Business Spotlight

Berkley Edge

Formed in 2025, Berkley Edge was established to deepen Berkley's commitment to the Excess & Surplus (E&S) market by providing professional liability and casualty insurance solutions for small to mid-sized businesses with particularly challenging risk profiles. Working exclusively through wholesale brokers, we specialize in hard-to-place and distressed risks, combining experienced underwriting expertise with advanced technology and data-driven insights to deliver responsive, targeted coverage and exceptional service. 

 

Berkley Edge offers a broad portfolio of professional liability and casualty products, including allied health, management liability, professional liability, excess casualty, general casualty, manufacturers’ and contractors’ coverage, and small business solutions. This specialized product suite enables us to provide creative, customized insurance solutions for complex risks that often fall outside the appetite of the standard market, helping wholesale brokers address challenging client needs with confidence. 

 

The Berkley Edge Advantage

  • Founded to serve the growing demand for professional liability and casualty solutions in the E&S market
  • Specializes in hard-to-place and distressed risks for small to mid-sized businesses
  • Works exclusively through wholesale brokers to provide creative solutions for complex exposures
  • Offers a broad range of professional liability and casualty products tailored to unique and emerging risks
  • Combines underwriting expertise, data-driven insights, and advanced technology to deliver responsive service and timely decisions
