Company / About

We Help Brands Succeed

DoubleVerify provides the industry’s leading Media Effectiveness Platform. Utilizing trusted measurement data and dynamic AI optimization, DV maximizes campaign effectiveness and drives tangible business outcomes for advertisers wherever they run their digital media.

We Build Trust and Transparency Across The Digital Ad Ecosystem

DV offers innovative, industry-leading solutions that empower all players in the ecosystem to navigate the complex digital landscape with confidence, ensuring trust and transparency across media campaigns.

Visit Our Trust Center

Innovation

We Offer Smart Solutions to Complex Challenges

DV has fueled digital media quality and performance for over 15 years — driving success for advertisers and supply partners. Our AI-powered solutions enable brands, marketplaces, commerce platforms and publishers to work smarter when addressing complex challenges like combating fraud, ensuring brand suitability and providing performance insights that drive real outcomes.

2025

DV Acquires Rockerbox, Launches DV MAP

DV acquires Rockerbox, adding outcome measurement and attribution capabilities to its suite of performance measurement and optimization solutions and establishing the DV Media AdVantage Platform for Advertisers.

2024

DV Launches News Accelerator

DV establishes the News Accelerator initiative to further align DV's product innovation with the needs of the news industry, and encourage advertiser spending on news and journalism.

2023

DV Acquires Scibids

DV acquires Scibids, extending its leadership in independent AI powered campaign optimization solutions.

2022

DV Expands Social Partnerships

DV expands its partnership with TikTok to provide post-campaign brand suitability measurement and announces media quality authentication in Netflix.

2021

DV Launches Brand Suitability Tiers

DV launches Brand Suitability Tiers, allowing clients to take advantage of 90+ unique brand suitability settings.

2020

DV Launches Authentic Attention & Custom Contextual

DV launches DV Authentic Attention® and DV Custom Contextual — a new paradigm for ensuring digital ad effectiveness in a cookieless world.

2019

DV Makes Key Acquisitions

DV acquires Zentrick, a digital video technology company and Ad-Juster, a leading data platform for digital publishers.

2018

DV Launches Authentic Brand Suitability

DV launches Authentic Brand Suitability — driving performance and efficiency by aligning pre- and post-bid brand suitability controls.

2017

DV Extends Coverage into Social

DV extends verification to YouTube and Facebook, and partners with Snap on brand suitability initiative — helping to ensure brands are covered across all buying platforms.
