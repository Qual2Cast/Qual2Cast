Our Mission

Our Promise

We are – Lifting Care. Everywhere.

In every interaction, whether in person or through the many ways we touch lives, we commit to trust, compassion, and excellence. It is how we will show up every day, everywhere.

We are lifting awareness – So no patient is misdiagnosed, and no provider is left in the dark.

We are lifting access – By breaking down cost barriers and complexity with innovations that simplify the system for everyone.

We are lifting product innovation– Expanding the reach and relevance of therapy through smart, proven, patient-first devices that are more connected, more portable, and more intuitive.

We are lifting the patient experience – Clearing the path, reducing the noise, and providing proactive support from diagnosis to daily life.

What We Do

At Tactile Medical, we specialize in developing at-home therapy devices to treat lymphedema and chronic venous insufficiency.

Our approach to supporting patients living with chronic swelling combines:

  • Innovative devices: Advanced pneumatic compression devices backed by clinical research offer targeted relief from swelling and related symptoms.
  • Personalized care: A nationwide team of Territory Account Managers and Patient Education Consultants who ensure patients receive personalized setup, training, and follow-up.
  • Comprehensive support: Reimbursement experts, patient advocates, and clinical staff streamline access and offer guidance on navigating insurance claims, condition-specific education, and more.

Our combination of products and services ensures that tens of thousands of patients annually receive the at-home treatment necessary to better manage their chronic conditions. Not only are patients able to improve their quality of life, but our solutions help increase clinical efficacy and reduce overall healthcare costs. Discover our lymphedema solutions to learn how pneumatic compression devices like the Nimbl™ or Flexitouch® Plus system can help you.
