Financial Risk Business Consulting Solutions

Adapt for Today and Prepare for Tomorrow

Whether you’re looking for deep insights or help quantifying future financial risks, our consulting team can provide you with specialized knowledge and guidance to reveal important findings and turn them into new opportunities. Our team of highly skilled consultants, actuaries, data scientists, pharmacists, accountants, and other specialists work closely with you to help you identify and understand the critical elements that matter to your business.

Objective Insight You Can Rely On

Brown & Brown transforms the way companies understand and manage risk, administer benefits, and achieve success. Our breadth of consulting services for payers, providers, and plan sponsors is broad and multi-disciplinary. This includes work for large, sophisticated businesses in the following areas:

  • Actuarial Services: Our breadth of actuarial services includes captive and self-funded programs, employee benefits, property and casualty, reinsurance, and stop-loss.

  • Consulting Services: We help make a difference for our customers through objectivity, high-touch consulting, and state-of-the-art analytics in areas such as health and welfare, mergers & acquisitions, pharmacy, and retirement benefits.

  • Healthcare Claim Audit Services: Our team offers seasoned professionals skilled in health claim adjudication and cost management. Services are provided in areas such as employer benefit and claims audits, stop-loss services, and reviews of plan documents, policies, and claims.

Our Consulting Approach

Brown & Brown Consulting focuses on a "customer first" approach. Our model consists of:

  • Focusing on building long-term satisfaction by keeping everything in-house rather than outsourcing
  • Recognizing that all work produced is the property of the customer and may be shared
  • Developing practical solutions based on the specific needs and requirements of the individual customer
  • Utilizing a centralized technical services unit for all work product to ensure proper quality control
