Coating Solutions

Where innovation meets endless possibilities

For more than six decades, AZZ Precoat Metals has been at the forefront of advanced metal coating and treatment technologies, helping customers develop products that combine durability, performance, efficiency, and visual appeal. From high-performance systems to emerging specialty finishes, AZZ coil coating solutions are engineered to support evolving product requirements across a wide range of industries and applications.

Flexible sourcing paired with deep technical expertise

Deep technical expertise and an extensive understanding of coatings, substrates, treatments, laminates, and films allow customers to identify the right solution for their specific operational, environmental, and aesthetic requirements. Whether the goal is improved corrosion resistance, enhanced weatherability, energy efficiency, durability, or product differentiation, coating systems are selected and optimized to support long-term product performance and manufacturing success.

As North America’s leading independent provider of coil coating services, AZZ Precoat Metals delivers the flexibility to evaluate and source from a broad range of suppliers and technologies rather than being limited to a single coating system or substrate provider. This independent approach allows greater flexibility in identifying the best-performing solution for each application.

Performance-driven coating technologies

Coating systems are designed to support a wide range of manufacturing and performance requirements across construction, HVAC, appliances, transportation, containers and packaging, doors and windows, and consumer products.

Coil coatings are engineered to enhance:

  • Corrosion resistance
  • Weather and UV resistance
  • Abrasion resistance
  • Long-term color retention
  • Product durability
  • Energy efficiency
  • Surface appearance

By combining advanced coating technologies with tightly controlled coil coating processes, customers benefit from consistent finishes, improved product reliability, and lasting field performance.

Finishes that enhance product appearance and market appeal

Beyond corrosion protection and durability, coil coating solutions can create distinctive visual and tactile characteristics that enhance product appearance and support brand differentiation. From custom colors and decorative finishes to printed patterns, embossed textures, and natural-look effects, AZZ helps customers achieve the aesthetic performance their markets demand while maintaining the durability and long-term performance of coil-coated metal systems.

PCM Xtremes

Defying harsh conditions, ensuring durability

When demanding conditions are the norm, our PCM Xtremes™ formulations excel against exposure to UV rays, moisture, extreme temperatures, corrosive environments, and aggressive substances/contaminants. With unmatched durability and resilience, these coatings ensure your products withstand the harshest environments while maintaining their pristine appearance.

View Xtremes Coatings

Strategic collaboration with vendors and research facilities

Strong partnerships with global coating suppliers, treatment providers, and research facilities allow AZZ Precoat Metals to continuously evaluate emerging technologies and innovative material solutions.

As an independent coater, AZZ Precoat Metals can assess a broad range of substrates, coatings, films, laminates, and treatments to identify the best possible solution for each application. Dedicated internal resources focused on assessment, evaluation, testing, and recommendation help ensure coating systems align with both manufacturing requirements and long-term performance objectives.

This collaborative approach helps customers gain access to innovative technologies, advanced material systems, and coating solutions tailored to evolving market demands.

Innovation built around customer needs

Not every project fits within standard coating specifications. When existing solutions do not fully meet application requirements, the AZZ Precoat Metals Innovation Team works closely with customers and suppliers to develop new approaches engineered around specific manufacturing, environmental, or aesthetic goals.

From specialty finishes and advanced protective coating systems to emerging sustainable technologies, innovation efforts focus on helping customers improve product performance, operational efficiency, and long-term value.

Combined with responsive support, integrated processing capabilities, and one of North America’s largest independent coil coating networks, AZZ delivers industrial coating solutions designed around real-world manufacturing demands and evolving customer expectations.
