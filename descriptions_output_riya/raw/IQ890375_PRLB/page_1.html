About Us

Advancing Global Innovation Through Digital Manufacturing

 

Our Story

Hello! Welcome to the digital manufacturing revolution. Protolabs is the world’s fastest manufacturing service enabling companies across every industry to streamline production of quality parts throughout the entire product life cycle.

Founded in 1999, we radically reduced the time needed to produce molded prototypes through complex software that automated the manufacturing process. Over the next two decades, we added CNC machining, 3D printing, and sheet metal fabrication, and later launched Protolabs Network, our global network of manufacturing partners. Our 25+ years of growth and expanding capabilities provide customers with the best digital manufacturing experience in the industry.

Our Mission

We shape the future by bringing customer ideas to life across every stage of the product life cycle.
