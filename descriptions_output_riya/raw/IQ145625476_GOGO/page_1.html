About Gogo

Gogo has been the trusted connectivity infrastructure partner for business aviation and military/government for three decades. Rather than promoting a single network or technology, Gogo has a network-neutral approach, helping customers navigate an increasingly complex connectivity landscape by combining deep market knowledge and global aviation expertise to identify, integrate and support the connectivity solution best suited to their aircraft, mission and operational requirements.

Providing access to a complete connectivity ecosystem, all through a single trusted provider, Gogo offers onboard hardware, software, air-to-ground and multi-orbit, multi-band satellite connectivity, service plans,and 24/7/365 multilingual customer support. Gogo is dedicated to customer success through agnostic advice, long-term partnerships, enterprise-grade cybersecurity, data privacy, flexible financing, predictable long-term pricing, and future-proofed technology.

From light business aircraft to executive airliners and specialized government platforms, Gogo delivers secure, reliable connectivity that enables customers to fly, work and connect with confidence.

The Power of Collaboration

In Dec 2024, Gogo and Satcom Direct joined forces to deliver powerful best-in-class, aviation-grade solutions and customer support that cater to the evolving demands of the aviation industry. Together, we are setting new standards for inflight connectivity and operational efficiency.

Key Benefits of the Partnership

  1. World Class Personal Customer Support:
    Joining together two award winning aviation customer support organizations with the ability to leverage the most advanced monitoring and visibility tools, infrastructure and personnel.
  2. Comprehensive Solutions:
    From tip to tail, no matter what or where you fly Gogo and SD have a solution to meet the needs of every mission, budget and business aircraft.
  3. Unmatched Security:
    The combined strengths of Gogo and Satcom Direct in advanced cybersecurity solutions safeguard data and communications against modern threats, ensuring peace of mind for all onboard.
  1. Seamless Global Coverage:
    High-performance aviation-grade global solutions. Whether flying domestically or internationally, the combination of SD and Gogo’s technologies ensures consistent connectivity anywhere on the planet.
  2. Streamlined Operations:
    The partnership supports operational efficiencies through enhanced data management and reporting tools, ensuring informed decision-making at every stage of a flight.
  3. Aviation's Future is Connected
    As the demands on business aviation evolve, the partnership between Gogo Business Aviation and Satcom Direct ensures that operators remain at the forefront of technological innovation. Together, these brands are not just meeting the expectations of today’s connected world—they are exceeding them, paving the way for the future of inflight communication, entertainment and productivity.
