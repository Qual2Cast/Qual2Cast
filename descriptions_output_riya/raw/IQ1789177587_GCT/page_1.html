ABOUT GIGACLOUD
GIGACLOUD TECHNOLOGY IS AN ONLINE GLOBAL B2B SOLUTIONS PROVIDER THAT IS REINVENTING THE SUPPLY CHAIN FOR WHOLESALE BUYERS AND SELLERS OF LARGE PARCEL MERCHANDISE
OUR MISSION
GigaCloud Technology is an online global B2B solutions provider that is reinventing the supply chain for wholesale buyers and sellers of large-parcel merchandise.
Our GigaCloud B2B Marketplace seamlessly connects suppliers and resellers across the globe in real time.
OUR GIGACLOUD MARKETPLACE
The GigaCloud B2B Marketplace is transforming the wholesale supply chain for big and bulky merchandise, seamlessly connecting suppliers and resellers worldwide. Our end-to-end platform integrates product discovery, transactions, and fulfillment, backed by a global logistics network across the U.S., U.K., Germany, Canada, and Japan. With thousands of SKUs and proprietary technology for real-time market insights, our Marketplace simplifies sourcing, selling, and shipping—creating a more efficient, connected global supply chain.
