Semiconductors

Built on decades of materials science and process know-how, Veeco is uniquely positioned to deliver smart solutions that remove barriers to nanoscale devices and advanced packaging production. Veeco provides advanced capital equipment that addresses front-end processes and advanced packaging needs, tackling challenges like 3D device architectures, EUV photomask production, and material integration for high-performance, low-power devices. Through collaboration and innovation, Veeco enables progress across the entire semiconductor supply chain

READ MORE

Compound Semiconductors

The compound semiconductor market is one of Veeco’s driving forces, fueled mainly by 5G/6G, display, power electronics, and photonics end-markets. Veeco delivers advanced process equipment such as MOCVD and MBE systems, which are vital for producing high-performance devices used in applications such as datacom and telecom, electric vehicles, and AR displays. Our technologies enable both R&D and high-volume manufacturing, helping customers meet demands for innovation and scalability.

READ MORE

Data Storage

The promise of generative AI requires fundamental changes in our data storage infrastructure. For decades, Veeco has been a key partner for technology leaders in advancing hard drive innovation while driving down costs through ion-beam etch and deposition processes that push the capabilities of thin-film magnetic heads for high areal density hard disk drives.

Laser Processing Systems

Veeco has developed revolutionary technology to enable thermal annealing solutions at the most advanced technology nodes. Veeco delivers transformative low-cost-of-ownership, HVM production-proven laser spike annealing solutions to support the most advanced technology nodes.

Lithography Systems

Veeco is the market leader in advanced packaging lithography systems designed to address the increasing challenges of smaller line and space patterning. Veeco lithography systems are Making a Material Difference with their industry-leading optical focus capability, proven high-volume production, and warped wafer handling for advanced packaging, LED, MEMS, and power device applications.

Ion Beam System & Sources

Veeco's unmatched ion beam know-how delivers proven etch and deposition performance that has enabled the data storage and MEMS markets for decades. Our advanced ion beam sputtering, deposition, and etch systems achieve maximum uniformity and repeatability to meet the requirements of microelectronic devices.

SiC CVD System

Lower your cost of ownership with Veeco’s new EpiStrideTM system for silicon carbide epitaxy. The EpiStrideTM system enables high-performance chemical vapor deposition of silicon carbide for both 6 and 8-inch wafer production, leading to the lowest cost of ownership per wafer and making it ideally suited for silicon carbide power electronic devices used in electric vehicles, renewable energy, and industrial applications.

MOCVD Systems

Veeco offers a range of industry-leading GaN and As/P MOCVD systems designed to maximize throughput while lowering cost of ownership for a variety of applications including display, 3D sensing, LiDAR, micro-LED display, and optical data communications. Designed to advance next-generation devices, Veeco allows users to customize their systems for superior uniformity and repeatability with low defectivity to maximize value.

READ MORE

Advanced Wet Processing

Surface preparation using sustainable practices is critical to ensuring the reliability of advanced microelectronics destined for AI and high performance computing, automotive applications, medical devices, 5G/6G and cloud storage. Veeco’s WaferStorm and WaferEtch platforms are the industry choice for several critical solvent-based processes as they provide high uniformity combined with low defectivity and flexible design to meet a wide range of process requirements.

MBE Technologies

When you’re creating thin films of unique compound semiconductor materials one crystal at a time, you need a molecular beam epitaxy (MBE) system that is highly configurable. Veeco provides the industry’s broadest line-up of innovative and reliable MBE systems.

Physical Vapor Deposition Systems
