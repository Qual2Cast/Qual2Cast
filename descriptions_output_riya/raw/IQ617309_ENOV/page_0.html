Press & Events

Enovis Expands Spinalogic® Bone Growth Stimulator Indication to Include Cervical Fusion

September 30, 2026
Dallas, TX — September 30, 2026 — Enovis™ Corporation (NYSE: ENOV) announced that Spinalogic has received U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) clearance for an expanded indication that includes adjunct use following primary anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) surgery of two motion segments, or three vertebrae, from C4–C7 in skeletally mature patients.

Compex and Kilian Jornet Reunite to Drive Performance, Recovery and Innovation

September 28, 2026
September 28, 2026 -- Compex, a global leader in electrical muscle stimulation (EMS) technology and part of Enovis™, a global medical technology innovator, today announced the renewal of its longstanding partnership with Kilian Jornet, one of the world’s most accomplished endurance athletes, supporting the launch of the new Compex Ultimate EMS range.
