Home-based Care Expertise and Capabilities

As a home-based care company, Accendra Health is uniquely positioned to meet the needs of the growing healthcare market.

Together, our trusted and leading brands, Apria and Byram Healthcare, bring nearly 90 years of combined experience in promoting health beyond the hospital in communities across the country.

Care You Can Trust. Expertise You Can Count On.

Part of Accendra Health, the Apria and Byram Healthcare product brands offer home healthcare equipment and related services to help improve health outcomes and the affordability of care in the home.

Apria: Founded in 1995, Apria specializes in delivering durable medical equipment (DME) supplies and in-home clinical support for conditions such as sleep apnea, comprehensive respiratory care, and negative pressure wound therapy.

Learn more about Apria’s fully integrated programs at Apria.com.

Byram Healthcare: Established in 1968, Byram specializes in providing DME supplies and education for chronic conditions such as diabetes, urology, ostomy, wound care, incontinence, and breast pumps.

Learn more about Byram Healthcare’s wide selection of products at ByramHealthcare.com.
