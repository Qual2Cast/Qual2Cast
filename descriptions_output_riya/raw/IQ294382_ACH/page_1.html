Accendra Health

Healthcare is complicated. Accendra Health makes it a little easier by Bringing Care To Life™.

At Accendra Health (formerly known as Owens & Minor, Inc.), care is a promise. A promise that gives healthcare professionals peace of mind that their patients can get the care they need. A promise offering confidence to people that they can manage their health and live life on their own terms. A commitment to connecting patients, providers, and insurers so healthcare works better for everyone.

With deep expertise promoting health outside the hospital and a presence in communities nationwide through our Apria and Byram Healthcare brands, Accendra Health does more than just deliver the essentials. We overcome obstacles. We educate and provide answers. We make it possible for patients and families to access healthcare when and where they need it, helping to improve outcomes for millions of people each year who receive care at home.

Healthcare is complicated, but Accendra Health is here to help make it a little easier. We focus on the details of bringing care into the home, empowering patients with critical products, services, and support so they can focus on the moments that matter most.

Our Purpose

At Accendra Health, our Purpose drives us. Bringing Care To Life™ means meeting people where they are and delivering care that fits their lives beyond traditional settings.

Our Mission

Our Mission is to provide innovative solutions that ensure patients get the care they need in the comfort of their homes.

Our Vision

Our Vision is to be a leading partner in care for medical equipment, supplies, and solutions in the home for individuals, families, and caregivers.

Our IDEAL Values

Our IDEAL Values define our standards and are embedded in everything we do, from our internal operations to patient engagement.
