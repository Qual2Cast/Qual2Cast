Our people

A growing team with deep roots

At Green Thumb, we believe in creating a more inclusive cannabis culture, shaping the industry in a way that makes all of us proud and normalizing well-being through the power of the plant. As we continue to grow, our shared values keep us grounded—we are humble, hardworking, grateful people who value transparency and integrity.

Making an impact

Growing for Good

As a team, we strive to create lasting, positive change in the communities we serve. Our Growing for Good social impact program is how we put our beliefs into practice and focus on four key pillars of progress: Restorative Justice, Inclusion & Belonging, Environmental Stewardship and Community Engagement.
