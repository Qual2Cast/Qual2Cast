Meet the Executive team

Leonard Livschitz

CEO and Director

Leonard Livschitz has been the Chief Executive Officer of Grid Dynamics, a NASDAQ-listed company, since 2014 and a member of its Board of Directors since 2006. A seasoned leader with a wealth of entrepreneurial and executive experience, Leonard has successfully navigated roles in early-stage start-ups as well as multinational Fortune 100 corporations. Before becoming CEO of Grid Dynamics, Leonard co-founded Luxera, an LED smart system company, where he served as President and CEO from 2010 to 2014. Leonard draws on over 30 years of experience in the high-tech industry, holding executive and management roles across sales, engineering, R&D, and manufacturing at companies including Ford Motor Company, HP, and Philips. With a strong interdisciplinary background in business and engineering, Leonard has deep expertise in technologies, applications, and trends in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Cloud Computing, Digital Commerce, Solid-State Lighting, Smart Home solutions, and the Internet of Things (IoT). He also possesses extensive expertise in the Semiconductor and Automotive industries. Leonard holds a Master of Science in Electrical Engineering from Case Western Reserve University, Cleveland, Ohio, and a Master of Science in Mechanical Engineering from Kharkiv Institute of Technology, Ukraine.

Anil Doradla

CFO

Anil Doradla joined Grid Dynamics in November 2019, bringing over 20 years of diverse experience across the finance industry, Wall Street, and the technology world. He most recently served as Chief Financial Officer of Airgain, Inc. (NASDAQ: AIRG), a provider of advanced antenna technologies that enable high-performance wireless networking. Prior to Airgain, Anil Doradla worked on Wall Street as an equity research analyst at William Blair, covering a broad range of the technology sector that included ITO and BPO Services, Semiconductors, Technical Software, and Wireless Communications. In that role, Mr. Doradla performed in-depth analyses of public companies across a range of market capitalizations that included several IPOs. Before William Blair, Mr. Doradla held a variety of senior finance, strategy and technology roles with Caris and Company, Deutsche Bank AG, AT&T Labs, and LCC International.

Yury Gryzlov

COO and Head of Europe

Yury Gryzlov joined Grid Dynamics in 2007 as the company’s first QA Manager, and quickly established himself as a talented and versatile operations manager. As the company grew, Mr. Gryzlov took on ever-increasing responsibilities, leading to his current role as Chief Operating Officer, where he is in charge of all facets of the company’s operations, including budgeting, legal, HR, IT, office management, pricing, and recruiting. Previously, Mr. Gryzlov served as VP of Operations in Europe, and was responsible for all aspects of our people strategy, including: hiring, developing, and retaining Grid Dynamics personnel offshore. Prior to that, Mr. Gryzlov was the Deputy Director of the Saratov Engineering center, where he managed all of the daily operations. He holds a Masters of Science degree in Biophysics from Saratov State University, Russia.

Rajeev Sharma

Managing Partner of APAC

With over three decades of diverse expertise spanning engineering, military, and entrepreneurship, Rajeev Sharma is a trailblazing technology and business expert with a distinctive approach to leadership. His journey from combat in the Indian army to rocket propulsion engineering, and ultimately to corporate leadership, reflects a commitment to continuous learning, resilience, and traditional values. As the Managing Partner of APAC at Grid Dynamics, Rajeev fosters a culture of data-driven decision-making and engineering rigor, navigating dynamic technology trends with a dual approach of R&D innovation and customer-first services.
Rajeev has Master’s degrees in Management & Systems Design from MIT Sloan, and in Space Engineering & Rocketry (BIT MESRA) from Birla Institute of Technology. He has held leadership roles at prominent organizations like Polaris Software, Dynalead Inc., Ness Digital Engineering, and the Cognitive Engineering Group at Pactera Edge. His accolades include the Scientist of the Year award for the design and development of Agni-III Solid Rocket Propulsion systems, and he is a published author, with a book titled “Intuitively Rational”.

Vasily Sizov

CRO and Head of Americas

As Chief Revenue Officer (CRO) of Grid Dynamics, Vasily Sizov leads the company’s global revenue strategy, go-to-market execution, and revenue operations. He also serves as Head of Americas, with full P&L responsibility for the region. Since joining Grid Dynamics in 2016, Mr. Sizov has built several enterprise accounts from zero into the company’s largest revenue contributors. Across that time, he has played a central role in shaping the company’s sales, account management, and business operations functions. Before Grid Dynamics, Mr. Sizov led international business development as Head of Enterprise and Industrial Software at the Skolkovo Foundation. Earlier in his career, he held CIO and executive roles across software engineering and manufacturing companies. Mr. Sizov holds a Master’s in Applied Mathematics from Cherepovets State University, an MBA in Strategic Management from World Technological University UNESCO, and a CIMA Diploma in Business Performance Management (UK).

Dr. Eugene Steinberg

CTO

Dr. Eugene Steinberg serves as Chief Technology Officer (CTO) of Grid Dynamics. A founding engineer and most recently, Distinguished Technical Fellow, Dr. Steinberg brings over two decades of expertise in artificial intelligence, machine learning, information retrieval, and scalable distributed systems to his leadership role.
At Grid Dynamics, he has been instrumental in conceiving and scaling the company’s technology practices, spearheading mission-critical transformation programs for Fortune 1000 clients while pioneering breakthrough solutions in AI, search, and cloud-native platform development. His vision and hands-on leadership have established Grid Dynamics as a recognized innovator in digital transformation services.
Dr. Steinberg oversees the company’s technology strategy, practice development, and innovation initiatives. His expertise spans enterprise AI applications, cloud platforms, data engineering, and modern application development—core capabilities that drive the delivery of high-impact solutions for global enterprises.
Prior to becoming CTO, he built the company’s search and digital commerce practices from the ground up and pioneered many retail AI solutions, including visual search and conversational commerce. He joined Grid Dynamics in 2006 as its first employee and founding engineer, establishing core technology foundations while delivering transformative projects for clients including PayPal, eBay, Google, Macy’s, Home Depot, and Nike.
Dr. Steinberg holds a Ph.D. in Mathematics and Mechanics from Saratov State University, where he focused on numerical analysis of nonlinear dynamic systems.

Rahul Bindlish

SVP, Global Head of Partnerships and Marketing

Rahul Bindlish has 30+ years of experience in IT services and consulting. He specializes in helping Tier-1 enterprises with their AI & digital initiatives to improve business performance and drive industry leading growth. Throughout his career, Mr. Bindlish has been recognized as an innovative leader who excels in building high-performing teams and fostering strong customer relationships. He is currently the Senior Vice President & Global Head of Partnerships and Marketing. Mr. Bindlish holds a Master’s degree in Business and Management from the Asian Institute of Management.
