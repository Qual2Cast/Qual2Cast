BUILT WITH YOU IN MIND
FEATURES & INNOVATIONS

There’s a lot you can measure on a MasterCraft. The horsepower. The ballast. The wave. The technology. You can count the 78 patents and more than 49 awards earned across innovation, customer service, and manufacturing. But the real measure of a boat is what it makes possible. For 2027, MasterCraft continues pushing the experience forward with smarter tech, refined performance, and thoughtful improvements across the lineup. Together, they make the boat more intuitive to use and your time on the water more enjoyable. 

 

The specs tell you what it can do. The memories show you why it matters.

LEGACY. POWER. PRECISION. PROGRESSION.
WHY MASTERCRAFT

For nearly 60 years, MasterCraft has built boats around a simple belief: performance should open the door to what comes next. It’s why every MasterCraft is engineered with purpose, from the wave and power to the technology, comfort, and details you notice every time you’re onboard. Because performance isn’t just a number on a spec sheet. It’s the confidence to push further, the freedom to change course, and the opportunity to turn an ordinary day into something worth remembering. 

 

MasterCraft. Performance creates possibility.
