Disciplined Investors, Driven by Growth

Performance powered by a clear strategy, strong partners and healthcare assets that deliver essential care.

At National Healthcare Properties, our success is fueled by our hands-on approach to asset management and the symbiotic relationship between complementary asset classes. High-quality outpatient medical facilities with creditworthy healthcare provider tenants generate consistent performance, while strong partnerships with top industry operators and our high-quality needs-based assets propel our growth in the thriving and supply-constrained senior housing market. By leveraging the unique advantages of each asset class, we are well-positioned to capitalize on the aging of America and deliver long-term value to our shareholders.
