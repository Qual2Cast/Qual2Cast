FAQ

Is Dave a bank?

Dave is a financial technology company, not a bank. We partner with Coastal Community Bank, Member FDIC, or another partner bank, to provide accounts and services. These are FDIC-insured.

How do I use Dave?

1. Download the Dave app on iOS or Android

2. Open the app and tap Join Dave

3. Answer a few quick questions about yourself and your bank

4. Once set up, you’ll get access to ExtraCash and other Dave features. Getting started takes just a few minutes.

Is Dave right for me?

Dave is built for the millions of Americans who need a little help covering expenses between paychecks without the hassle of credit checks or high-interest loans. Rent, bills, gas, inflation—there are countless reasons why you might need access to interest-free money. Dave is your judgement-free solution.

Does Dave require a credit check?

Dave does not require a credit check at any point. We look at the bigger-picture beyond your credit score or history, to provide money solutions that work for you.

What bank does Dave partner with?

Dave proudly partners with Coastal Community Bank, an FDIC-insured banking service. Learn more about Coastal Community Bank here.

Are Dave accounts safe?

We’re serious about your security and go beyond standard practices to keep you and your data safe. 

Dave Checking accounts are backed and FDIC-insured up to $250K through Coastal Community Bank, Member FIDC, which means your account is insured up to $250,000 by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation in case of bank failure. Conditions apply³.

We offer 24/7 monitoring to protect accounts against unusual activity and logins from suspicious devices. Your account includes enhanced fraud monitoring systems that catch suspicious and fraudulent activity to keep your account safe.

Can I use Dave without downloading the app?

Dave’s products and services are best used through the mobile app. Managing your account, accessing ExtraCash, and contacting support all require the app.
