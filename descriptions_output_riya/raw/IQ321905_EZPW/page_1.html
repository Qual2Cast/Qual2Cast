Who we are

Formed with 16 pawn stores in 1989, EZCORP has grown into a leading provider of pawn loans in the United States and Latin America. At our pawn stores, we also sell merchandise, primarily collateral forfeited from pawn lending operations and used merchandise purchased from customers. We are dedicated to satisfying the short- term cash needs of consumers who are both cash and credit constrained and providing an industry-leading customer experience.

We provide numerous convenient options to obtain short-term cash, including non-recourse pawn loans collateralized by personal property. We also sell pre-owned merchandise to consumers seeking good value. Headquartered in Austin, Texas, EZCORP is traded on NASDAQ under the ticker symbol EZPW.

Our Customers

At EZCORP, we are dedicated to providing our customers with a variety of short-term cash solutions to make ends meet. Our goal is to help everyone get the funds they need quickly, conveniently, and professionally in order to satisfy their personal financial expenses. EZCORP will continue to expand geographically to help serve additional customers in new markets, and introduce new products in order to better serve each individual customer and their unique needs.
