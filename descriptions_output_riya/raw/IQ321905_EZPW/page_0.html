Sustainability

Reuse & Recycle

Since the day we opened our doors, our goal has been the same: to make our customers' lives easier. Our mission is to put the "reuse" in Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, and do good for the environment by extending the useful life of millions of items and strongly contributing to the circular economy.

In 2022 alone, EZCORP recycled more than 5.6 million pre-loved items across our family of brands. If everyone bought one pre-loved item instead of a new one, we would save our planet from billions of pounds of waste.
