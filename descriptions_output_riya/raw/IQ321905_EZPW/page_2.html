What We Do

Buy

EZCORP provides customers the opportunity to convert merchandise they no longer need into instant cash. We buy quality used items ranging from gold and diamond jewelry to electronics, video games, musical instruments, tools, sporting goods, and more. We pride ourselves on fair and accurate appraisals. Quite simply, we help our customers get more money for their things.

Sell

In today’s economy, a growing number of consumers demand value, and EZCORP delivers. We sell an array of quality used items at budget-friendly prices. In fact, our customers save up to 70% off regular retail prices every day. Our selection of merchandise is wide and ever-changing and includes gold, jewelry, electronics, tools, sporting goods, luxury handbags, shoes & sneakers, musical equipment and much more!

Loan

Customers rely on EZCORP for loans that are fast, convenient, and confidential. We go above and beyond to encourage responsibility and are guided by the principles of honesty, integrity, and fairness. We provide instant cash loans on items of value, including gold and diamond jewelry, electronics, computers, musical instruments, tools, video games, firearms (US only) and more. EZCORP works closely with every customer to help them get the money they need.
