ACM Research

ACMR’s Advanced Production Tools for Leading Edge IC Fabs

ACMR’s multi-product portfolio of tools supports IC, compound semiconductors, wafer-level packaging, and wafer manufacturing applications.

Our wide range of advanced product offerings support multiple semiconductor processing steps including single-wafer and batch wet cleaning, electroplating, thermal deposition, stress-free polishing, PECVD, ALD, and track equipment. ACMR delivers customized, high-performance, sustainable tool and process technology solutions that improve productivity, efficiency,  and yield for high-volume manufacturing at low cost of ownership.

Our proven track record of collaborating with our industry leader customers gives us a technical edge to meet their wide range of processing requirements on advanced technology nodes for up to 12” wafer size. Backed by our IP portfolio of more than 448 internationally granted patents, ACMR is committed to providing customers with innovative technology, world-class products, and strong service and support around the globe.

ACM Research, Inc. was founded in California in 1998 to supply capital equipment to the global semiconductor industry. We provide product development, manufacturing, and product support through our subsidiaries in Shanghai, China and Inchon, South Korea.  Our global sales and service organization supports customers throughout Asia, North America, and Europe.
