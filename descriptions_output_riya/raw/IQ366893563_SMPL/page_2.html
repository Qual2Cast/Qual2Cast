Our Brands

 

Atkins® 

Grounded by science and more than 100 clinical studies, the Atkins low carb lifestyle has helped millions of people achieve their personal weight management and health goals. Our portfolio of products includes nutritional bars, ready-to-drink shakes, confectionary treats and frozen meals to support a low carb/low sugar lifestyle, with 100% free access to all tools including the website, 1600+ recipes, meal planner, mobile app, community forums, and more. 

 

 

Quest® 

Making the foods you crave work for you, and not against you, Quest offers a diverse array of nutritional bars, ready-to-drink shakes, cookies, chips and pizza that are all rooted in the core principles of great taste, while minimizing net carbs and sugar. Utilizing complete protein and innovative ingredients with metabolic advantages, Quest products represent freedom to enjoy foods you crave, whenever those cravings hit. 

 

 

OWYN™ 

Uniting truth, transparency, and great taste, OWYN is a plant-based nutrition company that is true to its name, Only What You Need. Our product portfolio includes protein shakes and protein powders that are high in protein, low in sugar, and made with simple ingredients. Our name is rooted in our commitment to clean labels and an allergen-friendly promise, ensuring that all products are top-9 allergen friendly and third-party tested, allowing you to sip confidently without compromising on taste.
