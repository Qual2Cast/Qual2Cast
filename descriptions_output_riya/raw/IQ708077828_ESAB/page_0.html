Our businesses.

ESAB Corporation’s three business platforms provide industry-leading solutions in connected fabrication technology, gas control and gas distribution, and advanced inspection and monitoring technologies for critical assets. Together, they help customers improve productivity, enhance quality, and protect critical assets through innovation and continuous improvement.
