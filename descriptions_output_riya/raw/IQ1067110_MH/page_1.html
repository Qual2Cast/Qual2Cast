Company History

At McGraw Hill, we are driven by our passion to create the highest quality, most accessible solutions for learners and educators around the world, from PreK through higher education, professional learning and beyond. 

2026

January McGraw Hill expands literacy franchise with launch of Summit! for grades 6-8 and Soar! for grades 9-12, offering a digital experience powered by data-driven insights to personalize learning.

January Philip Moyer named McGraw Hill’s new President and Chief Executive Officer, effective February 9, 2026. 

2025

July McGraw Hill became a public company on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol “MH."

March McGraw Hill acquires Essaypop, an interactive, research-based writing solution for K-12. 

September McGraw Hill announces Sharpen Advantage, a new AI-powered enterprise offering that expands Sharpen study app into a custom solution built for higher education institutions.

October McGraw Hill launches Clinical Reasoning, a digital solution for medical students using GenAI to provide lifelike patient simulations to help learners practice real-world care.

November McGraw Hill introduces Teacher Assistant, a new GenAI-powered chatbot that provides educators with instant guidance to streamline lesson planning.

2024

March McGraw Hill introduces ALEKS Adventure: a new AI-based math program for grades K–3.

August McGraw Hill announces new GenAI tools like AI Reader and Writing Assistant that provide personalized learning experiences for students and help educators more effectively support students from K–12 through Higher Education and beyond. 

October McGraw Hill transitions from traditional textbook edition publishing cycle with new Evergreen™ Delivery Model.

2023

September McGraw Hill announces two new digital platforms, Access mobile app and First Aid Forward™, to help medical professionals and students easily access trusted content that evolves and updates as medicine changes.

2022

August McGraw Hill launches Sharpen, a mobile study app that delivers learning via a continuous content feed, bite-sized videos, swipeable study tools, and a personalized activity dashboard.

December McGraw Hill acquires Boards & Beyond, a provider of on-demand video libraries and comprehensive online resources for medical students. 

2021

March McGraw Hill acquires Kidaptive, Inc., an adaptive and personalized learning company.

May McGraw Hill acquires Triad Interactive, developer of SIMnet online training platform.

July McGraw Hill sold to Platinum Equity.

November McGraw Hill acquires Achieve3000, a leader in differentiated instruction and learning acceleration.

2019

The Company's total digital billings (based on the last 12 months) were greater than 50% for the first time.

2018

McGraw Hill launches innovative textbook rental program, adding to affordable options available for college students.

2016

March McGraw Hill's sales of digital units in the higher education market overtakes print for the first time in company history.

September McGraw Hill acquires Redbird Learning, a leading digital personalized learning company. 

2015

McGraw Hill begins offering digital materials via Inclusive Access, a course material affordability program that delivers digital learning resources to college students at a significantly reduced cost.

2013

January SmartBook™, the world's first adaptive e-book, launches.

March McGraw Hill’s education unit is sold to Apollo Global Management.

June McGraw Hill acquires personalized learning software company ALEKS Corporation.

2009

McGraw Hill Connect® launches; an all-digital teaching & learning platform for higher education – reaching millions of learners within months. 

2004

McGraw Hill’s AccessMedicine online platform of medical education resources launches in the US, going on to become a foundational resource in medical schools across the globe.

1989

McGraw Hill College Division introduces the first computerized publishing system (Create), allowing instructors to customize textbooks.

1988

The Book Company acquires the school and college division of Random House, Inc.

1946

McGraw Hill Publishing Company, Inc. Founded the first international division of the company.

1917

The McGraw and Hill Companies merge to form the McGraw Hill Publishing Company, Inc.

1888

McGraw Hill was founded in 1888, when James H. McGraw, co-founder of McGraw Hill, purchased the American Journal of Railway Appliances.
