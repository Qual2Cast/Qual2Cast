LMS Integration with McGraw Hill Online Learning Platforms

McGraw Hill online education platforms seamlessly integrate with learning management systems (LMS) to bring our tools to your classroom.

McGraw Hill Online Learning Platforms Frequently Asked Questions

An online learning platform is a digital software system designed to deliver and track educational performance. Online learning platforms, or digital learning platforms, support teachers by making it easier to share, review, and grade assignments. Students have their work tracked through the platform which keeps them connected with their instructor to have a shared understanding of their progress.

Progress is tracked through integration between the Learning Management System (LMS) and the platform itself. Instructors have the ability to modify and adjust scores as needed, while the platform can measure student engagement through various factors like watch time, reading progress, or scores for additional features like knowledge checks and quizzes.

McGraw Hill’s digital learning platforms support mobile learning by giving students access to their textbook with the ReadAnywhere app. Students can download the app to their phone, tablet, or laptop making it easier for them to read on the go.


Using Sharpen, students have access to additional content to study in ways that work best for them. Our AI study app has concept videos, flashcards, content summaries, guided solutions, and more to support student learning in as little as five minutes, wherever they are.

Online learning platforms provide instructors with a smoother way to manage student assignments, grades, and feedback while integrating into their LMS.

Students benefit from online learning platforms because they can learn at their own speed and review information easily and as often as they need. Online learning platforms also present information using different types of media which help students understand content in the ways that work best for them.

Our online learning platforms can integrate with several learning management systems such as Blackboard, D2L Brightspace, Canvas, and Moodle. We also offer Learning Tools Interoperability® (LTI) Advantage, the newest technology linking Learning Management Systems to online digital tools, providing better usability and increased security.

To learn more about our LMS integrations and tools, reach out to a Learning Technology Representative.

In a hybrid or in-person class, online learning platforms offer additional support to face-to-face engagement. Instructors can easily assign content and review progress in the platform while students have their tools and resources conveniently in one place.
