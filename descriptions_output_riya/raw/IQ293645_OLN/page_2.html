Products Improving Our World

Discover how Olin products enable a healthier, safer, and more productive world.

OLIN ALLYL CHLORIDE & EPICHLOROHYDRIN

Manufactured in Stade & Freeport, these are critical ingredients for wastewater treatment, protecting our health and ecosystems.

Read more >

OLIN BLEACH

Olin donates thousands of gallons of bleach produced in our Augusta, Becancour, Charleston, Freeport, Henderson, Lemont, McIntosh, Niagara Falls, & Santa Fe Springs sites to aid communities with disaster relief efforts to disinfect flood-damaged property.

Read more >

OLIN CHLORINE

Manufactured in Becancour, Charleston, Freeport, McIntosh, Niagara Falls, Plaquemine, & St. Gabriel, this is a critical ingredient for safer drinking water.

Read more >

OLIN EPOXY SYSTEMS

Produced in Baltringen, Freeport, Guarujá, Pisticci, Rheinmunster, Roberta, Stade, and Zhangjiagang, these provide reliable corrosion resistance and concrete protection for longer-lasting infrastructure.

Read more >

OLIN EPOXY LITESTONE

Products manufactured in our Baltringen, Freeport, Rheinmunster, Roberta, and Stade facilities are mission-critical ingredients for Composite Pressure Vessels, which safely transport and store green energy sources like hydrogen.

Read more >

OLIN METHYL CHLORIDE

Made in our Freeport & Stade facilities, this is used to make chemicals critical to the operation of wastewater treatment plants in most major cities.

Read more >

OLIN PERCHLOROETHYLENE

Manufactured in Plaquemine & Stade, this is used in brake degreasers to remove dust and debris from brake pads and rotors, providing safer stopping distances.

Read more >

OLIN POTASSIUM HYDROXIDE

Produced in Charleston, Tennessee, Olin’s KOH is used in deicing fluids to protect aircraft surfaces from ice accumulation for safer air travel.

Read more >

OLIN POTASSIUM HYDROXIDE

Made in our Charleston, Tennessee, facility to help create record-setting harvests.

Read more >

OLIN SODIUM HYDROXIDE

Produced in Becancour, Charleston, Freeport, McIntosh, Niagara, Plaquemine, & St. Gabriel, this is used to create the liquid-absorbing gel in diapers for improved sanitation and comfort.

Read more >

OLIN SYNTHETIC GLYCERIN

A key ingredient used to make insulin for diabetes medication is produced only by Olin in our Stade, Germany facility.
