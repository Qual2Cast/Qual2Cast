Products

Get Back On Track With Innovations for Active Healing

Pain Treatments

Bioventus offers three nonsurgical joint pain injection therapies: DUROLANE®, GELSYN-3®, and SUPARTZ FX®. These hyaluronic acid (HA) treatments supplement joint lubrication and may assist with mobility and comfort for individuals experiencing mild to moderate osteoarthritis joint pain.

The StimRouter® peripheral nerve stimulation (PNS) system is a long-term option for managing chronic peripheral nerve pain, such as pain related to neuromas, entrapment syndromes, intercostal neuralgias, and other peripheral injuries
and diseases.

Platelet Rich Plasma

The XCELL PRP® system processes whole blood to produce platelet-rich plasma, offering high platelet yield in a single 10-minute spin process.

Restorative Therapies

Using safe, painless ultrasound waves to activate cells near the site of the break, the EXOGEN® Ultrasound Bone Healing System stimulates the body’s natural healing process, helping fractured bones mend.

Surgical Solutions

Our comprehensive portfolio of surgical biologics offers a wide variety of bone graft solutions to meet the needs of any surgeon and their patients, across a broad range of patient needs, procedures, and costs. We also have a suite of minimally invasive therapeutic ultrasonic technologies based on the neXus® Ultrasonic Surgical Aspirator System optimized for hard and soft tissue removal.
