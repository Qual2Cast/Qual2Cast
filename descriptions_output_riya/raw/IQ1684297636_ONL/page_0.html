A Unique Focus on Suburban Net Lease Office

Orion Properties specializes in the ownership, acquisition and management of a diversified portfolio of high quality office buildings located in strong suburban markets across the U.S. The portfolio is leased primarily on a single-tenant net lease basis to creditworthy tenants.
