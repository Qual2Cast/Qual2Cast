About
SS&C Technologies

For close to 40 years, SS&C has developed the technology and services to power our clients' success. With industry-specific solutions from the alternatives space to healthcare to wealth management, SS&C is leading the way toward the future.

Built on Long-Term
Leadership

Founded and led for four decades by CEO Bill Stone, SS&C is defined by continuity, discipline, and long-term thinking. This approach has enabled sustained innovation, responsible growth, and enduring partnerships across financial services and healthcare organizations.

Three Strategic Priorities

As SS&C marks its 40th year, Rahul Kanwar outlines three strategic priorities that will shape our growth in 2026: deepening our focus on clients, building trust by empowering our people, and driving continuous innovation. Built to Solve What Matters.
Proven for What Comes Next.

From a basement startup in 1986 to a global financial technology leader, SS&C has helped organizations operate with confidence, precision and scale. Our platforms support front-, middle- and back-office operations across every major market, delivering resilience through every cycle of change.

SS&C Turns 40

40 Years of Progress at a Glance

A snapshot of how SS&C has grown, adapted, and led for four decades. From foundational software to software-enabled services and now AI-powered solutions, this timeline captures the key milestones, acquisitions, and investments that continue to shape the future of financial services.

1980s

1986

Securities Software & Consulting founded in Bill Stone's basement.

1987
  • The first year for SS&C goes well, and the company grows rapidly.
  • Stock market crashes. U.S. markets fall more than 20% on October 19, 1987.
1988

SS&C continues to expand its presence and grows to 38 employees.

1989

SS&C releases CAMRA, which becomes the first client-server, relational database investment accounting system for the insurance and asset management industry.

1990s

1991
  • FILMS introduced, adding front-office trading capability to its product portfolio
  • Offices are opened in Chicago and Los Angeles
1995
  • Annual revenue grows to $18M.
  • Acquisition of Chalke Group
1996
  • Company reincorporates as SS&C Technologies, Incorporated
  • Initial public offering of 3,750,000 shares of common stock

Early 2000s

2000

Y2K marks a significant change from the way financial services companies do business; they don't want to manage and build up back-office operations. ASP is where it's at and SS&C is prepared to take advantage of the trend.

2003

SS&C improves outsourcing revenues in Q4 of 2003 to $3.6M, a 21% increase over the $3.0M posted in Q3 of 2002

2005
  • Company Privatized with The Carlyle Group
  • Acquisition of FMC, expanding investment management technology and services

2010s

2010

SS&C again becomes a public company and trades on the NASDAQ under the ticker SSNC

2012
  • Forbes names SS&C to "100 Best Companies List"
  • Acquisition of Thomson Reuter's PORTIA
  • Acquisition of GlobeOp Financial Services S.A.
2015
  • Acquisition of Advent Software
  • Acquisition of Primatics Financial
2018
  • Acquisition of Intralinks
  • Acquisition of DST Systems
  • Acquisition of EZE Software

2020s

2020
  • Acquisition of Capita
  • Acquisition of Innovest Systems
2021

SS&C reaches $2T in assets under administration

2022
  • Acquisition of O'Shares ETFs
  • Acquisition of Hubwise Securities Limited
  • Acquisition of Blue Prism
2024

Acquisition of Battea Class Action Services

2025
  • Acquisition of CURO Fund Services
  • Acquisition of Calastone
