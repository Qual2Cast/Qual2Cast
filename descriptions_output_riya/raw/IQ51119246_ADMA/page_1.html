Advancing a new age of specialty immune globulin products

ADMA Biologics, Inc. (Nasdaq: ADMA), a U.S. based, end-to-end commercial biopharmaceutical company dedicated to the manufacturing, developing, and commercializing of specialty biologic products for the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases in the immune compromised and other patients at risk for certain infections. Our devotion to these underserved populations fuels us, and our hands-on approach to production and development sets us apart.

With three FDA-approved products, including ASCENIV™ (Immune Globulin Intravenous, Human–slra), BIVIGAM® (Immune Globulin Intravenous, Human), and Nabi-HB® (Hepatitis B Immune Globulin, Human), ADMA’s mission is to continue to commercialize specialty biologic products, targeted to specific patient populations for the treatment and prevention of certain infectious diseases and management of immune compromised patient populations.

Hear what drives us


We are dedicated to advancing a new age of specialty biologic products. Learn about the passion, values, and promise that drive ADMA Biologics.

ADMA Biologics is the only US-domiciled producer of
plasma-derived therapeutics

  • Rapidly growing and diversified commercial product portfolio
  • Strong patent portfolio supports pipeline optionality and long-term growth opportunities
  • World class, cGMP-compliant biologics manufacturing plant with up to ~600,000 L annual plasma fractionation and purification capacity
  • End-to-end supply chain control, including fill-finish capabilities supporting internal production and external CDMO opportunities
  • Operates 7 vertically integrated plasma collection centers and sources materials from an approximately ~260+ third party collection centers. Supply availability supports significant go-forward revenue and earnings growth opportunities
  • Three FDA-approved products: ASCENIV™ (Immune Globulin Intravenous, Human–slra), BIVIGAM® (Immune Globulin Intravenous, Human) and Nabi-HB® (Hepatitis B Immune Globulin, Human)
  • Thought leader in the development and commercialization of hyperimmune IG products
  • Consistent, reliable, and uninterrupted raw material supply
  • Proprietary yield enhancement production process designed to optimize output, supports increased manufacturing efficiency, margin expansion and long-term supply scalability
