WHAT BOKU ACTUALLY DOES

The platform built for local payments at global scale

Through a single integration, Boku helps merchants reach more customers, acquire subscribers, and move money globally through local payment infrastructure built for scale.

Online payments

Direct, tokenized connections to 200+ local payment methods across 60+ countries, including wallets, account-to-account payments, BNPL, and carrier billing.

Bundling

Boku powers bundled subscriptions through partner networks supporting 50 million active subscribers.

Money movement

Collections, FX, and cross-border settlement unified through licensed infrastructure spanning 40+ markets and relationships with tier-one banking partners.

Underpinning all of it is Boku’s licensing footprint, banking infrastructure, and operational expertise - so merchants can enter new markets without the operational burden of building market by market.
