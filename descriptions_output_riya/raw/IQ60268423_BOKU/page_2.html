Boku supports a wide range of online payment methods, including digital wallets, real-time bank transfers (A2A), and direct carrier billing.

Coverage spans 200+ local payment methods across 60+ countries, enabling merchants to accept the payment types customers already use in each market.

Local payment methods (LPMs) are payment options commonly used and trusted within speciﬁc countries or regions. They include digital wallets, real-time bank transfers, and direct carrier billing.

In many mobile-ﬁrst and emerging markets, LPMs are preferred over cards. Offering the right mix of local payment methods helps merchants reach more customers and improve checkout conversion.

Local payment methods are essential for international growth, particularly in markets where card usage is low.

If customers don’t see a payment method they recognize at checkout, they are more likely to abandon their purchase. By offering relevant local payment methods, merchants can:

  • Increase conversion rates
  • Reach customers who do not use cards
  • Improve the checkout experience
  • Unlock new revenue in high-growth markets

For many businesses, LPM support determines whether they can successfully monetize demand in a new market.
