Who We Are

FIRM OVERVIEW
View PDF
We are a global, multi-asset investment management platform focused on delivering high value-added strategies to sophisticated clients worldwide. For more than 30 years, we have built our business around a simple idea: exceptional investment talent—supported by the right structure—can drive superior long-term results.

Our Approach

We focus exclusively on active investing, combining disciplined processes with independent thinking to uncover opportunities across markets and asset classes.

Our Culture

We foster a talent-driven culture built on creative thinking, client alignment and a long-term perspective.

Our Commitment

We create an environment where investment talent thrives—driving differentiated strategies and lasting client relationships.

At Artisan Partners, we employ a thoughtful approach to growth that we believe sets us apart. We pursue growth deliberately—seeking opportunities that enhance long-term value for both our clients and our investment professionals. By aligning our strategies with global trends and maintaining an entrepreneurial mindset, we continue to evolve while staying true to our core philosophy.

What Defines the Artisan Partners Platform

Autonomous teams with full ownership of their investment process
Centralized operational support to reduce distractions
Aligned incentives through shared investment and equity ownership
Dedicated business leadership to manage infrastructure and client engagement
Our distinctive business model empowers investment teams to focus solely on delivering strong results for clients
