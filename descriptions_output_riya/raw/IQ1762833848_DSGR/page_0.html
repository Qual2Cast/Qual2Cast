Meet DSG.
Distribution Solutions Group.

Distribution Solutions Group (DSG) is a best-in-class specialty distribution company providing high-touch, value-added distribution solutions to the Maintenance, Repair and Operations (MRO), Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) and the industrial technologies markets. DSG was formed through the strategic combination of Lawson Products, a leader in MRO and C-parts distribution, Gexpro Services, a leading global supply chain services provider to the OEM market, and TestEquity, a leader in electronic test and measurement solutions.

Through its collective businesses, DSG is dedicated to being a one-stop solution provider and helping customers lower their total cost of operation by increasing productivity and efficiency with the right products, expert technical support and fast, reliable delivery. DSG serves 120,000+ long-standing customers in several diverse end markets supported by more than 3,000 dedicated employees and strong vendor partnerships. DSG ships from strategically located distribution and service centers to customers in North America, Europe, Asia, South America and the Middle East.

DSG Sustainability Statement

As a resource that delivers tangible products as well as logistical solutions, Distribution Solutions Group sees the opportunities from sustainable operations via multiple viewpoints. We work with our suppliers, customers and partners on the obvious and expected, such as recycling, waste management and reduction, efficiency and re-use – all the important issues responsible companies need in today’s world. We believe, however, that our greatest sustainability strengths come before a single box is shipped, before supply bins are loaded, before waste is assigned its sustainable destination. We work across our relationship spectrum to create activities, tools and proprietary processes that reduce or eliminate waste before it happens. For example, in addition to creating the best process to recycle boxes and packaging, we think about the best process to eliminate the need for the boxes and packaging in the first place. As a result, we lead the industry in our approach to sustainable operations through both post-use sustainability and pre-use waste reduction and elimination. It’s the right thing to do for our customers, environment, partners and business health.
