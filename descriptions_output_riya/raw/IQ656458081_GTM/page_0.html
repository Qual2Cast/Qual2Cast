FAQs

What is ZoomInfo?

ZoomInfo is an all-in-one AI GTM Platform that helps B2B sales, marketing, RevOps, and customer success teams find, win, and grow business. It combines verified data on 500M+ contacts and 100M+ companies with the GTM Context Graph, AI agents that act on it, and access from any tool your team already uses. ZoomInfo was founded in 2007, is headquartered in Vancouver, Washington, and trades on Nasdaq as GTM. Learn more about ZoomInfo.

Who uses ZoomInfo?

Sales, marketing, RevOps, customer success, and recruiting teams use ZoomInfo, along with developers building AI agents. Customers range from fast-growing startups to 8 of the 10 largest companies in the world and more than half of the Fortune 500.

How much does ZoomInfo cost?

Free to start with consumption credits based on usage. ZoomInfo doesn't charge by fixed seat tiers: teams pay for the credits they use, so cost scales with how much data and how many workflows they run. ZoomInfo Lite is a permanent free plan with no credit card required. See ZoomInfo pricing for current details.

Can I try ZoomInfo for free?

Yes. ZoomInfo Lite is a permanent free plan that requires no credit card and no commitment, and you can start a free trial of the full platform. Developers can also try ZoomInfo data headlessly for free through GTM.AI.

How does ZoomInfo get its data?

ZoomInfo builds its data from public web sources and company websites, SEC and regulatory filings, job postings, and a contributory network of business professionals. AI handles deduplication, and 300+ human researchers verify records. ZoomInfo covers professionals in their business capacity, not consumers, and processes 1.5B+ data points daily. How ZoomInfo gets its data.

Is ZoomInfo GDPR and CCPA compliant?

Yes. ZoomInfo processes professional contact data under GDPR's legitimate-interest basis, honors data subject rights, and holds ISO 27001, ISO 27701, SOC 2 Type II, TRUSTe GDPR, and TRUSTe CCPA certifications. Details are in the ZoomInfo Trust Center.

Is ZoomInfo an AI-powered platform?

Yes. AI runs through every layer of ZoomInfo. The GTM Context Graph reasons across ZoomInfo data, your CRM, conversations, and buying signals to show why accounts are moving. GTM Workspace gives sellers AI agents for account research, meeting prep, and deal prioritization. Agent Teams coordinates specialized agents that run complete GTM plays, such as reactivating a champion who changed jobs, within the approvals and limits your team sets.

Does ZoomInfo have an API or MCP server?

Yes. GTM.AI gives developers and AI agents headless access to ZoomInfo data and the GTM Context Graph through an API, CLI, and MCP server, and you can try it for free. The ZoomInfo MCP server connects Claude, ChatGPT, and custom agents to verified ZoomInfo data.

How is ZoomInfo different from other GTM and AI tools?

Most tools handle one slice of go-to-market work: a contact list, an intent feed, or an AI writing assistant. ZoomInfo connects verified data on 500M+ contacts and 100M+ companies, the GTM Context Graph, which explains why an account is moving rather than just flagging it, and AI agents that act on it across sales, marketing, and RevOps. See how ZoomInfo compares with Apollo.io, Cognism, and Lusha.

Does ZoomInfo integrate with our CRM and AI tools?

Yes. ZoomInfo integrates natively with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Microsoft Dynamics 365, plus 120+ sales engagement, marketing automation, and data warehouse tools, including Outreach, Salesloft, Marketo, and Snowflake. AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT connect through the ZoomInfo MCP server. See all integrations.
