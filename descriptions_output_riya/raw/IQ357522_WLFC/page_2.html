Our aeronautical services are the fastest way to locate aircraft parts and spares for commercial aircraft. Providing you with the right part, in the right place, at the right time is crucial to keeping your fleets in revenue service while maximizing uptime. We have accumulated inventory in nearly all major engine types and have amassed one of the industry's largest stocks of material.

We offer parts locator services and maintain a constantly changing inventory. At Willis Aeronautical Services, Inc. (Willis Aero), we have compiled a large engine parts inventory database from leading manufacturers and suppliers worldwide, enabling you to quickly source the parts they need.

Material Solutions and Services for Engines and Airframes

Our owned and consigned engine fleet includes over one hundred powerplants ranging from JT8-200s to PW4000s. The majority of these assets are in various stages of disassembly and parts sales processing. Our portfolio of transport category aircraft engines also includes CFM-56s, CF6s, and more, while our airframes are comprised of Classic B737 and A320 aircraft, with teardown A310s and B747-300s reduced to parts.

Aircraft owners and operators require efficient and reliable maintenance service solutions that deliver cost savings whilst ensuring quality and consistency on a global scale.

Our dedicated maintenance service team are on hand to mitigate operation maintenance risks with experienced aviation technicians and the support of over 20 years of consultancy assistance.

As a ‘Known Consignor’, validated by the CAA, we adhere to strict regulations for transporting cargo by air and have special procedures such as Customs Warehousing and Inward Processing Relief.

We are permitted to ship cargo as SPX from source without the need for any further security measures. This validation reduces costs and streamlines the handling of assets at the point of export.

Located at Teesside International Airport, Jet Centre by Willis, delivers a full range of airport FBO and ground handling services. Our team of highly trained professional, friendly staff provides ground handling services for business, private aviation, military and cargo flights.

In addition, the FBO offers a comfortable area for crew and passengers to relax with enhanced services including:

  • Hangar facilities
  • External aircraft washes
  • Complimentary refreshments and snacks
  • Access to pilot briefing room
  • Preferred rates on select hotels
  • Transportation and other related services

Jet Centre by Willis is situated just east of Darlington, located in Northeast England, and just west of Stockton-on-Tees. The airport serves the Northeast and North Yorkshire and every year hosts customers from all over the world who wish to take part in the grouse and pheasant season on the neighboring North Yorkshire Moors.

The FBO also caters to those wishing to visit their local industries nearby in Middlesbrough.
