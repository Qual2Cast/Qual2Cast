Global Innovative Medical Device Manufacturing and Design

During our 80-year legacy of cutting-edge medical device manufacturing and medical device development, we have established a solid patient-focused foundation in cardiac rhythm, cardio and vascular health, neuromodulation devices, and custom medical battery packs and chargers for global OEMs. Integer is a world leader in advanced medical device design and outsourcing across four continents. Our technology and manufactured components can be found in almost every global medical device brand you trust. Our commitment to manufacturing excellence ensures that your medical devices get to market faster with uncompromising reliability and quality. Innovation is key to Integer's growth as we continue to explore medtech's emerging markets.

Cutting-edge Medical Device Technologies

We provide innovative, high quality medical device technologies - including catheters, guidewires, introducers, delivery systems, tantalum capacitors, implantable ports, neuromodulation devices, pacemaker batteries, implantable and external batteries, and much more – to meet customers’ needs and help enhance the lives of patients worldwide. The products we design and manufacture range from finished device systems to Class III Medical Devices and hundreds of components dispersed among more than 15 markets. We also provide market-ready products to medical device OEMs. From product design to completed systems, our expertise in medical device development and battery design is foundational to the history of cardiac rhythm and cardio and vascular health.

Customer-centric Approach

At Integer, we blend our technology, expertise and resources to collaborate with many of the world's leading OEM medical device companies to amplify their innovation through our state-of-the art design and contract manufacturing capabilities. As a trusted medical device manufacturing partner, we support our customers with:

  • Unique design expertise and development solutions that strengthen and de-risk new product development
  • Market-ready guidewires and introducers to deliver unique therapies
  • Advanced medical device manufacturing to meet customers’ exact requirements and quality standards
  • Cell and custom battery pack design, development and manufacturing expertise to meet our customers' power requirements

Our deep expertise, flexible approach, vertically integrated supply chain and global footprint enable us to support you every step of the way to deliver successful, high-quality medical devices that meet and exceed expectations. And the knowledge of our quality experts goes beyond the basics of regulatory certification and compliance to help ensure your product meets all of today’s necessary and required standards. In short, we help our customers minimize risk and bring high-quality medical devices to market faster.

World Class Contract Manufacturing Excellence

As an established medical device manufacturer and medical device development company, we understand your clinical, development and manufacturing challenges. Our collaborative approach starts with assessing your unique requirements, suggesting adjustments to optimize product design and leveraging our years of experience to realize your project goals and get your product to market faster.

We provide leading medical device companies with comprehensive product development and product transfer processes. Our broad capabilities and expertise include:

  • Research, design and development
  • Testing and certification expertise to meet quality and regulatory requirements
  • Prototyping
  • Product lifecycle management
  • Global medical device manufacturing
  • Vertical integration and localized operations
  • Strategic sourcing and supply chain management

Using our proven, unique design expertise and development processes, we meticulously manage each step of the product lifecycle to help you deliver successful results.

High-quality Medical Device Outsource Manufacturing

Integer Holdings Corporation (NYSE: ITGR) is one of the largest medical device contract development and manufacturing organizations (CDMOs) in the world, serving the cardio and vascular, neuromodulation, and cardiac rhythm management markets. As a strategic partner of choice, we advance the goals of our medical device customers through industry-leading engineering and manufacturing, with a relentless commitment to quality, service, and innovation.
