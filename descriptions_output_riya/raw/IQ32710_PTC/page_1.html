How we help our customers

The global economy—and our everyday lives—run on the $14 trillion of manufactured goods produced each year.

With global supply chains, software-driven innovation, and ever-evolving customer demands, how these products are designed, manufactured, and serviced is more complicated than ever.

Our portfolio of software solutions manages data throughout a product’s lifecycle – driving excellence in engineering and design, efficiency in manufacturing and supply chain, and optimization in operations and service.
