Care Services

Ensign affiliates provide skilled nursing, rehabilitation, therapy, and assisted living services that leverage a ONE CLINICAL collaborative model of care focused on enhancing the lives of those they serve.

Collaboration That Makes a Difference

At Ensign-affiliated locations, exceptional care begins with collaboration. Through a ONE CLINICAL approach, nurses, therapists, physicians, and interdisciplinary care teams work together to provide coordinated, personalized care that supports each resident’s unique needs. This collaborative model helps each resident benefit from a seamless, individualized care experience designed to maximize that individual resident’s health and wellness.

Care Designed Around the Whole Person

Rather than treating individual conditions in isolation, Ensign-affiliated teams take a comprehensive view of residents’ health. By combining clinical expertise, compassionate caregiving, and evidence-based practices, residents obtain the support they need to recover from illness, regain function, maintain independence, and achieve their highest possible quality of life.

Driven by Data.
Focused on Outcomes.

The ONE CLINICAL approach is guided by clinical data and continuous improvement principles. Care teams monitor key quality measures and resident outcomes to identify risks early, evaluate progress, and develop targeted interventions that support better health and recovery. This commitment to measurement and accountability helps drive stronger outcomes while supporting overall quality of care and promoting regulatory excellence.
