Other insurance products

Hagerty offers exceptional insurance for classic and collector vehicles—driven by a deep passion for the road and decades of expertise. Through trusted partnerships, we also provide a range of coverage options to help protect more of what matters, all in one convenient place.

Business Insurance

Hagerty offers specialized business coverage to help keep you and the cars in your care protected.

Learn more

Event Insurance

Event Insurance is affordable, quick to issue and may offer protection against event cancellation, unforeseen accidents, and other event-specific liabilities.

Learn more

High Performance Drivers Education (HPDE) & Time Trial

Track day insurance — on the track and in the paddock - Hagerty covers all your high-performance driving needs.

Learn more

Marine Business Insurance

Marine Business Insurance protects against the unique risks found in the marine industry. Work worry free with this essential coverage through Hagerty.

Learn more

Overseas Shipping Insurance

Whether you’re transporting your collector car by sea or air, make sure you’re covered with international shipping coverage through Hagerty.

Learn more

Personal Umbrella Insurance

A personal umbrella policy is a low-cost, extra layer of protection that increases the basic coverage provided by your auto, home, marine or other personal insurance.

Learn more

Title & Dealer Bonds

Missing titles or dealer licensing requirements? With our trusted partners, you can quickly secure the bond you need to keep your classic or collector vehicle on the road.

Learn more
