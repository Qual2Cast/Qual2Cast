Booz Allen Acquires Ultra I&C Mission Solutions Business

Booz Allen has completed its acquisition of the Ultra I&C Mission Solutions business, now operating as the commercial product and solutions component of Booz Allen’s defense technology business. Together, our complementary capabilities in battle management, secure communications, encryption, and edge compute will accelerate delivery of interoperable, mission-ready technologies for national security.
