Your mission demands a digital mindset

Around the world, organizations are changing the way they operate in business, meet their missions, and address emerging threats. As technology solutions converge and connect in new ways, there are advanced opportunities to better serve and protect citizens and national interests. But with these opportunities come technical challenges. Enterprise systems must be able to quickly and easily adapt to new threats, while making sense of data pouring in from around the world.

New technical capabilities on their own won’t solve hard problems. With continuous pressure to modernize and accelerate innovation, organizations face the challenge of purposeful transformation. 

In this dynamic technology landscape, Booz Allen is a digital transformation partner that understands your requirements, technology environment, operating structure, and adoption readiness to address modernization with purpose. We're trusted to advance the nation’s most sensitive missions and are committed to delivering open, yet secure architectures so you can quickly and easily adopt new technologies. From keeping the public safe through a modern military, to ensuring citizens have access to government services, our solutions ensure that the right capabilities, with the most valuable data insights, are delivered at the moments that matter most.

Our Capabilities

From strategy to implementation, we build and integrate end-to-end technology solutions to help you improve outcomes across the mission lifecycle—even as it evolves. Because we focus on solving whole problems, we don’t just stop at technology. We help you embed the right policies, processes, training, and culture changes to drive digital adoption.

Cloud Solutions

Cloud offers the promise of working smarter and faster—but the journey requires myriad strategic and technical decisions. From strategy to operations, we help clients integrate cloud systems across the enterprise, aggregate industry solutions into a government-led ecosystem, and transform to support hybrid and multicloud environments.

Learn More
Digital Customer Experience

At every digital touchpoint, we have an opportunity to impact lives. Whether it’s creating seamless and personalized interactions for citizens who need benefits and services, or enabling our civil service to address customer needs through modern digital platforms, we deliver experience-driven solutions across the technology lifecycle.

Learn More
Software Development

Today’s dynamic technology landscape demands continuous and rapid innovation. We accelerate and automate platform and application delivery using open, cloud-native, and portable architectures that you control as your mission evolves. In addition, we reinforce our solutions with intelligence-grade cyber for systems you can trust—anytime, anywhere.

Learn More
Data Management

When data turns into insights that move seamlessly across an enterprise, users are smarter, safer, and more successful. We build digital ecosystems that converge complex platforms, so the right people have access to trusted data when they need it most. From the cloud to the tactical edge, we enable faster, informed decisions.

Learn More
Edge Computing

The power of edge computing is transforming the modern mission. Instead of relying on connectivity or waiting for information from the operation center, it’s enabling rapid access to data far beyond the centralized or connected IT network. With computing at the source, processing can happen at the point of data collection and in the moments that matter most.

Learn More
Human Performance

Human Performance Training transforms the way individuals prepare for and carry out their mission. We combine traditional strength and conditioning, advanced sports and data science, and immersive simulations to optimize the physical and psychological readiness, wellbeing, and performance of trainees. With our approach, elite athletes, warfighters, and first responders can improve decision making and effectiveness for their mission at hand—and develop physical, social, and mental fortitude beyond the field.

Learn More

Digital Accelerators

To help organizations move critical development forward at the pace required to meet the mission, Booz Allen offers open source solutions that fast-track development in secure, cloud-based environments.

Virtual Cloud Defense

To secure workloads and protect data in the cloud, Booz Allen developed Virtual Cloud Defense—a scalable security architecture suite that assures the highest levels of security during the cloud migration process and integrates the top commercially available solutions designed to meet organizational needs, as well as commercial, military, and federal government requirements.

Learn More
Solutions Delivery Platform

This cloud-native, container-based DevSecOps solution accelerates modern software development. Booz Allen has taken our extensive experience implementing DevSecOps and consolidated key lessons into a custom-built pipeline framework that enables continuous security monitoring and testing, allowing software development teams to focus on code and applications for pressing mission needs.

Key Partnerships

We're committed to delivering highly adaptable, open-yet-secure architectures that you control so you can quickly and easily bring in new partners and technologies as they emerge. Whether we build the solutions ourselves or team up with industry, academia, or non-traditional government partners, we help you access the best technology for your mission, working side by side with you to integrate it seamlessly into the unique constraints of your environment.

  • Amazon Web Services

    Booz Allen is an Amazon Web Services (AWS) Premier Business Partner, focusing on providing exceptional AWS cloud solutions and customer service across federal and commercial organizations. Ultra-secure cloud migration, storage, and big data analytics are among our priorities.

  • Microsoft

    Working across government and commercial accounts, we’re leveraging Microsoft offerings to apply emerging technology to the mission. Together we're focused on cloud, mobility, analytics, and security. Our collaboration spans a large range of markets and software development opportunities.

  • Red Hat

    We're one of Red Hat’s Premier Business Partners and have been partnering since 2013 on mission-driven architectures. Our focus is on bringing open-source business software solutions to public sector customers that fit their specific needs, especially utilizing cloud technology.

  • ServiceNow

    Booz Allen is a ServiceNow Premier partner and an implementations leader in the federal market with enterprise transformation initiatives across various government organizations. Our experience as trusted partners implementing the ServiceNow tool has facilitated intra-agency collaboration and communication for solutions to problems and approaches that have worked to modernize and transform our clients’ enterprises.
