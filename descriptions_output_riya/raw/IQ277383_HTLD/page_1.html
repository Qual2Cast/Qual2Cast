Industry Solutions


Delivering exceptional results to our customers.

Heartland Express customers represent the most successful businesses in the world. The top shippers in America depend on Heartland Express to improve business performance, deliver exceptional results to their customers, and drive overall value into their transportation supply chain. 

AUTOMOTIVE

As the performance needs of our shippers increase, Heartland Express has even more opportunity to differentiate our service and demonstrate the strength that our company was built on.  FedEx and other top shippers have recently awarded Heartland as their carrier of the year.

CONSUMER GOODS

Another consistent contributor to the economy, we appreciate the opportunity to serve our customers in the consumer goods space.  Unilever and other leaders in the consumer goods market have recently awarded Heartland Express as their best-in-class performer. 

FOOD

As an essential and consistent segment of the American economy, food distribution makes up a substantial portion of the customers we serve.  Heartland Express has been honored to accept performance awards recently from Quaker Oats, Gatorade, Kellogg Company, Nestle Waters, and many more. 

MANUFACTURING

Heartland Express serves the supply chain needs of many manufacturers in America, including supply operations, DC transfers, and vendor deliveries.  As supply-to-cash cycles get shorter and faster in the JIT environment, Heartland has excelled in meeting difficult requirements.  Whirlpool and other leading producers have recently recognized Heartland Express as their supplier of the year. 

PAPER AND PLASTIC

We serve many shippers that manufacturer and supply paper, plastic, and packaging products.  Cups, bowls, plates, containers, napkins, tissues, home and office paper - some of the many products that our drivers protect and deliver every day.   

RETAIL

Heartland Express excels at time-sensitive store replenishment, and has recently won performance awards from Sears, Walmart, Lowes, and many more.  We value this business and the strategic relationships that we have developed to execute for the retailers that we serve.
