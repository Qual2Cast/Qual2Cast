Who We Are

About EverQuote

Optimize Digital and AI Customer Acquisition Channels

Our mission is simple: be the leading growth partner for the P&C insurance industry.

Using insurance-specific data, technology, and marketing expertise amassed over 15 years, we help leading carriers and thousands of local agents engage high-intent consumers and bind profitable policies through digital customer acquisition channels. And we do it with unprecedented precision.

Our story

A Smarter Way for Carriers and Agents

In 2011, our founders saw an industry facing a difficult problem: Insurance shoppers needed – and wanted – a better way to navigate a confusing shopping process. And carriers needed – and wanted – a better way to find and serve those exact customers. They created EverQuote to make those connections smarter, faster, and more effective, helping carriers adapt and thrive in the era of digital customer acquisition.

Today, our rich data and acclaimed AI-powered technology help carriers find, engage and acquire high-intent consumers across channels, drive higher conversion rates and grow policies in force.

Our technology

Better Matching. Better Growth.

There’s no one-size-fits-all insurance product. Insurance providers have specific and evolving underwriting preferences, target customer segments, growth objectives, and profitability targets.

EverQuote’s proprietary data, predictive modeling, and AI-driven matching enable carriers to strike the right balance between those outcomes, by connecting the right consumer with the right provider at the right time. Our technology identifies high-intent shoppers, enhances providers’ targeting and bidding, and aligns both with providers’ specific underwriting preferences and needs.

Looking ahead

Built for what comes next

The way consumers find and shop for insurance is changing. AI assistants and emerging agentic channels are creating new sources of consumer engagement.

With a proven track record, EverQuote builds on our AI heritage and expertise to help carriers and agents compete in a new paradigm — harnessing new tools and capabilities to turn consumer intent into profitable growth.
