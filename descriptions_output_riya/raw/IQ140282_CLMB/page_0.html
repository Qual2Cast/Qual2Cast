Climb Global Solutions Delivers Globally

With our subsidiaries in the distribution and VAR space, our foot hold in the market and technology supply chain is strengthened in North America and Europe.

Climb Global Solutions bridges the gap between emerging technology manufacturers and the end user that are left unserved by broad distributors. Our specialized focus and size uniquely position us to find disruptive technology vendors and partner with them early, enabling strong organic growth for years ahead.

Broadline distributors are not able to meet the unique and specific demands of end users or provide the enablement and education to VARs that allows them to showcase their expertise to customers and win consistent business over longer periods of time. Climb Global Solutions is at the center of the action, putting the best-in-class emerging technologies in front of customers, not just as another SKU, but with high touch servicing and support to ensure the resellers can effectively market the product and win business.
