Who We Are

DHI Group, Inc. (NYSE: DHX) is a family of purpose-built talent platforms - each built for a specific professional community, not a one-size-fits-all marketplace. DHI Group powers ClearanceJobs and Dice, the platforms top tech talent turn to and employers rely on. Together, they connect nearly 10 million tech professionals; engineers, software developers, data scientists, cybersecurity experts, AI specialists, and more, with the employers who need them. Our AI-powered tools and proprietary skills algorithm do the heavy lifting, matching the right candidate to the right opening, fast.

More About Us

What We Do

At DHI, our mission is simple: connect employers with the technology professionals equipped to solve their hardest problems. ClearanceJobs and Dice each anchor a critical piece of the tech hiring ecosystem, built from the ground up for the communities they serve.

Our Portfolio

Dice is the largest technology-focused talent marketplace in the United States, built over more than 35 years with nearly 8 million technology profiles. Unlike generalized professional networking platforms, Dice organizes talent around more than 100,000 distinct technology skills, allowing recruiters to identify candidates based on the precise capabilities they need.

 

 

 

ClearanceJobs is the leading marketplace for professionals with active U.S. security clearances, serving approximately 1,700 customers, including Lockheed Martin, Booz Allen Hamilton, Leidos, Raytheon, and many others. ClearanceJobs has 2 million cleared candidate profiles, reinforcing our position as the industry's premier destination for cleared technology talent.
