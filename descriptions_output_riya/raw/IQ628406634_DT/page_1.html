Preserve data and business context automatically

Dynatrace turns isolated signals into valuable data by combining graph, event, time series, and NoSQL principles to automatically map relationships and causal dependencies across logs, traces, metrics, RUM data, security, and business events, all unified in one single data store.

Eliminate the complexity of cloud data at scale

Grail's indexless, schema-on-read architecture adapts automatically to ingested data, freeing your teams from the constant overhead of schema management and re-indexing as your environment evolves.

With always-hydrated hot and cold storage, both live and historical data is instantly accessible, so there’s no need to export it to external storage systems for implementation in Dynatrace Intelligence or other utilizations.

Get answers from your data in real-time

Powered by a Massively Parallel Processing (MPP) engine, Grail delivers a performance boost of up to 100 times, searching through hundreds of terabytes of data per second to provide reliable, real-time answers.

The patented Datawarping technology eliminates manual schema management and obsoletes indexes, reducing overhead by 90–99%.

Limitless contextual analytics with Dynatrace Query Language (DQL)

DQL puts advanced analytics at everyone’s fingertips. It combines distributed query execution with flexible, human-readable syntax to deliver instant answers from massive datasets. Teams can parse, transform, search, and analyze data on read, without needing indexing or pre-processing. Paired alongside advanced topology mapping, the deterministic and agentic AI capabilities of Dynatrace intelligence offer additional insight and context to the data queried.

Learn more

Secure, structured, and compliant by design

Grail combines enterprise-grade security with flexible data organization to keep every record protected and auditable.

It enforces fine-grained access permissions and role-based governance, with encryption in transit and at rest. Immutable storage, audit logging, data masking, and hard deletion ensure compliance with all strict privacy and regulatory standards.

Learn more

Intelligent, super-fast data ingestion

OpenPipeline ingests up to 1,000 TB of data per day into Grail, without sampling and blind spots. Data is automatically normalized using the Semantic Dictionary and mapped to topology and entity relationships through Smartscape.

This seamless integration connects all data, making it fully queryable and powering instant insights alongside AI-driven analytics for autonomous operations with Dynatrace Intelligence. You can further transform, filter, mask, and route data in real-time during ingestion, applying privacy controls and your own contextual enrichment before it reaches storage.
