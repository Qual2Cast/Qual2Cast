About Perdoceo Education

Perdoceo’s accredited academic institutions offer online, campus-based, and hybrid programs, providing quality education to a diverse student population. These institutions include Colorado Technical University (CTU)® – encompassing programming bootcamp Coding Dojo® – and the American InterContinental University System (AIUS) which comprises American InterContinental University®, Trident University International, California Southern University™, IT education provider DigitalCrafts, and medical education provider Hippo.

CTU and AIUS offer industry-relevant programs from non-degree professional development and associate degrees, up to the doctoral level. They utilize technologies like the award-winning Intellipath® adaptive learning platform and CTU Mobile App.

CTU programs have earned several awards and recognitions including:

  • Eleven consecutive years on U.S. News & World Report’s Best Online Programs lists and multiple Global Online MBA Rankings from CEO Magazine.

  • Recognitions by Military Times, the Military Order of the Purple Heart and other military-centric institutions.

  • Multiple awards for CTU’s Mobile App, Intellipath Platform, and Virtual Classroom, including three WOW Awards (the most recent in 2021), the 2022 Eduventures Innovation Award, and more.

AIUS’s awards and recognitions include:

  • Rankings upon U.S. News & World Report’s Best Online Programs lists including Best Online Bachelor's Programs, Best Online MBA Programs, and Best Online Graduate Education Programs.

  • Military-centric recognitions such as inclusion on the Military Times Best for Vets: Colleges list.

  • IMS Global 2016 Learning Impacts Awards Gold Medal for the AIU Virtual Classroom and the Best of Elearning! Award for AIU’s Intellipath® adaptive learning platform.

In addition to our online programs, AIU has ground campuses in Atlanta, Georgia and Houston, Texas; and CTU has a ground campus in Colorado Springs, Colorado.

Headquartered in Schaumburg, Illinois, Perdoceo Education has approximately 4,000 employees, both full and part-time, across the nation. We’re always looking for new ways to serve our student population—and passionate people to help us continue to refine and innovate the educational experience.

Perdoceo Education and its schools cannot guarantee employment, salary, or career advancement.
