Jerash Holdings manufactures and exports custom, ready-made sport and outerwear from Jordan. We make products for leading global brands that include The North Face, Timberland, New Balance, American Eagle, Calvin Klein, and Adidas. With 7 factories and 6 warehouses employing approximately 6,600 people, our annual capacity is more than 26 million pieces.

A Diverse Workforce

Jerash Holdings employs a highly skilled and diverse workforce. Local Jordanian workers, as well as workers from Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, India, Myanmar and Nepal work in harmony to produce the highest quality products for the global brands we support.

A Plan for Future Growth

The roadmap for future growth consists of utilizing our strong existing infrastructure and reputation for quality manufacturing to explore new global markets, as well as further enhance market share in the geographies we currently serve and among our existing customer accounts. Strategic acquisition opportunities will further diversify our product offerings, attract new customers, increase capacity and add to our growth.

Strong Financial Performance

Jerash Holdings has performed consistently well, with a visible runway for sustainable growth.

Global Certifications

Jerash Holdings holds multiple international certifications for production, supply, labor, social and ethical compliance. The company adheres to internationally established Accepted Quality Levels and continuously makes efforts to improve facilities and processes.

Production Facilities

7 factories, 6 warehouses, 6,600 people and unlimited possibilities. We conduct production operations in Jordan, allowing sales within the U.S. and EU without tariff or quota restrictions.

Constant Eye on Quality

Even though we produce over 26 million pieces annually, quality is our first priority because we manufacture clothing for the finest global brands with exacting standards.

Outdoor Clothing Experts

Our experience with customers who design clothing for the outdoors allow us to have a unique capability in manufacturing clothing made of high performance fabrics.

FULL SERVICE PRODUCTION

Our factories have full service production capabilities. Cutting, sewing, embroidery, printing, ironing, quality control, packing, along with trims and finished products warehousing comprise a start to finish process offering a complete manufacturing solution for our customers.

Design to Manufacturing

Utilizing exceptional development capabilities in converting designs to mass production, we combine our highly skilled workforce with state of the art technology to manufacture any product our customers can imagine.

Supply Chain Management

We capture maximum value from the entire production chain because every step in our manufacturing process focuses on efficiency and timely delivery to our customers.
