Biologics at the Core

Xtant is a leading biologics company with 25 years of clinical success, offering a broad range of orthobiologic solutions. Our vertically integrated operations, from donor recovery to distribution, ensure unmatched quality, traceability, and consistency. With state-of-the-art processing and patented, clinically proven technology, Xtant delivers industry-leading biologics you can trust.

Synergistic spinal implants

A complementary portfolio of cervical, thoraco-lumbar, and interbody spinal implant systems used in complex, deformity, and degenerative procedures. 

Our cervical, thoracolumbar, and interbody systems are engineered to pair naturally with Xtant biologics, giving surgeons a single, reliable source for biologic and fixation needs without compromising choice or performance.

A Mission Rooted in Respect

Every graft begins with a selfless act of generosity. At Xtant Medical, we are privileged to receive the extraordinary gift of human tissue donation. Each decision we make—from donor screening to final delivery—honors the trust of donors and their families by ensuring the highest standards of safety, quality, and clinical performance.

Blue and Green Day

National Donate Life Month

At Xtant Medical, we recognize our duty to advance innovation made possible by the gift of donation. Every product reflects the precision, performance, and progress surgeons rely on.

 

Every year, Xtant Medical proudly observes National Donate Life Month and Blue & Green Day. This celebration is dedicated to raising awareness about the lifesaving impact of organ, eye, and tissue donors, while honoring those who have given the ultimate gift of life through donation.
