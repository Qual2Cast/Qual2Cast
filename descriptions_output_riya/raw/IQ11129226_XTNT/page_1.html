About

Mission Statement

Our mission is to honor the gift of donation by allowing our patients to live as full and complete a life as possible.

About Xtant

Our Story

Headquartered in Montana, Xtant Medical combines the rigor of science with the heart of purpose. We started as a spin-off from Montana State University’s Center for Biofilm Engineering. We evolved into Xtant Medical, where today, we deliver a comprehensive biologics and fixation portfolio while staying true to our roots: honoring donors, serving patients, and innovating for the future. Our biologics are processed in a dedicated, state-of-the-art facility using patented technologies like BacteRinse™ to preserve essential growth factors while ensuring safety and clinical reliability. This focus allows us to honor every donor gift with the highest standards of quality and respect.

Our Quality Policy

Through an effective Quality System, Xtant Medical prioritizes our commitment to our patients, our donors and donor families.

  • We aim to improve the quality of life for our patients by designing, manufacturing and distributing medical devices and human tissues for transplant that are safe, effective and meet the needs of our customers.
  • We honor the gift of donation by enhancing our core competencies and maximizing utilization of the gift.

Our Values

  • Respect for the Individual
  • Responsiveness to our Customers
  • Responsibility to our Stakeholders
