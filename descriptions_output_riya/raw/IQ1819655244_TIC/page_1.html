Strengthening Assets, Securing Communities

TIC Solutions is a leading provider of critical asset integrity solutions, which includes tech-enabled testing, inspection, certification, and compliance (TICC) and engineering services. TIC Solutions serves diversified end markets that are essential to the communities where we live and work, including industrials, infrastructure, energy, midstream, and renewables.

Global Reach, Local Expertise

TIC Solutions’ 11,000 technical professionals operate out of over 200 locations across North America and abroad to deliver comprehensive asset improvement, maintenance, and reliability solutions to meet your specific needs.

Lifecycle Solutions for Critical Assets​

From infrastructure and building systems design and commissioning to testing, inspection, and compliance, TIC Solutions supports the entire lifecycle of assets and infrastructure through comprehensive solutions for engineering design, advanced inspection and nondestructive testing (NDT), Rope Access Technician (RAT) mitigation services, materials engineering services, lab analysis and destructive testing, proprietary software, and drone-enabled geospatial analytics.
