S&P 500 Earnings Season Update: October 16, 2026

At this early stage, the third quarter earnings season for the S&P 500 has seen a mixed start relative to expectations. While the percentage of S&P 500 companies reporting positive earnings surprises is above recent averages, the magnitude of earnings surprises is below recent averages. As a result of this mixed performance and downward revisions to EPS estimates for companies in the Health Care sector, the index is reporting lower earnings for the third quarter today relative to the end of last week and relative to the end of the quarter. Despite the decrease in earnings, the index is still reporting (year-over-year) earnings growth above 25% for the 3rd straight quarter.

John Butters
Earnings

October 14, 2026

Balancing Hyperscale Demand and Ratepayer Protections in Wyoming

Wyoming’s pitch to hyperscale tech developers has long rested on low-cost industrial power, available rural land, and an agreeable business climate. But as developers announce pipelines exceeding 5 GW of planned hyperscale capacity across the state, which includes multi-gigawatt campuses in Cheyenne, Evanston, and Casper, state regulatory dockets, not site-selection brochures, are defining the economics of deployment. With the Wyoming Public Service Commission (WPSC) reviewing 2026 general rate filings from PacifiCorp d/b/a Rocky Mountain Power (Docket No. 20000-710-ER-26) and Black Hills Power (Docket No. 20002-148-ER-26), the central issue has shifted from site acquisition to cost assignment: who pays for regional generation, high-voltage transmission interconnects, and local substation expansions, and how fast will those expenditures enter the retail rate base?

Ony Adeneke
Energy

From Investment Intent to Portfolio: The Rise of Conversational Portfolio Construction

Portfolio construction is entering a new phase. Over the past several decades, much of the innovation in portfolio construction has focused on the mathematics: richer objectives, more realistic measures of risk, increasingly sophisticated constraints, transaction costs, taxes, multi-asset class portfolios, and multi-period investment decisions. Those advances have made the underlying machinery much more powerful.

Georgi Mitov
Risk, Performance, and Reporting
