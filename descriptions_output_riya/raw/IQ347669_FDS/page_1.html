FactSet FAQs on Generative AI

Pricing

Generative AI helps financial professionals gain greater efficiency and find alpha. Companies can leverage generative AI for many tasks, such as drafting reports from tables of data, identifying main themes and sentiment in documents, or summarizing lengthy documents like regulatory filings or earnings call transcripts. 

With several years of experience in generative AI and nearly two decades in machine learning and NLP (Natural Language Processing), we're prepared to assist your firm during your digital transformation journey. FactSet’s generative AI enables higher productivity for data extraction, natural language understanding for search and chat, text generation, text summarization, entity identification, and sentiment analysis across FactSet’s digital platform. It is also the backbone of our NLP APIs, predictive signals, and document insights. Combining Large Language Models (LLMs) with FactSet’s trusted data leads to faster decision-making backed by superior data to speed financial workflows.

Protecting sensitive data is of the utmost importance. Companies must ensure their generative AI tools comply with applicable data protection laws, regulations, and industry standards. This includes implementing appropriate security measures, such as encryption, access controls, and data anonymization techniques, to safeguard sensitive data from unauthorized access or breaches.

FactSet’s Privacy Notice outlines our commitment to protect personal data in all FactSet applications, including our generative AI tools. FactSet has its own secure and private instances of Large Language Models and does not share client, confidential, or proprietary data with public generative AI models.

FactSet employs Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) methods that pair generative AI models with our own fact-based data to avoid data hallucinations.
