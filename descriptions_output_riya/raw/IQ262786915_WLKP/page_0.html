Company Profile

Westlake Chemical Partners (NYSE: WLKP) is a limited partnership formed by Westlake Corporation to operate, acquire and develop ethylene production facilities and other qualified assets. Our business and operations are conducted through OpCo, a recently-formed partnership between Westlake Corporation and Westlake Chemical Partners. Our assets include three ethylene production facilities in Calvert City, Kentucky, and Lake Charles, Louisiana, with an annual capacity of approximately 3.7 billion pounds and a 200-mile ethylene pipeline.

Production Capacity: Approximately 3.7 billion pounds
Public Company (2014): NYSE:WLKP
Headquarters: Houston, TX
Operating Sites: Calvert City, KY and Lake Charles, LA
Product Line: Ethylene
