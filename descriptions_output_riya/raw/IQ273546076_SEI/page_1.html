About Solaris Energy Infrastructure

Inspired by Innovation. Guided by Results.

Who We Are

Founded in 2014, Solaris Energy Infrastructure (NYSE: SEI) delivers proprietary power generation and distribution solutions, as well as logistics equipment and services, to clients in the data center, energy, commercial, and industrial sectors.


We deliver turnkey solutions using industry-leading technologies combined with exceptional service for superior customer performance.

The Solaris Difference

We deliver expertise and reliable technology where you need it, when you need it.

  • Operational Experience

    We leverage deep operational experience to deliver solutions engineered for the unique demands of high-density power workloads.

  • Rapidly Deployable

    We accelerate speed to market with power up to utility-scale deployed in weeks, not years.

  • Best-in-Class Uptime

    We deliver high quality power for maximum uptime, seamlessly managing load variability and keeping operations running.
