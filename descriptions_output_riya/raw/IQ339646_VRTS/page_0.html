Warning around company imposters

The investment management industry, including Virtus, has seen an increase in reports of fake websites and social media accounts impersonating firms and staff. These fraudsters use firm brands or names of representatives to try to scam and defraud others. The fraudsters impersonating Virtus or one of its investment managers are not authorized by Virtus and Virtus is not associated with accounts on social media and similar applications that request direct investments. Neither Virtus nor its investment managers offer direct investment opportunities to individuals through any social media platform. Please be vigilant if you are contacted by someone you don’t know offering you an investment opportunity.

You should always use caution if you receive an unsolicited investment proposal, requests for personal information or money transfers, or other solicitations. Virtus reserves the right to take all steps to protect its legal rights and reputation.

Please contact us if you suspect someone is impersonating our firm

If you have been contacted by a suspected fraudulent account, please report it to the social media platform, local authorities, and Virtus Customer Support.

If you are the victim of a cyber related crime, you can file a complaint on the FBI’s Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) website.

More information on imposter investment scams can be found online at the SEC’s Investor.gov website.
