Listed on the New York Stock Exchange and headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, Chemed Corporation operates two wholly owned subsidiaries: VITAS Healthcare Corporation and Roto-Rooter. VITAS is one of the nation's largest providers of end-of-life hospice care and Roto-Rooter is the nation's leading provider of plumbing, drain cleaning and water cleanup services.
