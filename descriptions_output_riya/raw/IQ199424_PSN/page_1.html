About Us

Life At Parsons

Founded in 1944, Parsons Corporation, a digitally enabled solutions provider, is focused on creating the future of the defense, intelligence, and critical infrastructure markets. From Earth to outer space, we deliver tomorrow’s solutions today. Equipped with the capabilities required to take on any defense, intelligence, or critical infrastructure challenge, our agile, innovative, and disruptive approach enables us to deliver solutions at the speed of relevance.

Our people are our greatest asset. We strive to be an employer of choice that engages employees in the community and creates rewarding career paths to cultivate a resilient workforce that is ready for the future.

Parsons Gives Back

Through our employee-driven program, we serve the communities in which we live, work, and play. Through charitable contributions and volunteerism our employees assist improving quality of life while making the world a better place daily.

LEARN MORE | GALLERY

Our Parsons Gives Back program is all about giving back to our communities. From exciting fundraisers to community clean-ups, we're committed to making a positive impact year-round.

Each December, Parsons Fellows donate to Toys For Tots bringing some extra holiday magic to those who need it most.

We have raised nearly $1.3 million for TAPS (Tragedy Assistance Program for Survivors) since 2007.

Regardless Of Your View, You're Always Parsons

Flexible work options (FWOs), including remote working on a permanent or hybrid basis, help us drive productivity, expand our talent pools, and improve sustainability. They enable us to deliver on our mission and vision in new ways.  We formally made FWOs our standard operating procedure in the United States and Canada and support flexibility where the nature of work, customer requirements, and corporate regulations and compliance allow for such considerations.
