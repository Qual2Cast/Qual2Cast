About UsIR Information

Creating an environment where medical institutions can focus on care

As a Medical Services Organization (MSO), SBC Medical Group Holdings Incorporated provides wide-ranging support for the growth of medical institutions, spanning management strategy, marketing, DX, and talent development. Our clinic network reaches more than 250 locations in Japan and overseas, and in 2024 we listed on NASDAQ. We continue to move toward our goal of becoming the most chosen medical group in the world.
