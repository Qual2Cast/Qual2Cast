How we operate

Well Lifecycle Solutions

Flowco provides end-to-end solutions to optimize your well's performance. Our solutions enhance production, sustainability, and long-term profitability. With an integrated approach, we enable you to maximize the value of your investment.

Initial Production Phase

Flowco's High Pressure Gas Lift (HPGL) systems are deployed immediately following well completion. Relying exclusively on surface equipment, HPGL allows the operator to maximize production and minimize downtime, ensuring optimized production during the most critical period.

Mid-Life Optimization

Following the initial phase of high-rate production, Flowco's Conventional Gas Lift systems take over, using specialized equipment on the surface and downhole to maintain production efficiency and maximize resource recovery.

Late-Stage Solutions

As the well matures, Flowco's Plunger Lift systems utilize the well's natural energy to continue oil and gas production, extending economic lifespan through cost-effective methods.

Methane Abatement & Digital Integration

Throughout the well lifecycle, Flowco's Vapor Recovery, methane abatement, and combined proprietary digital technologies work together to reduce emissions and support efficient, sustainable operations.
