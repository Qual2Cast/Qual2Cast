Production Solutions

Flowco Production Solutions provides life-of-the-well services that enhance oil and gas production. Optimize your wells with expert solutions.
Flowco Production Solutions

Life-of-the-Well Optimization

Optimizing oil and gas production is complex. It requires expertise, innovation, and a constant focus on reliability. At Flowco, we deliver solutions that support every stage of the well’s lifecycle—helping operators achieve peak performance and long-term value.

ESP & HPS Solutions

Electric Submersible Pump (ESP) systems provide powerful, downhole artificial lift for high-volume production, while Horizontal Pumping Systems (HPS) deliver reliable surface fluid transfer for applications like injection, disposal, and boosting. Together, these technologies integrate downhole and surface performance to support efficient, sustained operations. Designed and engineered to meet each well’s specific demands, they deliver consistent, dependable results in the field.

Learn more about HPS ->

Gas Lift Solutions

High Pressure Gas Lift (HPGL) accelerates production during the early stages of a well and is often followed by Conventional Gas Lift, integrating surface and downhole technologies for sustained performance. Flowco designs, installs, and maintains lift systems tailored to each well’s demands—delivering consistent, reliable results.
Learn more about HPGL->
Learn more about Conventional Gas Lift ->

Plunger Lift Solutions

Plunger lift optimizes oil and gas production by harnessing the well’s natural energy to unload liquids and sustain output. Flowco designs each plunger lift system to match the unique demands of your operation. With advanced automation and real-time monitoring, our technology improves efficiency, reliability, and performance throughout the life of the well.
Learn more about Plunger Lift Solutions ->

Digital Solutions

Flowco’s proprietary digital technologies give operators real-time insight to make informed, timely decisions. Our downhole gauges are built for extreme conditions, delivering accurate data from the wellbore to the surface. Integrated remote monitoring and automation systems connect gauges, devices, and controls to enhance visibility and operational efficiency.

Methane Abatement Technologies

Flowco’s proprietary methane abatement technologies help producers reduce fugitive emissions, capture valuable gas, and achieve their decarbonization goals.
