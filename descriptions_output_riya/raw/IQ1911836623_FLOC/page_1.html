About Flowco

We Provide the Right Solution, Every Time.

Flowco is a pure-play leader in production optimization, artificial lift, methane abatement, and vapor recovery for the oil and gas industry. Our focus is partnering with E&P operators to maximize production performance, cash flow, and well longevity from first production through late life.

Flowco focuses on the production phase of the well lifecycle, providing engineered systems and services that support reliable, long-term asset performance.

Built for the Production Phase

The production phase represents the longest and most valuable portion of a well’s life. As reservoir pressure declines, sustaining performance requires engineered solutions rather than assumed flow.

Flowco delivers technologies and services designed to support producing assets over decades. Its solutions are embedded in non-discretionary operating budgets and remain independent of short-term drilling activity. Customers are supported through durable equipment, responsive service, and systems designed to perform when uptime is critical.

Trusted by supermajors and leading independents, Flowco operates 4,300+ active systems across every major onshore U.S. basin.
Our Excellence

Integrated Solutions Across the Life of the Well

Flowco delivers a fully integrated platform across two business segments, Production Solutions and Natural Gas Technologies. Together, these segments support the full lifecycle of a producing well and enable seamless transitions as operating conditions change.

Production Solutions

Flowco designs and deploys artificial lift and production optimization systems that evolve alongside the well. Multiple Flowco technologies can operate simultaneously and transition over time to support changing reservoir conditions and production objectives.
01

High-Pressure Gas Lift (HPGL)

HPGL is a surface-controlled artificial lift technology with no downhole moving parts, engineered to lift from end of tubing from day one when rates are highest and the column is heaviest. It handles sand, high GOR, and deviated wellbores, stays serviceable at surface, and carries the same completion from initial production into decline.
Learn more
02

Electronic Submersible Pump (ESP)

ESP is a downhole artificial lift system that uses an electric motor and multistage centrifugal pump to move large volumes of fluid to the surface. Designed for high-rate production, ESPs provide the lifting capacity and drawdown needed for deep, deviated, and horizontal wells.
Learn more
03

Conventional Gas Lift

Fully integrated surface and downhole gas lift systems that are continuously optimized as well conditions change.
Learn more
04

Plunger Lift

Late-life artificial lift solutions designed to improve efficiency and recovery, enhanced through automation and hybrid configurations.

Automation & Digital Solutions

Methane Management as an Operating Advantage

Flowco’s methane abatement technologies enable operators to address emissions while improving operational efficiency. By capturing and repurposing fugitive methane, these solutions support compliance with evolving regulatory requirements and create measurable operational value.

Why Operators Work with Flowco

Flowco’s platform is vertically integrated and domestically manufactured to support long service life, high mechanical availability, and rapid response. Equipment is supported by field service teams located near customer operations and reinforced by digital capabilities that provide real-time visibility and predictive insight.

The combination of engineered hardware, operational support, and digital intelligence enables Flowco to deliver consistent performance in demanding production environments.

Built for the Journey
