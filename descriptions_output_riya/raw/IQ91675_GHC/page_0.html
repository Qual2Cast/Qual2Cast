Investor Overview

COMPANY PROFILE

Graham Holdings Company (NYSE: GHC) is a diversified holding company whose principal operations include educational services; television broadcasting; healthcare services; manufacturing; automotive; retail; media, e-commerce and digital services; restaurants; and custom framing.

Investment Strategy

We invest in companies with demonstrated earnings potential and strong management teams attracted to our long-term investment horizon. We are a diverse group of businesses sharing common goals and values but each with its own identity and workplace culture, and with management responsible for its operations.
