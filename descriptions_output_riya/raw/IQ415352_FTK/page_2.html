Energy Chemistry

PRESCRIPTIVE CHEMISTRY MANAGEMENT® (PCM™)

Every reservoir is unique, and even within the same reservoir, characteristics vary from one well to the next—from geology and hydrocarbon composition to the well trajectory and completion techniques.

Let us collaborate with you, to Enable and Protect your Reservoir™

Geologic Reservoir Analysis

Flotek collaborates closely with our customers to thoroughly assess the intricate geological characteristics of their reservoirs. This comprehensive evaluation is founded on extensive mapping, reservoir sampling, completion strategy analysis, predictions of decline curves, as well as considerations of wellbore depth and trajectory, alongside downhole temperature data. Our dedication to identifying and meticulously analyzing this dataset in partnership with our customers allows us to formulate our chemistry with pinpoint precision, which is designed to improve well performance and enhance recovery rates.

Prescriptive Design and Fluid Application

Upon the successful completion of a comprehensive geologic reservoir analysis, Flotek’s Global Research and Innovation Team, comprised of dedicated scientists and engineers, embarks on a series of rigorous tests and experiments. Their goal is to seamlessly integrate the oil and gas data contributed by our customer and information form public data sources with the insights garnered from the geologic reservoir analysis. This fusion of knowledge leads to the formulation of a customized total fluids solution for the customer.

At the heart of this offering stands our pioneering Complex Nano-Fluid® stimulation technologies, which have a demonstrated ability to expedite the extraction of oil and gas from the reservoir more efficiently than conventional methods. Complementing this core technology, is our comprehensive suite of total fluid solutions, which includes traditional friction reducers and conventional stimulation additives. Together, these innovations empower us to provide a holistic approach to optimize oil and gas production.

Total Fluid System & Performance Analysis

During this final phase, Flotek implements your tailored prescriptive design, which can be delivered directly to your wellsite for cost optimization or through your preferred service provider. Through an ongoing and adaptable evaluation process, we seek to ensure the ideal utilization of our comprehensive fluid system. This rigorous analysis empowers Flotek to not only apply our knowledge to future completions but also to pinpoint opportunities for predictive learning, further enhancing our ability to provide innovative solutions.

SPECIALTIES

Drilling & Cementing

Flotek is a leading innovator in the realm of specialty polymers and surfactants, dedicated to engineering compounds that excel in overcoming the formidable challenges encountered in diverse industries. Our extensive range of high-performance additives is carefully crafted to elevate key functions, including rheology, suspension, fluids loss control, hole cleaning and other critical aspects in both water- and oil-based drilling fluids.

Furthermore, Flotek empowers you to tailor the chemistry and performance of cement to precisely match the unique demands of each well. This customizable cementing solution is designed to achieve optimal results, addressing the distinctive challenges posed by every well configuration.
