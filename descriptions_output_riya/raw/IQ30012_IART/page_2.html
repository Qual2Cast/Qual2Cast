Integra LifeSciences’ BioSkills Lab: Hands-On Surgical Training

Located in Plainsboro, New Jersey, the Integra LifeSciences BioSkills Lab is a 4,000-square-foot, state-of-the-art training facility where hands-on education, clinical expertise, and medical innovation come together to advance surgical care.

Designed to support healthcare professionals, the lab provides immersive, procedure-based training using Integra's broad portfolio of technologies across key clinical areas including surgical, ENT, neurologic, and regenerative medicine. Within a realistic surgical environment, participants can refine techniques, explore complex procedures, and gain practical experience that strengthens clinical knowledge and procedural confidence.

By combining advanced training capabilities with real-world clinical scenarios, the BioSkills Lab helps bridge the gap between innovation and practice, enabling clinicians to translate new skills into everyday patient care.
