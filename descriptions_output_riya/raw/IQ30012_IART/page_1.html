Integra Lifesciences

Who We Are

Founded in 1989, Integra LifeSciences is a global medical technology leader dedicated to restoring lives. We are advancing transformational care through impactful innovation in neurosurgery and tissue reconstruction, specialized fields that demand exceptional expertise and precision. Our portfolio of highly differentiated gold-standard products and solutions is built on decades of clinical evidence and trusted by healthcare professionals to improve patient outcomes. 


OUR HISTORY

1989

Dr. Richard Caruso founded Integra LifeSciences and the company acquired collagen technology.

1996

Integra® Dermal Regeneration Template approved by the FDA and is engineered to promote the regeneration of dermal tissue.

1999

Leveraging its tissue regeneration technology, Integra received FDA clearance of DuraGen® Dural Graft Matrix for use as a dura substitute for the repair of the dura mater.

2001

NeuraGen® Nerve Guide is FDA cleared and provides a protective environment across nerve discontinuities.

2003

Integra acquired Jarit® Surgical Instruments and enters the specialty surgical instruments market.

2015

Integra acquired SurgiMend® Collagen Matrix for Soft Tissue Reconstruction with a broad range of applications from hernia repair to plastic and reconstructive surgery.

2017

Acquired the Codman® Neurosurgery business and became the global leader of neurosurgical products.

2021

Acquired MicroMatrix® UBM Particulate for acute and chronic wounds, and Gentrix® Surgical Matrix for soft tissue repair.

2022

Acquired DuraSorb® Monofilament Mesh, a bioabsorbable matrix for the reinforcement of soft tissue.

2024

Acquired Acclarent, Inc., a pioneer in ear, nose and throat (ENT) surgical interventions.
