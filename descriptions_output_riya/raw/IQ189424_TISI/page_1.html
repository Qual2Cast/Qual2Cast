Rope Access, Fabrication, Turnaround Support and Engineered Line Lifts.

Home | Advanced Asset and Project Solutions

Where Complexity Meets Capability

When access is limited, conditions are extreme, or timelines are tight, Advanced Asset and Project Solutions brings the tools, people, and planning to get it done right. TEAM provides industrial project support services for complex maintenance, access, fabrication, turnaround, and asset integrity scopes. Our capabilities include rope access, custom manufacturing, engineered line lifts, shutdown execution, and maintenance planning to help clients address difficult access, schedule constraints, and critical asset needs.

Support That Strengthens the Whole

As part of TEAM360, Advanced Asset and Project Solutions fill the critical gaps between inspection, detection, repair, and heat treatment. Whether it’s building a specialty part, accessing a confined space, or coordinating an outage, these solutions ensure every other service lands cleaner, faster, and more efficiently. It’s that integration layer that makes everything work smoother. Whatever unexpected need your project or asset presents, we have a solution for that.
