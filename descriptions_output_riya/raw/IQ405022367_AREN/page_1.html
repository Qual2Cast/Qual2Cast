How do we get started?

Send a note to hello@cutterstudios.ai, or use the contact form. Tell us what you shoot and how often, and we'll follow up from there.

Can I use Cutter on its own?

Yes. It's a standalone product — plenty of people use nothing else. If you already run the Paradium stack it plugs into the rest of it, but that's an option, not a requirement.

What can I upload?

A link from YouTube, Vimeo or most podcast hosts, or an .mp4 straight off your drive. Interviews, streams, panels, podcasts, reviews — anything long.

Who owns what comes out?

You do, with a commercial licence. Nothing publishes anywhere until you say so.

How long does a run take?

Minutes for a typical upload, and it runs unattended — the clips are waiting next time you open the queue.
