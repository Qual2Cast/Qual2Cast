About Transcat

Transcat is proud to be a single-source provider of calibration services, test and measurement equipment, and other specialized services. Our goal is to ensure compliance and efficiency for our customers across highly technical industries while building strong relationships through our core values of listening, learning, acting, and helping. With Transcat, you’ll have trust in every measure and the confidence to focus on what matters – your business.

The Transcat Story

Founded in 1964, Transcat started as a manufacturer of industrial-grade calibration tools. Over time we transitioned into a distributor of test and measurement equipment before opening our first calibration lab in Rochester, NY. We’ve since grown to become a single-source provider of technical and professional services, a value-added distributor of high-quality test and measurement equipment, and a full-scale rental equipment company,  among other specialties.
