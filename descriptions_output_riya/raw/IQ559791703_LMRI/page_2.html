We Provide High Quality, Subspecialized Reads—Fast.

Our goal is to help you deliver timely, effective patient care with high-quality reports for optimal treatment planning.

Our Services

As part of Lumexa Imaging, Connexia utilizes established technology, workflows, customer support, and clinical expertise to deliver the results that referring providers and patients need.

Subspecialized reads across body, neuro, and musculoskeletal

Fast turnaround times to optimize care delivery, with STAT workflow capability

Proven IT infrastructure, platforms, and support

Expert clinical operations team ensures smooth end-to-end workflows

Robust quality infrastructure including peer learning and case review

Our Culture Is One of Connection—Among Each Other and to Our Customers.

We collaborate and communicate as a team to provide an excellent experience to our partners.

"Joining Connexia has been a wonderful fit, allowing me to thrive both professionally and personally. The practice enables radiologists to perform high-quality, subspecialty-focused radiology in an efficient and supportive setting. The flexible nature of a career with Connexia also allows me to maximize the time I spend with family and pursue personal interests outside of work. I cannot imagine a better work-life balance, and I am thrilled to be part of Connexia as we continue to grow and shape the future of the practice."
– Dr. Garth Brandal

I continue to be blown away by how committed, approachable, kind, and helpful everyone is. Even though I work remotely, I never feel alone, and I always feel satisfied with my workday.”
– Dr. Nadine Zeidan
