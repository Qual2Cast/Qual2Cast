Supporting communities
that need care most

As America's largest healthcare educator, we're addressing the healthcare workforce crisis at every career stage—from exploration through professional practice—enabling individuals to pursue their goals, navigate financial barriers and thrive in demanding roles that serve our communities.

Opening doors at every career stage

America faces a healthcare workforce crisis that threatens access to care for millions. Covista Open Doors is our multi-year impact commitment to build and sustain the healthcare workforce in the communities that need it most. It is advanced through Covista's strategic investments—corporate giving, employee match, nonprofit partnerships and grant cycles—alongside volunteerism and advocacy, and the Covista Foundation, an independent 501(c)(3) public charity that provides grants to nonprofits, scholarships and emergency financial support to the future healthcare workforce.

Our focus is simple: inspire young people to pursue healthcare, remove the barriers standing in students' way of graduating and support the mental health and wellbeing of healthcare workers. These three priorities—discover, achieve and thrive—guide every investment we make across the entire healthcare career continuum.

Discover

We inspire and support individuals to discover and pursue healthcare careers.

Opening doors for the next generation by creating pathways for underserved individuals to envision themselves in healthcare through hands-on experiences, mentorship and career exploration programs. 

Achieve

We break down financial barriers to help students achieve their healthcare ambitions.

Opening doors to degree completion for students facing financial barriers, through scholarship and wrap around services.

Thrive

We support the mental health and wellbeing of healthcare workers so they can thrive.

Opening doors to sustainable careers by partnering with healthcare organizations to address burnout, support mental health and keep talented professionals in the workforce.

"We remove barriers for students, support the wellbeing of healthcare workers, and inspire the next generation to serve their communities. That's our commitment."

Megan Noel
Chief Corporate Affairs Officer

Our commitment to sustainability

Building a resilient healthcare workforce requires sustainable practices across the enterprise, in our institutions and in our communities. We're committed to environmental stewardship, responsible operations and supporting both people and the planet as we work to address the healthcare workforce crisis.

Partnerships that open doors and transform lives

Dr. Lorna Breen Foundation supports the mental health and wellbeing of healthcare workers. Through evidence-based, system-change interventions, they help healthcare workers thrive in their demanding roles and continue serving communities. As the founding corporate partner of their national ALL IN: Caring for Caregivers  cohort, we're helping health systems remove structural barriers to mental health care and embed sustainable workforce wellbeing into healthcare operations.

NAF inspires young people to discover healthcare careers through hands-on experiences, industry-focused curriculum and connections to industry professionals. Our partnership expands the pipeline of future healthcare professionals by creating clear pathways for students from communities that need them most.

The Schwartz Center for Compassionate Healthcare partners with healthcare organizations to advance compassion for patients, care teams, and their healing relationships. Our investment expands access to proven programs, including Schwartz Rounds® and Stress First Aid, that reduce psychological distress in care teams, foster cultures of compassion, and increase empathetic care for patients – all of which drive improved health outcomes.

"NAF is excited to partner with Covista to help expose high school students to futures in healthcare. By bringing together NAF’s expertise in career-connected learning with Covista’s leadership in healthcare education, this partnership will create meaningful, real-world experiences that prepare students for in-demand careers and strengthen the future healthcare workforce."

Lisa Dughi
Chief Executive Officer, NAF

"We're proud to partner with Covista to bring the Schwartz Rounds program to five hospitals where their alumni serve. This investment reflects something we deeply believe: that compassionate care for patients starts with compassionate care for the people delivering it. Thank you, Covista, for championing the healthcare workers who hold the system together."

The Schwartz Center for Compassionate Healthcare

Where colleagues become community partners

Community Rounds is Covista's year-round volunteer program that connects colleagues to meaningful opportunities to strengthen communities, remove barriers for students and support future healthcare workers. From mentoring and assembling care kits for high schoolers to organizing medical supplies for disaster response, colleagues donate their time to expand access to healthcare careers and build a stronger, more resilient healthcare workforce for the future.
