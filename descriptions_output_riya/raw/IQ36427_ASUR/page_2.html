Why It Matters

Payroll tax filing requires specialized operational expertise

Payroll tax filing involves more than submitting reports to tax authorities. Organizations must manage filing requirements across federal, state, and local jurisdictions while maintaining accurate reporting records over time. Errors, missed deadlines, or unresolved agency notices can expose organizations to penalties, interest, and unnecessary operational disruption.

Payroll tax filing also requires specialized knowledge of filing requirements, agency processes, and jurisdictional rules. Asure Payroll Tax Management captures that expertise within the system itself, helping organizations manage filing requirements, track agency interactions, and maintain accurate records across jurisdictions without relying solely on scarce payroll tax specialists.

Bulk Filing Infrastructure

Infrastructure built for high-volume payroll tax filing

Asure operates as a bulk filer with the IRS and other tax authorities, submitting payroll tax filings at scale on behalf of organizations and their employees. Bulk filing allows tax submissions to move through the same high-volume processing channels used by major payroll providers, helping ensure filings are accepted, processed efficiently, and tracked consistently across jurisdictions.

The same infrastructure supports payroll operations serving more than 100,000 businesses and over 2 million employees. Organizations benefit from systems designed for scale, accuracy, and consistent interaction with tax authorities.

Because the infrastructure is purpose-built for payroll tax filing, organizations gain clearer operational visibility into filing activity, agency correspondence, and reporting records across jurisdictions.

What Payroll Tax Management helps you do

Manage payroll tax filing across jurisdictions

Coordinate payroll tax filing across federal, state, and local tax authorities with infrastructure designed for high-volume, multi-jurisdiction payroll environments.

Track and resolve agency notices

Maintain visibility into agency correspondence, track the history of notices and responses, and confirm when filing issues have been resolved.

Maintain reliable reporting records

Keep payroll tax reporting records organized and accessible so payroll and finance teams can support internal review, agency responses, and audit readiness.

Integrations

Works with your payroll systems

Asure Payroll Tax Management works alongside the payroll systems organizations already use.

This includes enterprise payroll platforms such as Workday, Oracle, and SAP, as well as payroll service bureau platforms and other payroll environments. Organizations can add specialized payroll tax filing infrastructure without replacing the payroll technology already running payroll.

Operational Flexibility

Flexible ways to operate

Organizations manage payroll tax filing differently depending on their internal resources and expertise.

Some maintain internal teams responsible for filing and agency correspondence. Others rely on specialized tax professionals to manage filing operations and notice resolution. As payroll tax expertise becomes harder to replace, many organizations choose a hybrid approach that combines internal oversight with external expertise.

Asure supports both approaches. Organizations can use the infrastructure directly or rely on experienced tax specialists to help manage payroll tax filing and agency interactions.
