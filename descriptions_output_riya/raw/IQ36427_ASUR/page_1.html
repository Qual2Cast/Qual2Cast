Who We Are

HCM Through the Lens of Entrepreneurs

Asure sees Human Capital Management through the lens of entrepreneurs and executives with an owner’s mentality. We understand that business owners need to build great teams—but must also allocate financial capital in areas that drive growth, not bloated back-office functions.

With headquarters in Austin, TX and offices throughout the United States, Asure provides HCM software and services in every state and territory. We build great HCM software that’s delivered via the cloud on a subscription basis—so it doesn’t require any IT overhead or a large capital purchase.

Experienced Leaders Driving Growth

Asure’s leadership team brings decades of experience in human capital management, technology, and building solutions that help businesses grow.

Pat Goepel

Eyal Goldstein

President and Chief Revenue Officer

Eyal Goldstein leads revenue strategy, go-to-market execution, and key operating functions across Asure’s growth-focused business lines. He brings deep experience building high-performing organizations and driving growth in competitive markets.

John Pence

Chief Financial Officer

Brings 20+ years of experience in HCM technology and payroll tax systems, with prior leadership roles at ADP and OneSource Virtual.
