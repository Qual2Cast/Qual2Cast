Innodata Federal is a new business unit of Innodata Inc. (NASDAQ: INOD) dedicated to helping government agencies harness the power of artificial intelligence.

We bring proven commercial AI solutions trusted by the world’s leading technology companies to U.S. government missions. Our teams support defense, intelligence, and civilian operations with security clearances, compliance readiness, and enterprise-scale delivery.

Full-Spectrum AI Solutions for Government Missions​

Proven Commercial Solutions, Purpose-Built for Federal Missions

AI Data Engineering & Model Training

High-accuracy data collection, annotation, and curation for computer vision, NLP, and multimodal AI systems. Supports imagery analysis, autonomous systems, sensor fusion, and more.

Generative AI Solutions

End-to-end support for LLM development and deployment, including supervised fine-tuning, RLHF, reward modeling, prompt engineering, RAG development, and more.

Agentic AI & Intelligent Automation

AI copilots and autonomous agents that streamline government workflows—from benefits adjudication to intelligence triage—with human oversight and compliance.

Model Safety, Evaluation & Red Teaming

Comprehensive testing, evaluation, and adversarial red teaming to identify vulnerabilities, ensure robustness, and verify compliance with federal AI ethics frameworks.

Procurement-Ready with Federal Compliance

Certified, registered, and ready to deliver through federal acquisition channels.
