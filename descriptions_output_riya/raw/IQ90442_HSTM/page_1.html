Healthcare’s people platform™

We develop and empower people to deliver the highest quality of care through our suite of  learning, clinical development, credentialing, and scheduling applications.

5,000+ Healthcare Customers
5.5M+ Healthcare Professionals
75+ World-class Partners
35 Years in Healthcare
1,000+ Employees
Based in Nashville, TN

We are the industry leader in healthcare workforce solutions

We are the #1 advisor for developing and empowering people to deliver the highest quality of care, working side-by-side with 5,000+ healthcare organizations for over 30 years to cultivate a more competent and engaged workforce.

Award-winning software & culture

We leverage cutting-edge technology and data-driven insights to develop solutions that streamline everyday tasks and empower healthcare organizations to achieve next-level results.

Recognized among the best in healthcare software

HealthStream was again named to G2’s 2026 Best Healthcare Software list—earning more Top 50 placements than any other vendor, with our Learning Center, SafetyQ®/ComplyQ®, and Jane® recognized among the industry’s leading solutions. This recognition reflects the strength of our connected workforce platform and solutions purpose-built for healthcare—supporting learning, safety, competency development, and workforce readiness across the enterprise.

HITRUST Certification Achieved for CredentialStream

This milestone showcases our compliance with the highest standards of information security and risk management in healthcare.This certification extends to all of the following: CredentialStream®, Network by HealthStream, CredentialStream Hub, Corporate enterprise systems

Winner of Over 30 Brandon Hall Excellence in Technology Awards

With over 30 Brandon Hall Excellence in Technology Awards, we continue to set the standard for innovation in healthcare workforce development. These honors highlight HealthStream's relentless drive to push boundaries in technology, all with the ultimate goal of improving healthcare outcomes.

Commitment to Customer
Satisfaction

We are proud to have received over 40 G2 and Capterra badges, a testament to our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction. These accolades reflect the positive impact of our solutions in transforming the healthcare landscape.

The Computerworld Smithsonian

We are a company rooted in innovation which drives our culture forward. The Smithsonian presented HealthStream with the Computerworld Smithsonian Medallion in recognition of its achievement to innovatively use technology to empower and advance society.

Best Companies to Work For: Private
Companies

We are proud to be recognized by U.S. News & World Report in their inaugural list of Best Companies to Work For: Private Companies.
This award highlights our commitment to creating a supportive and rewarding work environment, with competitive pay, work-life balance, career growth, and a strong sense of belonging. At HealthStream, we believe that by empowering our employees, we can continue driving innovation in healthcare workforce development and improving healthcare outcomes.

Patented technology

Our commitment to innovation is unmatched. HealthStream has received multiple technology patents such as Jane and Privilege with CredentialStream. These advancements reiterate our unwavering commitment to transforming the healthcare industry.

Industry-leading partnerships

Our partnerships with renowned healthcare associations, leading academic institutions, and respected thought leaders allow us to deliver innovative solutions that address the ever-changing needs of the healthcare industry.

Chief Executive Officer

Michael Collier

Chief Operating Officer & Executive Vice President

Michael M. Collier has been promoted to Chief Operating Officer (COO) & Executive Vice President (EVP) at HealthStream. He has served on HealthStream’s executive team since 2017 after joining the Company in August 2011. In his role as COO & EVP, Michael is now responsible for day-to-day operations throughout HealthStream—with executive oversight of the Company’s corporate development program (M&A), legal, human resources, business enablement, enterprise partnerships, success management, implementations, and onboarding departments as well as enterprise application operations—including Credentialing and Scheduling, among others. Prior to coming to HealthStream, he served as a corporate & securities attorney at Bass, Berry & Sims. Trisha L. Coady

Executive Vice President, Workforce Development Solutions

OUR VISION

To improve the quality of healthcare by developing the people who deliver care.

OUR VALUES

Our values inspire and guide the behaviors of all HealthStream employees within our organization, with our customers, and in our communities.

Behaving with Integrity

Honesty, trustworthiness, and mutual respect are the guiding principles for our behavior and every interaction at HealthStream

Delighting Customers

Our culture is customer-centric, where we strive to provide strong, positive experiences for our customers, often exceeding their expectations

Driving Innovation

We value ingenuity, creativity, freshness, originality, and entrepreneurship at every level of our work, from major initiatives to everyday tasks

Continuously Improving

We strive to constantly improve the quality of our products and solutions as our commitment to learn and improve extends to our  customer relationships

Delivering Meaningful Outcomes

We are focused on producing meaningful outcomes through our individual, everyday work responsibilities and, on a broader level, through our products and solutions provided to the healthcare industry

Streaming Good

We believe every employee has the responsibility to enact good works for others, starting from their individual team participation and office and then extending to the communities where they work and the healthcare industry at large

Careers at HealthStream

HealthStream employees, or HealthStreamers, are united through our company's vision statement to "improve the quality of healthcare by developing the people that deliver care." We take pride in bringing the vision to life as we work collectively to achieve business objectives and strive personally to grow as individuals
