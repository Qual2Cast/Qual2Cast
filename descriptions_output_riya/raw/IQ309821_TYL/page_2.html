Always Active

These technologies enable core functionality such as security, network management, account authentication, and accessibility. Without these technologies, services you have requested cannot be provided. Because these cookies are required for the site to function, they cannot be disabled.

Functional Technologies

Functional technologies allow our site to remember choices you make and provide enhanced, personalized features. These technologies may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages.

Examples of use: Remembering your language or region preferences, recalling your username for faster login, and customizing content based on your prior interactions with the site.

Performance technologies help us understand how visitors interact with our site by collecting information about page visits, traffic sources, and user behavior. All information collected by these technologies is aggregated and used to improve site functionality and user experience.

Examples of use: Counting visitors and traffic sources to measure and improve site performance, identifying which pages are most and least popular, and detecting errors or slow-loading pages.

Targeting Technologies

Targeting technologies are used to deliver advertisements that are relevant to you and your interests. These technologies may be set by us or by our advertising partners and are used to build a profile based on your browsing activity.
