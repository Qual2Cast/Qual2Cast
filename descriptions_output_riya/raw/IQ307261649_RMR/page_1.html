Who we are

RMR is a leading U.S. real estate investment manager with more than four decades of experience creating value across commercial real estate, private capital and credit strategies.

Our roots run deep in real estate, but our vision is forward-looking. Today, RMR manages a diverse platform of publicly traded REITs, a growing private capital business anchored by multifamily and commercial real estate lending and a nationwide operating infrastructure that gives us reach, insight and speed few can match.

Our mission: Create long-term value for our clients by managing their investments and assets with an ownership mindset. Our hands-on approach drives performance, unlocks opportunity and builds trust - empowering employees, investors and stakeholders to thrive.

Our vision: Leadership is earned through performance, discipline and foresight. We've proven our position as a trusted manager and aim to build on this foundation by expanding our reach, deepening expertise and unlocking opportunities for long-term value creation.

What we offer

Our products

Powered by a vertically integrated platform and deep operational foundation, we identify opportunity early, act with conviction and deliver through market cycles. With reach across both public and private markets and expertise spanning all major real estate sectors, we transform potential into measurable outcomes.

Through our family of publicly traded REITs, we provide access to diversified commercial real estate strategies across office, retail, senior living and healthcare and net lease sectors. Our REITs combine long-term ownership, disciplined capital allocation and operational excellence supported by our national platform.

  • Diversified Healthcare Trust | ~$6.8B AUM - Senior living, life sciences and medical office properties across 33 states and Washington, D.C. (Nasdaq: DHC).
  • Industrial Logistics Properties Trust | $2.6B AUM - Industrial and logistics assets located across 39 states, including strategic holdings in Oahu, Hawaii. (Nasdaq: ILPT).
  • Office Properties Income Trust | $5.4B AUM - Office and mixed-use properties, primarily leased to investment grade tenants. (Nasdaq: OPI).
  • Service Properties Trust | $9.9B AUM - Diversified portfolio of service-focused retail net lease properties and hotels in the U.S., Puerto Rico and Canada. (Nasdaq: SVC).
  • Seven Hills Realty Trust | $738M AUM - Originates and invests in first mortgage loans for transitional commercial real estate. (Nasdaq: SEVN).

RMR offers a growing suite of private investment strategies designed for today’s evolving real estate landscape, including:

  • Separate accounts: Customized solutions designed to align with investor-specific mandates, offering full transparency and control over asset selection, while benefiting from RMR's dedicated asset and management expertise and operational resources.
  • Joint ventures: Strategic partnerships offering access to RMR's best ideas, cross-sector expertise and vertically integrated platform demonstrated by a proven track record of value creation.
  • Traditional funds: Thematic, operator-led approach to investing, targeting the most compelling relative value and risk-adjusted opportunities across sectors and throughout the capital stack.


Our ability to structure capital creatively - equity, debt or hybrid - allows us to match each opportunity with the right strategy and partner.
