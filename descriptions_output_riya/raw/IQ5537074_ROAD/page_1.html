Our Company

Founded on Partnership

Founded in 2001, Construction Partners (Nasdaq: ROAD) was built on a clear and focused vision: to acquire and partner with locally led infrastructure companies that share a commitment to quality, safety, and family values. Together, this network of companies builds the transportation systems that connect people, strengthen economies, and support long-term growth across the regions we serve.

What We Do
Building Connections
Construction Partners is an asphalt-centered infrastructure company that builds, repairs, and maintains the surfaces that keep communities moving, including highways, local roads, interstate corridors, airport runways, bridges, and commercial sites. Across the fast-growing Sunbelt region, our locally led teams deliver dependable projects that improve connectivity, enhance safety, and support regional growth.

OUR Strategy
How We Create Value
A proven operating model built for execution and long-term value, strengthened through strong partnerships and a focus on building better markets.

Growth Strategy
PILLAR ONE
Local-Market Model

Locally led companies build trusted relationships with customers, suppliers, and communities. Each operates with regional autonomy while following shared standards that ensure quality, safety, and performance across every market.

PILLAR TWO
Vertical Integration

Ownership of key materials, including aggregate facilities, liquid asphalt terminals, and hot-mix asphalt plants, provides control over quality, scheduling, logistics, and cost. This integrated network delivers consistency, efficiency, and reliability from production through final paving.

PILLAR THREE
Balanced Markets

A diverse mix of highway, road, airport, and commercial projects keeps crews and facilities efficiently utilized across multiple regions in the nation’s fastest-growing states. This balance maintains steady workloads, strengthens market stability, and supports dependable long-term performance.

PILLAR FOUR
Growth Strategy

Disciplined organic growth, greenfield development, and strategic acquisitions expand capacity and capabilities while preserving cultural alignment and local leadership. This approach strengthens the network, creates opportunity, and drives sustainable growth over the long term.

Safety & Sustainability
Built on Safety
Safety and sustainability guide every part of the business. The well-being of employees always comes first, supported through training, planning, and a culture built around working safely. We invest in modern plants, dependable equipment, and recycling practices that improve efficiency, manage costs, and reduce environmental impact. By keeping these priorities at the core of our business, we strengthen performance, manage risk responsibly, and create long-term value in the communities we serve.
Safety first, always. We train, plan, and look out for one another so everyone goes home safely at the end of the day.
Investing in better tools. Modern plants, reliable equipment, and smart systems help us work efficiently and safely.
Respecting the environment. Recycling materials and improving our processes reduces waste and conserves resources.
Building for the long term. Every decision we make balances safety, sustainability, and value for the communities we serve.
