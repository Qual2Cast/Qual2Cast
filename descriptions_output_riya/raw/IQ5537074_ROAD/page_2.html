Shaping the Future of Transportation
Core Construction Services
Construction Partners builds and maintains transportation infrastructure in regions with consistent, long-term demand for investment. Partnering with state and federal agencies, municipalities, school districts, and military installations, we deliver essential public projects that support growth and provide stability through changing economic conditions. Our work with private developers and commercial property owners expands that foundation, adding balance and opportunity across some of the nation’s fastest-growing markets.

An Integrated Network
Materials & Supply Chain
Our vertically integrated network gives us control over the materials that define quality and performance. From aggregate facilities and liquid asphalt terminals to hot-mix asphalt plants, we manage each step of production to ensure consistency, reliability, and precision in every project. This integration strengthens schedules, stabilizes costs, and supports our teams in delivering durable, high-performing pavement across every market we serve.
Hot-Mix Asphalt
We supply materials for paving projects and external customers through a network of hot-mix asphalt plants. Controlling production allows us to manage costs, maintain quality, and deliver reliable schedules across every market we serve.
Aggregates
We produce and supply aggregates that form the foundation of every construction project. Operating our own facilities secures material availability, improves efficiency, and adds value through external sales.
Liquid Asphalt Terminals
We operate strategically located terminals that provide a steady supply of liquid asphalt to plants and projects. Managing this flow reduces risk, enhances efficiency, and ensures consistent performance across operations.
