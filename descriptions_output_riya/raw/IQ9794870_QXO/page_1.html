About QXO

Headquartered in Greenwich, CT, we specialize in helping contractors, homebuilders, distributors, and suppliers streamline operations, improve efficiency, and grow their businesses with confidence.

With deep roots in the building materials sector, we understand the fast-paced demands of a growing industry. Our technology supports every step of the supply chain—from product availability and inventory management to order tracking and customer service—so our clients can focus on what they do best: building and delivering quality results.

With a network of over 28,000 professionals, 1,150 locations across all 50 U.S. states and seven Canadian provinces, and a fleet of more than 10,000 vehicles across North America, QXO is deeply committed to providing our customers with the products and services they need to stay competitive. Through strategic investments in digital tools and a customer-first mindset, we ensure you have access to the right products and services, at the right time, with the right information—every time.
