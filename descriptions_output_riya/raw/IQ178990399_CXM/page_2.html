Frequently asked questions

A consumer intelligence platform (CIP) is a software that provides extensive insights on your customers, competitors and market. It does this by analyzing data from various sources like social, web, digital interactions, surveys, and even offline interactions. 

For example, Sprinklr Insights is an AI-native consumer intelligence platform that unifies data from 30+ digital and social channels, 400K+ media sources and over a billion websites, powering business and brand-critical insights on a single platform. 

Any team that wants to understand their market, customer, or competitors will benefit from a consumer intelligence platform. Here’s how leading brands benefit from Sprinklr Insights, our AI-native CIP:  

  1. Marketing: Segment, automate campaigns, and monitor 30+ channels. Dow Chemical identified 125K more valuable mentions using Sprinklr, gaining deeper insights into global markets.
  2. Support: Spot issues and improve SLAs. Moen cut complaint response times by 18× in a year. 
  3. Product: Prioritize features via consumer signals. Microsoft analyzed 8.6B mentions to double their project output. 
  4. Retention: Spot churn drivers and deliver proactive support. boAt tracked 10K+ touchpoints and validated 6K+ claims in real time. 
  5. Sales/Intel: Refine messaging via sentiment. Chick-fil-A relaunched a sauce after a 923% surge in mentions, flipping sentiment from 73% negative to 92% positive. 

When evaluating a customer intelligence platform, consider these five key factors:

  1. Data coverage: Look for broad integrations like social, CRM, surveys, product and location reviews, etc. For example, Sprinklr Insights supports four key consumer intelligence workflows: Voice of the Customer, Brand Monitoring, Crisis Management, and Competitor Insights.
  2. Identity resolution: Aim for unified profiles via integrations with CRMs, CDPs, and authentication workflows. Sprinklr offers OOTB audience reports, profile impersonation analysis, and LinkedIn/X (Twitter) audience studies.
  3. Analytics and AI: Seek verticalized AI, predictive models, anomaly detection, and real-time dashboards. Sprinklr delivers 90%+ accurate and contextual insights using AI.
  4. Activation: Look for workflow triggers, campaign tools, and API integrations to analyze and act from the same platform.
  5. Scalability and ROI: Look for enterprise-grade architecture and data security, modular pricing, and ROI dashboards.

AI is at the core of Sprinklr’s consumer intelligence platform. Sprinklr AI+ is an advanced AI offering that brings the power of Generative AI within Sprinklr. You can use Sprinklr AI+ to create content such as social media captions, post ideas, in-post copy, headlines, and even blogs and newsletters.

You can use Sprinklr AI+ to get always-on assistance from Copilots, automate workflows, and inform decision-making across marketing service, social, and insights.

Sprinklr Insights supports 30+ social and digital channels, 400K+ media sources, and 1B+ websites and review sites — along with the ability to ingest owned data. Here’s a deep dive:  

  • Social and media (owned, paid, and earned): X (formerly Twitter), Reddit, Instagram, VK, TikTok, Facebook, Twitch, Sina Weibo, Snapchat, Bluesky, YouTube 
  • Editorial sources: Web (news, blogs), print, TV, radio 
  • Forums and review sites: Online discussion boards, online feedback, Quora, Stack Exchange 
  • Other sources: Podcasts, videos, classifieds, WordPress 
  • FPDI or first-party data ingestion: Import messages and related custom data as an Excel file, and ingest and view the data in Sprinklr’s Social Listening module
