    Programmatic Advertising Solutions FAQ

    WHAT IS PROGRAMMATIC ADVERTISING?Expand

    Programmatic advertising refers to the automated buying and selling of digital ad inventory using technology and algorithms. It involves the use of demand-side platforms (DSPs) by advertisers to bid on and purchase ad impressions in real-time auctions, while supply-side platforms (SSPs) enable publishers to offer their ad inventory for sale. Programmatic advertising utilizes data, targeting options, and real-time optimization to deliver relevant ads to the right audience at the right time, making the ad buying process more efficient and effective. It allows for precise audience targeting, scalability, and improved campaign performance through automation and data-driven decision-making.

    WHAT IS TARGETED ADVERTISING AND HOW DOES IT COMPARE TO PROGRAMMATIC?Expand

    Targeted advertising refers to the practice of delivering ads to specific and relevant audiences based on people’s characteristics, behaviors, or preferences. It involves tailoring ad content and placement to reach a desired audience segment, increasing the relevance and effectiveness of the ads. Programmatic advertising, on the other hand, is a method of auction-based buying and selling ad inventory that utilizes automated technology and data-driven algorithms to optimize the ad buying process in real-time. While targeted advertising focuses on reaching specific audiences, programmatic advertising is a broader approach that leverages data, automation, and real-time bidding to deliver targeted ads efficiently and at scale. Programmatic advertising often incorporates targeted advertising techniques to maximize the precision and impact of ad campaigns.

    HOW DOES PROGRAMMATIC ADVERTISING WORK?Expand

    Programmatic advertising works through an automated process that involves various stakeholders and technologies. Advertisers use demand-side platforms (DSPs) to set campaign parameters, define target audiences, and determine bidding strategies. These DSPs then participate in real-time auctions (organized by Supply-side platforms (SSPs), where ad placements are bought and sold. SSPs enable publishers to make their ad inventory available for auction. When a user visits a website or app, placement becomes available and is instantly auctioned off among participating DSPs. The DSPs assess several data provided by the SSPs, including the audience data, such as demographics and browsing behavior, and place a bid on behalf of the advertiser. The highest bidding DSP wins the auction, and the applicable advertiser’s ad is instantly delivered to the user's device. This entire process takes place within milliseconds, allowing advertisers to deliver highly targeted ads in real-time to their desired audience.

    WHAT ARE EXAMPLES OF PROGRAMMATIC ADVERTISING?Expand

    There are several examples of programmatic advertising that demonstrate its diverse applications. For instance, a retail brand can leverage programmatic advertising to dynamically serve personalized product recommendations to individual users based on their browsing history or purchase behavior. A travel company can use programmatic advertising to target potential customers who have shown interest in vacation destinations, delivering tailored ads showcasing relevant travel deals. Additionally, a healthcare organization can utilize programmatic advertising to reach specific demographic groups with targeted messaging promoting preventive care services or health-related products. These examples highlight the versatility and effectiveness of programmatic advertising in delivering relevant and timely messages to the right audiences.

    WHAT ARE THE BENEFITS OF PROGRAMMATIC ADVERTISING?Expand

    Programmatic advertising offers several key benefits for advertisers. Firstly, it enables precise targeting, allowing advertisers to reach specific audiences based on demographics, interests, and behaviors. This results in more relevant and personalized ad experiences for users. Secondly, programmatic advertising offers real-time optimization, allowing advertisers to adjust their campaigns in real-time based on performance data, maximizing their return on investment. Additionally, programmatic advertising automates the ad buying process, increasing efficiency and reducing manual effort. It also provides comprehensive reporting and insights, allowing advertisers to track and measure the success of their campaigns. Overall, programmatic advertising offers increased targeting precision, efficiency, and data-driven decision-making, leading to more effective and impactful ad campaigns.

    WHAT’S THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN PROGRAMMATIC AND DISPLAY ADVERTISING?Expand

    Programmatic advertising and display advertising are related but distinct concepts. Display advertising refers to the practice of delivering visual ads, such as banner ads or rich media ads, on websites or mobile apps. It encompasses the creative aspects of designing and delivering visual advertisements to target audiences. Programmatic advertising, on the other hand, refers to the automated buying and selling of digital ad inventory using technology and algorithms. It focuses on the process of ad buying, utilizing data, targeting options, and real-time optimization to deliver ads efficiently and at scale. While programmatic advertising can include display advertising, it extends beyond it by incorporating other ad formats and channels, like video, mobile, audio, digital out-of-home and connected TV, and employing advanced targeting and automation techniques.
