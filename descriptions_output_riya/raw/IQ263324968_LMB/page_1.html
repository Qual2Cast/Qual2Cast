WHO WE ARE

Limbach Holdings, Inc. is a leading building systems solutions firm. We partner with building owners and facilities managers to revitalize and maintain mission-critical mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems—ensuring buildings are always ready to perform when it matters most.

At Limbach, we deliver solutions that support life’s most important moments. By providing reliable, high-performance building systems, we strive to be an indispensable partner to our customers.

OUR CORE VALUES.

CORE PURPOSE:

We deliver and maintain critical systems that support life’s most important moments.

OUR VISION:

To be an Indispensable Building Systems Solutions Partner, combining national reach with local presence to deliver proactive solutions for mission-critical facilities.

WE ACHIEVE THIS VISION BY:

  • National Building Systems Solutions Platform

    We are shaping a national presence where owners experience Limbach as a single, trusted provider, delivering consistent outcomes through an integrated, scalable platform.

  • High-Performance Building Systems Engineering

    We envision and deliver customer solutions that rival the sophistication, intelligence, and performance of leading OEM offerings.

  • Local Presence, National Advantage

    We build enduring national relationships through a connected ecosystem of branch locations and partners, operating as one unified network in service of our customers’ most complex needs.
