CHANGING THE GAME

Flutter is the world’s #1 online sports betting and gaming operator. We’ve shaken up the industry, and we’re just getting started. We’re on a mission: a mission to change the game.

What makes us different

We’re ambitious, we never stop pushing for better, and we back bold thinking. We welcome innovation from every part of the business, and we support each other’s success through the Flutter Edge. That means sharing the best product ideas, technology, and expertise, and using our scale to make good ideas go global.
