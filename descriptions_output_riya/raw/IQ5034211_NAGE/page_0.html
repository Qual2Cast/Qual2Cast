PIONEERING THE INDUSTRY STANDARD

Niagen® is the benchmark

Most dietary supplement ingredients have no independent quality standard. Niagen Bioscience spent over six years sponsoring the scientific work required to create one. The U.S. Pharmacopeia—the independent authority behind prescription drug standards—has now published an official monograph for NR Chloride. Niagen, our patented ingredient, is compliant with that standard.
