About American Financial Group

American Financial Group is committed to delivering financial solutions that fulfill today’s needs and tomorrow’s dreams. Our Specialty P&C Insurance Group offers specialized commercial products that help businesses manage financial risk with insurance products that meet their specific and ever-changing financial risk exposures.

Our insurance operations are members of Great American Insurance Group, whose history dates back to 1872, with the founding of Great American Insurance Company. Approximately 8,700 employees in approximately 75 office locations worldwide provide the strength and expertise that make AFG a leader in the specialty property and casualty marketplace.
