Ethics & Compliance

Our core values guide everything we do.

Integrity, Quality, Humanity, Commitment, and Innovation -- these values define how we show up for each other and for those with whom we do business. Since 1837, John Deere has been a responsible and successful enterprise, committed to conducting business ethically and in compliance with all applicable laws. We may have been named a 19-time World's Most Ethical Companies honoree, but we never take it for granted.

Reporting a Compliance Concern

When employees or business partners observe or suspect behavior that conflicts with our core values, one of our Codes of Conduct, company policies, or laws and regulations, we encourage them to report it. We provide multiple channels for asking questions or reporting concerns. The John Deere Compliance Hotline is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Local country phone numbers can also be found by visiting the Hotline website. Anonymous reporting is available where allowed by law.

We strictly prohibit retaliation against any individual who reports a concern or who assist in any manner in an investigation.

We are committed to providing a workplace where every employee is treated with dignity and respect.

For nearly 200 years, John Deere has been dedicated to helping our customers succeed. That commitment starts with our people. Meeting the evolving needs of our customers requires attracting, developing, and retaining exceptional talent.

We are focused on building strong, high-performing teams where different perspectives, ideas, and experiences contribute to innovation and better outcomes for our customers.

As a federal contractor, we regularly review our programs and practices to ensure they align with applicable laws and regulations. As part of that ongoing work, we are evolving how we approach employee programs, event, and partnerships to strengthen alignment with our business and create a more consistent employee experience.

Our focus remains unchanged: building strong teams to help our customers succeed, now and for generations to come.
