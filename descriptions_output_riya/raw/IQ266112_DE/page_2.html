Coverage To Keep You Running

Your peace of mind is important. Each John Deere PowerGard™ Certified machine is sold with a PowerGard Protection Plan1 that covers up to three years (or 2,000 hours) of your choice between comprehensive or limited coverage. The plan covers all the main components, electronics, hydraulics, cab, and air conditioning.

Explore the PowerGard Protection Plan

What Machines Qualify?

All machines sold pass the full John Deere PowerGard Certified Comprehensive Inspection, including a total fluid scan analysis, and service completed by a John Deere technician with genuine John Deere parts. Machines that qualify include:

  • 7R, 8R/8RT Series Tractors and 4WDs up to 10 years old or newer with less than 5000 engine hours.

  • Up to 7 years old or newer with less than 2000 engine hours on S-Series and X-Series Combines, Self-Propelled Sprayers, Nutrient Applicators, Self-Propelled Forage Harvesters, Cotton Harvesters, and Windrowers.
