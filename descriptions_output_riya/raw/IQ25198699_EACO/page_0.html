About EACO Corp.

EACO Corporation (EACO), incorporated on September 26, 1985, is a holding company comprised of its wholly-owned subsidiary, Bisco Industries, Inc. (bisco) and bisco’s wholly-owned Canadian subsidiary, Bisco Industries Limited (collectively, the Company).  The Company engages in the distribution of electronic components and hardware through its three  primary divisions: bisco, National-Precision and Fast-Cor.  Through its divisions, the Company supplies parts used to manufacture products in a range of industries. Primary industries serviced include aerospace & defense (A&D), industrial equipment, fabrication, printed circuit board (PCB), medical, communication, computer, automotive and marine. While the Company has global reach, it operates and employs team members in North America (United States/Canada/Mexico) and Southeast Asia (Philippines).
