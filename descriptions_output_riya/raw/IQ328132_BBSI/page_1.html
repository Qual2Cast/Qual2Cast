Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about BBSI's workforce management solutions.

How long has BBSI been in business?

Barret Business Services was founded in 1951 and has more than 75 years of experience supporting businesses with workforce management, risk mitigation, and operational guidance.

What makes BBSI different from other PEOs?

BBSI differentiates itself through dedicated local support teams, industry‑specific expertise, a strong focus on risk management, and a people‑first service model backed by integrated HR and payroll technology.

What types of businesses does BBSI work with?

BBSI works with businesses of all sizes and structures, including small to midsize businesses, large and multi‑location companies, franchise systems, and professional partners such as brokers and CPAs.

 

Does BBSI support both white‑collar and labor‑intensive industries?

Yes, BBSI supports a wide range of industries, from white‑collar and professional services to high‑risk, labor‑intensive environments, tailoring workforce solutions to each business’s operational needs.

What services does BBSI provide?

BBSI provides comprehensive PEO services including payroll and HR, risk management, workers’ compensation, compliance support, employee benefits, staffing, strategic planning, and workforce technology.

How does BBSI support businesses across multiple locations?

BBSI delivers consistent workforce solutions through standardized processes, centralized technology, and local expertise, helping businesses maintain compliance and operational alignment across locations and states.

How does BBSI support brokers, CPAs, and franchise networks?

BBSI partners with brokers, CPAs, and franchise organizations to provide scalable workforce solutions that support multiple clients or locations while preserving trusted relationships and operational consistency.

What is myBBSI?

myBBSI is BBSI’s integrated HRIS platform that centralizes payroll, HR administration, document management, recruiting, onboarding, and timekeeping in one secure, accessible system.

Is BBSI accredited and compliant with industry standards?

Yes, BBSI is ESAC‑accredited, SOC 1 certified, a NAPEO member, and governed by an independent board of directors, meeting rigorous financial, ethical, and operational standards.

Why do businesses choose BBSI as their PEO partner?

Businesses choose BBSI for its combination of local expertise, national scale, strong compliance and risk focus, integrated technology, and long‑term partnership approach.
