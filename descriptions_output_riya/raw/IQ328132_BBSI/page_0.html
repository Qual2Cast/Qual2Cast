Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about BBSI's workforce management solutions.

Why do businesses partner with BBSI?

Businesses partner with BBSI to reduce administrative burden, manage workforce risk, offer competitive benefits, and gain expert guidance that helps them operate more efficiently and grow with confidence.

How does BBSI’s PEO model work?

BBSI partners with your business to manage payroll, HR, benefits, compliance guidance, and risk management, while you maintain full control over your employees and day‑to‑day operations.

What services does BBSI provide?

BBSI offers payroll administration, HR consulting, workers’ compensation and risk management, health benefits and retirement plans, staffing and recruiting, and a unified HRIS platform to manage everything in one place.

How is BBSI different from other PEOs?

BBSI pairs nationwide resources with local, dedicated specialists, providing hands‑on support from professionals who understand your business, industry, and regional regulations.

Does BBSI work with small businesses or large companies?

BBSI supports businesses of every size and stage, from growing small businesses to larger, multi‑location organizations across a wide range of industries.

Does BBSI offer staffing and recruiting services?

Yes. BBSI provides end‑to‑end staffing and talent solutions, including direct placement, contract‑to‑hire, temporary and seasonal staffing, plus screening, onboarding, and retention support.

What industries does BBSI serve?

BBSI supports businesses across many industries, including construction, manufacturing, healthcare, hospitality, transportation, professional services, franchises, and more.

How does BBSI help with workers’ compensation and risk management?

BBSI delivers workers’ compensation coverage, pay‑as‑you‑go premiums, claims management, safety training, and loss control strategies designed to reduce risk and control costs.

Can BBSI help my business offer better employee benefits?

Yes. Through the PEO model, BBSI helps businesses access competitive medical, dental, vision, and retirement plans, along with full benefits administration and compliance support.

How do I get started with BBSI?

You can connect with a local BBSI expert using the form below to discuss your business needs, explore pricing, and build a customized workforce solution that fits your goals.
