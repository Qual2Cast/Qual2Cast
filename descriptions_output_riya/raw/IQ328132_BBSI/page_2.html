Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about BBSI's workforce management solutions.

What is a PEO, and what businesses benefit most from partnering with one?

A PEO, or Professional Employer Organization, partners with businesses to manage HR, payroll, benefits, workers’ compensation, compliance, and recruiting through a co‑employment relationship. PEOs are especially beneficial for startups, growing companies, and businesses of any size that want to reduce administrative burden, manage risk, and focus more time on running their operations without building large internal teams.

How can I identify which PEO is best for my business?

The best PEO for your business is one that understands your industry, offers comprehensive workforce support, and provides dedicated, local expertise rather than a one‑size‑fits‑all approach.

BBSI stands out by pairing each client with a local Business Unit that delivers hands‑on support across HR, payroll, risk management, and long‑term strategy, ensuring solutions are tailored to your business’s specific needs and growth goals.

What makes BBSI different than other PEOs?

BBSI stands apart from other PEOs by pairing businesses with dedicated local teams that provide hands‑on, industry‑specific support instead of a call‑center model. With a strong focus on risk management, compliance, and long‑term strategy, supported by integrated technology, BBSI delivers personalized workforce solutions that scale with each business’s needs.

What industries does BBSI serve?

BBSI serves businesses of all sizes across a wide range of industries, including white‑collar and professional services, construction, manufacturing, restoration, landscaping, transportation, hospitality, and more. With industry‑specific expertise and scalable support, BBSI tailors workforce solutions to meet the unique needs of both office‑based organizations and high‑risk, labor‑intensive operations.

How does BBSI combine people and technology?

BBSI combines dedicated local experts with integrated HR and payroll technology to deliver hands‑on support backed by real‑time data. Clients work directly with experienced professionals while using a centralized platform to manage payroll, HR, compliance, timekeeping, and reporting efficiently and securely.

How much do BBSI's services cost?

BBSI works individually with business owners to evaluate the health of their business and create a pricing structure that fits their needs, all with the ability to scale as the business grows.

Preliminary rate calculations are based on:

  • Number of employees

  • Percentage of full-time and part-time personnel

  • Employee turnover

  • Careful analysis of loss history

  • Level of risk associated with each job function

  • Experience modification (X-Mod)
