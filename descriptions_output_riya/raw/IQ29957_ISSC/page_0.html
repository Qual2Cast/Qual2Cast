Innovative Aerosystems

Tailored Avionics. Versatile Engineering. Manufactured In-House.

Innovative Aerosystems designs, engineers, manufactures, certifies, and supports advanced avionics solutions for commercial, military, business, and general aviation aircraft worldwide. From integrated flight decks and autopilots to autothrottles, flight management systems, mission computers, air data systems, displays, engine instrumentation, and navigation technologies, our scalable solutions are engineered to meet the unique requirements of every platform.

As one of the few U.S.-based avionics manufacturers with vertically integrated engineering and production, we control every stage of development—from concept and certification to manufacturing and lifecycle support. This enables us to deliver tailored, mission-ready solutions with the quality, reliability, and responsiveness our customers depend on. Whether modernizing legacy aircraft or developing next-generation capabilities, Innovative Aerosystems provides the expertise, flexibility, and innovation to keep fleets flying safely, efficiently, and ready for the future.
