Who We Are

Who We Are
Leading the Way in Sustainable Water Solutions

Zurn Elkay Water Solutions supplies the industry’s widest range of clean water solutions for drinking water, hygiene and sustainable water management. Headquartered in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, Zurn Elkay Water Solutions works with customers around the globe to deliver products and systems that enhance and ensure water quality, safety, hygiene, flow control and conservation.

Education

Students and staff should be focused on learning. Our sustainably inspired, touchless and tamper-resistant water solutions are designed to keep campuses flowing with healthier hydration, better hand hygiene, fewer repairs and more water savings. From the classroom to the restroom, the lab to the cafeteria, Zurn Elkay products and performance make the grade. 

Office and Retail

When it comes to high-traffic locations like offices and retail, durability and aesthetics matter. Zurn Elkay water solutions offer the performance and efficiency needed for successful operation day in and day out. Our commitment to hygiene and sustainability means added value in protecting health and reducing water waste. Smart plumbing products take cleaning efficiency to a new level with data and insights that maximize decision making and reduce downtime. 

Hospitality

Water systems have a major influence on guests’ comfort and safety. From restrooms and kitchens to walkways and parking lots, how water flows and drains is fundamental to a safe and pleasing environment for all. Our durable, affordable and easy-to-maintain Zurn Elkay solutions help keep business moving around the clock. 

Multifamily

Keeping maintenance down and resident satisfaction high requires innovative and effective water solutions. From lead-reducing filters to energy-efficient fixtures, we have a range of commercial-grade Zurn Elkay solutions to help create high-quality spaces where people love to live. 

Fire Protection

At Zurn Elkay, our expanded line of fire protection solutions means that from backflow preventers to fire valves and every step in between, our products work together for lasting performance that is simple to maintain and repair. Our systems also exceed all fire sprinkler code requirements and offer easier installation. 

Healthcare

At Zurn Elkay, we understand what’s at stake in a medical setting, where proper hygiene and bacteria management are paramount. From behind-the-wall water systems to touchless fixture systems, our hygienic, performance-driven and reliable water solutions help provide protection for those who need it most. 

Restaurant

Restaurants use water at every step, from food prep to guest experiences in restrooms to grease removal. Encompassing healthier hydration from hygienic handwashing systems and backflow preventers to grease and oil separators, our Zurn Elkay water solutions are designed to keep businesses of all sizes cooking with quality, compliance and sustainability.  

Residential

Elkay residential products combine performance and style, with faucets, sinks and drinking water solutions that make everyday tasks easier and put clean water within reach. Designed with hygiene and sustainability in mind, Elkay residential products support a safer, healthier life at home.

Government

As a single source provider of durable, commercial-grade plumbing products, we can help you stay in compliance with federal, state and local requirements as well as achieve LEED® certification through water use reduction. With Zurn Elkay, compliance doesn’t have to be complicated.
