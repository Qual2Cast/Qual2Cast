Identity solutions

See how our identity solutions help advertisers do more

Leverage advanced identity solutions across each stage of activation and measurement without relying on third-party cookies.

Leave the cookie behind?

Savvy marketers are building the new identity fabric of the internet — one that drives addressability across all digital advertising channels, including many where cookies are not present.

Reach desired audiences without cookies

Brands are using their own first-party data along with solutions like Unified ID 2.0 (UID2), European Unified ID (EUID), and our cross-device graph to optimize targeting and deliver personalized experiences in a privacy-conscious way.
