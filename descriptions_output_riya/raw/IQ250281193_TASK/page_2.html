What we do

Our services

Data & engineering
  • Labeling & mapping: High-fidelity labeling for localization and road rule understanding

  • Geofencing & market launch: Regional transcription and localization for expansion into new territories

  • Knowledge management: Building and maintaining dynamic Internal Knowledge Bases (IKB)

  • Manual data collection: R&D support for Level 4 and Level 5 autonomy sensor validation
Field operations
  • Safety drivers: Structure testing across metropolitan and highway validation

  • Roadside recovery: Physical troubleshooting and manual vehicle recovery in complex environments

  • Safety telematics: Monitoring driving behaviors and vehicle health via third-party tracking systems

  • Site operations: Depot management and coordination of physical fleet logistics
Remote assistance (RA)
  • Remote intervention: Real-time support ranging from path validation to manual trajectory control

  • Stuck analysis: Troubleshooting and resolving vehicle stranding using proprietary remote tools

  • Remote depot ops: Automated fleet parking, charging alignment and vehicle reallocation

  • Rider support: Real-time passenger communication and technical reassurance during interventions
Emergency response
  • 24/7 dispatch: Handling physical security events, collisions and medical emergencies

  • Law enforcement coordination: Real-time liaison with first responders and local authorities

  • Incident compliance: Comprehensive post-event documentation and regulatory reporting
