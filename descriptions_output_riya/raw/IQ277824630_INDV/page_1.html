Our Company

For more than 25 years, Indivior has been dedicated to advancing evidence-based treatment for people living with opioid use disorder (OUD). Today, we are entering a new era — one that honors our history while sharpening our purpose: to bring meaningful treatment within reach for every person living with OUD.

Guided by science, compassion, and a belief that recovery should be possible for everyone, we continue to innovate so that effective treatment becomes more accessible and stigma becomes a barrier of the past.

Our Culture

We are united by a profound commitment to people living with OUD and to the families and communities affected by the disease. Our culture is rooted in integrity, innovation, and an unwavering belief in what’s possible when science, purpose, and compassion come together.

Every day, our teams work to challenge stigma, expand access to evidence-based treatment, and accelerate progress toward a future where long-term recovery is the expectation — not the exception.

Our Leadership

Indivior is led by our Board of Directors and our Executive Committee with expertise in addiction medicine, global pharmaceutical and biotechnology executive management, and risk and financial management.

Our Team

Our people are our driving force. Scientists, advocates, and innovators — each of us contributes to advancing OUD science and delivering treatments that support long-term recovery.

We value integrity and collaboration, and we have a deep sense of responsibility to the people and communities we serve. We are always looking for passionate, mission-driven individuals who want to help restore hope, one life at a time. 

Join our team

Our Focus

We believe OUD is a chronic but treatable brain disease — and that with the right combination of medication, therapy, and support, long-term recovery is possible.

Our approach is grounded in uncompromising science, continuous innovation, and a commitment to making treatment more accessible. From research and clinical development to education and advocacy, everything we do is designed to restore hope and independence for the people we serve.
