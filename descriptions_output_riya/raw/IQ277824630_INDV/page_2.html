Our Products

Our Prescription Medicines

At Indivior, every product we develop is rooted in science, compassion, and an unwavering commitment to help people with opioid use disorder (OUD) achieve long-term recovery. Our medications are designed to address the complex realities of addiction — helping people living with OUD regain control of their health, their dignity, and their future.

We work closely with clinicians, researchers, and the individuals who depend on our treatments to ensure that every medicine supports a more independent and sustainable recovery journey.

Long-Acting Injectables (LAIs) for OUD

LAIs represent an innovative advancement in the treatment of OUD. Unlike daily oral medications, LAIs provide a consistent, extended release of medication over time to help maintain stability and reduce the challenges of daily dosing.

By supporting adherence and minimizing the ups and downs associated with daily treatment, LAIs empower our patients to focus more on recovery and life beyond addiction. For many, they represent a vital bridge to long-term stability, independence, and meaningful recovery.

FAQS

Long-acting injectables (LAIs) for OUD are prescription medications administered by a healthcare professional that deliver medication continuously over a defined period of time. This steady delivery can help reduce cravings, minimize the risk of withdrawal, and support consistent recovery progress.

Treatment decisions should always be made in partnership with your healthcare provider. Together, you can determine whether a long-acting injectable aligns with your treatment goals, medical history, and lifestyle needs.

LAIs for OUD are backed by years of scientific research and clinical evidence. They are approved by health authorities and shown to be both safe and effective when used as prescribed under the supervision of a qualified healthcare provider. Learn more about our science.

LAIs for OUD are administered as an injection by a healthcare professional in a clinical setting. Depending on the medication, injections may be scheduled over a period of time, offering flexibility and peace of mind for patients, caregivers, and providers alike.
