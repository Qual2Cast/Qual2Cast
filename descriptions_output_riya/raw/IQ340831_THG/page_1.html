Why The Hanover

Experience the Hanover difference

Offering you the best combination of insurance protection, value and price

At The Hanover, we approach insurance as an opportunity to tailor a coverage plan that provides not just value but peace of mind - for the business you've built, the car you drive, the place you call home. 

Together with our agents, we offer standard and specialized insurance protection for small and mid-sized businesses, as well as for homes, automobiles and personal items. 

We're here to protect what matters most to you.

Personal insurance

Personal insurance

We provide the insurance you need to protect your car, home, watercraft and more. Whether you're single, married or empty nesters, we can offer you tailored coverage options for your needs with the services and convenience you expect.

Business insurance

Business insurance

Your business is like no other. That's why we offer offer tailored, flexible insurance solutions designed to meet your business needs today and tomorrow. Our industry experts will partner with you and your agent to help you identify and minimize risk.

There when you need us most

There when you need us most

Every claim is important to us. We give you peace of mind that we will be there when the unexpected happens, working to get you back to business, on the road or home. We've been delivering on this promise for more than 170 years.

Connecting you to expertise

We recognize that purchasing insurance can be complicated and that your needs may be too complex for an online form or a short phone call. That's why we work solely with independent insurance agents who are experts in your local market and will take time to understand your personal and business insurance needs.
