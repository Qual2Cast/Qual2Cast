MyVeeva for Patients

Make Trial Participation Easier

Enhance patient experience with a single point of access for study documents, actions, and communications, meaning patients can easily view study information and stay in touch with their clinical site.

Celerion enhances Patient-Centricity and Improves Efficiency with Veeva eConsent
Overview

Make clinical trial participation more accessible and convenient

With MyVeeva for Patients, study participants can:

Access important information

Easily find trial documents, additional resources, and contact information from the study homepage.

Manage appointments

Keep up-to-date with where, when, and how their upcoming study visits will take place, including linking to virtual visits directly from the app.

Request help

Flag questions and request non-urgent support without having to call the site directly.

Complete eConsents

Review and complete informed consent, with easy-to-follow sections and interactive elements such as videos and questions. Learn more

Complete ePROs

Receive notifications when an ePRO is due and make survey completion easier with a modern experience on web or native app. Learn more
