Deere & Company manufactures equipment and develops technology for agriculture, construction, forestry, and turf maintenance under the John Deere brand. Its equipment portfolio includes agricultural tractors, harvesting machinery, compact tractors, mowers, utility vehicles, excavators, and forestry equipment. These products serve farmers, builders, forestry operators, and customers maintaining residential, commercial, and recreational land. The company combines physical machinery with precision technology and connected operating tools. Agricultural applications include automated equipment and targeted spraying technology that uses cameras, machine learning, and robotics to distinguish weeds from crops. Connected machines can transmit operating information to cloud-based tools, allowing customers to monitor equipment condition and manage work using operational data. Deere supports equipment ownership and use through replacement parts, service resources, precision upgrades, and a dealer network. Dealers provide product information, equipment availability, parts, and service assistance. The company also offers financing resources associated with its equipment business. Its overall offering combines machinery, digital tools, and ongoing support to help customers perform fieldwork, harvest crops, build infrastructure, maintain land, and manage equipment throughout its working life.
