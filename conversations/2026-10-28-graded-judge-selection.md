# 2026-10-28: graded judge as an optional peer-selection route

`v0/graded_peer_selection.py` applies the shared [0–2 product/customer rubric](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1rp6H-IXhfZ09NbOObzPreLvGaXjOugEUblYcHo31IQg/edit#gid=1963357707) to the frozen cosine z ≥ 2 candidate pool. Each target-level CLI call scores its candidates independently from saved website passages; no cosine rank, reviewer grade, or financial field enters the prompt. Candidate grades are validated against source citations from both companies. Grade 2 precedes grade 1, cosine similarity breaks ties, and at most ten peers are retained. Grade 0 and insufficient evidence are excluded. The original `valuation.estimate` uses cosine weights after peer selection. Two rules are reported: grade 2 only, and grade 2 followed by grade 1. The four original v0 routes are unchanged.

The batched protocol returned 2,138 decisions for all 160 fixed SIC2 holdout targets: 327 grades of 2, 831 grades of 1, 824 grades of 0, and 156 insufficient-evidence results. There were 1,982 non-null grades, all with target and peer citations. The 2-only rule estimates 116/160 targets (72.5%; 37.18% median absolute percentage error on those targets). The 2-then-1 rule estimates 155/160 (96.9%; 37.32% median APE). The existing binary judge estimates 110/160, and cosine estimates 160/160. On the 155 targets shared by 2-then-1 and cosine, the median paired APE change is 0.00 percentage points (2,000-draw bootstrap 95% interval −1.86 to +1.83); 75 targets improve and 76 worsen. Thus the graded route improves coverage over the binary judge but has no established valuation-error advantage over cosine. It remains optional.

Four of the five targets without a 2-then-1 peer have unusable saved target-site passages (BKH, BBWI, WMK, OTCM); Etsy's seven candidates all receive 0. The `coverage_details.csv` table records this per target. On the 36 PCTY/WEN development pairs, the batched protocol agrees with the defined reviewer majority on 26/32 and with unanimous labels on 11/11. It agrees with the earlier individual-call judge on 31/36; that diagnostic used a larger per-company passage budget. The golden-set ranking table retains Riya's, Barbara's, and Matthew's labels separately. The 2-then-1 route's mean NDCG@10 is 0.819, 0.953, and 0.970 under their respective labels, versus cosine's 0.871, 0.844, and 0.910. This two-target, reviewer-dependent result does not establish general ranking superiority.

Results: `outputs-local/comps_pilot_targets/holdout_completion/graded_peer_selection/` contains the 160 target judgments, grades, ranked peer lists, coverage details, valuation estimates, and pairwise comparisons. `outputs-local/comps_pilot_targets/golden_set/graded_batch_check/` contains the 36-pair diagnostic and peer-ranking metrics. The updated `v0/notebooks/gold_and_holdout_integration.ipynb` presents the procedure and results. Focused tests pass, and the original four-route plus no-matching estimates reproduce their saved values to floating-point precision.
