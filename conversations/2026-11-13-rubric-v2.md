# 2026-11-13: rubric v2, four yes/no questions per pair (Matthew, plan item e, with Riya)

## Why

The mentor's Nov 8 reply to the round-2 numbers: "Make a more detailed rubric so that you have an
objective way to grade comps and facet fills. This may reduce person-to-person variability." On Nov 9
the team (Michael, Barbara, Matthew) and Professor Robbins agreed to grade yes/no questions and
derive the 0/1/2 score instead of asking reviewers for the score itself. Michael's V1 plan (Nov 10)
item e: Matt and Riya rework the rubric; e1 binary components, e2 batch 10 -> 6 or 7 comps per
target, e3 two more runs with the same reviewers.

What the labels so far showed (31 round-2 pairs, 293 of Barbara's round-3 pairs):

- 178 of Barbara's 293 are 1s; the sheet does not record which half (product or customers) matched.
- Matthew and Barbara split on 6 of 31 round-2 pairs, 5 of them a 1 against a 0: definitions, not facts.
- Of 101 zeros from two reviewers, only three of the six reasons were ever used (different business
  model 42, different customers 31, different industry 32, in round 2 + round 3 counts); scale,
  generic wording and wrong industry code never.
- Non-comp rate by B3 rank: 9% at rank 1, 67% at rank 10; 91 of 293 (31%) are zeros.

## What changed

- **New `v1/RUBRIC_V2.md`:** the four questions with definitions and examples (same product, same
  customers, same revenue model, same step in the chain), the derived score (2 = all yes; 1 = product or
  customers yes but not all four; 0 = both no), the several-businesses rule (answer against the one
  business the comp matches and name it in `business_graded_`), how to fill a sheet, why old 1s do not
  convert, what the agent grader change is, and the open items.
- **New `v1/scripts/rubric_v2.py`:** `derive_score`, `parse_answer`, `check_sheet` (blank / half-answered /
  invalid), `scores` (answers as booleans + `score_<reviewer>`), `agreement` (per question and on the
  score, per reviewer pair), `convert_sheet` (round-2 layout -> v2 layout, old scores kept as
  `old_score_<r>` / `old_reason_<r>` where filled, never translated). CLI: `convert` (never
  overwrites), `check`, `scores`.
- **New `v1/scripts/tests/test_rubric_v2.py`:** 13 tests, all pass.
- **New `outputs-local/v1/golden_set_round3/labels/round3_sheet_v2.csv`:** the 262 round-3 pairs (40
  targets) in the v2 layout, reviewers matthew and barbara, every answer blank. The old
  `round3_sheet.csv` is untouched.
- **`v1/GOLDEN_SET_ROUND2.md`:** a note that the score section is superseded from round 3 on.

Nothing in the agent loop, the notebooks or the metrics changed. The 2/1/0 scale is kept, so
`golden_set.precision_at_k` and friends, the agent-grader comparison and the valuation checks read
the derived scores unchanged.
