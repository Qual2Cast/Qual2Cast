Appendix B. Evidence and reproduction map

Original all-company experiment. Source [1] contains the original methods, same-SIC/no-matching controls, equal-count comparisons, bootstrap analysis and ten case studies. Runs: 20261019T080322Z and 20261019T080416Z. The published source is preserved; its 535-company results are reported, not recomputed by this editorial build.

Earlier team evidence. Riya's pre-output labels, collection changes and historical profile audits are documented in [2]. They are neither superseded by an empty review template nor evidence that the new model decisions were independently graded. The original 19-company analysis remains in notebooks/integrated_company_analysis.ipynb and its matching reports directory. It showed development sensitivity to financial reranking, not a confirmed improvement.

Paylocity relevance check. The original 535-company page-text run in [2] reported embedding NDCG@20 of 0.85 and recall@25 of 0.91 against Riya's labels, versus NDCG@20 of 0.80 and recall@25 of 0.64 for its combined score. Random ordering had NDCG@20 of 0.03. A later masked-input run used 534 companies and reported embedding NDCG@20 of 0.90; it is a different input snapshot. These one-target results concern retrieval relevance, not out-of-sample valuation accuracy.

Integrated experiment. Run 20261020T033357Z-f5389a uses private-evidence-2026-10-19-v4. Its 672 configurations cross nine retrieval methods, review alternatives, eight factor choices and four aggregators, with smaller few-shot/RAG arms. Exact arm coverage is saved in comparison_scope.json; not every possible method combination was run. The 6,720 rows, prompts, responses and source modules are embedded in the notebook. The new compact table is recomputed from unreviewed_controls.csv and combination_results.csv for the integrated pilot; the all-company row is transcribed from pinned source [1].

Financial fields. The 15-row field_selection.csv specifies purpose, target/peer side and availability. Explicitly supplied LTM revenue, EBITDA and debt may be used; stock-based target features are not required. Public-peer 10-K descriptions, database crosswalks and WRDS Ask AI are potential extensions, not completed integrations. Capital IQ identifiers do not imply a verified Orbis/Preqin identifier mapping.

Growth and labels. Source [4] preserves the team's original held-out sequence and EDGAR outcome construction. The notebook's rules/LLM/intersection comparison uses 145 held-out-C pages later inspected during development. It scores article/type pairs, not exact event counts, forecast errors or economic value. The broader event evaluation includes sources beyond the narrow private-target deployment policy; its accuracy does not establish private-target source coverage. In the current ten targets, eligible ordinary-site growth evidence covers only PCTY, BV and WING.

Reproduce. Use Python 3.12+ and pipeline/requirements-replay.txt. Download team_company_workflow.ipynb and run its code cells in order. It extracts its checksum-bound bundle into a local directory, replays recorded inference and recomputes downstream results without downloading a model. Source-level integration instructions are in pipeline/TEAM_WORKFLOW.md. Kethan's separate experiment requires its branch and source access. No teammate's original notebook or shared Google Doc was rewritten for this memo.

Team progress source. Qual2Cast: Team Progress Check, 19 October 2026. Its results are reconciled with the newer merged workflow; an unverified proposal is not reported as completed work.
