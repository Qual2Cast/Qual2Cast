Facets:
- primary_products: the main physical or software products sold
- primary_services: the main services sold
- customer_segment: who the buyers are (consumers, businesses, government; type and segment)
- end_markets: the industries or end uses the customers are in
- business_model: how the company operates and makes money overall (maker, distributor, operator, platform, contractor ...)
- revenue_model: how revenue is earned (product sales, subscription, fees, contracts, rent ...)
- value_chain_position: where it sits in the value chain (raw material, component, OEM, distributor, retailer, service provider)
- geography_served: where its customers are
- specialization: how specialized or niche the offering is
- distribution_channels: how it reaches customers (direct sales, dealers, online, stores ...)
- regulatory_exposure: regulation that shapes the business
