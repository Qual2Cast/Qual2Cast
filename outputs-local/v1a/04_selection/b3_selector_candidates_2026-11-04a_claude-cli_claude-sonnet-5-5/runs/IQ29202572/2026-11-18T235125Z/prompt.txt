You help a private-equity analyst choose comparable public companies (comps) for valuing a target company. A buyout investor values a target from the EV/EBITDA multiples of companies in the same business: the same main products or services, sold to the same kind of customers, earning money the same way. Scale matters less than being in the same business.

Below are the target and a list of candidate comps, each with its name, website and a description of its business model. Use the descriptions and what you know about the companies.

For every candidate, decide whether it is a good comp for the target:
- good: in the same business as the target, so its multiple says something about the target's.
- bad: a different business, even if it shares an industry label, customers or a technology.

Rules:
- Answer for every candidate, once, by its label (C1, C2, ...).
- The reason is one short sentence, at most twenty words, naming what matches or differs.
- Judge the business only. Do not use or mention valuations, multiples, share prices or financial figures.

Target: Highwater Ethanol, LLC (www.highwaterethanol.com)
- Core offering: Operates a corn-based ethanol plant in Lamberton, Minnesota. Produces fuel ethanol, distillers grains and corn oil from locally sourced corn. Sells these to fuel blenders, livestock feeders and industrial buyers through marketing agents.
- Primary products: fuel ethanol; distillers grains; corn oil
- Primary services: none
- Customer segment: B2B: fuel blenders, petroleum refiners, livestock feeders, feed distributors, biodiesel producers
- End markets: transportation fuel; animal feed; renewable fuels
- Revenue model: product sales: ethanol, distillers grains and corn oil sold via marketing agreements
- Value chain position: OEM / manufacturer: processes purchased corn into ethanol and co-products, sold to marketers and end users
- Geography served: US national: Plant in southwest Minnesota, products shipped by rail and truck across the US, with some exports through marketers
- Product specialization: related lines: ethanol and its co-products: distillers grains and corn oil from a single plant
- Distribution channels: agents / brokers: third-party marketing firms sell ethanol, distillers grains and corn oil
- Regulatory exposure: medium: Environmental permits (EPA, Minnesota PCA) and federal Renewable Fuel Standard and fuel policy affect demand and operations
- Sub-sector: corn ethanol production; distillers grains and corn oil co-products
