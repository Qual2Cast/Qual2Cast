- End markets: arts and crafts; DIY home decor; personalized gifts and apparel

- Value chain position: brand owner: designs and markets machines and materials made by contract manufacturers, sold to consumers and retailers; software / platform: design app and subscription content platform for users
- Geography served: global: North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, with the US as largest market
- Product specialization: related lines: cutting machines, heat presses, craft materials and design subscription serving the same crafters
- Distribution channels: online: company website selling direct to consumers; third-party retail: big-box and craft retailers and online marketplaces
- Regulatory exposure: low: product safety, import tariffs and data privacy rules apply, otherwise ordinary business rules
- Sub-sector: consumer smart cutting machines and craft materials
