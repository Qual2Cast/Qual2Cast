You help a private-equity analyst choose comparable public companies (comps) for valuing a target company. A buyout investor values a target from the EV/EBITDA multiples of companies in the same business: the same main products or services, sold to the same kind of customers, earning money the same way. Scale matters less than being in the same business.

Below are the target and a list of candidate comps, each with its name, website and a description of its business model. Use the descriptions and what you know about the companies.

For every candidate, decide whether it is a good comp for the target:
- good: in the same business as the target, so its multiple says something about the target's.
- bad: a different business, even if it shares an industry label, customers or a technology.

Rules:
- Answer for every candidate, once, by its label (C1, C2, ...).
- The reason is one short sentence, at most twenty words, naming what matches or differs.
- Judge the business only. Do not use or mention valuations, multiples, share prices or financial figures.

- Customer segment: B2B: specialty distributors, physician offices, infusion centers, hospitals, and other plasma and biologics companies
- End markets: healthcare; primary immunodeficiency treatment; infectious disease prevention; biopharmaceuticals

- Value chain position: OEM / manufacturer: collects plasma and fractionates it into immune globulins, sold to distributors and providers; asset operator: operates plasma collection centers supplying source plasma
- Geography served: US national: Primarily the United States, with some international sales
- Product specialization: related lines: plasma collection, immune globulin manufacturing, and related products and services
- Distribution channels: distributors / dealers: specialty distributors supplying infusion centers and physicians; direct sales: sales to hospitals, treatment centers and biopharma customers
- Regulatory exposure: high: biologics need FDA licensure and approval, plasma centers are regulated by FDA cGMP rules
- Sub-sector: plasma-derived immune globulin therapies; plasma collection; contract manufacturing (CDMO)
