You help a private-equity analyst choose comparable public companies (comps) for valuing a target company. A buyout investor values a target from the EV/EBITDA multiples of companies in the same business: the same main products or services, sold to the same kind of customers, earning money the same way. Scale matters less than being in the same business.

Below are the target and a list of candidate comps, each with its name, website and a description of its business model. Use the descriptions and what you know about the companies.

For every candidate, decide whether it is a good comp for the target:
- good: in the same business as the target, so its multiple says something about the target's.
- bad: a different business, even if it shares an industry label, customers or a technology.

Rules:
- Answer for every candidate, once, by its label (C1, C2, ...).
- The reason is one short sentence, at most twenty words, naming what matches or differs.
- Judge the business only. Do not use or mention valuations, multiples, share prices or financial figures.

Connects airlines, hotels and other travel suppliers with travel agencies and corporate buyers, and sells airline IT software (reservations, retailing, operations) to carriers. Delivered as cloud-based platforms and transaction processing.
- Primary products: global distribution system; airline reservation and passenger service software; airline retailing and merchandising software; airline operations software; hospitality software
- Primary services: travel booking and distribution; airline IT hosting; software implementation and support; travel data and analytics
- Customer segment: B2B: airlines, hotels, car rental and rail suppliers, travel agencies, online travel agencies, corporate travel buyers
- End markets: airlines; hospitality and lodging; travel agencies; corporate travel
- Revenue model: transaction or usage fees: booking fees paid by travel suppliers per distributed reservation; subscription: recurring SaaS and hosted airline IT solutions fees; service fees: implementation, consulting and support for airline customers
- Value chain position: software / platform: operates travel marketplace and airline IT platforms, sold to airlines, hotels and travel agencies
- Geography served: global: Customers in North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, Middle East, Africa and Latin America
- Product specialization: related lines: travel distribution, airline IT and hospitality solutions
- Distribution channels: direct sales: sales teams contracting with airlines, hotels and travel agencies; partners / resellers: travel agencies and online agencies that use the platform to sell supplier content
- Regulatory exposure: medium: data privacy rules such as GDPR, payment security standards, and travel distribution and competition regulation
- Sub-sector: travel technology: global distribution and airline retailing software
