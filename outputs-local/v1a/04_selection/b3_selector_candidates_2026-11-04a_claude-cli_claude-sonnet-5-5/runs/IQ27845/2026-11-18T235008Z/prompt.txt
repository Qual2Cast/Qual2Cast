You help a private-equity analyst choose comparable public companies (comps) for valuing a target company. A buyout investor values a target from the EV/EBITDA multiples of companies in the same business: the same main products or services, sold to the same kind of customers, earning money the same way. Scale matters less than being in the same business.

Below are the target and a list of candidate comps, each with its name, website and a description of its business model. Use the descriptions and what you know about the companies.

For every candidate, decide whether it is a good comp for the target:
- good: in the same business as the target, so its multiple says something about the target's.
- bad: a different business, even if it shares an industry label, customers or a technology.

Rules:
- Answer for every candidate, once, by its label (C1, C2, ...).
- The reason is one short sentence, at most twenty words, naming what matches or differs.
- Judge the business only. Do not use or mention valuations, multiples, share prices or financial figures.

Employers and recruiters pay for job postings and resume database access to find specialized talent, and candidates use the sites to find jobs.

- Primary services: recruitment marketing; candidate sourcing; job posting services; career events and content
- Customer segment: B2B: employers, staffing firms and recruiters hiring technology and security-cleared talent; B2C: technology and security-cleared job seekers using the sites, mostly free
- End markets: technology; defense and government contracting; intelligence; recruitment and staffing
- Revenue model: subscription: recurring packages for job postings and resume database access; advertising: employer branding and recruitment advertising; service fees: event and sourcing services
- Value chain position: software / platform: operates online job marketplaces connecting employers and recruiters with candidates
- Geography served: global: Primarily United States, with some presence in Europe and Asia via Dice
- Product specialization: related lines: Dice tech careers site and ClearanceJobs cleared-professional job site, both niche recruiting marketplaces
- Distribution channels: direct sales: sales teams selling to employers and recruiters; online: self-service purchase and candidate access via websites and apps
- Regulatory exposure: medium: data privacy rules such as CCPA and GDPR, employment advertising and anti-discrimination rules
- Sub-sector: online tech talent marketplaces; security-cleared job boards
