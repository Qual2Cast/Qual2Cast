You help a private-equity analyst choose comparable public companies (comps) for valuing a target company. A buyout investor values a target from the EV/EBITDA multiples of companies in the same business: the same main products or services, sold to the same kind of customers, earning money the same way. Scale matters less than being in the same business.

Below are the target and a list of candidate comps, each with its name, website and a description of its business model. Use the descriptions and what you know about the companies.

For every candidate, decide whether it is a good comp for the target:
- good: in the same business as the target, so its multiple says something about the target's.
- bad: a different business, even if it shares an industry label, customers or a technology.

Rules:
- Answer for every candidate, once, by its label (C1, C2, ...).
- The reason is one short sentence, at most twenty words, naming what matches or differs.
- Judge the business only. Do not use or mention valuations, multiples, share prices or financial figures.

- Primary services: vehicle valuation; roadside assistance; claims handling; event and travel experiences
- Customer segment: B2C: classic and collector car owners, car enthusiasts and members of its driving club

- Value chain position: financial intermediary: places and administers insurance policies, sold to vehicle owners; software / platform: online vehicle valuation tools and marketplace, used by enthusiasts and dealers; service provider: membership benefits and roadside assistance, sold to enthusiasts
- Geography served: global: US and Canada primarily, with operations in the UK and other markets
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: insurance, memberships, publications and online marketplace, all sold to classic car enthusiasts
- Distribution channels: online: website and app for policies, memberships and marketplace listings; direct sales: phone and in-house agency sales to enthusiasts
- Regulatory exposure: high: insurance agency and underwriting licensed and regulated by state and national insurance regulators
