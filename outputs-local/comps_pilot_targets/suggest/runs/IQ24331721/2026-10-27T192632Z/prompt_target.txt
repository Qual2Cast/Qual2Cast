Target: InfuSystem Holdings, Inc. (www.infusystem.com)
- Core offering: Rents, sells and services infusion pumps and related supplies for patients receiving drug infusions at home and in clinics. Serves home infusion providers, oncology practices and hospitals in the US and Canada, delivering pumps to patients and maintaining the pump fleet.
- Primary products: infusion pumps; infusion supplies and disposables
- Primary services: pump rental and fleet management; pump repair and maintenance; pump setup and patient support
- Customer segment: B2B: home infusion providers, oncology practices, hospitals, ambulatory infusion centers
- End markets: healthcare; home healthcare; oncology
- Revenue model: rental or leasing: pump rental fees paid by providers for pumps in patients' care; product sales: sales of infusion pumps and disposable supplies; service fees: pump repair, maintenance and setup services; healthcare reimbursement: payments tied to infusion therapy billed to Medicare and private payers
- Value chain position: asset operator: owns and manages a fleet of infusion pumps deployed to patients; service provider: repairs, maintains and supports pumps, sold to infusion providers and clinics; distributor: buys pumps and supplies from device manufacturers and supplies them to providers
- Geography served: North America: US nationwide and Canada
- Company size: mid-sized
- Product specialization: related lines: infusion pump rental and services, pump sales, and disposable infusion supplies
- Distribution channels: direct sales: sales and rental contracts with infusion providers and clinics; other: pumps delivered to and picked up from patients' homes
- Regulatory exposure: high: infusion pumps are FDA-regulated medical devices, Medicare and private payer reimbursement rules apply
