Target: Blackbaud, Inc. (www.blackbaud.com)
- Core offering: Sells cloud software and data services that help nonprofits, schools, universities and healthcare organizations raise funds, manage donors, students and operations. Delivered mainly as subscriptions hosted online, with implementation and support services.
- Primary products: donor management software; fundraising and peer-to-peer giving software; education administration software; financial management software; constituent engagement software
- Primary services: implementation and consulting; data and analytics services; payment processing; training
- Customer segment: B2B: nonprofit organizations, private schools, colleges and universities, hospitals and healthcare foundations; B2G: public school districts and government-funded agencies
- End markets: nonprofits; education; healthcare; philanthropy
- Revenue model: subscription: recurring software subscriptions for donor, education and financial management platforms; service fees: implementation, consulting, training and support services; transaction or usage fees: fees on payments and donations processed through its platforms
- Value chain position: software / platform: takes in customer data and sells cloud platforms to nonprofits and schools; service provider: takes in implementation and data work and sells services to its software customers
- Geography served: global: Mainly United States, also Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, Ireland and other countries
- Company size: among the largest
- Product specialization: related lines: fundraising, donor management, education administration, financial management and healthcare software sold to mission-driven organizations
- Distribution channels: direct sales: direct sales teams selling to large nonprofits, schools and healthcare organizations; partners / resellers: channel partners and technology marketplaces that resell or integrate its products; online: online self-service sign-up and product marketplace listings
- Regulatory exposure: medium: data privacy and student data rules such as FERPA, GDPR and state privacy laws, plus payment card security standards
