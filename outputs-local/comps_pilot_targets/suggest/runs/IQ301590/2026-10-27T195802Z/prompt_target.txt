- Primary services: mortgage origination; mortuary services; cemetery interment services; pre-need funeral planning
- Customer segment: B2C: individuals and families buying life insurance, home mortgages, and pre-need cemetery and funeral plans
- End markets: insurance; real estate finance; death care

- Value chain position: financial intermediary: underwrites life policies and originates mortgages for individual borrowers; asset operator: owns and runs cemeteries and mortuaries, selling interment and funeral services to families
- Geography served: US national: life insurance and mortgages in many states, cemeteries and mortuaries in Utah, Texas, Arizona, Missouri
- Company size: mid-sized
- Product specialization: diversified: separate life insurance, mortgage lending, and cemetery and mortuary businesses
- Distribution channels: agents / brokers: insurance agents selling life policies to individual customers; own locations: mortgage branch offices and loan officers serving home buyers; direct sales: cemetery and mortuary staff selling pre-need and at-need services to families
