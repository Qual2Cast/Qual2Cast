Target: Suburban Propane Partners, L.P. (www.suburbanpropane.com)
- Core offering: Distributes propane, fuel oil and refined fuels to residential, commercial, industrial and agricultural customers across much of the US. It delivers fuel by truck from its terminals and branch locations and also sells tanks, equipment and related services.
- Primary products: propane; fuel oil; gasoline and diesel; kerosene; propane cylinders and tanks
- Primary services: fuel delivery; tank rental and maintenance; installation and repair; energy management plans
- Customer segment: B2C: homeowners using propane or heating oil for heating, cooking and water heating; B2B: commercial, industrial, agricultural and motor fuel customers
- End markets: residential heating; commercial buildings; agriculture; industrial processing; motor fuel
- Revenue model: product sales: propane, heating oil and refined fuels delivered to customers; service fees: installation, repair and maintenance of tanks and equipment; rental or leasing: customer propane tanks and equipment rented under agreements
- Value chain position: distributor: buys propane and fuel from refiners and wholesalers, sells to end users; retailer: sells fuel and equipment through local branch locations to households and businesses; asset operator: owns and runs storage terminals, delivery trucks and customer tanks
- Geography served: US national: Operates across many US states, concentrated in the Northeast, Mid-Atlantic, Midwest and parts of the West
- Company size: among the largest
- Product specialization: related lines: propane, fuel oil and refined fuels sold through the same branches and customers
- Distribution channels: own locations: company-operated branch and terminal locations; direct sales: fuel sold directly to households and business accounts; online: online account and bill payment for customers
- Regulatory exposure: medium: pipeline, storage and transport safety rules from DOT, PHMSA and EPA, plus state fuel regulations
