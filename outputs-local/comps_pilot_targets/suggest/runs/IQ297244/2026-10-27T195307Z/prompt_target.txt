Target: Brown & Brown, Inc. (www.bbrown.com)
- Core offering: Insurance brokerage and risk management firm that places commercial property and casualty, employee benefits and personal insurance for businesses and individuals. It earns commissions and fees by arranging coverage with insurance carriers and advising clients on risk.
- Primary products: commercial property and casualty insurance; employee benefits insurance; personal insurance; specialty program insurance
- Primary services: insurance brokerage; risk management consulting; claims administration; program administration
- Customer segment: B2B: businesses across industries, employers buying benefits, program agencies and retail insurance agencies; B2C: individuals buying personal insurance such as auto and homeowners coverage
- End markets: construction; healthcare; hospitality; manufacturing; real estate
- Revenue model: commissions or management fees: commissions paid by insurance carriers on policies placed for clients; service fees: fees for consulting, claims handling and program administration
- Value chain position: financial intermediary: places insurance policies between clients and carriers, earning commissions from carriers; service provider: provides risk advice and claims services to businesses and individuals
- Geography served: US national: all 50 states, with smaller offices in Canada, the UK, Ireland and Bermuda
- Company size: among the largest
- Product specialization: related lines: retail brokerage, wholesale brokerage, national programs and services, all selling insurance and risk solutions
- Distribution channels: direct sales: in-house account executives selling to commercial and personal clients; own locations: retail offices across the United States and abroad; agents / brokers: wholesale brokerage serving independent retail insurance agencies
- Regulatory exposure: high: insurance brokers must hold state insurance licenses, regulated by state insurance departments
