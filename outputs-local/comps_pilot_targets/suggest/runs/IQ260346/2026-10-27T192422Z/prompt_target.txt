Target: Chesapeake Utilities Corporation (www.chpk.com)
- Core offering: Provides regulated natural gas and electric distribution, plus propane and related energy services, to homes and businesses, mainly on the Delmarva Peninsula and in Florida. It earns regulated rates on its networks and margins on propane and energy marketing.
- Primary products: natural gas; electricity; propane
- Primary services: pipeline transportation; natural gas marketing; energy construction services
- Customer segment: B2B: commercial and industrial businesses, wholesale and transportation fuel customers; B2C: residential homes and households
- End markets: residential energy; commercial and industrial; power generation; transportation fuel
- Revenue model: product sales: natural gas, electricity and propane sold to customers; transaction or usage fees: regulated distribution and transportation rates based on usage; service fees: energy services and related field work for customers; project or contract: pipeline and infrastructure construction under contract
- Value chain position: distributor: takes in natural gas and power and delivers it to end customers through its networks; retailer: sells propane to homes and businesses; asset operator: owns and runs pipelines, distribution networks and propane facilities
- Geography served: US regional: Delaware, Maryland, Florida, Pennsylvania and Ohio
- Company size: mid-sized
- Product specialization: related lines: regulated natural gas, electric distribution, propane, pipeline and energy marketing
- Distribution channels: own locations: company-operated propane and operations facilities; direct sales: direct delivery and billing to utility customers
- Regulatory exposure: high: utility rates set by state public service commissions and interstate pipeline rules set by FERC
