Target: Paylocity Holding Corporation (www.paylocity.com)
- Core offering: Sells cloud-based payroll, human capital management and workforce software to mid-sized employers in the United States. Delivered as a subscription platform with implementation and support services.
- Primary products: payroll software; HR management software; talent management software; time and labor software; benefits administration software
- Primary services: payroll processing; tax filing and compliance; implementation services
- Customer segment: B2B: mid-sized private employers across many industries
- End markets: human resources; payroll; professional services; healthcare; manufacturing; retail
- Revenue model: subscription: recurring fees for software modules, paid per employee per month; service fees: implementation, training and payroll tax filing services; interest or premiums: interest earned on client funds held between payroll debit and payment
- Value chain position: software / platform: develops and hosts its own cloud HR and payroll platform, sold to employers; service provider: runs payroll processing and tax filings for employer clients
- Geography served: US national: serves employers across the United States
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: payroll, HR, talent, time and benefits modules sharing one platform and customer base
- Distribution channels: direct sales: in-house sales team selling to employers; partners / resellers: referral and integration partners such as accounting and benefits firms; online: self-service platform used by client employers and employees
- Regulatory exposure: medium: payroll tax filing and money movement rules, plus data privacy and security requirements
