Target: Affiliated Managers Group, Inc. (www.amg.com)
- Core offering: Holds stakes in independent investment management firms and supports them with capital, distribution and operations. Sells investment management services to institutional and individual investors through these affiliates, earning fees on assets managed.
- Primary products: mutual funds; separate accounts; alternative investment funds; wealth management portfolios
- Primary services: investment management; investment advisory; wealth management; distribution support
- Customer segment: B2B: pension funds, endowments, foundations, insurers, sovereign and corporate institutions; B2C: individual and high-net-worth investors served through affiliate wealth management firms; B2G: public pension plans and other government retirement systems
- End markets: asset management; retirement savings; wealth management; financial services
- Revenue model: commissions or management fees: asset-based management and advisory fees, and performance fees, paid by funds and clients for investment management
- Value chain position: financial intermediary: takes in client capital and invests it through affiliate managers, selling investment products to institutions and individuals
- Geography served: global: North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific and other regions through affiliate firms
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: diversified: multiple affiliate firms running equity, fixed income, alternatives and multi-asset strategies for different clients
- Distribution channels: direct sales: affiliate institutional sales teams selling to pensions and endowments; partners / resellers: financial advisers, wirehouses and wealth platforms that place client assets
- Regulatory exposure: high: affiliates are registered investment advisers and broker-dealers overseen by the SEC, FINRA and foreign regulators
