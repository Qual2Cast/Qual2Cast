Target: Boku, Inc. (www.boku.com)

- Primary products: carrier billing platform; digital wallet; payment processing software
- Primary services: payment processing; fraud prevention; merchant onboarding; payment reconciliation
- Customer segment: B2B: digital content merchants, online game publishers, streaming services, ride-hailing and e-commerce businesses
- End markets: digital content; gaming; streaming; ride-hailing; e-commerce
- Revenue model: transaction or usage fees: fees merchants pay per processed consumer payment
- Value chain position: software / platform: payment platform connecting merchants to mobile operators and payment methods, sold to digital merchants; financial intermediary: collects consumer payments and settles funds with merchants
- Geography served: global: Europe, North America, Asia-Pacific, Latin America and other regions through mobile operator partnerships
- Company size: mid-sized
- Product specialization: niche specialist: mobile and carrier billing payment processing
- Distribution channels: direct sales: merchant accounts sold and managed directly by the company; partners / resellers: mobile network operators and payment aggregators that carry the payment method
- Regulatory exposure: medium: payment services rules, anti-money-laundering and data privacy rules in multiple countries, and card industry security standards
