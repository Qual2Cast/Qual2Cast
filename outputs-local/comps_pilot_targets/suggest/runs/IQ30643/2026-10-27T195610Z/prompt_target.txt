Target: Labcorp Holdings Inc. (www.labcorp.com)
- Core offering: Provides clinical laboratory testing, drug development services and diagnostic information to healthcare providers, pharmaceutical companies, and patients. Runs a large network of patient service sites and laboratories, and bills payers, providers, and patients for testing.
- Primary products: diagnostic lab tests; diagnostic imaging services; drug testing kits; genetic testing; medical devices and reagents
- Primary services: clinical laboratory testing; contract clinical research; central laboratory services; drug development support; patient specimen collection
- Customer segment: B2B: hospitals, physician practices, health systems, pharmaceutical and biotechnology sponsors, employers; B2C: individual patients ordering consumer-directed lab tests; B2G: government health programs and public agencies
- End markets: healthcare; life sciences; pharmaceuticals; biotechnology; insurance
- Revenue model: healthcare reimbursement: payments from insurers and government programs for lab testing; service fees: fees for clinical trial and drug development services; product sales: diagnostic kits, reagents and devices sold to labs and providers; project or contract: multi-year contracts with pharmaceutical sponsors for trial services
- Value chain position: service provider: performs testing and trial services, selling to providers, insurers and sponsors; asset operator: operates laboratory and patient service site networks; OEM / manufacturer: makes some diagnostic reagents and kits sold to other labs
- Geography served: global: United States and many countries across Europe, Asia and other regions
- Company size: among the largest
- Product specialization: diversified: diagnostics laboratory services, clinical trial and drug development services, and related patient testing lines
- Distribution channels: direct sales: sales teams selling to hospitals, pharma sponsors and health systems; own locations: patient service centers and laboratory sites; partners / resellers: health system and hospital outreach lab partnerships; online: consumer-directed test ordering through websites
- Regulatory exposure: high: CLIA-certified laboratory testing, FDA rules for diagnostics and trials, and HIPAA privacy rules
