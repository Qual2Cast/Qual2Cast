Target: NVR, Inc. (www.nvrinc.com)

- Primary services: mortgage origination; title insurance; homeowners insurance brokerage

- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: homebuilding under several brands, plus mortgage banking, title and insurance services for homebuyers
- Distribution channels: direct sales: in-house sales staff at communities selling to individual homebuyers; own locations: model homes and sales offices in its communities
