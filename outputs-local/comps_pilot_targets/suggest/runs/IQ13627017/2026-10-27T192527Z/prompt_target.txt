Target: Emergent BioSolutions Inc. (www.emergentbiosolutions.com)

- Primary services: contract development and manufacturing; biologics fill-finish; process development
- Customer segment: B2G: US and foreign government stockpile and biodefense agencies, such as BARDA and the Department of Defense; B2B: pharmaceutical companies buying contract manufacturing, wholesalers and pharmacies buying overdose products
- End markets: government biodefense; public health; pharmaceutical; healthcare
- Revenue model: product sales: vaccines, antitoxins and overdose products sold to governments and wholesalers; project or contract: government procurement and development contracts for stockpiled countermeasures; service fees: contract development and manufacturing services for other drug makers; licensing or royalties: royalties on overdose products sold by licensing partners
- Value chain position: OEM / manufacturer: makes vaccines and antitoxins in its own plants, sold to governments and wholesalers; brand owner: markets overdose nasal spray made with partners, sold to pharmacies and wholesalers; service provider: manufactures biologic drugs for other companies, which supply the drug and pay fees
- Geography served: global: Sells to US federal and state governments and to foreign governments and health agencies worldwide
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: biodefense vaccines, antitoxins and antidotes, and contract manufacturing for drug makers
- Distribution channels: direct sales: direct government contracts and sales to national stockpile agencies; distributors / dealers: wholesalers that supply overdose products to pharmacies; third-party retail: pharmacies and retailers selling the overdose nasal spray over the counter
- Regulatory exposure: high: vaccines and drugs need FDA approval and licenses, manufacturing under FDA good manufacturing rules
