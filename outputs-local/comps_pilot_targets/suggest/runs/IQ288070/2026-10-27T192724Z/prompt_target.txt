Target: Middlesex Water Company (www.middlesexwater.com)
- Core offering: Regulated water and wastewater utility that supplies drinking water and collects and treats wastewater for homes, businesses and institutions, mainly in New Jersey and Delaware. It also operates water and wastewater systems under contract for municipalities and private owners. It delivers service through owned treatment plants, wells, pipelines and pumping stations, and rates are set by state utility regulators.
- Primary products: potable water
- Primary services: wastewater collection and treatment; contract operation and maintenance of municipal water systems; water system construction and repair
- Customer segment: B2C: residential water and sewer customers; B2B: commercial and industrial water customers; B2G: municipalities that contract for operation of their water and wastewater systems
- End markets: residential water supply; commercial and industrial water use; municipal utilities
- Revenue model: service fees: metered charges for water and wastewater service under regulated tariffs; project or contract: contract operation and maintenance fees and infrastructure work for municipalities and others
- Value chain position: asset operator: owns and runs water treatment plants, wells, pipelines and wastewater systems, sells water service to customers; service provider: operates water and wastewater systems under contract for municipalities and private owners
- Geography served: US regional: New Jersey and Delaware
- Company size: mid-sized
- Product specialization: related lines: regulated water utility, regulated wastewater service and contract utility operations, which share customers, assets and technical staff
- Distribution channels: direct sales: utility service billed directly to residential, commercial and industrial customers; other: contract operations agreements with municipalities and private system owners
- Regulatory exposure: high: regulated utility rates and service in New Jersey and Delaware, overseen by state public utility commissions, plus federal and state drinking water and wastewater standards
