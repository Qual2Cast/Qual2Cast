Target: ADMA Biologics, Inc. (www.admabiologics.com)
- Core offering: Develops, manufactures and sells plasma-derived immune globulin therapies for patients with primary immune deficiency and related immune disorders. It collects plasma through its own US donor centers and sells the finished treatments to specialty pharmacies, distributors and infusion providers.
- Primary products: intravenous immune globulin; subcutaneous immune globulin
- Primary services: none
- Customer segment: B2B: specialty pharmacies, distributors, hospitals and infusion clinics that treat immune-deficient patients
- End markets: healthcare; specialty pharmaceuticals; plasma-derived biologics
- Revenue model: product sales: immune globulin therapies sold to pharmacies, distributors and providers
- Value chain position: OEM / manufacturer: manufactures immune globulin from collected plasma, sold to pharmacies and distributors; asset operator: owns and runs US plasma collection centers that supply raw plasma for manufacturing
- Geography served: US national: sells across the United States through specialty channels, manufacturing and plasma collection are US-based
- Company size: mid-sized
- Product specialization: niche specialist: focused on immune globulin therapies for immune deficiency and related disorders
- Distribution channels: distributors / dealers: specialty distributors that supply pharmacies and infusion providers; direct sales: direct sales to hospitals and infusion clinics treating patients; own locations: company-owned plasma collection centers supplying raw material
- Regulatory exposure: high: needs FDA biologics licensing and plasma establishment approvals to manufacture and sell, plasma collection is also FDA-regulated
