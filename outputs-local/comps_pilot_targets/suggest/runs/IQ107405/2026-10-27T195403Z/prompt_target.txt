Target: D.R. Horton, Inc. (www.drhorton.com)
- Core offering: Builds and sells single-family detached and attached homes, mostly to first-time and move-up buyers, and develops residential lots for its own communities. It acquires and develops land, builds homes with purchased materials and subcontractors, and sells them through its own sales centers, with mortgage financing offered to buyers.
- Primary products: single-family detached homes; attached townhomes; residential lots
- Primary services: mortgage origination; title and escrow services; rental property management
- Customer segment: B2C: individual homebuyers, including first-time, move-up and active adult buyers, renters of build-to-rent homes
- End markets: residential construction; residential real estate; home mortgage lending; rental housing
- Revenue model: product sales: homes sold to buyers at closing; interest or premiums: interest income on mortgage loans to home buyers; rental or leasing: rent paid by tenants of its build-to-rent homes; other: sales of finished lots to other homebuilders
- Value chain position: OEM / manufacturer: builds homes from purchased materials on land it develops, sold to individual homebuyers; financial intermediary: originates mortgages for its buyers, sold to investors or lenders; asset operator: owns and leases build-to-rent homes to renters
- Geography served: US national: homebuilding in more than two dozen states across the United States
- Company size: among the largest
- Product specialization: related lines: single-family homes, attached homes, lot development, mortgage and title services, build-to-rent homes
- Distribution channels: own locations: model homes and sales centers in its communities; online: company website for browsing homes and starting purchases; agents / brokers: real estate brokers who bring buyers to its sales centers
- Regulatory exposure: medium: building codes, zoning, environmental and land-development rules, mortgage lending licenses for its financing subsidiary
