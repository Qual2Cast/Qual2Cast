- End markets: life science research; clinical diagnostics; pharmaceutical development; food safety testing
- Revenue model: product sales: instruments, reagents, antibodies and diagnostic test kits sold to labs and hospitals; service fees: instrument service contracts, training and custom development work; licensing or royalties: patent licenses for digital PCR and related technologies
- Value chain position: OEM / manufacturer: makes instruments, reagents and diagnostic kits, sold to labs and hospitals; brand owner: designs and sells research and clinical brands, some made with contract partners, to end users
- Geography served: global: sells in North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific and other regions worldwide
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: life science research products and clinical diagnostics, sharing lab customers and instrument platforms
- Distribution channels: direct sales: sales teams selling to large research and hospital accounts; distributors / dealers: independent distributors in many countries and regions; online: online catalog and ordering for research reagents
- Regulatory exposure: high: clinical diagnostic products need FDA clearance or approval and comply with CLIA and IVD device rules
