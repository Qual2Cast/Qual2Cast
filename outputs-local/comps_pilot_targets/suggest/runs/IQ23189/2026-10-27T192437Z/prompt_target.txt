Target: Corebridge Financial, Inc. (www.corebridgefinancial.com)
- Core offering: Provides annuities, life insurance and retirement plan products to individuals and employers in the US. It funds these products with premiums and deposits, invests the money, and pays benefits and withdrawals over time.
- Primary products: fixed and variable annuities; life insurance; group retirement plans
- Primary services: retirement plan administration; asset management; institutional investment services
- Customer segment: B2C: individuals saving for retirement and buying life insurance; B2B: employers sponsoring retirement plans, institutional investors
- End markets: retirement savings; life insurance; asset management
- Revenue model: interest or premiums: premiums on life and annuity contracts, investment income on policy reserves; commissions or management fees: fees on assets under management; service fees: fees for administering retirement plans
- Value chain position: financial intermediary: takes premiums and deposits from savers, invests them and pays benefits to policyholders; service provider: administers retirement plans for employers and plan participants
- Geography served: US national: all US states, through independent and employer channels
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: life insurance, retirement annuities, group retirement plans and asset management
- Distribution channels: partners / resellers: banks and broker-dealers that sell its annuities and life policies; agents / brokers: independent insurance agents and financial professionals; direct sales: sales teams serving employers and retirement plan sponsors
- Regulatory exposure: high: insurance products need state insurance department approval, variable annuities are overseen by the SEC and FINRA
