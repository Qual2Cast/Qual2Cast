Target: XPO, Inc. (www.xpo.com)
- Core offering: Provides less-than-truckload freight transportation across North America for businesses shipping palletized and smaller freight. It moves shipments through a network of service centers and a fleet of tractors and trailers.
- Primary products: none
- Primary services: less-than-truckload freight transportation; regional and interregional freight delivery; freight consolidation
- Customer segment: B2B: manufacturers, retailers, distributors and other businesses shipping freight
- End markets: manufacturing; retail; construction; industrial; consumer goods
- Revenue model: service fees: per-shipment freight charges paid by shippers
- Value chain position: service provider: transports shippers' freight, sold to manufacturers, retailers and distributors; asset operator: owns and runs trucks, trailers and service center terminals
- Geography served: North America: US, Canada and Mexico through a national service center network
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: niche specialist: LTL freight transportation only, after separating its other logistics businesses
- Distribution channels: direct sales: national accounts and shipper contracts sold by its own sales teams; own locations: service center terminals where freight is received and dispatched; online: digital platform for booking and tracking shipments
- Regulatory exposure: medium: DOT and FMCSA safety, driver hours-of-service and vehicle emissions rules
