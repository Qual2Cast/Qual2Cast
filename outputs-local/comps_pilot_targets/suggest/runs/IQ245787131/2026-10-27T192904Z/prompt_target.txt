Target: SmartStop Self Storage REIT, Inc. (www.smartstopselfstorage.com)
- Core offering: Owns, operates and manages self-storage facilities that rent storage units to individuals and small businesses, mainly in the US with some properties in Canada. Revenue comes from unit rentals and ancillary fees, and the company also manages storage properties for third-party owners.
- Primary products: storage units; packing and moving supplies
- Primary services: self-storage rental; property management for third-party owners; tenant insurance
- Customer segment: B2C: individuals storing household goods, vehicles and personal items; B2B: small businesses and commercial tenants renting storage space
- End markets: consumer storage; residential moving; small business storage
- Revenue model: rental or leasing: customers pay monthly rent for storage units; service fees: tenant insurance and administrative or ancillary fees; product sales: boxes, locks and moving supplies sold at facilities; commissions or management fees: fees for managing storage properties owned by third parties
- Value chain position: asset operator: owns and runs self-storage buildings, takes in rent from storage tenants; service provider: manages storage facilities and tenant services for property owners and tenants
- Geography served: North America: primarily US states such as Texas, Florida, California and Arizona, plus Canadian properties in Ontario
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: niche specialist: self-storage facilities only
- Distribution channels: own locations: company-owned storage facilities where customers rent units; online: website and online rental reservations for storage units
- Regulatory exposure: medium: property, zoning, consumer and securities rules, plus REIT tax requirements, no operating license needed
