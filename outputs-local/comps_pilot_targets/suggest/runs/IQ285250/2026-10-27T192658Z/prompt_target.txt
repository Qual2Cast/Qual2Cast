- Primary services: digital marketing services; commercial printing; direct mail distribution

- End markets: local retail; automotive; real estate; healthcare; legal and public notices
- Revenue model: advertising: print and digital ads sold to local businesses; subscription: print and digital news subscriptions paid by readers; service fees: commercial printing and marketing services for businesses; other: fees for legal and public notice placements
- Value chain position: service provider: produces local news and sells audiences and advertising to local businesses and readers; OEM / manufacturer: prints its newspapers and commercial jobs in its own plants, sold to readers and businesses
- Geography served: US national: dozens of local markets across many states, mainly Midwest, Mountain West and Southeast
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: newspapers, digital news and advertising, commercial printing and marketing services
- Distribution channels: direct sales: in-house advertising sales teams selling to local businesses; online: company websites and mobile apps for digital news and subscriptions; third-party retail: newsstands and retail stores selling single copies; other: home delivery of print editions by carriers and mail
- Regulatory exposure: medium: labor, postal and data privacy rules, state public notice laws for legal advertising
