Target: Aveanna Healthcare Holdings Inc. (www.aveanna.com)

- End markets: healthcare; home health care; pediatric care
- Revenue model: healthcare reimbursement: government program and managed care payments for nursing, therapy and personal care visits
- Value chain position: service provider: delivers nursing and therapy services in patients' homes, paid by payers such as Medicaid and managed care plans
- Geography served: US national: many states across the United States
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: pediatric nursing, adult home nursing, pediatric therapy and personal care, all delivered in the home
