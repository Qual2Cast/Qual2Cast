- End markets: consumer credit; banking; financial services
- Revenue model: other: collections on purchased charged-off consumer debt portfolios
- Value chain position: financial intermediary: buys charged-off consumer debt from lenders and keeps the collections; service provider: collects debt for its own portfolios from consumers
- Geography served: global: US nationwide, also debt collection operations in Europe
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: niche specialist: purchase and collection of charged-off consumer debt only
- Distribution channels: direct sales: phone, mail and digital contact with debtors and debt sellers; online: consumer payment portals and websites for paying debts
- Regulatory exposure: high: debt collection under the FDCPA and state collection licenses, overseen by CFPB and state regulators
