Target: OTC Markets Group Inc. (www.otcmarkets.com)
- Core offering: Operates electronic marketplaces for over-the-counter securities, along with market data and corporate disclosure services. It sells trading access, quotation data and compliance services to public companies, broker-dealers, investors and data vendors, and earns fees from listings, data subscriptions and trading activity.
- Primary products: market data feeds; quotation and trading tiers for OTC securities; corporate disclosure platform; investor research data
- Primary services: issuer compliance and disclosure services; alternative trading system access for broker-dealers; corporate news distribution
- Customer segment: B2B: public and reporting companies, broker-dealers, institutional investors, financial data vendors
- End markets: securities trading; capital markets; financial information services
- Revenue model: subscription: market data subscriptions and annual tier fees paid by quoted companies; transaction or usage fees: trading fees on the alternative trading system, data feed usage fees; service fees: fees for disclosure, news distribution and compliance services
- Value chain position: software / platform: operates electronic quotation and trading systems that broker-dealers and investors use to trade OTC securities; service provider: takes in fees from public companies for disclosure and compliance services and sells data to market participants
- Geography served: global: primarily US-listed OTC securities and US broker-dealers, also foreign issuers and international investors
- Company size: mid-sized
- Product specialization: related lines: OTC marketplace tiers, market data, and issuer disclosure services, which share the same public company and investor customers
- Distribution channels: direct sales: sales teams selling tier listings and data subscriptions to companies and firms; online: electronic trading and public quotation websites for investors and brokers; partners / resellers: financial data vendors and brokers that redistribute market data
- Regulatory exposure: high: operates an alternative trading system and broker-dealer activity overseen by the SEC and FINRA
