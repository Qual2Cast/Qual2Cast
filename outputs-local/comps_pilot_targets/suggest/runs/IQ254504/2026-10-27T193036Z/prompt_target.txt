Target: W. R. Berkley Corporation (www.berkley.com)
- Core offering: Underwrites specialty property and casualty insurance and reinsurance for businesses and some individuals. Sells mainly through independent agents and brokers, earning premiums while paying claims and investing reserves.
- Primary products: commercial liability insurance; commercial auto insurance; property insurance; workers' compensation insurance; professional liability insurance; reinsurance
- Primary services: claims handling for insured businesses; risk management for policyholders
- Customer segment: B2B: businesses across many industries buying liability, property and workers' compensation coverage; B2C: individuals buying personal auto and property coverage
- End markets: commercial construction; transportation; healthcare; manufacturing; professional services
- Revenue model: interest or premiums: premiums paid for insurance policies, plus investment income on held funds
- Value chain position: financial intermediary: takes in premiums from businesses and individuals and bears the insured risk
- Geography served: global: all US states, plus Europe, Asia, Latin America and Canada
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: commercial specialty, excess and surplus, professional liability, workers' compensation, reinsurance and international insurance units
- Distribution channels: agents / brokers: independent agents and wholesale brokers placing business with the company; direct sales: in-house underwriters selling directly to some insureds; own locations: regional offices across the US and in international markets
- Regulatory exposure: high: insurance licensing and solvency rules enforced by state insurance regulators, the NAIC and foreign insurance regulators
