Target: Universal Health Services, Inc. (uhs.com)
- Core offering: Operates acute care hospitals, behavioral health facilities and outpatient services that treat patients in the US and UK. Patients, insurers and government programs pay for care delivered in its owned facilities.
- Primary products: none
- Primary services: acute care hospital services; behavioral health treatment; emergency care; outpatient and ambulatory care
- Customer segment: B2C: patients receiving hospital, emergency and behavioral health care; B2B: commercial health insurers and managed care plans paying for patient care; B2G: Medicare and Medicaid programs paying for patient care
- End markets: healthcare; behavioral health
- Revenue model: healthcare reimbursement: payments from Medicare, Medicaid, commercial insurers and patients for hospital and clinic care
- Value chain position: service provider: delivers hospital and behavioral health care, billing payers and patients for treatment; asset operator: owns and runs hospitals and treatment facilities, selling bed days and procedures to patients and payers
- Geography served: global: US states nationwide, plus behavioral health hospitals in the United Kingdom
- Company size: among the largest
- Product specialization: related lines: acute care hospitals, behavioral health facilities and outpatient services sharing patients and referral networks
- Distribution channels: own locations: owned acute care hospitals, behavioral health facilities and outpatient clinics; other: physician referrals and emergency department admissions
- Regulatory exposure: high: hospital and facility licensing by state health departments, plus Medicare and Medicaid certification rules from CMS
