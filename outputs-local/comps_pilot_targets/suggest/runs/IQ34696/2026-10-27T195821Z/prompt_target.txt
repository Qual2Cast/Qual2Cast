- Company size: large
- Product specialization: niche specialist: supply chain collaboration and EDI software and services for retail trading partners
- Distribution channels: direct sales: direct sales teams selling to retailers and suppliers; partners / resellers: technology and service partners that refer or implement the platform
- Regulatory exposure: low: only normal business rules and data security and privacy obligations apply
