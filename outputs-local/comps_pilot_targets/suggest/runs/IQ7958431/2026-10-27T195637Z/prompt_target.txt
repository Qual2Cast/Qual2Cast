Target: National CineMedia, Inc. (www.ncm.com)
- Core offering: Sells pre-show and on-screen advertising across a network of movie theaters, reaching moviegoers before films. It also distributes live and recorded alternative content events to theaters and shares ad revenue with theater exhibitor partners.
- Primary products: on-screen cinema advertising; lobby and digital place-based displays; alternative content events
- Primary services: advertising sales and campaign management; event distribution to cinemas; audience measurement and reporting
- Customer segment: B2B: advertisers, advertising agencies, movie studios, event promoters
- End markets: advertising; entertainment; live events
- Revenue model: advertising: advertisers pay for screen time and placements in theaters; service fees: event producers and promoters pay to distribute content to theaters
- Value chain position: service provider: sells and operates advertising across partner theaters, paying exhibitors a share of revenue, sold to advertisers and agencies; distributor: distributes live and recorded events to cinemas, taking in event fees, sold to event producers and promoters
- Geography served: US national: Network of theaters across the United States
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: cinema advertising, digital place-based media, and alternative content events sharing theater audiences and channels
- Distribution channels: direct sales: in-house sales team selling to national and local advertisers and agencies; partners / resellers: theater exhibitor partners hosting screens and displays
- Regulatory exposure: low: Normal business rules and advertising standards, no special industry license required
