- Value chain position: OEM / manufacturer: designs hardware and sources manufacturing from contract partners, sold to channel partners and enterprises; software / platform: develops security operating systems, cloud platforms and threat intelligence services sold to customers

- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: network security hardware, network access and switching, endpoint and email security, and security operations software sharing customers and channels
