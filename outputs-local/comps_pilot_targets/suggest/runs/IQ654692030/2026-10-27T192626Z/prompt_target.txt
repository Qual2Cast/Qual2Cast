Target: IAC Inc. (www.iac.com)
- Core offering: Owns and operates digital media brands that publish lifestyle, health, food, home and personal finance content for consumers, and sells advertising and commerce placements against that audience to brands and agencies. Revenue comes from ads on its websites and apps, sponsored content, and affiliate commissions on product recommendations.
- Primary products: digital publications; print magazines; recipe and cooking sites; health information sites; personal finance sites
- Primary services: custom advertising and sponsored content; affiliate commerce; audience data and marketing services
- Customer segment: B2C: readers and consumers who use its websites, apps and magazines; B2B: advertisers, brand marketers, advertising agencies and retail partners
- End markets: digital advertising; publishing; consumer lifestyle; health information; retail and e-commerce
- Revenue model: advertising: display, video and sponsored content sold to brands and agencies; commissions or management fees: affiliate commissions when readers buy recommended products; licensing or royalties: licensing of editorial content and brand names to third parties
- Value chain position: brand owner: owns consumer media titles and sells their audiences to advertisers; service provider: creates custom marketing content and campaigns for advertisers
- Geography served: global: Largest audience in the United States, with international editions and audiences in other countries
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: lifestyle, health, food, home and personal finance brands that share advertisers and digital audiences
- Distribution channels: online: own websites and mobile apps where readers access content directly; partners / resellers: search engines, social media platforms and content syndication partners; direct sales: advertising sales teams selling to brands and agencies
- Regulatory exposure: medium: consumer data privacy and advertising rules, including FTC endorsement and affiliate disclosure rules
