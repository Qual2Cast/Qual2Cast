Target: DexCom, Inc. (www.dexcom.com)
- Core offering: Designs, manufactures and sells continuous glucose monitoring systems that track glucose through a wearable sensor. Sold to people with diabetes, and to pharmacies, distributors and healthcare providers, with readings shown on a receiver or smartphone app.
- Primary products: continuous glucose monitoring systems; disposable glucose sensors; transmitters; receivers; smartphone app
- Primary services: none
- Customer segment: B2C: people with diabetes who wear sensors and use the app; B2B: pharmacies, durable medical equipment suppliers, distributors, health systems, employers and insurers
- End markets: diabetes care; healthcare; consumer health
- Revenue model: product sales: sensors, transmitters and receivers sold to patients, pharmacies and distributors; healthcare reimbursement: payments from insurers and government programs for covered glucose monitoring
- Value chain position: OEM / manufacturer: designs and manufactures sensors and transmitters, sold to patients and healthcare channels; software / platform: smartphone app and data sharing platform used by patients and clinicians
- Geography served: global: United States is the largest market, also Europe, Asia Pacific and other countries
- Company size: among the largest
- Product specialization: niche specialist: continuous glucose monitoring systems only
- Distribution channels: third-party retail: retail pharmacies that dispense sensors to patients; distributors / dealers: durable medical equipment suppliers and specialty distributors; online: direct-to-consumer online ordering for over-the-counter glucose monitoring; direct sales: sales teams serving health systems, clinics and employer programs
- Regulatory exposure: high: medical devices needing FDA clearance, reimbursement rules from CMS and private insurers
