Target: Brookdale Senior Living Inc. (www.brookdale.com)
- Core offering: Operates senior living communities offering independent living, assisted living, memory care and skilled nursing to older adults. Residents pay monthly fees privately or through government health programs, and the company also manages communities owned by third parties.
- Primary products: none
- Primary services: assisted living care; memory care; skilled nursing; independent living; community management
- Customer segment: B2C: older adults and their families paying for senior housing and care; B2G: Medicare and Medicaid programs reimbursing skilled nursing and therapy
- End markets: senior living; healthcare; senior care
- Revenue model: service fees: monthly rent and care fees paid by residents; healthcare reimbursement: Medicare and Medicaid payments for skilled nursing and therapy services; commissions or management fees: fees for managing communities owned by third parties
- Value chain position: asset operator: owns or leases and runs senior living communities, taking in resident fees and selling care to residents; service provider: delivers personal, nursing and therapy care directly to residents
- Geography served: US national: communities across many states in the United States
- Company size: among the largest
- Product specialization: related lines: independent living, assisted living, memory care and skilled nursing communities sharing residents and care services
- Distribution channels: direct sales: community sales staff meeting prospective residents and families; own locations: tours and move-ins at its own communities; agents / brokers: referrals from hospitals, discharge planners and placement agencies
- Regulatory exposure: high: licensed assisted living and skilled nursing operations under state licensing agencies, plus Medicare and Medicaid certification rules
