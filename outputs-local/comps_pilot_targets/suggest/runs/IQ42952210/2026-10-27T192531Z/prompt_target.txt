- Primary services: aftermarket maintenance and service; technical support for desalination plants
- Customer segment: B2B: desalination plant owners, engineering and construction contractors, water system integrators; B2G: municipal and government-owned water utilities
- End markets: water desalination; industrial process water; wastewater treatment
- Revenue model: product sales: energy recovery devices and high-pressure pumps sold to plant builders and owners; service fees: aftermarket service, maintenance, and replacement parts for installed equipment
- Value chain position: OEM / manufacturer: builds energy recovery devices and pumps, sold to desalination plant builders and owners; service provider: maintains installed equipment for plant operators under service contracts
- Geography served: global: Installed base across Middle East, Asia, Americas, Europe, and Australia desalination plants
- Company size: mid-sized
- Product specialization: niche specialist: focused on pressure energy recovery devices and related high-pressure pumps for desalination
- Distribution channels: direct sales: sales teams selling directly to plant owners and large engineering contractors; partners / resellers: system integrators and pump partners that include the equipment in packaged plants
- Regulatory exposure: medium: pressure equipment safety standards and water quality and environmental rules that apply to desalination plants
