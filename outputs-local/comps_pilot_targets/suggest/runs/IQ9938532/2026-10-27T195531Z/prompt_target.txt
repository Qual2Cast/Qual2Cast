Target: Herbalife Ltd. (www.herbalife.com)
- Core offering: Sells nutrition, weight management, energy and sports, and personal care products worldwide. Products are sold mainly through independent distributors who buy them for personal use and resale, and the company manufactures most of its products itself.
- Primary products: nutrition shakes and meal replacements; protein and nutritional supplements; energy and sports drinks; personal care products
- Primary services: nutrition clubs; weight-management coaching; distributor training
- Customer segment: B2C: individual consumers buying for personal use, including independent distributors who buy products for themselves
- End markets: nutrition and weight management; sports and fitness; personal care
- Revenue model: product sales: nutritional, weight-management and personal care products sold to distributors and consumers; other: membership and sales aid fees paid by distributors
- Value chain position: OEM / manufacturer: makes its nutrition and personal care products in its own facilities, sold to distributors and consumers; brand owner: designs and markets its own brands, sold through its distributor network
- Geography served: global: sells in many countries across North America, Europe, Asia, and Latin America
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: nutrition shakes, weight management, energy and sports products, and personal care, sharing the same distributor channel
- Distribution channels: partners / resellers: independent distributors who buy products and resell them to customers; online: company website and app for ordering products; own locations: company-operated nutrition clubs offering products and coaching
- Regulatory exposure: medium: dietary supplement and food labeling rules from FDA and FTC, plus multi-level marketing and advertising rules
