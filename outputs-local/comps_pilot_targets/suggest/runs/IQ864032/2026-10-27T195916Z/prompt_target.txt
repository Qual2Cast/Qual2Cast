Target: United Parks & Resorts Inc. (www.unitedparks.com)
- Core offering: Operates regional theme parks with marine animal habitats, animal shows, thrill rides and water attractions. It sells park admission, annual passes and in-park food and merchandise to families and tourists across the United States.
- Primary products: food and beverage; merchandise; souvenirs; annual passes
- Primary services: theme park admission; live entertainment shows; animal care and rescue; group and event hosting
- Customer segment: B2C: families, tourists and local residents buying tickets and annual passes
- End markets: family entertainment; leisure and tourism; hospitality
- Revenue model: service fees: admission tickets and access to park attractions; subscription: annual passes and memberships; product sales: food, beverages and merchandise sold inside parks
- Value chain position: asset operator: owns and runs its theme parks, animal habitats and rides; service provider: takes in guest admission and spending and sells experiences to individual visitors
- Geography served: US national: parks in Florida, Texas, California, Virginia, Ohio and other US states
- Company size: among the largest
- Product specialization: related lines: marine animal parks, animal-themed parks, water parks and themed resort attractions
- Distribution channels: own locations: company-operated theme parks; online: websites and apps for ticket and annual pass sales; partners / resellers: travel agents and ticket resellers
- Regulatory exposure: medium: animal welfare rules under USDA and the Animal Welfare Act, state and local ride safety and health inspections
