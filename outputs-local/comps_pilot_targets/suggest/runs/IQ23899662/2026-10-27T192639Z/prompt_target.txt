Target: International Money Express, Inc. (www.intermexonline.com)
- Core offering: Provides international money transfers, mainly sending remittances from the US to family members in Latin America and the Caribbean. Senders pay at retail agent locations or online, and recipients collect cash or receive funds in bank accounts.
- Primary products: none
- Primary services: money transfers; currency exchange
- Customer segment: B2C: individuals, largely immigrants, sending money to relatives abroad
- End markets: consumer remittances; financial services
- Revenue model: transaction or usage fees: fees per transfer and currency exchange spreads paid by senders
- Value chain position: service provider: runs transfers and payouts for individual senders; financial intermediary: moves money between senders and recipients through payout partners
- Geography served: North America: sends from the US to Mexico, Central and South America and the Caribbean
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: niche specialist: single line: consumer money transfers
- Distribution channels: partners / resellers: retail agent locations accepting cash from senders; online: company website and mobile app
- Regulatory exposure: high: licensed money transmission requiring state licenses, FinCEN and anti-money laundering rules
