Target: eHealth, Inc. (www.ehealthinsurance.com)
- Core offering: Online health insurance marketplace that helps individuals, families and Medicare beneficiaries compare and enroll in plans from insurance carriers. Licensed agents assist by phone and online, and carriers pay commissions on enrolled policies.
- Primary products: Medicare plans; individual and family health plans; life insurance; dental and vision plans
- Primary services: insurance plan enrollment assistance; licensed agent advice; plan comparison
- Customer segment: B2C: Medicare beneficiaries, individuals and families buying health and life insurance; B2B: insurance carriers that pay commissions on enrolled policies
- End markets: health insurance; life insurance; senior health
- Revenue model: commissions or management fees: carrier commissions paid for each enrolled insurance policy
- Value chain position: financial intermediary: sells carrier policies to consumers, paid by carriers, sold to insurance carriers and consumers; software / platform: online comparison and enrollment platform for consumers and agents
- Geography served: US national: all 50 states through carrier networks
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: Medicare, individual and family health, life, dental and vision plans sold through shared agents and platform
- Distribution channels: online: website for consumers to compare and enroll in plans; direct sales: licensed agents selling by phone to consumers; agents / brokers: licensed insurance agents placing policies with carriers
- Regulatory exposure: high: insurance producer licensing by state regulators, Medicare marketing rules from CMS, HIPAA and telemarketing rules
