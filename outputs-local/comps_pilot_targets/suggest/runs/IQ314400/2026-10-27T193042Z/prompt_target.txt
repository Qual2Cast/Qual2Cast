Target: Williams-Sonoma, Inc. (www.williams-sonomainc.com)
- Core offering: Sells home furnishings, kitchenware, decor and outdoor goods to households and trade clients through its own stores, websites and catalogs. It designs much of its range as private-label goods made by outside manufacturers.
- Primary products: kitchenware; furniture; home decor; bedding and bath; lighting
- Primary services: interior design services; gift registry; furniture delivery and assembly
- Customer segment: B2C: households furnishing homes, gift buyers and wedding registrants; B2B: interior designers and hospitality businesses through a trade program
- End markets: home furnishings; residential retail; hospitality
- Revenue model: product sales: home goods sold in stores, online and through catalogs; service fees: delivery, assembly and interior design services
- Value chain position: brand owner: designs private-label home goods made by outside manufacturers; retailer: sells to households and trade clients through stores and websites
- Geography served: global: US stores and websites, stores in Canada and Australia, international franchise partners in Mexico, Middle East and Asia
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: kitchenware, furniture, home decor, bedding, outdoor and kids' furnishings
- Distribution channels: own locations: company-owned retail stores and design studios; online: company websites and e-commerce apps; direct sales: trade program for interior designers and hospitality clients; partners / resellers: international franchise partner stores
- Regulatory exposure: medium: consumer product safety rules, such as CPSC standards, consumer data privacy laws
