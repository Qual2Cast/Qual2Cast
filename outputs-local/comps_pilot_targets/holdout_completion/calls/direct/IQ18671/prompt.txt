- Company size: among the largest
- Product specialization: diversified: separate businesses: lithium, bromine specialties and refining catalysts, each with different customers
- Distribution channels: direct sales: long-term supply contracts with battery makers and large chemical customers; distributors / dealers: chemical distributors serving smaller industrial customers; own locations: company-owned plants and production sites supplying products directly
