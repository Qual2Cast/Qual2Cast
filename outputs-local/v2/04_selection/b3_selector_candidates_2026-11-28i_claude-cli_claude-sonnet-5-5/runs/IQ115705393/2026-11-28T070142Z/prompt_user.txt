Target: Airbnb, Inc. (www.airbnb.com)
- Core offering: Operates an online marketplace connecting hosts who list homes and other stays with guests seeking short- and long-term accommodations. It also offers bookable local experiences and services. It earns fees from hosts and guests on each booking through its website and apps.
- Primary products: none
- Primary services: online lodging marketplace; booking of local experiences; host and guest payment processing; services such as guest support and host tools
- Customer segment: B2C: Travelers booking stays and experiences, individual hosts listing properties; B2B: Professional property managers and boutique hotels listing inventory
- End markets: travel and tourism; short-term lodging; hospitality; local experiences
- Revenue model: commissions or management fees: service fees charged to guests and hosts as a share of each booking; transaction or usage fees: fees on experiences and services booked through the platform
- Value chain position: software / platform: two-sided marketplace matching hosts' listings with guests, via website and apps; financial intermediary: processes payments between guests and hosts, holding funds until check-in
- Geography served: global: Listings in over 220 countries and regions worldwide
- Product specialization: related lines: stays marketplace, experiences and services, all on one booking platform
- Distribution channels: online: Airbnb website and mobile apps; partners / resellers: channel managers and property-management software integrations feeding listings
- Regulatory exposure: medium: Short-term rental laws, local zoning and registration rules, tax collection, consumer protection and data privacy rules
- Sub-sector: online short-term rental and travel experiences marketplace
- Publicly funded: no
