Acima Leasing is a technology company based in Draper, Utah, that focuses on providing flexible lease-to-own solutions for customers across a wide range of durable goods and retailers. The company has partnered with thousands of retailers to offer its lease-to-own options at the point of sale. Upbound Group, Inc.
Brigit is a holistic financial health app based in New York City, New York that helps customers budget better, get their earned wages early, build their credit through savings, protect themselves from identity theft, and find ways to earn and save money. Brigit helps everyday Americans build a better financial future.
Rent-A-Center was and is based in Plano, Texas. The company specializes in renting furniture, appliances, electronics, and computers to customers with flexible lease-to-own payment options and the option to purchase or return the items at any time. The company has grown rapidly over the years, with multiple locations across the United States and Puerto Rico.
Get It Now! and Home Choice offer a retail installment sale option at multiple stores in Wisconsin and Minnesota and offer customers a convenient way to own the best name brands in furniture, appliances, electronics and computers today.
RAC Mexico consists of multiple stores across Mexico that offer customers with little or no credit history the ability to rent with the option to buy goods from the best brands of furniture, appliances, computers, electronics, cell phones, mattresses and motorcycles.
RACFI or Rent-A-Center Franchise International, is a franchise business that specializes in providing rent-to-own home furniture, appliances, electronics and other durable items. RACFI has multiple franchised stores in the United States including Rent-A-Center and RimTyme locations.
