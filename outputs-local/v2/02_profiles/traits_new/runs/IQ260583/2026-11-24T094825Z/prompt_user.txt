About Us | Chemed Corporation
The Company’s day to day operating businesses are managed on a decentralized basis. There are few integrated business functions between the operating units and Chemed (such as sales, marketing or purchasing). Since its inception, the Company has engaged in twelve significant acquisitions or divestitures of diverse business units.
Chemed conducts its business operations in two segments: the VITAS segment (“VITAS”) and the Roto-Rooter segment (“Roto-Rooter”). Roto-Rooter provides plumbing, drain cleaning, excavation, water restoration and other related services to residential and commercial customers.
VITAS Healthcare Corporation is one of the nation's largest, long-standing mission-focused providers of end-of-life are services. Based in Miami, Florida, VITAS operates 59 hospice programs in 15 states (Alabama, California, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Kansas, Missouri, New Jersey, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Texas, Virginia, and Wisconsin) and the District of Columbia. Please visit the VITAS website at www.vitas.com for more information.
Roto-Rooter Plumbing and Water Cleanup
Roto-Rooter offers an ever-expanding variety of plumbing repair and maintenance, drain cleaning and water cleanup services to homeowners, businesses and municipalities. Roto-Rooter operates multiple company-owned branches and independent contractors and 345 franchisees. The total Roto-Rooter system offers services to more than 90% of the U.S. population and approximately 30% of the Canadian population. Please visit the Roto-Rooter website at www.rotorooter.com for more information.
