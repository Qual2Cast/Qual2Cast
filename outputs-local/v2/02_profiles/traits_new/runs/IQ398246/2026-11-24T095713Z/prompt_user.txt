Investor Relations - Kingstone Companies, Inc.
Kingstone Companies, Inc.
Kingstone Companies, Inc. (“Kingstone”) is a northeast regional property and casualty insurance holding company whose principal operating subsidiary is Kingstone Insurance Company (“KICO”). KICO is a New York domiciled carrier writing business through retail and wholesale agents and brokers. KICO is actively writing personal lines and commercial auto insurance in New York, and was the 11th largest writer of homeowners insurance in the State of New York. Kingstone is also licensed in New Jersey, Rhode Island, Massachusetts, Connecticut, Pennsylvania, New Hampshire, and Maine.
