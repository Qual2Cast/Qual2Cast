The InterGroup Corporation is a Delaware corporation as a successor to Mutual Real Estate Investment Trust, a New York real estate investment trust created.
Over the years, the Company has evolved into a diversified operating company with operations in multi-family and commercial real estate and an interest in the hospitality industry through its investment in the Hilton San Francisco Financial District.
The InterGroup owns and operates real estate throughout the United States, with a concentration in Texas and Southern California. Its properties include 16 apartment complexes, two single family houses, one commercial building and one unimproved parcel of land.
