Autonomous Technology | Robotic Mowers and More | The Toro Company
Learn about the innovative autonomous technology being developed across the brands of The Toro Company.
We are committed to driving autonomous solutions in the markets we serve to increase productivity, deliver more consistent results and alleviate some of the challenges our customers face. That includes developing a variety of autonomous solutions for residential, golf and commercial applications and beyond. These solutions are targeted at enabling our customers to do more with less as they face ongoing labor challenges. Our goal is not to replace labor with autonomous products, but to allow staff to focus where they’re needed most — doing more detailed work that requires more skill and decision-making.
When it comes to autonomous technology, it’s not just about innovation – it’s about knowing the turf. Backed by over 100 years of experience in golf course maintenance, Toro’s Greensmaster® eTriflex™ 3360 with GeoLink® Mow delivers with precision, efficiency and superior results. Ideal for intensive fairway mowing, this machine helps golf courses do more with less by allowing superintendents to supervise one or more units while performing other high-value work on the golf course.
With Exmark’s undeniable track record of durability, longevity and ease-of-use, the Turf Tracer with XiQ autonomous technology unleashes a new generation of innovation for landscape professionals. Capable of mowing in manual or autonomous modes and featuring a fully integrated safety system, this machine enables landscapers to leverage their labor resources to do more, more efficiently.
When productivity matters most, the PROLINE™ AMI with autonomous mowing intelligence helps contractors maximize their time and resources, while delivering precise and consistent results. In autonomous mode, crews can map the property, set their mow pattern, and focus on tasks like trimming while the PROLINE mows with repeatable accuracy.
Cutting-Edge Technology for Effortless Mowing
Engineered with cutting performance at its core, the Toro® Turf Pro™ autonomous mower can mow up to 18.5 acres with precision and efficiency. Designed for rough mowing on the golf course, the Turf Pro leverages GNSS+RTK technology for a consistent mowing pattern with minimal overlap. The mobile app allows superintendents to remotely control their fleet of robots at anytime from anywhere.
The Toro® Range Pro™ autonomous golf ball picking robot is designed to streamline the labor-intensive task of collecting golf balls from the driving range. Capable of collecting an impressive 19,500 balls in 24 hours, the Range Pro is a game-changer for golf courses looking to save time and labor while maintaining a clean range for golfers.
