NRC Health is a healthcare experience company in Lincoln, Nebraska. For 45 years, we've helped health systems understand patients, consumers, and employees.
NRC Health is innovating the future of healthcare

NRC Health (National Research Corporation) is a healthcare experience company headquartered in Lincoln, Nebraska. we help health systems, hospitals, and medical groups understand and improve the experiences of patients, consumers, and employees. NRC Health draws on insights from 122 million patient and consumer voices.
