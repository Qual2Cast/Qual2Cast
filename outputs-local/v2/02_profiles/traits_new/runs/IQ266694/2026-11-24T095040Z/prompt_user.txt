About Digi | Digi International
Digi International Inc. (Digi) has been a pioneer in wireless communication, forging the future for connected devices and responding to the needs of the people and enterprises that use them.
Delivering Scalable Solutions for What’s Next
Digi International Inc. has been a pioneer in communications, shaping the future of connected devices while meeting the evolving needs of businesses and the people they serve. Long before the Internet of Things had a name, Digi was building connectivity solutions adapting to new network standards, advancing data communications, and enabling connectivity across emerging technologies.
Today, Digi International delivers a comprehensive ecosystem of solutions across its business units. The Digi business unit provides secure, reliable connectivity and infrastructure for business critical applications, including embedded products, console servers, USB and serial connectivity, and out-of-band (OOB) management, along with SOC 2–compliant cellular routers for always-on connectivity. SmartSense by Digi delivers sensor-based monitoring and compliance solutions that protect assets and ensure quality. Particle by Digi accelerates IoT innovation with an integrated platform for device connectivity, cloud services, and lifecycle management. Opengear by Digi delivers industry-leading resilient network access and automation for data centers and distributed IT environments.
Together, these business units form a portfolio of focused solution providers, each addressing specific IoT and enterprise challenges. Backed by the strength of a company with over 40 years of experience, Digi delivers trusted, secure solutions for today’s critical IT and operational needs, empowering organizations to confidently deploy and manage their connected infrastructure.
