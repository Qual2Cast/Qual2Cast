Powell Industries | About
Discover Powell Industries, a Houston-based leader in electrical engineering and power solutions, with a legacy of innovation and reliability.
1947: Powell Manufacturing Company is founded by William E. Powell, in Houston, Texas.
1968: Powell develops the Power Control Room (PCR) concept for a project in Ponce, Puerto Rico for the Union Carbide Company.
A 75 year legacy and a bright future.
Powell was as a metal-working shop to support the local Houston, Texas petrochemical facilities. While we began small, the company has grown to become the strategic supplier of choice for highly complex and integrated systems for the distribution and control of electrical energy and other critical processes.
