Services | Smith Midland Corporation
Smith-Midland Corporation is a full-service precast concrete manufacturer.
We work closely with our customers to ensure the optimal delivery of products and provide additional value- added services such as:
Full installation of SlenderWall architectural cladding panels, including engineering, erection, insulation, fire-stopping and caulking. These services apply to new construction, re-cladding or over-cladding applications.
One-call-does-it-all. J-J Hooks Safety Barrier rental services include logistics planning for construction sites or secured events, delivery, installation, repositioning of barriers during the project, pick-up when complete, and a full line of accompanying accessories.
Easi-Set and Easi-Span Precast Concrete Buildings, including engineering of standard and custom structures, site preparation, installation, and full outfitting.
Installation of our traditional architectural precast concrete panels and custom architectural products.
Installation of sound wall panel-and-post systems.

About | Smith Midland Corporation
Over sixty years of knowledge working for you
From our emergence in the precast concrete marketplace as Smith-Cattleguard, our focus has always been on creating innovative products to solve problems and contribute to the world around us. We started the company with a new idea – a precast concrete cattleguard to keep the cows from roaming on local farms – but the vision of our founder, David G. Smith, and his son, Rodney I. Smith, soon propelled the company in new directions.
David Smith founds the Smith-Cattleguard Company on the family farm. Son, Rodney Smith, joins company.
First underground precast concrete utility vault product developed for power companies. Utility product line expands.
Smith-Cattleguard outgrows its first facility on the family farm and moves into a new 10,000 sq. ft. plant at the current location.
Concrete Safety Systems company formed to rent precast concrete highway safety barrier.
Easi-Set all-precast concrete buildings developed. Easi-Set Worldwide formed to license Smith-Midland-developed products across the globe.
Reidsville, North Carolina plant opened to meet growing production demands. Roderick Smith would become General Manager.
First sound wall system, “Sierra Wall,” introduced to transportation product line.
The Virginia plant is one of the first round of multiple plants certified in North America by the National Precast Concrete Association (NPCA). Currently certified by both PCI and NPCA.
Smith-Midland develops and patents the J-J Hooks highway safety barrier connection system. Easi-Set licenses the product worldwide.
SlenderWall precast/steel-stud modular building panels developed and licensed by Easi-Set. First project installed, Health Insurance Plan building in North Brunswick, NJ.
Smith-Midland Corporation goes public (SMID) as the holding company for multiple divisions. Rodney Smith is named Chairman.
SlenderWall installed on the tallest building in Virginia, The Westin Luxury Hotel and Residences in Virginia Beach.
Easi-Set licensed producers surpass the 1,000,000 sq. ft. milestone of SoftSound absorptive sound wall production.
Hopkins, South Carolina plant opens – allowing Smith-Midland to reach customers across the mid-Atlantic and southeastern markets.
Ashley Smith chosen as CEO of the Smith-Midland Corporation, the third generation of Smith family leadership.
A second larger manufacturing facility opens in Reidsville, NC greatly expanding capacity to serve customer across Smith-Midland full market area
J-J Hooks total production reaches a milestone of 16 million linear feet worldwide – enough to reach across the United States from New York to Los Angeles.
