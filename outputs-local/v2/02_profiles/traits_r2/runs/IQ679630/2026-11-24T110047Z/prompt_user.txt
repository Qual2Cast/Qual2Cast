Technical Services – AMC Global Media
is a full service network programming origination and distribution company, supplying an array of services to the cable, satellite and broadcast industries. AMC Global Media Broadcasting & Technology’s experience in program delivery in both high-definition and standard-definition is unparalleled in the industry. With more than 30 years of practical and theoretical experience, AMC Global Media Broadcasting & Technology employs first-rate engineering and operations staffs, as well as network services groups, including affiliate engineering, network operations, traffic and scheduling, that can provide seamless day-to-day delivery of any network.
AMC Global Media Broadcasting & Technology is located in Bethpage, New York. This state-of-the-art technical facility consolidates origination and satellite communications functions in one 62,000 square foot facility – an industry first – and keeps AMC Global Media at the forefront of technology, providing both standard – and high-definition digital infrastructure.
AMC Global Media Broadcasting & Technology’s current client base includes AMC’s own AMC, IFC, SundanceTV and We TV, as well as other premier clients including MSG Network, MSG Plus, MSG Varsity, Mid Atlantic Sports Network and SPORTSNET NY. AMC Global Media Broadcasting & Technology delivers services to these high-quality entertainment services 24 hours a day, seven days a week.
