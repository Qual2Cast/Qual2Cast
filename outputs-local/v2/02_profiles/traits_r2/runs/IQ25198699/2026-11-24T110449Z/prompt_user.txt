EACO Corporation (EACO), incorporated on, is a holding company comprised of its wholly-owned subsidiary, Bisco Industries, Inc. (bisco) and bisco’s wholly-owned Canadian subsidiary, Bisco Industries Limited (collectively, the Company). The Company engages in the distribution of electronic components and hardware through its multiple primary divisions: bisco, National-Precision and Fast-Cor. Through its divisions, the Company supplies parts used to manufacture products in a range of industries. Primary industries serviced include aerospace & defense (A&D), industrial equipment, fabrication, printed circuit board (PCB), medical, communication, computer, automotive and marine.

bisco primarily sells product to original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) and contract manufacturers (CMs). bisco’s sells a wide range of products with a strong focus on Aerospace and Defense hardware, commercial hardware, electro-mechanical hardware, access hardware, fasteners, and electronic components. bisco also provides customized services and solutions for a range of production needs, including special packaging, bin stocking, kitting and assembly, bar coding, electronic requisitioning, and integrated supply programs, among others.
Fast-Cor is the distributor’s source for electronic components and hardware. For over 60 years Fast-Cor has focused on servicing wholesale distributors of electronic components and hardware. As specialist in working with other B2B distributors, Fast-Cor has built out a suite of value added services specific to supporting this fast-pace high volume industry.
National-Precision focuses on servicing original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) in the aerospace and defense (A&D), Industrial equipment and fabrication industries. Additionally, National-Precision has a hardware focused product offering with emphasis on both mil-spec and commercial hardware.
