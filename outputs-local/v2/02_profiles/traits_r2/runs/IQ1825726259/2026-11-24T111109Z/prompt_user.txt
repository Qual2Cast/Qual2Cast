Phoenix Education Partners | Overview
Phoenix Education Partners, Inc. is the parent company of University of Phoenix, Inc., a pioneer in online education for working adults. University of Phoenix provides access to higher education opportunities that enable students to develop the knowledge and skills necessary to achieve their professional goals, improve the performance of their organizations and provide leadership and service to their communities.
