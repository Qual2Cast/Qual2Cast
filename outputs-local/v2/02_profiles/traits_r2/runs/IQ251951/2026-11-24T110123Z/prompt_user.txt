Driven by a legacy of innovation, Astec works behind the scenes, transforming challenges into progress and helping build the infrastructure that physically connects the world. OUR BUSINESS LANDSCAPE AND MARKET PHILOSOPHY
At Astec, we design and build the infrastructure that connects the world, and it’s the connections with people that truly move us forward. we brought creative thinking and state-of-the-art technology to industries ready for innovation, backed by a corporate culture known for putting customer service first. Today, our market-leading brands serve a wide range of industries, providing equipment and solutions that are reliable, productive, and sustainable.
Our mission is simple: to design and build state-of-the-art equipment that enables our customers to deliver industry-leading results efficiently and profitably. We combine innovative products with world-class training, education, and support, helping our customers succeed at every stage of their projects.
For our customers, we strive to be the most innovative company in our industry, offering advanced, durable, and environmentally responsible solutions. From equipment to education, service, and support, we go beyond what our competition provides to ensure our customers thrive.
Safety is our first core value, and we invest in training, personal protective equipment, and technology to advance our safety programs — always striving for zero harm.
