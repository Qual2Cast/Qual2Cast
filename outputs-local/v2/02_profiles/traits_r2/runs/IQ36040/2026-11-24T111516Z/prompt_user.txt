Universal Health Services, Inc. Through its subsidiaries, the company operates 30 Acute Care hospitals, more than 380 Behavioral Health inpatient facilities, 35 freestanding emergency departments and multiple outpatient facilities and ambulatory care centers in 40 states in the U.S., Washington, D.C., Puerto Rico, Ireland and the United Kingdom. UHS Inc. subsidiary Talkspace has virtual behavioral health services throughout the United States and Puerto Rico. Prominence Health offers health plans and provider support, and Independence Physician Management develops and manages physician services and urgent care centers.
