[P1.2] Legacy of Leadership in Entertainment and Hospitality
[P1.3] Headquartered in Milwaukee, Marcus Corporation is a leader in the entertainment and hospitality industries, with significant company-owned real estate assets. Marcus Corporation’s theatre division, Marcus Theatres®, is the fourth largest theatre circuit in the U.S. and currently owns or operates 975 screens at multiple locations in 17 states under the Marcus Theatres, Movie Tavern® by Marcus and BistroPlex® brands. The company’s hospitality division, Marcus® Hotels & Resorts, owns and/or manages 17 hotels, resorts and other properties in eight states.
[P1.4] As the nation's fourth largest theatre circuit, Marcus Theatres leads with state-of-the-art amenities and the latest technologies that elevate the moviegoing experience. Marcus Hotels & Resorts is a leading force in hotel management, development and food and beverage – delivering exceptional service and long-term value for both hotel owners and guests. Together, these divisions create a balanced resilient platform.

[P2.2] Since our founding with a single theatre, Marcus Corporation has grown to 78 theatres and 17 hotels and resorts across 19 states with significant company-owned real estate assets.
[P2.3] What began as a single-screen theatre in Ripon, Wisconsin, has become a nationwide leader in entertainment. From those humble beginnings, Marcus Theatres has grown into the fourth largest theatre circuit in the country.
[P2.4] Over the years, the Marcus Theatres moviegoing experience has continued to evolve, embracing the latest technologies and amenities to deliver an outstanding moviegoing experience. Our guests enjoy luxury DreamLounger recliner seating, premium large format screens, amazing sound and compelling food and beverage offerings. Each innovation reflects a commitment to quality and connection – transforming a night at the movies into an experience to remember.
[P2.5] We entered the hospitality industry and made our first major purchase with the iconic Pfister Hotel in Milwaukee. Since then, we have grown into a leading hotel, ownership and management company with a diverse portfolio across the country.
[P2.6] Our properties span independent and branded upper upscale hotels, resorts and lifestyle destinations, limited service lodging, along with award-winning restaurants and bars. Over the decades, we have built deep expertise in hotel management, turnarounds, development and historic renovations. With more than 4,500 rooms and over 345,000 square feet of meeting and event space, our portfolio reflects a longstanding commitment to excellence, innovation and high-quality service.
