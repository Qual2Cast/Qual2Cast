Company: Marriott Vacations Worldwide Corporation (www.marriottvacationsworldwide.com)
Industry (SIC 70): Hotels and Other Lodging Places
About this industry group: ADR is room revenue per room sold; RevPAR is room revenue per available room over the same period. Neither is total hotel revenue per room. Separate owned, managed, franchised and system room populations. STR/CoStar glossary.

Facets:
- lodging_operating_format (Lodging / operating format). Examples: Full-service hotel; Limited-service hotel; Holiday camp.
- room_inventory_ownership_scope (Room inventory / ownership scope). Examples: 3,500 owned rooms; 20,000 managed rooms; 80,000 system rooms.
- adr_room_operators (ADR [room operators]). Examples: $175 room revenue/sold room-night; $220 room revenue/sold room-night.
- revpar_room_operators (RevPAR [room operators]). Examples: $130 room revenue/available room-night; $160 room revenue/available room-night.
- room_occupancy_room_operators (Room occupancy [room operators]). Examples: Sold room-nights / available room-nights: 74%; Same-property occupancy: 80%.
- ownership_management_franchising (Ownership / management / franchising). Examples: Owned hotels; Third-party managed hotels; Franchised hotel brands.
- hotel_demand_seasonality (Hotel demand / seasonality). Examples: Summer resort peak; Weekday corporate demand; Conference-led group demand.
- brand_fee_contract_economics_asset_light_operators (Brand fee / contract economics [asset-light operators]). Examples: Franchise royalty on hotel room revenue; Base management fee on hotel revenue; Incentive fee on defined hotel profit.
