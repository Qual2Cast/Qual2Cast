[P2.3] Delivering Best-in-Class Drybulk Shipping Services
[P2.4] Genco Shipping & Trading Limited is a U.S.-based drybulk shipowner focused on the seaborne transportation of commodities globally. Headquartered in New York City, with offices in Singapore and Copenhagen, Genco Shipping runs a truly global 24-hour operation offering a full-service logistics platform to our customers around the world. Our wholly owned high quality, modern fleet of dry cargo vessels consists of the larger Newcastlemax and Capesize vessels, as well as the medium-sized Ultramax and Supramax vessels, enabling us to carry a wide range of cargoes, including iron ore, grain, steel products, bauxite, cement, nickel ore among other commodities, along worldwide shipping routes.
[P2.5] Since our inception Genco has developed a leading reputation as an owner of high-quality, modern tonnage that meets strict operational, safety, and environmental standards. Our unrelenting focus on achieving the highest industry standards has enabled us to earn the trust of leading multinational charterers.
[P2.6] Genco’s sizeable and leading drybulk platform, which consists of 44 vessels and a team of experienced and dedicated onshore and seaborne professionals, is instrumental in our ability to serve as an essential link in international trade. Consistent with our focus on meeting the exacting needs of customers, we draw upon our considerable internal commercial management to provide a full service logistics solution and through our joint venture, GS Shipmanagement Pte.
[P2.7] Engaging in Responsible Business Policies
[P2.8] Our success building an organization concentrated on achieving the highest level of integrity has translated into measurable results. We intend to continue to focus on this important value, along with our commitment to operational excellence, as we seek to further our industry leadership.
[P2.9] Genco maintains memberships and certifications from a number of organizations including MACN (Maritime Anti-Corruption Network) and TRACE, the world’s leading anti-bribery standard setting organization. TRACE helps companies conduct business ethically and in compliance with the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act, U.K. Bribery Act, and other anti-bribery legislation. TRACE certification signifies that we have completed internationally accepted, due diligence procedures and are recognized as a “pre-vetted” partner for multinational companies seeking to do business with suppliers, agents, and consultants who share their commitment to commercial transparency.
