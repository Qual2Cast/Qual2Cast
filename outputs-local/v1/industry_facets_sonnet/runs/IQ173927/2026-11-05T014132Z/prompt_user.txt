[P2.1] Crane Co. Process Flow Technologies | Valves, Pumps & Controls
[P2.2] Crane Co. delivers process flow technologies for chemical, energy, water, nuclear, and industrial markets, including valves, pumps, instrumentation, and controls.
[P2.3] Crane’s Process Flow Technologies segment is a global provider of highly engineered products and systems, including sensing, instrumentation, controls, and flow control products. With more than 170 years of engineering excellence and expertise, Crane literally wrote the book on flow control with the introduction of the Flow of Fluids Handbook (TP410) that is still used as a definitive source in the industry.
[P2.4] Today, with proprietary technology, precision engineering, and material science innovation, we are solving our customers’ toughest challenges in mission-critical applications that require high-reliability in many of the harshest and most hazardous environments such as cryogenics, nuclear, pharmaceutical, chemical and petrochemical, and wastewater.
[P2.5] Crane ChemPharma & Energy designs and manufactures a variety of high-performance products for the regulation and control of fluids and gases. Its trusted brands offer customers a complete and innovative product portfolio designed for the most demanding corrosive, erosive, and high purity applications.
[P2.6] Primary industries served include cryogenics, pharmaceuticals and biotechnology, chemical and petrochemical processing, and power generation. As an integrated fluid handling solutions provider, Crane ChemPharma & Energy is well equipped to respond to complex global projects with comprehensive product packages and timely proposals to simplify our customers’ supply chain on a local level worldwide.
[P2.7] Crane Pumps & Systems designs and manufactures innovative pump solutions for the effective and efficient transportation of wastewater. The business is focused on solving complex customer challenges, whether helping drive efficiency with designs that reduce expensive clogging and decrease energy usage, or to improve operational performance across an aging infrastructure. Its technical specialists can provide the expertise needed to accurately evaluate service conditions and specify the most effective pumping solutions, and that commitment to excellence is carried through to the manufacturing, sales, distribution and service components of the business.
[P2.8] Crane Nuclear is a premier provider of sensors, diagnostic flow control diagnostic and calibration equipment, on-site services, and certified “N-stamp” valves for the global nuclear power market. Serving the nuclear power industry Crane Nuclear was a pioneer in valve diagnostic and testing technology, and the industry looks to Crane Nuclear to provide a highly skilled and qualified workforce to perform valve maintenance, ensuring plant safety and reliability.
[P2.9] The combined comprehensive nature of product and service offerings makes Crane Nuclear a full solution provider in the commercial nuclear power industry.
[P2.10] Crane Building Services & Utilities
[P2.11] Crane Building Services & Utilities (Crane BS&U) provides engineered flow control solutions that enable customers in the HVAC and Utility sectors to achieve reliable, efficient, and sustainable system performance. With operations across the UK and internationally, the business supports partners with high‑quality products, technical expertise and a service approach driven around specific applications.
[P2.12] The portfolio includes trusted and renown brands across key markets. In Building Services, Crane Fluid Systems, Hattersley and NABIC offer a comprehensive range of valves, fittings and safety solutions for the heating and ventilation in the commercial building market. For the Water sector, Viking Johnson and Helden delivers industry‑leading pipe connections and repair technologies. In the Gas sector, WASK and Sperryn offer ‘a mains to meter’ supply of fittings, valves, regulators and associated equipment that support safe and efficient gas distribution.
[P2.13] Barksdale designs and manufactures a broad portfolio of control instrumentation and control valves that measure pressure, temperature, level, flow and speed. The business’ high-performance control products primarily serve niche applications across a variety of end markets including manufacturing, water, hydrogen, energy, and transportation. With a global footprint, brand recognition and exceptional quality, Barksdale is able to provide unique and customized solutions to customers across our diverse markets.
[P2.14] Panametrics is redefining precision in industrial measurement with breakthrough solutions for measuring and analyzing moisture, oxygen, liquid, steam, and gas flow across numerous industries. With over 60 years of expertise, Panametrics continues to innovate—developing technologies and flare emissions management systems that enable customers to achieve unprecedented levels of efficiency, safety, and sustainability.
[P2.15] The 2026 acquisition of optek-Danulat expanded Panametrics’ portfolio of solutions, adding optical sensing inline process control capabilities including turbidity meters, colorimeters, and sensors for UV, pH and conductivity. Our commitment to progress transforms performance across critical industries, from pharmaceuticals, healthcare, water, chemical processing, and food and beverage, to energy and oil & gas.

[P3.3] Doing Business the Right Way
[P3.4] Holding ourselves to the highest standards of business practices and corporate citizenship on which our company was founded.
[P3.5] On, Crane’s founder R.T. Crane, who was in his early 20s at the time, wrote the resolution that has been the foundation of the company’s culture for more than 150 years: “I am resolved to conduct my business in the strictest honesty and fairness; to avoid all deception and trickery; to deal fairly with both customers and competitors; to be liberal and just toward employees and to put my whole mind upon the business.”
[P3.6] This resolution is just as relevant today as it was when it was written, and generations of Crane’s managers have faithfully followed its teachings.
[P3.7] Founding of R.T. Crane Brass & Bell Foundry in Chicago. Crane produces valves, fittings, and specialty castings for a growing industrial America.
[P3.8] As Northwest Manufacturing Co., Crane supplies essential steam components to the railroads and enters the steam heating business. Crane Elevator Company begins making hoists, elevators and steam engines, becoming one of the major names in the field for 30 years.
[P3.9] Crane pioneers improvements in manufacturing, including the use of multiple purpose machines invented by R.T. Crane and a conveyor system of moving molds and pouring metal – the beginning of line production in foundries.
[P3.10] Crane’s business is booming. Branch houses extend the company’s business to the Western U.S.
[P3.11] R.T. Crane establishes a metallurgical laboratory, probably the first in the Midwest.Crane products have the strength and quality to meet the challenges of ever higher fluid pressures and temperatures.
[P3.12] Crane produces steel valves and fittings in its own steel foundries.
[P3.13] Crane’s rigorous approaches to metallurgical science and material testing become the model for the valve and fitting industry.
[P3.14] Crane Company conceives the idea of the modern bathroom, creating the first practical decorative bathroom ensembles. The American bathroom becomes a sign of affluence and social pride. Crane plumbing fixtures, many designed by noted industrial designer Henry Dreyfuss, are celebrated for their styling, color and innovation.
[P3.15] Company-owned branch distribution operations grow to 190 from only 86.
[P3.16] To meet wartime demand, Crane increases steel valve output capacity four-fold to 25,000 tons annually. The U.S. Navy requires from 1,500 to 15,000 valves per ship.
[P3.17] After the war, Crane supplies flow control products for aircraft. Hydro-Aire, a manufacturer of highly developed precision aircraft products is acquired.
[P3.18] Industrial expansion is furthered through domestic and international acquisitions such as Chapman Valve, Cochrane Corporation, Deming Company and Chempump.
[P3.19] Crane is well-positioned in four major areas of concentration: fluid and pollution control, steel, building products and aircraft/aerospace.
[P3.20] Crane exits the plumbing and plumbing distribution businesses in the U.S.
[P3.21] Evans leads Crane as a growth company with a strong acquisition program in areas of fluid handling, engineered materials, merchandising systems, aerospace and controls.
[P3.22] Crane changes the name of its Fluid Handling segment to Process Flow Technologies to better convey the key strengths and core competencies of the business: providing proprietary and highly engineered process flow technology to our customers.
