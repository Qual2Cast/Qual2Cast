[P2.1] About - Tootsie
[P2.2] Tootsie Roll Industries is now based in Chicago and distributes its confections worldwide. Still family-run, the company focuses on quality and affordability while embracing advanced technologies. It has earned recognition from Forbes as one of “America’s 200 Best Small Companies” and from Staton Financial Advisors as one of “America’s Finest Companies.
[P2.3] Tootsie’s brands include some of the most familiar candy names: Tootsie Roll, Tootsie Pop, Charms Blow Pop, DOTS, Andes Mints, Sugar Daddy, Charleston Chew, Dubble Bubble, Razzles, Caramel Apple Pop, Junior Mints, Cella’s Chocolate-Covered Cherries, and Nik-L-Nip. They’re sold in a wide variety of venues, including supermarkets, warehouse and membership stores, vending machines, dollar stores, drug stores, and convenience stores.
[P2.4] Together, the Tootsie brands resonate strongly among every age group, culture, and demographic; for every occasion and event; and during every economic climate, qualifying them as truly enduring, iconic American confections.

[P2.6] Tootsie Roll Industries began when Leo Hirschfield invented the Tootsie Roll in New York City and named it after his daughter. Introduced the candy initially sold for one penny. Now headquartered in Chicago, the company operates across North America and distributes to countries worldwide. Tootsie combines a family-run corporate culture with an entrepreneurial, progressive management style focused on delivering high-quality products at competitive prices. Through constant investment in advanced equipment and efficient operations, Tootsie has earned recognition from Forbes as one of “America’s 200 Best Small Companies” and from Staton Financial Advisors as one of “America’s Finest Companies.
