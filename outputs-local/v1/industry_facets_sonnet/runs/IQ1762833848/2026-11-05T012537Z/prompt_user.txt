[P1.3] Distribution as a Competitive Advantage
[P1.4] Game-changing people, process and productivity move more than product. We move results.
[P1.5] Meet DSG. Distribution Solutions Group.
[P1.6] Distribution Solutions Group (DSG) is a best-in-class specialty distribution company providing high-touch, value-added distribution solutions to the Maintenance, Repair and Operations (MRO), Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) and the industrial technologies markets. DSG was formed through the strategic combination of Lawson Products, a leader in MRO and C-parts distribution, Gexpro Services, a leading global supply chain services provider to the OEM market, and TestEquity, a leader in electronic test and measurement solutions.
[P1.7] Through its collective businesses, DSG is dedicated to being a one-stop solution provider and helping customers lower their total cost of operation by increasing productivity and efficiency with the right products, expert technical support and fast, reliable delivery. DSG ships from strategically located distribution and service centers to customers in North America, Europe, Asia, South America and the Middle East.
[P1.8] Lawson Products sells and distributes specialty products to the industrial, commercial, institutional and government maintenance, repair and operations markets through Lawson Products, Partsmaster, Bolt Supply House, and Kent Automotive. The company is dedicated to helping customers in the U.S. and Canada lower their total cost of operation by increasing productivity and efficiency.
[P1.9] Gexpro Services is a leading global supply chain solutions and mission-critical C-parts provider lowering the total cost of production for blue-chip manufacturing customers through vendor-managed inventory, kitting, strategic sourcing and global logistics. Gexpro Services is uniquely positioned to deliver rapid improvements in fulfillment as well as process and product quality.
[P1.10] TestEquity is the largest, most comprehensive provider of test solutions proudly supporting the aerospace, defense, automotive, electronics, education, and medical industries. Through its brands: TestEquity, Techni-Tool, and Jensen, the company stocks the most components to help design and build electronics, with the understanding that highly specialized technology demands highly personalized service.
[P1.11] A team of distribution, industry and business leaders apply their combined expertise to help DSG customers leverage their supply chain as a competitive advantage – from mission-critical strategies to day-to-day supply.
[P1.12] As a resource that delivers tangible products as well as logistical solutions, Distribution Solutions Group sees the opportunities from sustainable operations via multiple viewpoints.

[P2.9] Go Forward Combined Business Management​
[P2.10] Distribution Solutions Group aims to have a largely decentralized operating structure. While much of the decision making will remain in the operating companies, this decentralized structure does not mean a passive ownership structure. While keeping operating company day-to-day decisions decentralized, DSG aims to enable nimble execution, empowering the management teams to deliver on their strategic objectives and enhance operational performance. Overall, the go-to-market sales process remains largely unchanged with each operating company’s leadership continuing at the helm.
[P2.11] This centralized capital deployment will employ a strategy centered on business model over end market. DSG will prioritize highly accretive, synergistic opportunities, and natural bolt-on’s. Overall, the strategy will hope to highlight simplicity and velocity over complexity. This centralized process orientation will serve to promote capital discipline and modest overall leverage across the platform.
[P2.12] Operating company management teams will work hand-in-hand in order to leverage collective capabilities while maintaining the current status quo of the decentralized operating structure. Leadership will engage in sharing of operational best practices across the platform to leverage DSG’s rich experience across end markets and supply chains. Management will aim to maintain the trust, respect, and alignment across management teams that they have cultivated over their collective histories.
