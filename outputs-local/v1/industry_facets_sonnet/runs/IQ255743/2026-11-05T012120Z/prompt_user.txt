About this industry group: Medical devices and nonmedical instruments require different regulatory and performance fields. FDA 510(k) clearance, PMA approval and other routes are distinct statuses, not interchangeable certification labels. FDA device pathway.

Facets:
- instrument_device_family (Instrument / device family). Examples: Diagnostic imaging systems; Industrial sensors; Laboratory analytical instruments.
- measurement_performance_specification (Measurement / performance specification). Examples: ±0.01°C temperature accuracy; 1 μm positional resolution; Stated imaging spatial resolution.
- clinical_industrial_research_use (Clinical / industrial / research use). Examples: Hospital diagnostics; Factory process control; Research laboratories.
- product_specific_regulatory_status (Product-specific regulatory status). Examples: FDA 510(k)-cleared device; FDA PMA-approved device; Nonmedical industrial instrument.
- consumables_service_attachment (Consumables / service attachment). Examples: Proprietary reagent cartridges; Recurring calibration service; One-time equipment sales.
- calibration_qualification_requirement (Calibration / qualification requirement). Examples: Annual traceable calibration; On-site validation; Factory-only recalibration.
