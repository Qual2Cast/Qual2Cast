About this industry group: Theme parks, fitness clubs, sports and gaming have different unit economics. Gaming win percentages are not ticket yields, and membership counts are not visits.

Facets:
- recreation_entertainment_activity (Recreation / entertainment activity). Examples: Theme park; Fitness clubs; Professional sports promotion.
- venue_service_capacity (Venue / service capacity). Examples: 20,000 permitted guests/day; 5,000 arena seats; 2,000 fitness members/location.
- visits_event_attendance (Visits / event attendance). Examples: Three million annual park visits; 80 home events/year; One million paid admissions/year.
- admission_yield (Admission yield). Examples: Ticket revenue / paid admissions: $65; Event ticket revenue / attendance: $90.
- pass_membership_mix (Pass / membership mix). Examples: 25% of visits from passholders; 60% of revenue from memberships.
- seasonality_utilization (Seasonality / utilization). Examples: Summer-led park attendance; Evening fitness peak; Event-day-only arena operation.
- gaming_economics_gaming_businesses (Gaming economics [gaming businesses]). Examples: Gross gaming revenue; Slot-machine count; House win as share of wagered amounts.
