[P2.3] Marlin Gas Services is a premier provider of virtual pipeline solutions, with nationwide mobile gas delivery of compressed natural gas (CNG), liquefied natural gas (LNG) and renewable natural gas (RNG). Marlin offers short- and long-term natural gas solutions when pipeline supplies are not available, traditional methods can’t meet customer requirements, or during pipeline outages. Marlin Gas Services has two permanent CNG terminals in Delaware and Florida, as well as a CNG fill terminal in Georgia, operated by our sister company, Marlin Compression. It also operates one of the largest fleets of CNG steel tube trailers to provide energy solutions for various applications nationwide. Marlin continues to expand the territories it serves, while leveraging its patented technology to serve LNG and RNG. In fact, Marlin is positioned to be a leading transporter of RNG — bridging the pipeline gaps and serving as a virtual pipeline between bio-gas producers and local distribution companies or end-use customers.
