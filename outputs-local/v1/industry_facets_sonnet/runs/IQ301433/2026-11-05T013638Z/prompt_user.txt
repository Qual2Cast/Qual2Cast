Company: SEI Investments Company (www.seic.com)
Industry (SIC 62): Security and Commodity Brokers, Dealers, Exchanges and Services
About this industry group: Separate asset managers, brokers, dealers and exchanges. AUM, AUA and assets under custody describe different roles and may overlap; do not add them together or treat them as interchangeable company scale.

Facets:
- financial_market_service (Financial-market service). Examples: Retail brokerage; Investment advisory; Securities market making.
- client_instrument_exposure (Client / instrument exposure). Examples: Retail equities and ETFs; Institutional fixed income; Listed derivatives.
- assets_under_management_asset_managers (Assets under management [asset managers]). Examples: $80 billion discretionary AUM; $20 billion non-discretionary AUM.
- assets_under_administration_custody_platforms (Assets under administration / custody [platforms]). Examples: $100 billion client AUA; $300 billion assets under custody.
- revenue_fee_economics (Revenue / fee economics). Examples: Asset-based advisory fees; Brokerage commissions; Principal trading revenue.
- client_trading_activity (Client trading activity). Examples: One million executed trades/day; Ten million options contracts/month.
