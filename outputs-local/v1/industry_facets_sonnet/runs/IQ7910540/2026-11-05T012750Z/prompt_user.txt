Company: Forestar Group Inc. (www.forestar.com)
Industry (SIC 65): Real Estate
About this industry group: Owner, broker and third-party property-manager models require different fields. Physical occupancy, economic occupancy, rent per unit and rent per area are distinct measures. A company being a REIT does not itself assign it to SIC 65: official SIC 6798 is under major group 67. REIT classification.

Facets:
- property_real_estate_service_type (Property / real-estate service type). Examples: Multifamily apartments; Industrial warehouses; Property brokerage.
- ownership_operating_role (Ownership / operating role). Examples: Direct property owner; Property manager for third parties; Real-estate broker.
- occupancy_by_basis_owners (Occupancy by basis [owners]). Examples: Physical leased-unit occupancy: 95%; Leased-area occupancy: 92%; Economic occupancy: 94%, issuer-defined.
- lease_term_owners (Lease term [owners]). Examples: Typical residential lease: 12 months; Rent-weighted unexpired lease term: 6.5 years.
- rent_by_property_unit_owners (Rent by property unit [owners]). Examples: $2,200/unit/month; $25/ft²/year base rent.
- property_market (Property market). Examples: Sun Belt metropolitan areas; Central London offices; Regional industrial corridors.
- property_income_fee_model (Property-income / fee model). Examples: Rental income and recoveries; Percentage-of-rent management fee; Brokerage commission per transaction.
