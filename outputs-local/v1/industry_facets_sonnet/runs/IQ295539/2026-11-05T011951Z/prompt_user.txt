About this industry group: Combined ratio is an underwriting measure for P&C operations, not a universal life-insurance profitability metric. Preserve gross/net, calendar/accident-year, catastrophe exclusions and the issuer’s expense-ratio basis. Life/annuity and health businesses need liability/benefit measures instead. NAIC terminology.

Facets:
- insurance_business_line (Insurance business line). Examples: Commercial P&C; Life and annuity insurance; Health insurance.
- risk_underwriting_distribution_model (Risk underwriting / distribution model). Examples: Direct primary insurer; Reinsurer; Delegated underwriting with retained risk.
- premium_exposure_mix (Premium / exposure mix). Examples: 60% commercial auto and 40% property premiums; Individual annuity-led sales.
- combined_ratio_p_c_underwriting (Combined ratio [P&C underwriting]). Examples: Calendar-year combined ratio: 96%; Accident-year ratio excluding catastrophes: 91%, separately defined.
- loss_lae_ratio_p_c_underwriting (Loss / LAE ratio [P&C underwriting]). Examples: Net loss and LAE / net earned premiums: 64%; Accident-year loss and LAE ratio: 60%.
- medical_benefit_ratio_health_insurers (Medical benefit ratio [health insurers]). Examples: Medical benefits / premium revenue: 82%; Issuer-defined medical loss ratio: 85%.
- reinsurance_catastrophe_protection (Reinsurance / catastrophe protection). Examples: Quota-share cessions; Excess-of-loss catastrophe cover; Retained catastrophe limit.
- liability_duration_guarantees_life_and_annuity (Liability duration / guarantees [life and annuity]). Examples: Fixed deferred annuities; Variable annuities with guarantees; Long-duration protection liabilities.
- investment_liability_matching (Investment / liability matching). Examples: Duration-matched bond portfolio; Interest-rate hedges for annuity guarantees; Separate-account investment assets.
- regime_specific_solvency_measure (Regime-specific solvency measure). Examples: U.S. insurance RBC ratio, denominator identified; EU Solvency II coverage ratio.

[P2.2] American Financial Group, Inc. is a holding company based in Cincinnati, Ohio and. Our insurance roots go back to 1872 with the founding of Great American Insurance Company. Today, through the operations of Great American Insurance Group, AFG is engaged in property and casualty (P&C) insurance, focusing on specialized commercial products for businesses. We are proud to provide insurance products that help businesses manage their unique financial risks and exposures.
[P2.3] AFG’s Specialty P&C Group is comprised of over 35 diversified businesses offering a wide range of specialty commercial coverages in three major groupings: Property and Transportation, Specialty Casualty and Specialty Financial.
[P2.4] Many of our businesses are among the top performers in their respective industries. Our business has entrepreneurial spirit at its core, with a focus on accountability. Business autonomy in our Specialty P&C operations facilitates agility in underwriting, claims and policy servicing, and enables these businesses to develop distribution strategies and relationships in the unique markets they serve.
[P2.5] By following a consistent, opportunistic strategy over many years and changing economic conditions, we have outperformed market indices over the long term while effectively managing portfolio risk.
[P2.6] With the power of generations behind it, AFG grows and changes with discipline and integrity. We're proud of who we are, where we've been and where we're going. Our industry is changing, and we continue to change as well.
