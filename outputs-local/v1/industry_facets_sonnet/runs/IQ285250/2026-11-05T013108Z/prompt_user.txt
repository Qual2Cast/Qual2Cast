About this industry group: Commercial printers, book publishers and periodical publishers have different operating drivers. Paid circulation and advertising dependence are publisher rows, not mandatory metrics for a commercial-printing plant.

Facets:
- printing_publishing_activity (Printing / publishing activity). Examples: Commercial printing; Newspaper publishing; Book publishing.
- print_digital_revenue_mix_publishers (Print / digital revenue mix [publishers]). Examples: 40% print and 60% digital revenue; Digital-subscription-led publisher.
- publication_print_market (Publication / print market). Examples: Trade journals; Educational books; Commercial packaging print.
- advertising_circulation_dependence_publishers (Advertising / circulation dependence [publishers]). Examples: 50% advertising revenue; Paid digital subscriptions; Newsstand copy sales.
- print_output_format_printers (Print output / format [printers]). Examples: 100,000 magazines/month; Web-offset printing; Short-run digital printing.
- paid_circulation_periodicals (Paid circulation [periodicals]). Examples: 75,000 paid print subscriptions; 200,000 paid digital subscriptions.
- rights_distribution_publishers (Rights / distribution [publishers]). Examples: Owned publishing rights; Third-party titles under license; Direct digital distribution.
