[P2.4] Lindblad Expeditions was built on a pioneering tradition of exploration that spans more than six decades—one rooted in a simple belief: that experiencing extraordinary places firsthand can inspire a lifelong commitment to protecting them. Through immersive, expert-led expeditions, we create opportunities for travelers to connect more deeply with the natural world. That philosophy has shaped every chapter of our history—from the pioneering voyages of the 1960s to the collaborations, innovations and experiences that continue to define Lindblad Expeditions today.
[P2.5] The Expeditions That Started It All
[P2.6] explorer Lars-Eric Lindblad brought the first tourists to Antarctica on a pioneering journey that laid the groundwork for modern expedition cruising. The following year, he led the first international travelers to the Galápagos Islands—another landmark voyage that helped shape this new era of exploration.
[P2.7] Widely regarded as the father of ecotourism, Lars-Eric saw travel not simply as a way to see the world, but as a way to change how people think about it. By opening these remote regions to thoughtful exploration, he demonstrated that travel could be a powerful force for learning and discovery, bringing people closer to wild places and the stories they hold.
[P2.8] Lars-Eric’s son, Sven Lindblad, founded Lindblad Expeditions to carry his father’s pioneering vision forward. He built a fleet designed to explore the world’s remote places, expanding the company’s reach to Baja California, the High Arctic and beyond. Under his leadership, Lindblad Expeditions refined the model Lars-Eric first introduced, pairing expeditions guided by experts with itineraries designed to foster a deeper understanding of the planet.
[P2.9] In this short film, Sven reflects on his father’s groundbreaking journey and the enduring purpose that continues to inspire new generations of explorers.
[P2.10] In Collaboration With National Geographic
[P2.11] Lindblad Expeditions had established itself as a leader in expedition travel, known for immersive, expert-led exploration and access to remarkable places. Together, the two organizations forged a groundbreaking collaboration that combined National Geographic’s expertise in science, education and storytelling with Lindblad Expeditions’ unmatched heritage of expedition cruising. With their relationship recently extended travelers can look forward to decades of immersive journeys inspired by discovery.
