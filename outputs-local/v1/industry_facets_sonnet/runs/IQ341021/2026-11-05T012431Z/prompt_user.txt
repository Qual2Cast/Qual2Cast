Industry (SIC 13): Oil & Gas Extraction
About this industry group: E&P and oilfield-service contractors share this broad major group but need different rows. Reserve/production fields apply to resource-owning operations; day rates and fleet utilization apply to service operations. Natural-gas pipeline activities can instead route to SIC 49. Scope.

Facets:
- operating_activity (Operating activity). Examples: Crude-oil E&P; Natural-gas production; Contract drilling and field services.
- production_mix_e_p (Production mix [E&P]). Examples: 65% oil by boe; Gas-weighted production; NGL-rich production.
- basin_field_exposure_e_p (Basin / field exposure [E&P]). Examples: Permian Basin; Gulf of Mexico offshore; Appalachian gas basin.
- reserve_measure_life_e_p (Reserve measure / life [E&P]). Examples: Proved developed producing reserves; Proved undeveloped reserves; 10-year reserves-to-production ratio.
- well_field_decline_e_p (Well / field decline [E&P]). Examples: 25% first-year well decline; 8% mature-field annual decline.
- development_intensity_e_p (Development intensity [E&P]). Examples: 40 wells drilled/year; 25 completed wells/year; Offshore tie-back development.
- production_cost_price_basis_e_p (Production cost / price basis [E&P]). Examples: Lifting cost: $12/boe; Brent-linked realized oil prices; Henry Hub-linked gas sales.
- service_line_fleet_oilfield_services (Service line / fleet [oilfield services]). Examples: Land drilling rigs; Pressure-pumping fleets; Well-maintenance crews.
- contract_utilization_oilfield_services (Contract / utilization [oilfield services]). Examples: $25,000 rig day rate; 80% marketed-rig utilization; Multiwell service agreement.
