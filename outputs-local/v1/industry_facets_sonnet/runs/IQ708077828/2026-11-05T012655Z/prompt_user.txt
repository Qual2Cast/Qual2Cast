Company: ESAB Corporation (www.esabcorporation.com)
Industry (SIC 35): Industrial and Commercial Machinery and Computer Equipment
About this industry group: Computer equipment is included here in the historical U.S. SIC structure; semiconductor devices are in SIC 36. Aftermarket and installed-base metrics apply to the equipment business, not automatically to every computer-related subsidiary.

Facets:
- machinery_computer_equipment_category (Machinery / computer-equipment category). Examples: Industrial compressors; Construction machinery; Computer servers.
- installed_equipment_base (Installed equipment base). Examples: 20,000 active compressors; 50,000 installed machine tools.
- aftermarket_sales_mix (Aftermarket sales mix). Examples: 35% parts and service revenue; Maintenance-led recurring revenue; Equipment-only sales.
- equipment_replacement_cycle (Equipment replacement cycle). Examples: 10–15-year machinery life; 3–5-year server refresh cycle.
- engineering_customization (Engineering / customization). Examples: Engineered-to-order machines; Configurable standard machines; Catalogue equipment.
- service_maintenance_requirement (Service / maintenance requirement). Examples: Quarterly preventive servicing; Remote condition monitoring; Customer-performed maintenance.
