[P3.21] Frequently Asked Questions About Merchant Services and Payment Processing
[P3.22] Merchant processing services allow businesses to accept and process electronic payments, including credit/debit cards, virtual cards, ACH transfers and mobile payments. These services ensure secure, fast and efficient transactions between customers and merchants.
[P3.23] Merchant service payment processing involves several key steps:
[P3.24] Transaction Initiation: The customer pays using a credit/debit card or mobile wallet.
[P3.25] Authorization: The payment processor verifies the transaction with the customer’s bank.
[P3.26] Approval: If approved, the merchant receives confirmation and the transaction is completed.
[P3.27] Settlement: The settlement stage finalizes the transaction by transferring the funds from the customer’s issuing bank to the merchant’s acquiring bank.
[P3.28] When a payment is processed, the following steps occur:
[P3.29] Authorization Request: The payment processor submits the transaction to the customer’s bank.
[P3.30] Verification: The issuing bank confirms available funds.
[P3.31] Approval or Decline: The transaction is either approved or rejected.
[P3.32] Settlement: If approved, funds are transferred to the merchant’s account, typically within the same business day with Priority Commerce’s solutions.
[P3.33] This process ensures a secure, efficient transfer of funds. What sets Priority Commerce apart is that payment processing is just one part of a connected commerce engine. Our platform ties together payments, banking, vendor disbursements, and reporting in one system, helping businesses manage their full revenue cycle with speed, control and clarity.
[P3.34] Any business that accepts electronic payments — whether in retail, e-commerce, hospitality or services — needs merchant services. These services provide customers with flexible payment options, improve security and streamline transaction management.
[P3.35] A payment gateway securely transmits payment information between a merchant and a payment processor, ensuring data encryption.
[P3.36] Merchant services cover a broader range of financial solutions, including payment processing, merchant accounts and point-of-sale (POS) systems. While a gateway is a component of merchant services, the latter manages all aspects of payment acceptance and processing.
