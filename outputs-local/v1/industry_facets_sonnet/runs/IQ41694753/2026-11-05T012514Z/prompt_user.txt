[P2.1] Who We Are - CVR Partners, LP
[P2.2] We are a growth-oriented limited partnership formed by CVR Energy, Inc., to own, operate and grow our nitrogen fertilizer business. Our nitrogen fertilizer manufacturing facilities, which primarily produce ammonia and urea ammonium nitrate (UAN) fertilizers, are located in Coffeyville, Kansas, and East Dubuque, Illinois.
[P2.3] Our Coffeyville plant is the only such operations in North America that uses a petroleum coke gasification process to make hydrogen, a key ingredient in its manufacturing process. Our East Dubuque nitrogen fertilizer plant uses natural gas as its feedstock to produce nitrogen fertilizer. our fertilizer facilities produced a combined 761,000 tons of ammonia, of which 243,000 net tons were available for sale, while the rest was upgraded to other fertilizer products, including 1,174,000 tons of UAN.
