APEI remains committed to social responsibility by providing affordable, accessible, and high-quality education in high-demand fields.

[P2.8] Rasmussen University, a national leader in nursing education and serves students across the U.S. online, and at campus locations throughout Minnesota, Illinois, North Dakota, Kansas, and Florida
