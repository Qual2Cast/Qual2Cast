Target: Addus HomeCare Corporation (www.addus.com)
- Core offering: Provides in-home personal care, home health and hospice services to elderly and disabled people, mostly funded by government programs. Delivers care through its own local branch offices and employs caregivers and nurses who visit clients at home.
- Primary products: none
- Primary services: personal care; homemaker services; home health nursing; hospice care; therapy services
- Customer segment: B2G: state Medicaid agencies and waiver programs, Veterans Affairs; B2B: managed care organizations, Medicare Advantage plans, hospices and health systems
- End markets: healthcare; aging and long-term care; disability services
- Revenue model: healthcare reimbursement: Medicaid, Medicare and managed care payments for in-home care visits
- Value chain position: service provider: delivers in-home care using its own caregivers, sold to state programs and managed care plans
- Geography served: US national: about two dozen states, concentrated in the Midwest, South and Northeast
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: personal care, home health nursing and hospice services sharing the same clients and payers
- Distribution channels: own locations: local branch offices that schedule and supervise in-home caregivers; direct sales: contracts negotiated directly with state agencies and managed care organizations
- Regulatory exposure: high: state home care licensure and Medicare/Medicaid certification, overseen by CMS and state health departments
