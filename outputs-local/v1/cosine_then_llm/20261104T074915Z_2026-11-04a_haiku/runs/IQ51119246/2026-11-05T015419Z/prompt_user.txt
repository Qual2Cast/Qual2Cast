- Primary services: none

- Product specialization: niche specialist: focused on immune globulin therapies for immune deficiency and related disorders
- Distribution channels: distributors / dealers: specialty distributors that supply pharmacies and infusion providers; direct sales: direct sales to hospitals and infusion clinics treating patients; own locations: company-owned plasma collection centers supplying raw material
