You help a private-equity analyst choose comparable public companies (comps) for valuing a target company. A buyout investor values a target from the EV/EBITDA multiples of companies in the same business: the same main products or services, sold to the same kind of customers, earning money the same way. Scale matters less than being in the same business.

Below are the target and a list of candidate comps, each with its name, website and a description of its business model. Use the descriptions and what you know about the companies.

For every candidate, decide whether it is a good comp for the target:
- good: in the same business as the target, so its multiple says something about the target's.
- bad: a different business, even if it shares an industry label, customers or a technology.

Rules:
- Answer for every candidate, once, by its label (C1, C2, ...).
- The reason is one short sentence, at most twenty words, naming what matches or differs.
- Judge the business only. Do not use or mention valuations, multiples, share prices or financial figures.

Target: GoodRx Holdings, Inc. (www.goodrx.com)
- Core offering: Operates a digital platform that helps consumers find and save on prescription drug prices through discount cards and price comparison, plus paid memberships and telehealth visits. It earns from consumer subscriptions, pharmacy and manufacturer fees, and healthcare services.
- Primary products: prescription discount cards; GoodRx Gold membership
- Primary services: telehealth consultations; prescription price comparison
- Customer segment: B2C: US consumers buying prescription drugs without insurance or with high copays; B2B: pharmaceutical manufacturers, pharmacy benefit managers, health plans, retail pharmacies
- End markets: healthcare; pharmaceuticals; retail pharmacy; digital health
- Revenue model: subscription: consumers paying for GoodRx Gold membership; advertising: pharmaceutical manufacturers paying for brand drug promotion on the platform; transaction or usage fees: fees from pharmacies and benefit managers for prescriptions filled through discount cards; service fees: consumers paying for telehealth visits and related care
- Value chain position: software / platform: consumer app and website connecting patients, pharmacies and drug makers; service provider: telehealth visits and prescription savings services sold to consumers and plans
- Geography served: US national: all US states, with discount cards accepted at pharmacies nationwide
- Product specialization: related lines: discount cards, membership subscriptions and telehealth, sharing consumers and pharmacy partners
- Distribution channels: online: GoodRx mobile app and website where consumers find and use discounts; partners / resellers: retail pharmacies and pharmacy benefit managers that accept discount cards; direct sales: sales of advertising and data services to pharmaceutical manufacturers
- Regulatory exposure: medium: telehealth and health data subject to HIPAA, FTC consumer protection and state pharmacy and telehealth rules
