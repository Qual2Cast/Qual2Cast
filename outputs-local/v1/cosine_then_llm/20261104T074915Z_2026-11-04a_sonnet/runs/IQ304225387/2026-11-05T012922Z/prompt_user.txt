Target: Planet Fitness, Inc. (www.planetfitness.com)
- Core offering: Franchises and operates low-cost, high-value gyms offering members cardio and strength equipment and fitness areas. It sells memberships to individual consumers, and franchisees pay it fees and buy equipment and merchandise to run the clubs.
- Primary products: gym equipment sold to franchisees; branded merchandise
- Primary services: gym memberships; personal training; franchising support
- Customer segment: B2C: individual consumers buying monthly gym memberships; B2B: franchisees operating Planet Fitness clubs
- End markets: consumer fitness; health and wellness
- Revenue model: subscription: monthly membership dues paid by gym members; licensing or royalties: franchisee royalty payments on club sales; product sales: equipment and merchandise sold to franchisees; advertising: national advertising fund contributions from franchisees
- Value chain position: brand owner: owns the brand and franchise system licensed to franchisees; service provider: runs gym services for members at corporate-owned clubs; asset operator: leases and operates some corporate-owned club locations
- Geography served: global: Clubs across all US states, plus Canada, Mexico, Australia, Spain and other countries
- Product specialization: related lines: gym memberships, franchising, equipment sales and branded merchandise
- Distribution channels: own locations: corporate-owned gym clubs; online: online sign-up and membership management through its app and website; other: franchised clubs run by independent franchisees
- Regulatory exposure: medium: FTC Franchise Rule and state franchise laws, consumer contract rules, health and safety standards
