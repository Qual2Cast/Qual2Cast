Target: American Public Education, Inc. (www.apei.com)
- Core offering: Provides online and campus-based postsecondary degree programs to adult learners, many of them military service members, veterans and public servants. Students enroll in courses and earn certificates and degrees, with tuition paid by students, employers or military tuition assistance programs.
- Primary products: none
- Primary services: undergraduate degree programs; graduate degree programs; professional certificates; nursing education; career training
- Customer segment: B2C: adult learners pursuing degrees and certificates for career advancement; B2G: military service members and veterans using federal tuition assistance benefits
- End markets: higher education; military and defense; public safety; healthcare
- Revenue model: service fees: tuition and fees paid by students, employers or military tuition assistance programs
- Value chain position: service provider: delivers degree and certificate instruction to students, sold to individuals, employers and military programs; software / platform: operates online learning platform for remote course delivery to students nationwide
- Geography served: US national: Students enrolled from all US states, plus some campus locations in several states and international online students
- Company size: mid-sized
- Product specialization: related lines: online university programs and campus-based nursing and career schools serving adult learners
- Distribution channels: online: fully online courses delivered over the internet to students nationwide; own locations: campus-based nursing and career training schools in several states; direct sales: admissions advisors enrolling students directly, including military education offices
- Regulatory exposure: high: accreditation and state licensing required, Title IV federal student aid rules enforced by the U.S. Department of Education
