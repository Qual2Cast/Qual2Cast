Target: Veralto Corporation (www.veralto.com)

- Primary services: installation and maintenance; service contracts; instrument calibration

- End markets: food and beverage; pharmaceuticals and life sciences; industrial manufacturing; water utilities; environmental testing
- Revenue model: product sales: printers, instruments, UV systems and consumables sold to manufacturers and utilities; service fees: installation, maintenance and calibration for installed equipment
- Value chain position: OEM / manufacturer: designs and builds printers, instruments and water treatment systems, sold to manufacturers and utilities; service provider: installs, maintains and calibrates equipment for existing customers
- Geography served: global: Sells worldwide, with major operations in North America, Europe and Asia
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: diversified: two businesses: product identification (coding and marking) and water quality instruments and treatment
- Distribution channels: direct sales: sales teams selling to large industrial accounts and utilities; distributors / dealers: independent distributors and dealers in many countries
- Regulatory exposure: medium: drinking water and environmental testing standards, plus food and drug labeling and traceability rules, such as EPA and FDA requirements
