Target: Uniti Group LLC (www.uniti.com)
- Core offering: Owns and leases fiber and copper communications networks to telecom carriers, and sells broadband internet service to residential and business customers in rural US markets. Network capacity is leased under long-term contracts and retail service is sold on monthly subscriptions.
- Primary products: fiber network capacity; dark fiber; copper network access; broadband internet
- Primary services: network leasing; fiber construction and maintenance; managed network connectivity
- Customer segment: B2B: wireless carriers, telecom operators, enterprises, data centers; B2C: residential broadband subscribers in rural areas
- End markets: telecommunications; wireless; enterprise connectivity; residential broadband
- Revenue model: rental or leasing: long-term leases of fiber and copper network assets to carriers; subscription: monthly broadband internet subscriptions; service fees: network construction, maintenance and connectivity services
- Value chain position: asset operator: owns and runs fiber and copper networks, sold as leased capacity to carriers; service provider: sells broadband internet service to households and businesses
- Geography served: US national: fiber and copper network assets across many US states, with retail broadband concentrated in rural southern and central states
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: network infrastructure leasing and retail broadband service sharing the same fiber and copper network
- Distribution channels: direct sales: contracts with carriers and enterprise customers; online: online sign-up and account management for broadband subscribers
- Regulatory exposure: medium: telecom carrier rules from the FCC and state utility commissions, plus REIT tax rules for the real estate structure
