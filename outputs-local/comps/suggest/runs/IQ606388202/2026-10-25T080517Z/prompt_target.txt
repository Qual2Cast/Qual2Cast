Target: Palomar Holdings, Inc. (plmr.com)
- Core offering: Underwrites specialty property and casualty insurance for hard-to-place and catastrophe-exposed risks, sold to homeowners and businesses through independent agents and wholesale brokers. It earns premiums from policies it underwrites and manages risk through reinsurance.
- Primary products: residential earthquake insurance; inland marine insurance; excess and surplus property insurance; commercial property insurance; crop insurance
- Primary services: none
- Customer segment: B2C: homeowners buying residential earthquake coverage; B2B: commercial property owners, contractors, agribusinesses, and specialty risk buyers
- End markets: property insurance; agriculture; construction; real estate; transportation
- Revenue model: interest or premiums: earned premiums on insurance policies, plus investment income on reserves; commissions or management fees: fees from program and managing general agent arrangements
- Value chain position: financial intermediary: takes in premiums and pays claims, selling policies to homeowners and businesses; service provider: underwriting and claims administration for policies it places through agents and programs
- Geography served: US national: nationwide, with the largest exposure in California and other earthquake-prone and catastrophe-exposed states
- Company size: mid-sized
- Product specialization: related lines: property and catastrophe-exposed lines sharing underwriting, reinsurance and distribution: earthquake, inland marine, excess and surplus, crop
- Distribution channels: agents / brokers: independent insurance agents and wholesale brokers; partners / resellers: managing general agents and program administrators
- Regulatory exposure: high: insurance licensing and rate and form approval by state insurance departments, surplus lines and reinsurance rules
