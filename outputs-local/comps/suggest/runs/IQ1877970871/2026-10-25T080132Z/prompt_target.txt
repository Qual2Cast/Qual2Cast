Target: Bowhead Specialty Holdings Inc. (bowheadspecialty.com)
- Core offering: Underwrites specialty commercial insurance for businesses with hard-to-place risks that standard insurers often decline. Sells policies through brokers and managing general agents, and earns premiums and investment income on the funds it holds.
- Primary products: excess and surplus lines casualty insurance; commercial property insurance; professional liability insurance; specialty liability insurance
- Primary services: none
- Customer segment: B2B: businesses with specialty or hard-to-place risks, and the brokers and agents placing their coverage
- End markets: construction; real estate; transportation; manufacturing; professional services
- Revenue model: interest or premiums: insurance premiums paid by policyholders, plus investment income on reserves
- Value chain position: financial intermediary: takes in premiums from insured businesses via brokers and pays their claims
- Geography served: US national: businesses across all US states through surplus lines and admitted insurance
- Company size: mid-sized
- Product specialization: related lines: casualty, property, professional and other specialty insurance lines sharing the same brokers and underwriting
- Distribution channels: agents / brokers: wholesale and retail insurance brokers placing business with the company; partners / resellers: managing general agents and program administrators that underwrite on its behalf
- Regulatory exposure: high: insurance licensing and rate and form approval by state insurance regulators, plus surplus lines rules
