Target: Bioventus Inc. (www.bioventus.com)

- Primary products: bone growth stimulators; knee viscosupplement injections; cartilage repair implants; bone graft substitutes; pain treatment devices
- Primary services: none
- Customer segment: B2B: orthopedic surgeons, hospitals, ambulatory surgery centers, physical therapy clinics
- End markets: orthopedics; sports medicine; pain management; surgical care
- Revenue model: product sales: devices and biologic products sold to hospitals, clinics and distributors
- Value chain position: OEM / manufacturer: makes devices and biologics from purchased materials and components, sold to clinics and hospitals; brand owner: designs and markets its own product lines, some made by contract manufacturers, sold to surgeons and providers
- Geography served: global: United States, with sales in Europe and other international markets
- Company size: mid-sized
- Product specialization: related lines: pain treatment devices, bone healing stimulators, surgical biologics and cartilage repair, sold to the same orthopedic customers
- Distribution channels: direct sales: sales representatives calling on surgeons and hospital purchasing groups in the United States; distributors / dealers: independent distributors in international markets
- Regulatory exposure: high: medical devices needing FDA clearance or approval and CE marking for European sales
