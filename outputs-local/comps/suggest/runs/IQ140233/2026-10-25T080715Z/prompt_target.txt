Target: Tripadvisor, Inc. (www.tripadvisor.com)
- Core offering: Operates an online travel platform with user reviews and listings of hotels, restaurants and attractions, and sells tours, tickets and restaurant reservations. Serves travelers and travel suppliers, earning mainly from advertising and booking commissions, delivered through websites and mobile apps.
- Primary products: travel review and listing website; tour and activity tickets; restaurant reservation platform
- Primary services: hotel metasearch; advertising for travel suppliers; tour and activity booking
- Customer segment: B2C: travelers researching and booking trips; B2B: hotels, restaurants, tour operators and attractions that advertise or list on the platform
- End markets: travel and tourism; hospitality; restaurants; leisure and attractions
- Revenue model: advertising: hotels and restaurants pay for clicks and leads from travelers; commissions or management fees: commissions on tour, activity and hotel bookings; subscription: software subscriptions for restaurants using reservation tools
- Value chain position: software / platform: takes in user reviews and supplier listings, sells to travelers and travel businesses; distributor: takes in tours and tickets from operators, sells them to travelers
- Geography served: global: websites in many languages covering destinations worldwide
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: reviews, hotel and restaurant listings, tours and restaurant bookings share the same traveler audience
- Distribution channels: online: websites and mobile apps used by travelers; direct sales: sales teams selling advertising to hotels and restaurants
- Regulatory exposure: medium: consumer protection, advertising and data privacy rules such as GDPR across many countries
