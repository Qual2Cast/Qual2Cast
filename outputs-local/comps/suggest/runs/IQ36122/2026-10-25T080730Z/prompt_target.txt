Target: Vail Resorts, Inc. (www.vailresorts.com)
- Core offering: Operates mountain resorts and ski areas, selling season passes, lift tickets, lodging, lessons and dining to skiers, snowboarders and summer visitors. It earns money from passes, daily tickets and on-mountain and resort services.
- Primary products: season passes; lift tickets; retail merchandise; food and beverage
- Primary services: ski and snowboard lessons; lodging; equipment rental; golf; summer mountain activities
- Customer segment: B2C: skiers, snowboarders and summer visitors, including season pass holders
- End markets: leisure travel and hospitality; winter sports; outdoor recreation
- Revenue model: subscription: season pass sales for access to resort mountains; transaction or usage fees: daily lift tickets; service fees: lodging, ski school, rentals and dining; product sales: retail merchandise and food and beverage
- Value chain position: asset operator: owns and runs ski mountains, lifts and resort facilities, selling passes and tickets to guests; service provider: takes in guest bookings and sells lessons, lodging and dining to visitors
- Geography served: global: US, Canada, Switzerland and Australia mountain resorts
- Company size: among the largest
- Product specialization: related lines: mountain resort operations, season passes, lodging, ski school, rentals and dining serving the same guests
- Distribution channels: direct sales: season passes and tickets sold directly to guests; own locations: resort ticket windows, lodging front desks and on-mountain shops; online: website and app sales of passes, tickets and lodging
- Regulatory exposure: medium: environmental and land-use permits for public forest land, plus safety, liability and data privacy rules
