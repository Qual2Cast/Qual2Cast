Target: Copart, Inc. (www.copart.com)
- Core offering: Operates online auctions and physical yards that sell damaged, salvage and total-loss vehicles on behalf of insurers and other sellers. Buyers are mainly dealers, rebuilders, exporters and individuals, who bid online and collect vehicles from the company's locations.
- Primary products: used vehicles; salvage vehicles; vehicle parts
- Primary services: online vehicle auctions; vehicle storage and yard services; vehicle title processing; vehicle transportation
- Customer segment: B2B: insurance companies, vehicle dealers, rebuilders, exporters; B2G: government agencies selling surplus or seized vehicles
- End markets: automotive; insurance; vehicle remarketing; export trade
- Revenue model: service fees: seller and buyer fees charged per vehicle sold at auction; transaction or usage fees: yard storage and late charges on vehicles held at its locations
- Value chain position: service provider: takes in vehicles from insurers and sellers and sells them at auction for a fee; software / platform: runs an online bidding platform connecting sellers with buyers worldwide; asset operator: owns and operates yards where vehicles are stored, inspected and released
- Geography served: global: US and Canada, with operations in the UK, Brazil, UAE and other countries
- Company size: among the largest
- Product specialization: niche specialist: vehicle remarketing auctions for salvage and total-loss vehicles
- Distribution channels: online: online auctions where buyers bid remotely; own locations: company-operated yards where vehicles are held and picked up
- Regulatory exposure: medium: state vehicle title, salvage and dealer licensing rules, plus environmental and transport rules
