Target: Alaska Power & Telephone Company (www.aptalaska.com)
- Core offering: Generates and distributes electric power and provides telephone, internet and cable services to remote Alaska communities. Sells electricity and communications service to households, businesses and public institutions, delivered over company-owned plants and networks.
- Primary products: electricity; telephone equipment; broadband modems
- Primary services: electric utility service; local and long-distance telephone; internet and cable TV; construction and operations services
- Customer segment: B2C: residential households in remote Alaska communities; B2B: local businesses, fish processors, mines and resource companies; B2G: government agencies, schools and community facilities
- End markets: electric utilities; telecommunications; fisheries; mining; government
- Revenue model: transaction or usage fees: electricity sold by the kilowatt-hour to customers; service fees: monthly telephone, internet and cable subscriptions
- Value chain position: asset operator: owns and runs hydro, diesel and other power plants and telecom networks, sells power and service to end customers; service provider: provides telephone, internet and cable services, takes in customer payments
- Geography served: US regional: Rural communities across Alaska, mostly not connected to the road system
- Company size: mid-sized
- Product specialization: related lines: electric power generation and distribution, telephone, internet and cable services
- Distribution channels: direct sales: customers billed directly for power and communications service; own locations: company offices and service centers in local communities
- Regulatory exposure: high: electric and telecom utility rates and service set by the Regulatory Commission of Alaska and FCC rules
