Target: Peloton Interactive, Inc. (www.onepeloton.com)
- Core offering: Sells connected fitness equipment, including exercise bikes, treadmills and rowers, plus paid streaming workout classes to home consumers. It designs the hardware and software, has it made by contract manufacturers, and sells online, through its showrooms and through retail partners.
- Primary products: connected exercise bikes; connected treadmills; rowers; strength accessories; fitness apparel
- Primary services: streaming fitness classes; equipment rental; delivery and setup
- Customer segment: B2C: Individual consumers buying home fitness equipment and class subscriptions
- End markets: consumer fitness; home fitness; health and wellness
- Revenue model: product sales: Connected fitness equipment, accessories and apparel sold to consumers; subscription: Monthly membership for live and on-demand streaming classes; rental or leasing: Monthly rental of bikes and treadmills
- Value chain position: brand owner: Designs and markets its own equipment, made by outside contract manufacturers; software / platform: Streaming app and class content, sold as a subscription to members; retailer: Sells directly to consumers through its website, showrooms and retail partners
- Geography served: global: US and Canada, plus UK, Germany, Austria and Australia
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: related lines: Connected bikes, treadmills and rowers, with classes, accessories and apparel sharing the same members
- Distribution channels: online: Company website and app for direct sales and subscriptions; own locations: Peloton showrooms in shopping areas; third-party retail: Sporting goods and online retail partners selling its equipment
- Regulatory exposure: medium: Product safety rules for consumer fitness equipment and consumer data privacy rules for member data
