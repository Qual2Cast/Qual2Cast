Target: Cantaloupe, Inc. (www.cantaloupe.com)
- Core offering: Provides cashless payment, telemetry and micro market software and hardware for unattended retail. Sells to vending, micro market and laundry operators, who use it to take payments and manage machines and inventory remotely.
- Primary products: payment terminals; telemetry devices; micro market software; vending management software
- Primary services: payment processing; cloud-based route and inventory management; device deployment and activation
- Customer segment: B2B: vending operators, micro market operators, laundry operators and other unattended retail businesses
- End markets: vending; micro markets; laundry; unattended retail
- Revenue model: subscription: recurring software and platform fees from operators; transaction or usage fees: payment processing fees on card and mobile transactions; product sales: payment terminals and telemetry devices sold to operators
- Value chain position: brand owner: designs and sells payment and telemetry devices made by outside manufacturers, sold to machine operators; software / platform: cloud management platform used by operators, paid through subscriptions; service provider: processes card and mobile payments for operators and takes a fee per transaction
- Geography served: North America: United States and Canada, with some customers in Europe
- Company size: mid-sized
- Product specialization: related lines: payment hardware, telemetry devices, cloud management software and micro market systems sold to the same operators
- Distribution channels: direct sales: in-house sales team selling to machine and micro market operators; partners / resellers: channel partners and equipment distributors that bundle the platform with vending machines
- Regulatory exposure: medium: payment card industry data security standards and US state data privacy laws apply to card processing
