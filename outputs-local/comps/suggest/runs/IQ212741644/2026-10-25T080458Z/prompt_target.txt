Target: News Corporation (newscorp.com)
- Core offering: Publishes news, business information and books, and runs online property listing platforms. It sells subscriptions and advertising to readers, advertisers and businesses worldwide, and its publishing units sell books through retailers and online stores. Its news and information brands are produced by newsrooms and data teams, while its property platforms sell listings and advertising to agents and home seekers.
- Primary products: newspapers; business news and data; digital news subscriptions; books; property listing portals
- Primary services: advertising services; data and risk information services; real estate listing services
- Customer segment: B2C: news and book readers, home buyers, renters and sellers; B2B: advertisers, real estate agents, brokers, financial professionals, corporate risk teams
- End markets: media and publishing; real estate; financial services; advertising; education
- Revenue model: subscription: digital and print news subscriptions for readers and professionals; advertising: display and digital advertising across news and property sites; product sales: print newspapers, books and e-books sold to readers; service fees: data, information and risk services sold to businesses; commissions or management fees: listing fees paid by real estate agents and brokers
- Value chain position: brand owner: owns and publishes newspaper, magazine and book brands sold to readers; service provider: provides news reporting and business information to subscribers and advertisers; software / platform: operates online property listing platforms connecting agents and home seekers
- Geography served: global: United States, Australia, United Kingdom and other English-speaking markets, plus international news and book readers
- Company size: among the largest
- Product specialization: diversified: news and information, digital real estate, book publishing, and regional newspapers
- Distribution channels: direct sales: advertising and data sales teams selling to businesses and agencies; online: news websites, apps and property portals accessed directly by users; third-party retail: bookstores and online retailers selling published titles; partners / resellers: news syndication and data partners distributing content to third parties
- Regulatory exposure: medium: media ownership and content rules, data privacy laws, and real estate advertising regulations across markets
