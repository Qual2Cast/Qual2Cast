Target: WEX Inc. (www.wexinc.com)
- Core offering: Provides payment and technology solutions to fleets, businesses and employers, including fleet fuel cards, corporate payment and virtual card programs, and health savings account administration. Sold mainly to fleet operators, companies and benefit plan sponsors, and delivered through card programs and an online platform.
- Primary products: fleet fuel cards; corporate payment cards; virtual card payment software; health savings account platforms
- Primary services: payment processing; benefits administration; fleet spend reporting and data services
- Customer segment: B2B: fleet operators, trucking and delivery companies, corporate buyers, employers and health benefit plan sponsors
- End markets: transportation and logistics; energy and fuel; corporate procurement; employee healthcare benefits
- Revenue model: transaction or usage fees: fees on card payments and fuel transactions processed through its networks; service fees: benefits administration and account fees paid by employers and plan sponsors
- Value chain position: service provider: processes payments and administers benefits for businesses, sold to fleets, employers and merchants; software / platform: online platforms for managing fleet spend, corporate payments and health accounts
- Geography served: global: Primarily United States, with fleet and payment operations in Europe, Australia and other countries
- Company size: large
- Product specialization: diversified: separate businesses for fleet payments, corporate payments and health benefits, each with different customers
- Distribution channels: direct sales: sales teams selling to fleets, corporate buyers and benefit plan sponsors; partners / resellers: fuel merchants, banks and benefit partners that offer its programs to their customers; online: web platforms for account management and payment
- Regulatory exposure: medium: payment card network rules, data security and privacy rules, and health information rules for benefit accounts
