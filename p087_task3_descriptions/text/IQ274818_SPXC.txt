SPX Technologies supplies engineered products through HVAC and detection and measurement businesses. Its HVAC portfolio includes industrial fans, blowers, exhaust systems, dampers, custom air handlers, cooling towers, fluid coolers, evaporative condensers, electric heaters, boilers, water heaters, and heat pumps. These products support air movement, cooling, heating, and water-based heating systems in residential, commercial, industrial, and specialized facilities. The detection and measurement business supplies equipment for locating and inspecting infrastructure. Products include cable and pipe locators, leak detection equipment, ground-penetrating radar, sewer inspection cameras, robotic crawlers, and related inspection software. These tools serve utilities, contractors, municipalities, and energy companies that inspect, maintain, or repair buried assets. Other offerings include marine lighting, buoys, navigation systems, radio-frequency communications technology, spectrum monitoring systems, tactical data links, and transit fare collection equipment. Customers include transportation agencies, infrastructure operators, government organizations, and defense and security users. Across its businesses, SPX combines specialized equipment with software, controls, inspection capabilities, and supporting services to address building systems, infrastructure maintenance, transportation payments, navigation, and communications applications.
