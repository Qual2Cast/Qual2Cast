Resolute Holdings is a management company that oversees the operations and capital allocation strategies of GPGI. Its business activity centers on managing a business platform, evaluating acquisition opportunities, and improving the performance of the businesses within that platform. It applies the Resolute Operating System as an operating approach for developing acquired businesses and supporting their growth. Resolute's stated selection criteria emphasize businesses in growing industries that can differentiate their offerings and establish strong market positions. Its approach includes investment in research and development, the use of technology to differentiate products, improvements in operating efficiency, and changes to sales and market development activities. It also considers opportunities to expand businesses through acquisitions. The company's offering is operational oversight and business development rather than a specified line of manufactured equipment. Its website describes a model built around acquiring businesses and working on their operations, innovation, and market position. The management activities described include coordinating operational improvements with investment decisions and supporting both internal growth initiatives and additional acquisitions within the managed platform.
