Astec Industries manufactures specialized equipment used in aggregate processing, asphalt production, concrete production, and road construction. Its portfolio supports the processing of raw materials and their conversion into materials used in roads and other infrastructure. Products include crushing equipment, screening equipment, conveying systems, asphalt plants, and concrete production equipment. For aggregate applications, Astec supplies mobile and stationary equipment that processes materials into required sizes and specifications. Its mobile range includes jaw, cone, and impact crushing plants, along with screening and conveying plants. Screening equipment can handle aggregates, recycled materials, and other feedstocks. These products support material preparation, separation, and movement within processing operations. Astec also supplies hot-mix and warm-mix asphalt production equipment and systems for concrete production. These product groups serve companies involved in producing construction materials and carrying out infrastructure projects. Its equipment is used across aggregate, mining, construction, and recycling applications, including concrete recycling. The company's offering spans individual machines and production systems, with dealer, parts, and service channels supporting customers that operate its equipment.
