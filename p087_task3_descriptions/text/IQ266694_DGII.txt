Digi International provides connectivity hardware, software, and services for connected devices, industrial systems, and enterprise networks. Its products include cellular routers and extenders, embedded wireless modules, system-on-modules, single-board computers, console servers, and USB and serial connectivity products. These offerings help customers build connected products and maintain communications between machines, sensors, and network infrastructure. Digi's software and managed services support remote device management, monitoring, security, and network availability. Its businesses include SmartSense, which provides sensor-based monitoring and compliance solutions; Opengear, which provides resilient network access and automation; and Particle, which combines device connectivity, cloud services, and lifecycle management. The broader portfolio also includes managed network connectivity services. Customers operate in sectors such as manufacturing, transportation, healthcare, retail, agriculture, energy, utilities, and smart-city infrastructure. Applications include monitoring assets and environmental conditions, connecting industrial equipment, maintaining access to distributed information technology systems, and managing devices in demanding environments. Digi also offers implementation, application development, and wireless design services. Its business combines components embedded in customers' products with finished networking equipment and services for operating connected infrastructure.
