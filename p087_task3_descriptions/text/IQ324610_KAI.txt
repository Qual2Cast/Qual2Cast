Kadant supplies technologies, engineered systems, equipment, and services used in industrial processing. Its operations cover flow control, industrial processing, and material handling. These businesses address the movement of fluids and materials, the conversion of fiber and wood into useful products, and the reliability and efficiency of industrial machinery. The flow control business provides engineered products and systems for fluid, power, and data transfer, together with doctoring, cleaning, and filtration technologies. These offerings support machine operation, energy utilization, and production performance. The industrial processing business supplies machinery, components, and engineered systems for fiber processing and wood processing, including applications involving fiber-based and renewable products. Kadant's material handling portfolio includes conveyors, vibratory feeders, and balers used to move or prepare bulk and discrete materials for further processing. Customers operate in paper, packaging, wood products, mining, food processing, energy, and other process industries. The company also offers automation and asset performance monitoring tools that help customers monitor equipment, anticipate failures, and improve operating decisions. Its overall offering focuses on industrial production processes, resource use, equipment performance, and material flow.
