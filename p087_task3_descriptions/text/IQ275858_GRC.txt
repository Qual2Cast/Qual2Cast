Gorman-Rupp Company The Gorman-Rupp Company, founded in 1933 designs, manufactures and sells pumps and related equipment (pump and motor controls) for use in water, wastewater, construction, dewatering, industrial, petroleum, original equipment, agriculture, fire suppression, heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC), military and other liquid-handling applications. With locations in the United States, Canada, the Netherlands, Belgium, Ireland, and South Africa, Gorman-Rupp markets these products through its wholly owned subsidiaries and a broad network of distributors, through manufacturers' representatives, through third-party distributor catalogs and by direct sales in the United States and internationally.
