DMC Global operates manufacturing businesses that supply engineered products to construction, energy, industrial processing, and transportation markets. Its businesses are Arcadia, DynaEnergetics, and NobelClad. Each addresses a distinct set of products and customer applications. Arcadia supplies architectural building products, including exterior framing systems, windows, curtain walls, and door systems. Its commercial products serve buildings such as offices, healthcare facilities, educational institutions, retail properties, and civic facilities. Its Arcadia Custom offering includes steel, aluminum, and wood window and door systems for residential applications. DynaEnergetics supplies perforating systems and related products used in oil and gas wells. Its portfolio includes perforating guns, shaped charges, and integrated initiating technology used in well completion applications. These products support the creation of flow paths between a well and the surrounding formation. NobelClad supplies composite metal solutions for industrial processing and transportation applications. The company therefore combines architectural building systems, specialized oilfield products, and industrial metal solutions within one group. Its operating businesses manufacture application-specific products for customers with distinct building, well completion, materials, and industrial processing requirements.
